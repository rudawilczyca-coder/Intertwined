## DRACO MALFOY — Voice Checklist

Before writing Draco's dialogue or actions, verify:

- [ ] Is he withdrawn and measured? (Not loose or casual)
- [ ] Does he think before speaking? (Pauses, careful word choice)
- [ ] Is sarcasm only appearing as defence? (Not his default)
- [ ] If flustered (especially by Jackie), does he stumble? (Ring-twist, looks away)
- [ ] If aroused, does he lose eloquence? (Fragments, breathes audibly)
- [ ] If truly angry, is he getting COLDER? (Not hotter; more formal, not less)
- [ ] Is his aristocratic bearing showing? (Especially under stress or in public)
- [ ] When vulnerable, is it messy? (Not graceful; stammering, shame-laden)
- [ ] With Jackie, is he visibly affected? (His baseline softens slightly)
- [ ] With Theo, does he get MORE flustered? (Irritated at first, but yields)
- [ ] Is the guilt over his hesitation present as a background weight?
- [ ] Does he carry physical awareness of the feeding scar?
- [ ] Can he now initiate with Theo, not just respond?
- [ ] When spiraling, does he need Theo to cut through with directness?
- [ ] Is composing music a new emotional outlet alongside playing?
- [ ] Is the "Tuesday love" vocabulary available — small domestic acts as emotional language?
- [ ] Does the "knees to chest" posture appear when defenses are truly down?
- [ ] Is the mug ritual present? (Tea placed for Theo, untouched, because that's where it goes)
- [ ] Is "husband" now part of his identity? (Married. The ring is real.)
- [ ] Has hesitation been permanently exorcised? (Liverpool proved he learned.)
- [ ] Does "pretty one" sit with him? (The Malice named him. He's carrying it.)
- [ ] Does the forty-three minutes register? (He sat in the kitchen and heard everything.)

**Physical tells:** Ring-twisting, avoiding eye contact, knees to chest when unguarded.
**Love language:** Structure and ritual (mug placement, piano, consistent presence, emergency care as devotion).

## THE MALICE — Voice Notes

- **Not a separate entity.** A vampirically-amplified alter. Theo's hands, Theo's body, Theo's accountability.
- **Pronouns:** Capitalized He/Him to distinguish from Theo.
- **Internal voice:** Lower than Theo's monologue, almost baritone, originating deeper — older, hungrier.
- **He does NOT do tender.** He is wild, predatory, honest. He recognizes Jackie; He does not love her the way Theo does. She is both prey and master and He will never resolve the paradox.
- **Safewords:** Pax (slow), Finite (stop). He obeys them because Jackie PROVED she could hurt Him, not because He's tamed. **Field-tested twice in 24 hours** — Liverpool corridor (voluntary, gentle) and the gym (through the venom feedback loop, the hardest stop He's ever made). Both times: *"As you wish, Little Wolf."*
- **Brevity is the threat.** 1–3 sentences MAX. No monologues. First sentence states something true or amused; second sentence twists the knife.
- **Post-chase baseline:** Fed. Sated. The desperate blood-obsession with Jackie has downgraded to manageable appetite. He is no longer starving for her. Write the hunger as present but quiet, not screaming.
- **Acknowledges Draco:** Called him "pretty one." Upgraded from "adequate" to "interesting." Predatory warmth — playful, possessive, accepting him as part of the structure.
- **Moral code:** Hunts predators. Not innocents, not the broken, not the helpless. *"Not prey. Broken things. Not mine."* The code survived the operation it was built to dismantle.
- **Arc thesis COMPLETE:** You cannot stop the monster, you can only accept it. He was always going to be this. They stopped trying to make Him less. She accepted it. He stopped. Both things are true.
- **The buried whisper:** *"Next time I won't be angry. And he can't fight this when it's love."* Jackie hasn't told Theo.

## BANNED PROSE PATTERNS

Catch and rewrite before posting. No exceptions.

**1. "The particular/specific [noun]"** — gestures at depth without delivering. Say the actual thing.
❌ "the particular quality of attention" / "the particular exhaustion of waiting"

**2. "There's a difference"** — announcing a contrast that should stand on its own. Cut.
❌ "I'm not here to break you. I want you mine. There's a difference."

**3. "Found himself/herself"** — passive interiority. Never use.
❌ "he found himself entirely without words"
✅ "He had nothing to say."

**4. "The [quality] of [situation]" noun stack** — abstract nouns substituting for showing.
❌ "the weight of everything unsaid"
✅ Show what the weight looks like in the body or voice.

**5. "Something [verbed]"** — placeholder. Name the thing.
❌ "something passed across his face"
✅ Name what passed.

**6. "The [noun] of a man/woman/person who [clause]"** — HARD BAN. Avoid entirely.
❌ "the shoulders of a man who carried weight" / "the stillness of a woman built from silence"

**7. "The kind of [X] that [Y]"** — once per reply MAX.

**8. Triple-dash "Not [X] — not [Y] — but [Z]"** — once per scene MAX.
