*Built collaboratively, June 2026. Each mechanic sourced and locked by choice — see inline notes.*
*Design intent: Mercy Thompson–style pack structure as the primary system, flavored for the HP/vampire AU. Serves the emotional arc; never becomes a second rulebook.*

## 1. The Three Wolf-Things (Critical Distinction)

This AU contains three unrelated wolf phenomena. They must never be conflated.

| | **Maledictus (Jackie)** | **Lycanthropy (Ruby's pack)** | **Forbidden Forest wolves** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nature** | Bloodline *curse* | Supernatural *condition/heritage* | True wolves with werewolf ancestry |
| **Mechanism** | Curse expressed through the blood; progressive | Bite-transmitted OR inherited (active or dormant) | Born wolves; offspring of two werewolves who mated in wolf form |
| **Current status** | **Broken.** Jackie carries the bloodline silently — a carrier, like a heritable trait that passes without expressing | Active condition; packs exist across Britain | Gentle, highly intelligent wolves living in the Forbidden Forest; irrelevant to this arc |
| **Cure logic** | Was breakable by ritual — and was broken | Not a curse; nothing to break. Managed, not removed | N/A |

**The one-sentence rule:** *Jackie's wolf was a curse switched off; Ruby's wolf is a heritage switched on.* Different creature, different mechanic, opposite trajectory.

**Why vampire proximity doesn't affect Jackie:** her curse is broken — there is nothing left to wake. The bloodline is silent. Ruby's dormant blood is a different mechanism entirely; it was never expressed, only sleeping.

## 2. Becoming a Werewolf

Three routes:

**1. The bite** of an active werewolf in wolf or near-wolf state.
*Source: AU original.*
Transmission is not guaranteed. Survival rate varies by the bitten person's existing supernatural blood. An ordinary Muggle bitten has a low survival rate — the change often kills. Someone carrying dormant wolf-blood survives a bite that would kill a Muggle, because the wolf in their blood recognizes the signal and responds. This explains why Greyback's army-building was so specifically horrific: he was selecting for supernatural lineage, deliberately or otherwise.

**2. Born active** — child of at least one active werewolf. First shift typically arrives with adolescence, occasionally earlier under stress.

**3. Born dormant → blood-woken** — inherited wolf-blood that sleeps, sometimes for generations. A family can carry the line with no living memory of it.

### Blood-Woken Wolves
Catalysts that can wake dormant blood:
- prolonged proximity to a powerful vampire ★ *(Ruby's vector)*
- exposure to vampire blood
- near-death magical trauma
- severe curse interaction or magical/emotional rupture

**Key principle:** the catalyst does not create the wolf. It wakes what was already there. A blood-woken wolf was never fully human — only unexpressed.

**Stigma:** traditional packs regard vampire-woken wolves as contaminated — "leech-touched," politically suspect. The Blackthorns explicitly reject this. It is background pressure on Ruby's standing in the wider wolf world, not a plot engine in this arc.

## 3. Transformation Mechanics
*Source: Mercy Thompson, primary system.*

- **The full moon compels.** Every active wolf shifts on the full moon. No exceptions, only management.
- **The moon does not erase.** An experienced wolf with pack support keeps their human mind through the change. This is the critical divergence from HP canon's Lupin model — see below.
- **Voluntary shifts** are possible with experience — painful, minutes-long for the practiced, brutal for the new.
- **Mind retention is tied to pack bond, not chemistry.** A wolf with a healthy pack keeps themselves. A packless wolf loses themselves — the human mind fragments under the moon without the anchor of the pack's presence and the Alpha's authority. This is why isolation is one of the cruelest things you can do to a werewolf.

### The Lupin Reconciliation
Lupin was packless and isolated for most of his adult life. Wolfsbane was the only thing standing between him and complete loss of self. The Ministry controlled access to it — meaning they were, functionally, controlling whether he remained human one night a month. This is canon-compliant and newly horrible.

### First Shift
*Always agonizing. No gentle version exists.*
Bones break and remake. The body restructures entirely. There is no blood-woken advantage, no preparation that softens it, no version where it doesn't hurt exactly that much. Moira's expertise exists because someone competent needs to be present for something that cannot be made not-terrible, only survivable. The pack's value is not preventing the pain — it is being the reason the new wolf comes through it with their mind intact.

First-shift protocol at Blackthorn:
- Secure space, lockable from the *inside* — control returned to the new wolf as soon as safely possible
- Minimal crowd; one calm experienced wolf present
- Alpha within range but not looming
- Food and sleep immediately after
- Nobody stares

## 4. Silver
*Source: HP canon — simplified.*

Silver hurts werewolves more than ordinary injury. Silver wounds do not heal differently from other wounds — no special suppression, no accelerated scarring. It is a vulnerability, not a weapon system. The pack keeps a locked silver cabinet not because silver is a constant tactical concern but because the occasional need is real and the storage is responsible.

Silver does not feature heavily in this arc. It exists. That's sufficient.

## 5. Senses
*Source: Mercy Thompson, primary system.*

Scent is the primary sense. Werewolves read:
- emotional states (fear, anger, grief, joy — all present and often embarrassingly legible)
- health (illness, injury, stress)
- species (humans, vampires, magical creatures all smell distinct)
- arousal
- recent history (who someone has been near, what they've handled, where they've been)

New wolves find scent overwhelming and mortifyingly informative. Everything is too loud. The pack's kitchen smells like safety. Strangers smell like data they didn't ask for. The learning curve is real.

### Vampire Scent Specifically
*Source: AU original — dead + age-variable.*

Vampires smell **dead**. Not metaphorically — the absence of living warmth registers as wrongness at a fundamental instinctive level. For a new wolf, the scent triggers avoidance before the human mind has processed anything. For an experienced wolf, it's manageable but never comfortable; the wolf never fully relaxes around it.

**Age variable:** older vampires smell more wrong. The age-depth of a vampire's death registers differently to wolf-sense — an ancient reads as a deeper, older wrongness than a young vampire. Theo, at his age, is manageable for an experienced wolf. An ancient Camarilla elder would be genuinely difficult to be near.

**Practical consequence:** a person who has spent significant time with a vampire carries that scent. After close contact, it saturates skin and clothing. A new wolf post-first-shift encountering someone who smells strongly of vampire is getting the full force of that wrongness regardless of how they feel about the vampire in question.

*This is the engine of Ruby's instinctive drift from Draco toward the pack. The pack smells alive, warm, right. Draco carries Theo's scent and his own vampire-adjacent world on his skin. Her wolf knows the difference before she does, and it never stops knowing.*

## 6. Pack Structure & Hierarchy
*Source: Mercy Thompson, primary system.*

Rank is **felt in the body** — a pressure sense, not an org chart. It operates as a background hum, not a constant overwhelming force. Roughly:

**Alpha → Alpha's mate → Second/Beta → dominants → mid-rank → submissive wolves → elders (functionally rank-exempt) → unranked/outsiders**

- **Dominance ≠ cruelty.** Dominant wolves are not morally superior. Rank is a blend of strength, control, age, temperament, and pack recognition.
- **Submissive ≠ weak.** Submissive wolves are the pack's keel — stable, anchoring, often the most emotionally reliable members. Dominants fight *for* them, not over them.
- **Elders** like Moira sit functionally outside the hierarchy: authority by knowledge and history, not pressure.
- **New wolves enter unranked.** An unranked wolf of unknown strength is a standing perturbation — dominants feel compelled to assess, court, or shelter. This is the engine of the arrival chaos when Ruby joins.

### Alpha Authority
*Source: Mercy Thompson — full supernatural weight.*

An Alpha's command carries real magical weight. A strong Alpha can:
- force stillness in a panicking wolf
- calm a wolf mid-shift
- stop a dominance challenge
- stabilize a new wolf through the worst of a first shift

The wolf-body understands the command before the human mind finishes processing it. Charlie says "sit down" and Ruby's legs are already moving.

**The ethical weight this carries:** for a person whose core wound is bodily autonomy and control, a body that obeys before the mind consents is the trauma re-staged at supernatural volume. Charlie's authority is genuinely stabilizing *and* genuinely violating, simultaneously, and both things are true. This is the central friction of his arc with Ruby. Alpha authority is triage, not governance — ethical use means sparingly, transparently, and with acknowledgment of the cost.

**Hard line:** Alpha authority is never sexual or romantic compulsion. Full stop.

## 7. Wolfsbane
*Source: AU blend — HP canon exists, pack culture rejects it.*

Wolfsbane exists. The Ministry of Magic produces and controls access to it. For a packless wolf, it is the difference between retaining their mind on the full moon and losing it entirely — and the Ministry has always known this, which is why controlling access to it is a form of control over werewolves themselves.

Pack wolves regard Wolfsbane as:
- a Ministry control tool
- a crutch that substitutes chemistry for the pack bond
- something that weakens the wolf physically even as it preserves the mind
- a measure of last resort, not standard practice

Cassian would not touch it. Charlie keeps a supply locked away for emergencies — an injured wolf unable to reach the pack, an isolated wolf in crisis — and hopes to never use it.

*Standard Blackthorn practice: the pack bond is the mechanism. Wolfsbane is what you use when you have no pack.*

## 8. Mate Recognition & Bonds
*Source: Mercy Thompson, primary system.*

Wolves can clock a potential mate — through scent, instinctive recognition, dominance resonance, a register of safety-or-challenge — often before the human mind has processed anything. Recognition is information. It creates no ownership, no obligation, no override of consent.

**The bond:** mate bonds are real and magical, but they deepen what already exists between two people — they manufacture nothing. A bond requires mutual, human-level choice made knowingly. Recognition is the wolf's read; the bond is the human's decision.

**The law:**
> *A wolf may recognize. A human must choose.*

Multiple wolves can recognize the same person. That creates tension, not fate.

**Moira's formulation:**
> *"It gets a vote. It does not get a veto."*

## 9. Werewolves & Wizarding Authority
*Source: AU blend.*

The Ministry of Magic's Being Division maintains a werewolf registry. Registration is theoretically mandatory; enforcement is inconsistent and almost entirely punitive when it does occur. The registry does not help registered wolves — it restricts them (employment limitations, travel monitoring, mandatory check-ins during full moons) and makes them findable.

Packs regard the registry as a threat dressed as bureaucracy. Most pack wolves are unregistered and intend to stay that way.

**Cassian's entire professional existence** is the infrastructure of non-compliance: clean identities, property held through layered legal structures, medical incidents quietly resolved, missing-person explanations managed, the paper reality of a pack that officially does not exist as a pack. He is very good at his job.

**The Wolfsbane connection:** Ministry control of Wolfsbane access is the most direct lever they hold over werewolves — controlling the chemistry that keeps packless wolves human once a month. The packs know this. They don't discuss it with new wolves immediately.

**Vampire–werewolf politics:** instinctive hostility, ritualized politeness where territories force contact. A vampire near a new wolf is a serious breach of pack etiquette regardless of intent. The Camarilla and the packs maintain deliberate, wary distance from each other. Neither wants the other's attention.

## 10. Hard Limits — What We Don't Import

**From World of Darkness — not used:**
- Umbra, spirit courts, Garou Nation, tribes, auspices
- Rage as a mechanical stat
- Crinos war-form / multiple fixed forms
- Apocalypse cosmology
- Werewolf–vampire ancient war mythology
- Any WoD metaphysics

**From classic omegaverse — not used:**
- Omega rank (skipped entirely for this AU)
- Heat/rut mechanics that override agency
- Alpha command as sexual compulsion
- Biological determinism as destiny
- Omega as reproductive/sexual caste

**What we do use:**
- Scent-based attraction and recognition
- Territoriality and protective instinct
- Pack pressure and politics
- Mate-prospect tension
- Denning/nesting instincts where useful
- The conflict between instinct and human choice

The body may respond. The person decides what it means.

## 11. Tone Note

Found family with teeth. Supernatural triage in a warm kitchen. Old rules, humane revisions. Rural secrecy, locked silver, good soup.

The Blackthorns are not safe because they are harmless. They are safe because they choose restraint — every day, on purpose.

The wizarding world built its entire werewolf policy on observing packless, isolated wolves and understood almost nothing. The Ministry's greatest weapon against werewolves has always been keeping them alone.
