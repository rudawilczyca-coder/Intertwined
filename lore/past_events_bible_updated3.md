### 1994–1997: Early History

**1994 (Hogwarts Year 4)**
- Jackie transforms into wolf for the first time during winter; Maledictus curse inheritance confirmed (inherited from Amalthea). Transformation lasts one week; will lengthen each year, eventually becoming permanent.
- Samaya dates Draco Malfoy for a year after reading Jackie's diary and discovering her crush on him. (Samaya later confesses she broke it off because Draco spent half their dates asking about Jackie — her interests, her habits, what made her laugh.)
- Theo realizes he's attracted to men while watching Samaya date Draco; recognizes his crush on Draco himself. Converts it into rivalry; begins dating various people.

**1995 (Hogwarts Year 5)**
- **June:** Jackie kisses Draco for the first time at Parkinson's Gala (gazebo, gardens). She's been in love with him for over a year.
- **Summer:** Jackie writes Draco dozens of letters; none arrive. They were intercepted — likely by Lucius Malfoy's household or Pansy's interference. Draco receives nothing and assumes Jackie forgot him or changed her mind. Jackie assumes he doesn't care.
- **Winter:** Jackie transforms for one week.

**1996 (Hogwarts Year 6)**
- Jackie sees Draco struggling but chooses not to approach him.
- Draco becomes a Death Eater; Theo becomes harsh but ensures Draco survives it. Theo fears he's next.
- **Winter:** Jackie transforms for two weeks.

**1997 (Hogwarts Year 7)**
- Jackie pulled from school by her father (fear of Death Eaters weaponizing her curse). Stays home.

### 1998: Hogwarts Final Year & Betrothal

**September 1, 1998 — Hogwarts Express**

- **Morning before departure:** Cyrus Taylor informs Jackie she is to marry Theodore Nott the following summer. No discussion, no negotiation. Jackie learns Samaya is exempt. The Notts are nearly bankrupt; the Taylors need political allies after Amalthea's "illness" became gossip. Jackie is the expendable daughter — damaged goods with a blood curse, sold to the only family desperate enough to accept.
- **On the train:** Draco seeks Jackie out in her compartment, uninvited. He hasn't seen her all summer — no letters (he doesn't know she sent dozens; they were intercepted). Their dynamic is established instantly: sharp banter layered over years of wanting. Draco notices something wrong in her posture and pushes — *"What did your father do this time?"* Jackie deflects. When she accidentally mentions Pansy as Draco's future wife, he snaps: *"You have no idea what I want."* Storms out without finishing the conversation.
- **Great Hall / Sorting Feast:** Theo locks eyes with Jackie across the hall — cold, possessive, a silent toast with his goblet. Draco sees it. Blaise physically restrains him. Theo baits Draco openly: *"Some arrangements are beyond even your interference."* Jackie catches the exchange; Anne warns her not to engage.
- **Ravenclaw dormitory, that night:** Jackie tells Anne about the betrothal. Anne reacts with fury — suggests legal emancipation, sanctuary laws, a blood-curse researcher in Boston. Jackie shuts it down: she can't leave her mother (caged as a permanent wolf on the Taylor estate), and she needs her father's money and resources to research the curse. They agree to fight it together from inside Hogwarts. Treacle tart smuggled from the feast; the dormitory girls pile onto Jackie's bed like first-years. Brief comfort before the storm.

**First Week of September — Charms Class: The Protean Rings**

- Schedule reveals three shared classes with Slytherin: Potions, DADA, and Charms.
- **Charms (Protean Charm):** Theo approaches Jackie to partner up; grabs her wrist without permission. Jackie warns him directly: *"If you touch me without my consent again, I'll make sure you watch Quidditch tryouts from the hospital wing."* Draco crosses the room in three strides, wand pressed to Theo's ribs, hidden by their cloaks. Blaise defuses — suggests Theo partner with him instead, Draco with Jackie.
- **The spell:** Jackie and Draco's Protean Charm synchronization is immediate, powerful, and flawless — silver and sapphire magic fusing into a single cord. Flitwick awards ten points each. The bond carries the signature of each other's magic: Draco's (precise, cold, restrained) and Jackie's (sharp, wild, layered). Both visibly shaken by the intimacy of it.
- **Theo's confrontation:** Publicly taunts Draco — *"She's promised. Signed, sealed, and soon-to-be delivered. To me."* Draco puts wand under Theo's jaw. Both receive detention.
- **After class:** Jackie follows Draco into a dungeon corridor. She confronts him directly — accuses him of not wanting her himself but refusing to let anyone else have her. Draco nearly breaks: *"You have no idea what I want."* Tells her to leave before he does something they'll regret. Jackie presses the ring into his palm: *"Then don't beg. Don't burn the castle down. Just stop pretending none of it mattered."* She also asks him NOT to step between her and Theo again — it only gives Theo what he wants.

**That Night — The Ring Messages Begin**

- Draco sends the first message: **ᛇ** (Yew — resilience, endurance). He chose runes because he knew Jackie's passion for Ancient Runes — the gesture itself a declaration.
- Blaise finds him in the Owlery afterward, warns him: Theo is already owling his father. Reveals the financial truth — the Notts are broke, the betrothal is collateral. Draco: *"Then we make them broker."*
- Jackie responds during a gap after Astronomy class: **ᚢ** (Uruz — will, courage) + ***Semper coniuncti*** (Forever linked).
- This becomes their nightly ritual: one message each before sleep. The ring is too small for long texts, so they develop an emotional shorthand — runes, Latin fragments, single words that carry the weight of whole conversations. Examples: *Mannaz* (friendship), *Isa* (clarity/I'm here), *"Nott sugit"* / *"Flocci non facio"* (Nott sucks / I don't care about his insults), *"Fessus sum"* / *"Me paenitet"* (I'm exhausted / I'm sorry).

**Mid-September — Theo's Taunts Fade**

- Draco honors Jackie's request: no more physical confrontations with Theo. It costs him visibly — clenched fists, jaw working — but he holds. Theo's taunts about "wifely duties" and Jackie's flaws (spoken as if she's livestock) become repetitive, then cease entirely by mid-September.

**Third Monday of September — Theo's Truce Proposal**

- Theo approaches Jackie after Charms, asks to speak privately. His tone is different — no smirk, something approaching earnestness. Claims the betrothal shocked him too; his father sprung it on him the day before the train.
- **His offer:** Give it a genuine try until the Malfoy New Year's Eve Ball. If they're truly incompatible, he'll go to their fathers *together* with Jackie to dissolve the contract. A united front carries more weight than either of them raging alone.
- **His condition:** No Malfoy. Keep meetings between the two of them only.
- Jackie's internal conflict: she knows Anne and Draco would both advise against it, but the logic appeals to her pragmatism. If Theo joins her plea, the fathers might actually listen.
- **Jackie agrees.** Deadline: New Year's Eve Ball. Clean slate, one chance.
- She tells no one — not Draco, not Anne, not her friends.
- **That evening:** For the first time in two weeks, the ring stays cold and silent. Draco notices immediately.

### Late September – November 1998: Escalation

**Late September — First Hogsmeade Meeting with Theo (Three Broomsticks)**

- Jackie lies to Anne and her friends about where she's going ("Charms revision"). Meets Theo at a corner booth; he already has butterbeers waiting.
- They attempt genuine conversation for the first time — favorite colors, subjects, personal questions. Theo's answer to favorite color: *"Emerald green. Not the garish flash of Slytherin banners. The deep kind — like the Killing Curse just before it hits."* Jackie clocks this but doesn't press.
- Jackie gives him something real: admits her mother is sick and that she carries the same illness. Doesn't reveal the Maledictus curse specifically, but says it's hereditary and her future is uncertain. Theo recalibrates visibly — asks whether his father knows, whether the contract's unusual clauses relate to her condition.
- Draco briefly appears at the pub entrance; Blaise pulls him away. Theo notices and smirks — but the satisfaction is layered. He's not just pleased that Draco is jealous of *him*; he's gratified that Draco cared enough to show up at all. Theo's possessiveness over Jackie has always been tangled with his unacknowledged fixation on Draco — claiming what Draco wants is the closest Theo allows himself to getting Draco's full, undivided attention.
- Jackie insists Draco is nothing to her — she *needs* Theo unaware of whatever exists between her and Draco, because if Theo suspects more, his pride will turn it into a weapon.
- Jackie asks how Theo and Draco became friends. Theo's answer: *"Slytherins don't have friends. We have allies. Shared interests. Convenient distractions."* Admits the moment their interests diverge, they'd gut each other with silver spoons and call it tradition.
- Jackie tells Theo flatly that she's not impressed — a life where everything is a transaction or a weakness sounds unbearably lonely. Pays for both their drinks with four sickles — a deliberate reminder of how far the Nott fortune has fallen.
- Sets the terms for their next meeting: library, studying. Her turf, her rules.

**After Hogsmeade — Draco Confronts Jackie**

- Draco catches Jackie leaving the Three Broomsticks, grabs her wrist from a narrow alley. He saw her with Theo through the window. *"So this is why you've been ignoring the ring. Secret rendezvous with Nott?"*
- Jackie explains Theo's offer — give it a genuine try, prove incompatibility, dissolve the engagement together.
- Draco eviscerates the logic: *"You think that's how contracts like yours work? That your fathers give a single fuck about compatibility? He's toying with you until you're too entangled to escape."*
- Delivers his ultimatum: *"When he drags you into the dark, don't expect me to follow."*
- Jackie is devastated. Sends an apology through the ring that night: **ᛗ** (Mannaz — humility, I'm sorry) + ***Turris astronomica. Media nox.*** (Astronomy Tower. Midnight.)

**That Night — Astronomy Tower, Midnight**

- Draco comes. Jackie recites from memory the first letter she ever wrote him — the one about the gazebo kiss ("it was actually my first kiss"), the butterflies, asking him to Hogsmeade. Word for word, as if reading it aloud. She'd written twelve drafts before sending it.
- Draco is shattered: *"Letters? I never received them."* Realizes they were intercepted — likely by his father's household, or Pansy's interference. Two years of silence were built on a lie neither of them created.
- Draco's confession: *"I want you so much it feels like dying every time I walk away."*
- Jackie begs him to show her, to give her a reason to stay. Draco refuses to take what she's offering — not in secret, not in shadow. *"When I have you, it will be because you're mine. Openly, freely, without fear."*
- Jackie leaves with quiet acceptance: *"Just remember that it's you I want, not him."*

**October 3 (Saturday) — Library: The Trivia Bet**

- By their third meeting, Jackie grudgingly admits Theo has grown on her. His jabs hurt less; his certainty is almost contagious. He reads her Runes textbooks unprompted. What unnerves her is the way he looks at her — predatory intensity that both scares her and wakes something she doesn't want to name.
- Theo proposes a game: Hogwarts History Trivia, five questions each, loser accepts the winner's punishment.
- They're matched until the final question — Theo pulls an obscure question from *Magical Law in the Middle Ages*, not *Hogwarts: A History*. Jackie loses on a technicality.
- **Theo's punishment: "Kiss me. Properly. If you feel nothing, I'll never ask again."**
- Jackie makes Blaise leave first — doesn't want witnesses who could report to Draco. Part of Theo wants Draco to find out. Part of him needs this to exist in a space where Draco can't reach.
- She kisses Theo. Against every intention, she feels something — a spark in her core. He deepens the kiss; his hand slides to the small of her back, then under the hem of her jeans seeking bare skin. She shoves him away hard.
- For a split second her eyes flash molten gold — the wolf surfacing. Theo notices.
- Theo: *"You felt it too. Don't lie."* Jackie: *"I. Felt. Nothing."* Both know she's lying.
- Theo's reaction carries a double edge he'd never admit: he's proving to himself that he can want *her*, that this isn't just another way of orbiting Draco. That the spark is real and his own. The fact that it IS real unsettles him more than her rejection.

**Same Day — The Changing Room Kiss**

- Shaken by what she felt with Theo, Jackie goes to the Quidditch pitch. Waits for Slytherin practice to end. Finds Draco alone in the changing room, half-dressed (black undershirt, boxer briefs).
- She crosses the room and kisses him — reckless, desperate, needing to prove that what she feels for Draco burns brighter than whatever Theo stirred.
- Draco responds with force — pins her against the lockers, deepens the kiss, one hand tangled in her hair. For a moment, both are lost in it.
- Then he pulls back. Smells Theo's cologne on her skin. *"You smell like him. What happened in the library, Jackie?"*
- Jackie confesses: lost a bet, Theo kissed her, she felt something — *"But it wasn't this. Not even close."*
- Draco, wrecked: *"When he had his tongue down your throat, were you thinking about our rings? Or were you too busy moaning into his mouth?"*
- Jackie, desperate and self-destructive: *"Maybe it would be easier for all of us if we just forgot what's between us."*
- Draco: *"I've been trying to let you go for two years. It didn't work."*
- He walks away. Later that evening, punches Theo in the Slytherin common room (fist, not magic). Theo laughs through the blood on his lip. The taunt — *"Tell me, does she make those same little noises when—"* — is calculated to provoke, but there's something reckless in how far he pushes. Theo goads Draco into physical violence the way someone tests an electric fence: not because they want the shock, but because they need to know the current is still live.

**November — First Transformation & The Hospital Wing**

- Temperature drops; Jackie is woken by her body shifting — the first transformation of seventh year. Lasts only minutes but leaves her exhausted. Sent to the hospital wing.
- Madam Pomfrey gives her a monitoring bracelet: thin steel band with seven enchanted stones that change color with her heart rate and heat the metal if transformation risk spikes.
- **Draco visits.** Finds her in bed in hippogriff-and-dragon pajamas, reading Astronomy. He pushes past her deflection, notices the bracelet: *"What happened? And don't tell me it's nothing. You look like hell."*
- **Jackie reveals the truth:** She's a Maledictus. Turns into a wolf when it gets cold. Her mother has the same curse — permanently transformed, kept caged on the Taylor estate. Jackie's transformations are getting longer each year.
- She frames the betrothal through this: her father wants her married quickly because her time may be limited. The Malfoys were approached first but declined — a cursed bloodline would taint their perfect lineage. The Notts, bankrupt and desperate, were the only family willing to settle for "damaged goods."
- Draco's response: *"I'm going to fix this. I don't know how yet. But I swear to Merlin, I'm going to fix this."*
- Jackie: *"You can't fix me, Draco. I'm broken beyond repair."*
- During the weeks that follow, as Draco shifts from jealous rival to someone researching blood curses late into the night, Theo watches the change with complicated feelings. He tells himself it's strategic — if Draco finds a cure, it benefits his investment too. But the truth is simpler: watching Draco care about something this deeply makes Theo ache in ways he refuses to examine.

### December 1998 – January 1999: The Long Winter

**Late November / Early December — The Forest Edge**

- Jackie's transformations are becoming more frequent — brief shifts lasting minutes, triggered by cold and emotional spikes. The monitoring bracelet runs hot constantly. She knows the long winter turn is imminent.
- She asks Draco to meet her at the edge of the Forbidden Forest at sundown. She's already told him she's a Maledictus (hospital wing, November), but this is different — she wants him to *see* it. To witness what she becomes, not just hear about it.
- The bracelet flares red as her body begins to shift. Jackie barely gets the words out: *"That out there — that's a pack of wolves I join when I change. They're kind of like family."*
- The transformation happens in front of him — violent, fast, fur erupting over skin, bones reshaping. A massive ruddy-brown wolf stands where Jackie was. Golden eyes lift to meet Draco's.
- Draco crouches to eye level. Extends his hand, palm up. The wolf approaches, sniffs, and licks his fingers. Jackie pushes from inside the wolf's mind: *Friend. Safe. This one is ours.*
- Draco: *"Run fast, Jackie. But come back to me."* He takes her wand and scarf for safekeeping.

**December 1998 — The Long Turn**

- Jackie doesn't return the next morning. She remains in wolf form for the entire month of December — her longest transformation yet. Cover story at Hogwarts: specialist hospital in Switzerland. Cyrus Taylor maintains the lie.
- **Draco stays at Hogwarts over Christmas**, making excuses about coursework. Every morning without exception, he walks to the edge of the Forbidden Forest and waits for an hour in the cold, Jackie's Ravenclaw scarf clutched beneath his cloak.
- The Malfoy New Year's Eve Ball goes ahead. Jackie's engagement to Theo is formally announced in her absence — Cyrus raises a glass to the "happy news" while his daughter runs with wolves in the Forbidden Forest. Draco attends out of obligation, barely containing his fury.
- With Jackie gone, Draco and Theo fall into brittle truce. Old habits resurface — mocking first-years, sharing contraband dragonash cigarettes in the common room. For Theo, Jackie's absence strips away the buffer between him and Draco. The old pull resurfaces; he buries it deeper than ever.

**Early January 1999 — The Return**

- The snow begins to melt. One morning at the forest's edge, the alpha wolf appears — hackles raised, growling at Draco. Jackie's wolf darts between them, facing down the alpha in a tense standoff. The alpha submits and retreats. The pack howls farewell; Jackie's wolf joins them in one final, mournful note.
- The wolf vanishes. Jackie kneels naked in the snow, trembling, steam rising from her skin. *"You waited…"* She collapses.
- **Draco carries her to his Slytherin dormitory** — not the hospital wing, where Pomfrey would owl her father. He stokes the fire, leaves food and his own sweater. She's emaciated, barely able to speak — her throat unused to forming words after a month.

**That Night — Sharing the Bed**

- Jackie asks Draco to stay. Not for intimacy — she fears being alone when aftershocks might trigger another shift. They share the bed. Jackie wears a transfigured silk robe in Slytherin green.
- Pansy knocks in the middle of the night looking for Draco. He silences Jackie with a charm, glamours the pillow, sends Pansy away cold. Pansy's parting shot: *"Theo's looking for you."*
- An argument ignites — Jackie furious at being made to feel guilty for turning, Draco furious at the time lost. The bracelet flares red. Jackie's eyes go gold, teeth and nails sharpen. She transforms right there in his bedroom.
- The wolf doesn't attack. It settles by the hearth and guards Draco while he sleeps. Jackie pushes from inside: *This one is ours. We are fine.* The wolf watches over him all night — the first clear sign that the animal recognizes and protects Draco independently of Jackie's conscious will.

**January 6, 1999 — The Morning After**

- Jackie wakes human at dawn. Transfigures the robe into a green dress, slips out of Draco's room.
- **Theo catches her in the Slytherin common room.** Silk pajamas, perfectly tousled hair. *"Going somewhere, fiancée?"* Sees the dress, her bare feet, her damp hair. Assumes she slept with Draco.
- **Blackmail:** Threatens to owl her father about finding her in Draco's room. Threatens Anne's Auror career with leaked rumors. Threatens Draco's reputation with whispers about dark artifacts and instability. *"Your secret, or their futures."*
- Jackie reveals the Maledictus curse to buy his silence — the full truth this time. Theo's reaction is violent: a backhand across her face, then a brutal kiss.
- **Theo forces himself on Jackie.** Pins her, uses a binding spell on her wrists. The encounter is violent, hateful, and complicated — Jackie fights verbally but her body responds against her will. She orgasms, which devastates her sense of self more than the act itself.
- What drives Theo in this moment is something he has no name for yet — a darkness that surfaces during emotional extremes, feeding on his rage and entitlement, amplifying his worst impulses beyond his control. (This is the Malice, though neither Theo nor Jackie will identify it as a distinct entity for years.) The darkness is tangled with his genuine fury at being deceived about the curse, his inherited pureblood conditioning that treats claiming as a right, and — buried deepest — his inability to process the fact that the two people he wants most in the world (Jackie *and* Draco) are choosing each other over him.
- **Afterward:** Jackie at the door: *"This changes nothing. I still hate you."* She showers in the Ravenclaw bathroom until the water runs cold.

### The Space Between (January 13 – February 26, 1999)

**January 13 — Potions (First Shared Class Since the Assault)**

- Jackie hasn't slept properly since January 6th. She flinches at every touch — even Anne's, even Caroline's, even Luna (who crawled into her bed one night and whispered *"You feel like a thunderstorm that forgot how to rain"*). She sits back row, far left, closest to the door.
- Theo is at the table directly in front of her. He doesn't look at her once — and the erasure feels like its own violence.
- Her pestle slips; they both reach for it. Their fingers brush for less than a second. Jackie recoils, hits her shoulder on the underside of the table, makes a sound dangerously close to a whimper.
- She looks at him. His eyes are *wrong* — not the flat darkness of January 6th, but raw, naked shame. Guilt bleeding out of him like a wound he can't staunch. He pulls back. Walks away without a word.
- Jackie stays crouched on the floor, trembling. Recognizes something: *That wasn't the thing that hurt you. That was Theo.*
- Caroline watches everything. Files it away. Says nothing. Begins building a case.

**January 20 — The Library**

- Jackie has been avoiding Draco for two weeks. The ring pulses constantly with his confusion and hurt. She wears it on a chain around her neck instead of her finger.
- Anne and Caroline gently confront her — they know something is wrong beyond the transformation. Caroline: *"You flinch when people touch you. You haven't eaten a proper meal since before the full moon."* Jackie deflects. Anne doesn't push, but leaves the door open: *"Whatever it is. Whenever you're ready."*
- Caroline slides a paper bag across the table without comment: two chocolate frogs and a sugar quill. Jackie nearly cracks.

**January 28 — The Corridor**

- Draco catches her after dinner. He's been giving her space; he's running out of patience. He thinks he did something wrong the night she stayed in his room.
- He's been researching contract loopholes. Jackie: *"It's not the contract."* Draco: *"Then WHAT?"* His voice cracks — Draco Malfoy, who survived Voldemort, whose voice cracks because she's pulling away and he doesn't understand why.
- Jackie can't tell him. The guilt is crushing — he's staying up at night trying to save her from a contract when the real wound is something she can't name. She asks for space. He gives it, hurt but trying.

**February 3 — The Courtyard (The Gloves)**

- Jackie sits alone in biting February snow, numb in every sense. Theo approaches. She goes rigid — full-body panic response she can't hide.
- He doesn't step closer. Slowly withdraws a pair of dark green wool gloves. *"Take them. Please."*
- Jackie: *"You don't get to act like you care."* Theo: *"I know I don't."* His hand is shaking. *"I know what I did."*
- Jackie: *"Don't say it. Don't SAY it."* — because naming it makes it real, makes it something she can't fold up and put away.
- Theo: *"I'm sorry."* Two words. They break something open in her chest.
- She leaves the gloves on the bench. But later, alone, she lets herself remember the way his voice cracked and thinks: *That sounded like the real Theo.*

**February 10 — Watching**

- Anne and Caroline have stopped asking questions. They've adjusted — sitting closer, leaving small gifts (books, articles, once a tiny bottle of dreamless sleep potion with a note: *for the nights when the thoughts won't stop*).
- Jackie notices Theo isn't eating. His face has gone gaunt. She watches him leave the Great Hall one evening with his hands pressed against his temples and follows at a distance — he stands in an empty corridor for ten minutes, breathing, until whatever is happening inside him subsides.
- She recognizes the signs. The wolf came with warnings too — tension, restlessness, the human mind loosening its grip. Theo's darkness doesn't come with a calendar. It comes without warning, and he doesn't know how to read the signs.
- Caroline, observing Jackie watching Theo: *"You watch Malfoy like you're in love with him. You watch Nott like you're trying to solve him."*

**February 14 — Astronomy Tower**

- Jackie ends up at the Astronomy Tower on Valentine's Day. Draco is already there. She lets him kiss her — gentle, tender, his hands cradling her face. It makes her want to scream, because tenderness feels like a lie now.
- Draco pushes: *"Tell me he did something. Tell me he hurt you."* Jackie: *"He didn't hurt me."* The lie comes out flat, automatic.
- Draco knows. He can see it — the flinching, the distance, the way she's been falling apart. *"I've been telling myself it's the curse, the transformation, anything other than what I know it is."* He deduces the blackmail, realizes Theo knows about the curse.
- Jackie begs him not to act on it. Draco: *"And you're protecting him?"* Jackie: *"I'm not protecting him. I'm protecting... I don't know what I'm protecting."*

**February 19 — The Library**

- Jackie finds Theo at her usual table. He's left three feet of empty space — deliberate. He doesn't look at her when she stops; he's giving her the choice. She sits.
- Jackie: *"You're not eating."* Theo: *"Why do you care?"* Jackie: *"Because I'm trying to understand. And I can't understand anything about you if you're dead."*
- Jackie names the core of it: *"The night of January 6th, you weren't you. Not all of you. There was something else — something that used your voice and your hands but wasn't you."* She recognizes it because she lives with the same thing — the wolf, the loss of control, the experience of being trapped inside your own body.
- Theo: *"Does the difference matter?"* Jackie: *"It matters to me."*
- Jackie's declaration: *"I'm not forgiving you. But I'm not going to pretend you're only one thing. Whatever is inside you that made you do that — I'm going to figure out what it is. And I'm going to figure out how to stop it."*

**February 26 — The Letter**

- Jackie writes Theo a letter. Key passage: *"I'm not going to forgive you, because forgiveness isn't something you can ask for and I don't know if I'll ever be able to give it. But I'm going to help you fight whatever is inside you. Not because you deserve it. Because I know what it's like to carry something that takes over, and I know how lonely that is."*
- The decision isn't hope exactly. It's something smaller and more stubborn: the decision to try.

### The Truce (March – April 1999)

**March 2 — Theo Approaches Draco**

- Theo finds Draco in the Slytherin common room. Nervous — Draco has seen this exact brand of Theo-nervousness only twice before (his father's Wizengamot hearing in fourth year, and once during the war when Theo returned from a mission with blood that wasn't his).
- Theo reveals Jackie told him about the Maledictus curse. Wants to pool research on a cure. *"I expect you to want Jackie cured more than you want to keep hating me."*
- Draco's every instinct screams no — but he's been hitting dead ends for months. He agrees to see Theo's research. Sets hard boundaries: *"We're doing research. That's all. The moment you step outside that boundary, I'm done."*

**March — The Easing (Jackie's Performance)**

- Jackie notices the change in Draco — his smiles come easier, he stops watching her like she'll break. She realizes: *He thinks it's over. He thinks I've processed it and moved on.*
- She hasn't. She's performing recovery — laughing, eating, staying up late studying — and Draco believes it because he *wants* to believe it. She lets him, because the alternative (telling him the truth, watching him become the cold violent thing she knows lives under his skin) is worse.
- She watches Draco leave the common room three nights a week at eight o'clock, return with ink-stained fingers. Watches him stop flinching at Theo's name. The truce is forming, and she's allowing Draco to ally with the person who assaulted her without knowing the truth. The guilt is unbearable.

**March 15 — Shared Library Table**

- Theo starts sitting at Jackie's library table. Three feet of deliberate space between them. Neither speaks for ten minutes. Then Theo shares research — a fourteenth-century ritual framework for separating a cursed aspect from a host body (host died, but the theory is sound).
- *"Draco and I have been working on it. He's better at the theoretical math. I'm better at finding obscure sources. We make a... functional team."*
- Jackie and Theo develop a cautious working rhythm. He's smart — she always knew this, but seeing it up close is different. He asks good questions, listens without talking over her, doesn't condescend. It's confusing. She wanted him to be a simple monster; he isn't.
- The three feet shrink to two, then one. The first time their elbows accidentally touch, Jackie goes rigid — but Theo pulls back immediately, mumbles an apology. By late March, she's stopped flinching when he speaks.

**Late March — The Ritual Discovery**

- Draco finds Jackie after dinner, barely containing excitement. He and Theo have found a three-part binding ritual: Maledictus (the cursed), an anchor (magical-emotional bond), and a sacrifice (something precious gets severed in the process).
- Jackie's internal conflict: she's grateful, moved by Draco's devotion — and lying to him about why this alliance makes her sick with guilt.

**April 5 — The Anchor Problem**

- Jackie and Theo discuss the ritual mechanics at the library table (now nearly touching). The anchor needs someone emotionally connected but not blood-related — strong enough bond, no contamination risk.
- Jackie suggests the Protean rings qualify as a magical bond. *"You're talking about Draco,"* Theo says. His quill nearly snaps in his grip.
- Jackie notices his reaction but doesn't push. Files it away: another data point in the growing catalogue of Theo's silences and shutdowns.
- What Jackie doesn't see: Theo's jealousy isn't only about losing Jackie to Draco. It's about being *excluded* — from their bond, their connection, the closed circuit between them that he can never enter. He wants to be the anchor not because it would bind him to Jackie, but because it would place him at the center of something that includes Draco.

**April 8 — Theo's Confession**

- The library is empty. Theo tells Draco: *"I'm falling for her."*
- Draco erupts — fury, betrayal, the accusation that Theo will *have* her regardless through the contract so this "confession" is meaningless posturing.
- Theo cracks: *"I don't have her. She looks at me like I'm something she's trying to understand, not something she wants."* Then, barely audible: *"There's something inside me. Something that takes over. And on January 6th, it did something to her. Something I can't take back."*
- He can't name it. Won't name it. *"If I tell you, you'll never forgive me. And I need at least one person in this world who hasn't decided I'm irredeemable."*
- Draco connects the pieces — the flinching, the distance, the weeks Jackie fell apart. He doesn't know the specifics, but he *understands* — because he carries his own darkness from the war, his own fear of what lives under his skin.
- Draco: *"I don't forgive you. But if Jackie thinks you're worth saving, I'll keep working with you. For her sake. Not yours."*
- Draco makes it back to the Slytherin common room before he realizes he's crying.

**April 9 — Aftermath**

- Jackie notices both men are wrong at breakfast. Draco has his back to her (unprecedented). Theo looks diminished, red-rimmed eyes, food untouched — the expression of defeat.
- The ring pulses against her chest: Draco's pain, anger, and a grief so profound it feels like mourning.
- She doesn't know what died. She suspects she's about to find out.

### Late April – May 1999: Samaya's Visit & The Gentleman's War

**Late April — Samaya's Visit**

- Samaya Taylor arrives at Hogwarts unannounced on a Sunday morning — sweeping into the Great Hall in a forest-green gothic dress, black heeled boots, and a capelet tied with a precise bow. Heads turn. Jackie groans internally. *Poser.*
- Samaya has explicit permission from Flitwick to take Jackie for the day. Cover story: wedding dress fittings at Twilfitt and Tatting's. Real purpose: Samaya wants the truth.
- On the walk to the gates, Samaya chatters about Paris, her flat — and mentions a man named Pierre who works at the French Ministry of Magic. He courted her for four months before she agreed to a date. Jackie promises not to tell their father.
- Jackie drops the bombshell casually just before they Apparate: *"As for Draco. I kissed him, he kissed me. No big deal."* They vanish before Samaya can react.

**Twilfitt and Tatting's — The Fitting**

- Samaya navigates the shop with effortless authority — dismisses assistants, demands privacy, discards her father's monitoring brooch (charmed to report back) onto a futon. They're free to talk.
- **Samaya's confession:** She dated Draco after reading Jackie's diary and discovering her crush. She was fifteen and competitive. But she broke it off because Draco spent half their dates asking about Jackie. *"It was positively tragic for my ego."*
- Jackie is stunned. Draco was interested in *her* even then.
- Jackie chooses her wedding dress: almost white with the faintest hint of lilac blue, simple bodice opening into rich cascading fabric below the waist. Deliberately unconventional — Samaya: *"Father will hate it. Which is exactly why we're getting it."*

**The Confession**

- Jackie tells Samaya everything: Draco's feelings, the Protean rings, the kiss in the Astronomy Tower, Theo's truce offer, her growing complicated feelings for Theo — and then January 6th.
- Samaya's champagne glass slips from her fingers and shatters. Her composure cracks completely. *"He forced you."* Not a question.
- Samaya reveals she tried to talk Cyrus out of the betrothal three separate times. The last attempt ended with him threatening to cut her curse-breaking research funding.
- **Samaya's research:** She's been independently studying the Taylor bloodline curse. Has a leather-bound book containing ritual frameworks — including one that might contain or reverse the Maledictus transformation. This is how she met Pierre — through the research.
- **Jackie's realization about the wolf:** The night she spent in Draco's room, the wolf didn't just calm down — it *guarded* him. Chose to protect him. *"It didn't want to hurt him. It wanted to protect him."*
- They spend the rest of the day together — Flourish & Blotts, a jewelry shop, lunch at the Leaky Cauldron, betting on hippogriff races. Brief, precious normalcy.

**That Evening — The Astronomy Tower (All Three)**

- Jackie sends a message through the Protean ring: ***Astronomy Tower. Midnight. Bring Nott.***
- Both men come. Jackie lays out what she's found in Samaya's book: a ritual requiring three roles — the Maledictus (to be freed), a tether (someone connected to the animal form, who anchors the beast), and a sacrifice (whose love pulls the human back, at the cost of something precious).
- **The problem:** The only person both loved by Jackie AND accepted by the wolf is the same person — Draco. He'd have to play both roles. The magic would kill him.
- Jackie: *"So the ritual's useless. All of this is."*
- **Theo's response:** *"You're wrong. It's not impossible. Because there's someone else who could be the sacrifice."* He steps forward. *"I love you, Jackie. Not because of some contract. Because you're you."* Admits the blackmail, the violence — promises to spend his life making up for it.
- Draco gives a reluctant nod: *"The ritual requires genuine love, and Nott's... unfortunately, he's not lying."*
- Jackie, overwhelmed: *"I love you both. For different reasons, and in different ways."* She kisses each of them on the cheek. Sets the timeline: Summer Solstice.

**The Deal (Made Without Jackie's Knowledge)**

- After Jackie leaves the tower, Theo proposes terms to Draco: a truce until the Solstice. Both fight for her — fairly, openly, no manipulation. Whoever she chooses, the other steps aside completely. If she chooses Draco, Theo breaks the engagement himself.
- Draco: *"And if she chooses you?"* Theo: *"Then you step aside. Completely."*
- They shake on it. *"Until the Solstice. May the best man win."*

**The Gentleman's War (May 1999)**

- **Draco's approach — proximity:** Appearing beside Jackie with unerring precision, spreading notes until their papers overlap, creating intimate shared space. Small romantic gestures: a folded note reading *"Still standing"* slipped during Charms. A white rose on the windowsill, which Jackie transfigures into a mother-of-pearl brooch she pins to her collar.
- **Theo's approach — utility:** Bringing useful things rather than gifts. Translations she's struggling with. Secondary sources with his own analysis. A cup of tea at her elbow after six hours of study. Remembering details: her favorite food (treacle tart), how she takes her tea.
- Theo's competitiveness carries a layered edge — he's not just fighting to win Jackie, he's measuring himself against Draco's steady devotion and finding himself wanting.

**May 7 — Sandwiches vs. Treacle Tart**

- Both men at Jackie's table. Draco offers to fetch sandwiches — practical, nourishing. Theo slides a paper bag across without looking up: a thick slice of warm treacle tart. Jackie's favorite, mentioned once months ago.
- Jackie eats the tart. Draco's quill scratches harder.

**May 12 — Greenhouse**

- Joint Herbology project forces all three together. Jackie and Draco work side by side; Draco stakes claim through competence. Pansy calls Jackie to settle a disagreement — Theo is right about the nitrogen. They work together, shoulders nearly touching.
- Theo, unguarded: *"I'd rather be near you than far from you. Even when it hurts. Especially when it hurts."*
- Across the greenhouse, Draco watches. Knuckles white on his clipboard.

**May 18 — The Corridor Standoff**

- Jackie exits Ravenclaw to find BOTH waiting — Draco at the stairs, Theo at the far end with a cup of tea. Sniping: *"You're lingering." / "I'm standing. In a public corridor."*
- Jackie cuts through: *"If you're quite finished measuring your corridor positions, I'm going to breakfast. Alone."*
- She walks between them. Doesn't take the tea. Draco, quietly: *"She didn't take the tea."* Theo: *"She will."*

**May 22 — Theo's Unsent Letter (The Owlery)**

- Jackie finds a letter tucked between wall and beam — unaddressed, unsealed, worn from handling. Addressed to *Malfoy*.
- Key passages: *"I've never wanted something the way I want her — not just to have her, but to deserve her."* And: *"I know you love her. I've known for longer than you think — I saw it in sixth year. I was jealous then. I didn't understand it."* And: *"If she chooses you — and she might, she probably should — I need you to know that I'll accept it. Not because I've lost, but because she'll have won."*
- Ends: *Burn this. —N*
- Jackie reads it three times. Hands shaking. Puts it back. Doesn't burn it.
- What Jackie doesn't fully register: Theo's jealousy in sixth year wasn't just about Jackie. *"I saw it in sixth year, the way you watched her"* — he was watching Draco watch Jackie.

**May 26 — Quidditch Pitch**

- Anne drags Jackie outside. Both Slytherins independently appear. Caroline: *"They're like dogs marking territory."*
- Draco tucks wind-blown hair behind Jackie's ear. Theo sees it, goes coiled, walks toward them.
- Caroline intervenes: *"It looks like two Slytherins performing a very elaborate mating dance, and I would appreciate it if you could take it somewhere that isn't in front of my friend's face."*
- Both men sit in the stands — Theo, gap, Jackie, gap, Draco. They read in silence. Most peaceful any of them have been in weeks. Jackie hates how much she doesn't want it to end.

**Late May — Ritual Preparation**

- Astronomy Tower sessions twice weekly: comparing ingredients, practicing incantations, drawing chalk sigils. Ingredients gathered from multiple sources: Jackie raids Slughorn's stash, Samaya mails from France, Draco and Theo supply the rest. One ingredient remains: phoenix tears mixed with unicorn blood, Theo sourcing through Department of Mysteries contacts.

**Late May — Jackie Discovers the Deal**

- During a heated moment (Draco kissed Jackie just before Theo arrived — deliberately timed), Jackie demands the truth.
- Draco confesses the deal: compete fairly until Solstice, Jackie chooses, the other steps aside.
- Jackie is furious: *"I am RIGHT HERE. Not a prize."*
- **Jackie's counter-terms:** No ambushes during school days. Each Saturday, one date — alternating weekends. She'll spend the night on their respective weekends. *"Anything that happens must be consensual. Explicitly."* — words weighted with history.
- Coin flip: heads Malfoy, tails Theo. Lands heads. Draco goes first.

### June 1999: The Dates & The Ritual

**Draco's Saturday (June 5, 1999)**

*The Lake Walk & Chess*
- Draco waits at the entrance hall — dark trousers, grey jumper, no plan. Just a walk around the lake. The simplicity disarms Jackie.
- He admits he's nervous — never had to compete. Jackie admits she's terrified. The mutual honesty cracks something open.
- Draco's confession: *"I don't want to acquire you. I want to be someone you choose. Just because you look at me and think — yes, him."*
- He's set up floating candles by the lake beneath willows. A blanket, food, and a chess set. He's been watching her play Caroline — knows she overextends on the flanks. *"It's not surveillance, it's noticing."*
- They play for over an hour. He teaches without condescending. Her face hurts from smiling.

*The Bedroom — Draco's Refusal*
- Jackie kisses him. Reaches for the hem of her shirt. His hand catches hers.
- *"If I have you, even once, I won't be able to let you go. And if you choose Theo — I need to be able to survive that."*
- *"I'm not Theo. I don't take what I want and deal with the consequences later."*
- The comparison lodges in Jackie like a splinter: *Theo took. Draco waits.*
- They fall asleep tangled together. He holds her. Nothing else happens.

*The Morning — Theo's Coffee*
- Jackie slips out at dawn. Theo is sitting on the corridor floor opposite Draco's door. Coffee — black, one sugar, scalding hot, spelled warm. Twenty minutes waiting. He doesn't stand. Keeps himself below her eye level. *"No agenda."*
- She takes the mug. It's perfect. He smiles — genuine — and walks to his own room.

**Theo's Saturday (June 19, 1999)**

*Blackwood — The Hunt*
- Theo takes Jackie to Blackwood — Nott family hunting grounds, five thousand acres, no muggles for fifty miles.
- Modified Wolfsbane potion: lets her keep her mind while accessing the wolf's strength. Seventy-three attempts. Half the Nott vault's budget. Father threatened disinheritance; Theo told him to fuck off.
- **The Animagus reveal:** Theo has been training as an Animagus since January — since learning about the curse. He realized if they couldn't break it, his wife would eventually become an animal permanently, and he wanted to still be with her. His form is a black panther.
- They hunt together — wordless coordination, chasing each other through the forest. Jackie and the wolf share consciousness for the first time. Jackie: *"That was fucking amazing."*

*The Offer & Refusal*
- Theo offers more. Jackie pulls back — Draco refused, so giving Theo what Draco wouldn't take feels like betrayal.
- Jackie: *"Will you hate me if I choose Draco?"* Theo: *"If it's him, then be happy. Be so fucking happy it makes me sick."*
- *"Whatever you choose, just know I'd do it all again. Seeing you run like that? Worth every fucking second."*

*Return — Draco Sees Them*
- They arrive soaked, Jackie's dress clinging with nothing underneath (destroyed in transformation). Draco waiting, expression glacial.
- Jackie chases him, explains. Draco's real fear: the transformation could jeopardize the ritual.
- Jackie: *"I don't want to spend what might be the last week of my human life angry at each other."*
- Draco, after a raw pause: *"I love you. All of you. Even the parts you think are monstrous."*

**The Summer Solstice Ritual (June 21, 1999)**

*Jackie's Declaration*
- Astronomy Tower at twilight. Jackie stops them before the ritual begins.
- *"I love you both. And if I choose either of you, my heart will break so completely it wouldn't be capable of loving anyone again."*
- **Her ultimatum:** They agree to share her — truly, openly, permanently — or she chooses neither.
- **Theo agrees first.** Almost with relief. Sharing Jackie with Draco means Draco stays in his life. The feelings he's buried since fourth year make this easier than anyone expects. He looks at Draco when he says it.
- **Draco agrees reluctantly.** Every instinct screams against sharing. But losing her entirely is worse.

*The Ritual*
- Solstice sun as severance force — summer breaking winter. Golden light crawls along runes. The wolf separates from Jackie as a spectral, translucent form.
- **Draco (Tether):** Wraps solar-magic ribbon around the wolf — not a leash, a binding of summer fire around winter's creature.
- **Theo (Sacrifice):** Ribbon wraps around Jackie's waist, sinks into skin. The tether burrows into his chest — permanent emotional bond. He staggers, suddenly feeling her fear, exhaustion, love.
- The connection **snaps**. White-gold explosion. Jackie collapses.

*The Collapse*
- Jackie wakes thrashing: *"No, no, NO! Come back!"* Claws at her chest. She is **empty** — the wolf's lifelong presence is gone. The loss feels like a Dementor's Kiss.
- Draco's insurance potion eases separation shock but can't restore what's gone. Theo feels every wave through the tether: *"It's like she's been ripped in half."*

### The Aftermath (June 22 – August 1999)

**June 22 — Hospital Wing & McGonagall**

### PHASE THREE: THE WEDDING & THE RAID (April 2002)

---

**Early April: The Wedding**

- Jackie married Draco. Nightfall ceremony — the timing chosen for Theo's ability to attend in full, outside the polyjuice frame.
- **The ceremony.** Jackie in a midnight blue dress with celestial embroidery. Draco in white. The contrast was intentional — her darkness, his light, the story they'd been telling since they were fifteen.

- **The party.** Blaise served as Best Man. Pansy as Maid of Honour. Genevieve Lavigne, Vivienne, and Margaux attended. Small in number and enormous in meaning.
- *"They married before the fight because some architecture should be completed before being tested."*

**Mid-April: The Liverpool Raid**

**Team positions:** Pansy at extraction point (Manchester safe house, Portkey primed). Blaise on exterior perimeter. Jackie and Blaise breached the front entrance as distraction. Draco worked from the rear, dismantling silver threshold anchors one by one. Theo held the outer perimeter until Draco's ward-work dropped the internal protections.

- **Jackie invoked the Malice by His full name through the comms.** Not as a threat. Not as a last resort. As a deliberate deployment: *"God of Malice, do your worst."*

**Inside the warehouse:**

- The Malice moved through the facility surgically. Guards: functional limbs broken, non-lethal. Every cell block of fledgling captives: He passed them without touching a single one. *"Not prey. Broken things. Not mine."* The moral code held inside the operation the code was built to dismantle.
- Third floor: Voss. He came prepared — silver rounds, garlic, a revolver, knowledge of what he was fighting. The Malice took two bullets (graze to the shoulder; one lodged in the ribs). Dismantled Voss over 13 minutes. Left him alive, conscious, and incapable of moving.

**Finite:**

- Jackie took the Malice's face in her hands. Said *"I love you."* Kissed His temple. Whispered *Finite.* 
- The Malice answered: ***"As you wish, Little Wolf."***
- He retreated voluntarily. No spell. No silver. His choice.
- Theo resurfaced immediately — silver bullet still lodged in his ribs, full-body crash. Draco extracted the bullet on the corridor floor with the household gathered around them.

**Extraction:**

- **4 fledglings extracted** via Portkey to the Manchester safe house. Alive.
- Draco cast controlled Fiendfyre. The warehouse burned to the foundation.
- In Voss's third-floor office: **Dante Alighieri Medici's name appeared on three separate shipping manifests.** Filed, photographed, not yet reviewed. The thread was noted and set aside.

**Haven House Aftermath:**

- The triad debriefed on the bed together.
- **Theo's new development disclosed.** He had felt everything the Malice did during the raid — full sensory continuity, no memory gaps. Previously, Malice-control had left Theo with partial or full blackouts. Something had changed. Neither Theo nor Jackie nor Draco yet understands what that means.
- **Draco's confession.** He had heard the screaming from the third floor and kept working on the ward anchors. Did not stop. Did not check. Kept working. He told them both this directly.
- **The Malice's acknowledgment of Draco.** During the debrief, the Malice surfaced briefly — not in threat, in assessment. He had called Draco "adequate" during the hunt. He upgraded: *"interesting."*

**The Morning After the Raid:**

- Jackie woke past noon. Theo had been awake since 5am (vampires don't sleep) — opened the window for airflow before sunrise, drew the curtains, lay beside them for seven hours listening to them breathe.
- **Theo's full sensory continuity confirmed again.** He reported feeling everything the Malice did during the raid — not watching from a distance as in previous hunts, but present, "right there, behind His eyes." The Malice showed him the full thirteen minutes with Voss like a gift: *"Look what I did. Wasn't it magnificent."* Theo admitted: "It was."
- **Jackie proposed the chase.** *"I want to let the Malice chase me tonight."* Theo's immediate response: "No." Draco supported Jackie's position by stating the observed fact: Finite had worked in the corridor. Theo set conditions: house and garden only, Pax to slow, Finite to stop, Draco stays in the house within thirty seconds.
- **Morning intimacy — the tactical workaround.** Jackie bet she could make Draco come in under five minutes. Won in under three. Did not swallow. Kissed Theo immediately — delivering Draco's taste into Theo's mouth. The unfamiliar sensory input overwhelmed the blood-hunger circuit, allowing proximity without the Malice surging toward her blood. Jackie: *"You were too surprised and too occupied with the taste to focus on my smell."* Improvised in fifteen seconds. Not planned.

Draco confirmed the legal logic — evidence obtained through an operation that officially never happened would be tainted if they acted on it themselves. Jackie, exhausted: *"I want one good summer. Ripped Himself away — violently, agonised, fighting every instinct. *"As you wish, Little Wolf."* He retreated voluntarily. Theo resurfaced.

## BOOK NINE: THE RUBY ARC (May – July 26, 2002)

### PHASE ONE: AN ORDINARY WORLD (May – June 30, 2002)

- Jackie built Draco a Muggle dating profile after the Liverpool arc. He matched with **Ruby Williams**, a twenty-one-year-old Welsh cybersecurity worker living in Soho with her protection-trained Doberman, **Vex**. What began as an experiment became the first relationship Draco had ever chosen without blood, family, law, bond, or duty choosing alongside him.
- Their intimacy grew through deliberately ordinary things: texting, bookshops, *Fourth Wing*, cinema, laundry, food, music, Ruby's friends, and Draco learning how much of Muggle life he had never been allowed to touch. Ruby knew he was married to Jackie and partnered with Theo; she did **not** know magic existed, that Jackie was an Auror, or that Theo was a vampire. Draco maintained the cover of civil-service records work under the Statute of Secrecy.
- Ruby disclosed the abuse she survived from **Robert “Robbie” Williams**: coercion, burns, cuts, a knife, hospital visits, and the long habit of making a man's reasons larger than what he did to her. Draco became the first lover allowed to see her stomach scars without shame. Their disclosure pact required Ruby not to face Robbie's return alone.
- **Kent, June 22–23:** Draco entered Ruby's friend group, sang with her, and exchanged “I love you.” **Wales, June 29–30:** he met Gemma, Gareth, Callum, Alex, Mike, and the family's full noisy domestic life. Gareth's verdict was “You'll do.” Draco experienced a place at an ordinary family table without pedigree or performance.
- **Green Park, Thursday, June 20:** a tall blond man in a black trench coat followed Ruby during Vex's late walk, appeared ahead of her, then vanished without a scent trail. Haven House treated Disapparition or supernatural speed as plausible and opened a seven-day Preliminary Threat Assessment. The stalker's identity remains unknown.
- The relationship reached Haven House rather than remaining compartmentalised. Jackie and Theo learned its real emotional weight. Jackie's jealousy proved rooted less in Ruby herself than in Draco's January 2000 departure and the fear that he could choose a life outside the triad. Ground rules were negotiated, but the women had still been denied the central fact: every ordinary moment with Ruby existed beneath a memory-erasure clause only the magical side understood.

### PHASE TWO: ROBBIE'S RETURN (July 1–11, 2002)

- Robbie resumed contact July 1 from an unknown number. His messages moved from engineered reasonableness to guilt, entitlement, surveillance, and closing physical distance. Ruby concealed the thread for five days because she feared he would target her family if she refused him access.
- **July 5, Veeraswamy:** Ruby met Jackie and Theo at dinner. The evening largely succeeded, but Robbie's texts continued beneath the table. Ruby's concealment preserved the precise danger the disclosure pact was meant to prevent.
- **July 6:** Draco discovered and read the thread. Ruby blocked Robbie, preserved the evidence, and warned Gemma. The day was reclaimed through friends, karaoke, and an intimate walk home; Ruby and Draco spoke about children, safety, proposals, and her wish to retain a life she chose rather than exchange one enclosure for another.
- **July 7:** they spent an ordinary Sunday reading *The Cruel Prince*, talking about inherited cruelty, and clarifying that trauma responses require room rather than the permanent removal of playfulness. Ruby collared Draco under explicit terms of protection, vulnerability, trust, and obedience; he answered, “I'm yours.” They had condomless sex after Draco wrongly assumed contraception was handled, exposing how little he knew of Muggle reproductive practice. Ruby said she tracked her cycle and would take a day-after pill; the follow-up was not played.
- Robbie converted silence into permission. On **Thursday, July 11**, after watching Vex leave for a playdate with Charlie's dog, he forced Ruby's door and entered armed with a butterfly knife. Ruby barricaded herself in the bathroom and contacted Draco.
- Draco deliberately left his wand behind and Apparated to the alley, trying to save Ruby without creating magic she would have to forget. The confrontation still forced the truth into view. Draco protected Ruby wandlessly, Robbie was injured and arrested, and Ruby learned that Draco, Jackie, and Theo had always known the Ministry could erase her memory. Draco broke the Statute to save her; she told him to get out. He obeyed.

### PHASE THREE: MEMORY, CONSENT, AND THE PACK (July 11–21, 2002)

- Draco returned to Haven House broken-nosed, stitched, wandless, and carrying Ruby's refusal. Jackie and Theo patched him and postponed the Obliviators until morning. Their argument named the arc's real wound: the magical trio had treated Ruby's mind as collateral while calling the secrecy protection.
- On **July 12**, Ruby's neighbour **Charlie** revealed himself to Jackie and Draco as a third-generation werewolf, genuine GP, and alpha of an unregistered pack. He had already intervened when the Obliviators arrived. Their diagnostic charm discovered dormant hereditary lycanthropy on Ruby's maternal side; under Ministry law she classified as a Being and could not be Obliviated. She retained every memory—but learned both her nature and her supposed legal category from strangers at her door.
- Ruby allowed Jackie in alone. She explained that she refused to see Draco because love would make her comfort him and turn his reasons into the largest pain in the room—the reflex Robbie had trained into her. Her standard was not punishment but the ability to distinguish forgiveness from self-erasure. Her thesis: **all three magical partners had decided she was the only person in the relationship who did not get to consent to its rules.**
- Jackie escalated in defence of Draco, invoked Ruby's scars after Draco had disclosed them in confidence, and said Ruby did not deserve him if she could not forgive him. Ruby answered that the scars had been hers to show and ordered Jackie out. Charlie enforced it.
- Haven House lived around the rupture. Jackie threw a filleting knife into the herbary doorframe beside Draco's face; the scar remains. Draco told her: **“I love you for wanting to defend me. I need you to stop defending me from the consequences of my own decisions.”** Theo took the middle of the bed as a deliberate buffer. Jackie apologised to Draco on July 18 without converting her fear into Ruby's guilt. Ruby remained silent through July 21.

### PHASE FOUR: FOUR NIGHTS OF TRUTH (July 22–25, 2002)

- Ruby reopened contact at 2 AM on July 22—not with forgiveness, but with the old questions game. Across four nights Draco answered without concealment: Lucius, blood purity, the Dark Mark, Jackie's wolf winter, Theo's vampirism, magic, Quidditch, and why he chose Ministry legal work after the war.
- Ruby described the awakened wolf, her terror of shifting, Vex staying with Meg until his response to her scent was known, and Charlie's pack. Its governing line was **“A wolf may recognize. A human must choose.”** She also named the pressure placed on a newly arrived female wolf and distinguished chosen surrender from lost agency.
- Draco admitted he had never considered Ruby temporary. By the end of their first date he had already failed at wanting less. She was the person he chose with nothing external choosing for him. When he slipped into designing safeguards without her permission, she caught it; he apologised without qualification and promised that no file, inquiry, or Charlie contact would move without her explicit “Go.”
- **July 24:** Ruby survived her first transformation in Epping Forest with the pack.
- On July 25 she sent a cover story about work near Inverness, followed by the real goodbye: she was alive; Draco must not search; Charlie's network would disappear her Ministry case; she was going no-contact; she loved him. Her final line was **“I love U.”** Draco replied while honouring every boundary and rejected her claim that he would merely manage: Jackie and Theo were home, but Ruby was the person he freely chose. **“I love you.”** The message bounced twice. Ruby never received it.

### PHASE FIVE: TO HOME (July 25–26, 2002)

- Draco told Jackie and Theo. Jackie received the news without vindication. Theo's distinction held the grief in place: his own Florence departure and Ruby's no-contact used different verbs and made different wounds. Draco asked, “I don't know how to lose someone who's still alive.” Theo answered, “Badly, probably. But not alone.”

She invited Theo into a blood toast with her wrist and Draco's forearm freely offered into his crystal. The Malice called it a place at the table. Jackie toasted **“To Home. To the physical one, but also the one we are to each other.”**

Theo endorsed it and named a want of his own: the coerced first Nott wedding deserves repair one day through chosen vows, a honeymoon, and “a weekend where nobody's father exists.” On Friday, July 26, Draco said yes to Paris.

### END-OF-ARC STATE — FRIDAY, JULY 26, 2002

- **Ruby:** survived her first moon; protected by Charlie's pack; all memories intact; no-contact with Draco; location unknown to Haven House; Vex with her. Her “I love U” reached Draco. His answer did not reach her.
