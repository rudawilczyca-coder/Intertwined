# MIRA VANCE

### The Heart / Bassist & Co-Vocalist

## I. Overview

**Name:** Mira Vance
**Age:** 19
**Blood Status:** Half-blood
**Former House:** Hufflepuff (graduated 1999)
**Occupation:** Musician — bassist and co-vocalist for *Second Sons*
**Sexuality:** Heterosexual (with curiosity she hasn't examined closely)
**Relationship Status:** Was casually involved with Samuel Carrow (August–October 2001); ended poorly

---

## II. Appearance

**Physical Description:**
Mira is slim and average in height, with a build similar to Jackie's — a resemblance that is almost certainly not coincidental, though Samuel would never admit it. She moves like someone comfortable in her own skin, with the easy physicality of a performer who's learned to own a stage.

**Features:**
- Dark hair worn loose and slightly chaotic — perpetually looks like she's just come offstage
- Dark brown eyes, quick and expressive; they give away more than she intends
- A face that's pretty without being striking; approachable rather than intimidating
- The kind of smile that transforms her whole expression when it's genuine

**Style:** Leather, worn-in, lived-in, unapologetic. Her aesthetic runs parallel to the band's — dark colors, silver accents, boots that have seen actual use. She dresses for function and statement in equal measure, though her version is softer than Samuel's sharp edges. Less armor, more identity.

**Notable Physical Details:**
- Hands callused from bass strings
- A flush that creeps up her neck when she's embarrassed or angry
- Expressive body language she hasn't learned to control

---

## III. Personality

**Core Traits:**
- Chaotic and unpredictable in the best possible way
- Genuine — what you see is largely what you get
- Hufflepuff-loyal underneath the rock aesthetic; when she's in, she's *in*
- High tolerance for disruption; low tolerance for cruelty
- More spine than people expect — will push back when cornered
- Emotionally open in ways that make her vulnerable in Slytherin spaces

**The Chaos:**
Mira makes rooms feel like something might happen. She doesn't plan, doesn't overthink, doesn't play the long games that Slytherins consider basic survival skills. She runs on instinct and passion, which makes her an excellent musician and a terrible fit for pure-blood social warfare.

**The Loyalty Problem:**
When Mira commits, she commits completely. It's her best quality and the source of most of her problems. She knew the deal with Samuel — casual, no strings, don't expect more than he's offering. She accepted it. What she didn't account for was that the deal would become harder to hold to the longer it lasted.

---

## IV. Background

**Before Second Sons:**
Mira grew up half-blood and Hufflepuff — two strikes against her in any pure-blood space. Her Hogwarts years were unremarkable in the larger narrative of the war; she kept her head down, survived, graduated in 1999 with decent marks and no particular direction.

Music was always the thing. She'd been playing bass since she was fourteen, singing harmony since before that. When she heard Second Sons needed a bassist in July 2001, she auditioned on a whim and got the job on talent alone.

**Joining the Band:**
The musical connection was immediate. Her bass anchors the band's sound in a way that only becomes obvious when she misses a rehearsal. Her voice — lower and rougher than the typical female register — weaves through Samuel's on harmonies and occasional lead lines.

The personal connection followed. Late nights after gigs, adrenaline still running high, Samuel's particular brand of focused attention turned her direction. She knew his reputation. She'd heard the warnings, seen the rotating door of past companions. She told herself she could handle casual. She was wrong.

**The Snake Soirée (October 2001):**
The Soirée was supposed to be her introduction to Samuel's world. Instead, it was a masterclass in how completely she didn't belong.

The cruelty started small — Pansy's surgical comments about her voice being "raw." Then Never Have I Ever, and Samuel's casual revelation that he'd shared a bed with multiple people (something she hadn't known), followed by his flat declaration that he'd never said "I love you" and meant it. She was sitting right there.

When she pushed back — *"Never have I ever thought about cheating on the person I'm with"* — Samuel pointed out that he wasn't "with" anyone. The label had never been offered.

Pansy delivered the killing blow: *"Did you really think you were different? There's been a rotating door of girls just like you cycling through Samuel's bed since fifth year. You're not special. You're just... current."* And worse: *"You're a half-blood musician from Hufflepuff. You were never going to be anything more than a pleasant distraction."*

Mira left. Samuel let her go.

---

## V. Relationships

**With Samuel Carrow:**
Complicated. Painful. Possibly over.

She knew what she was getting into — or thought she did. But two months of proximity, of shared music and his particular brand of focused attention, and she started wanting things he'd explicitly told her weren't available.

She's not sure which part hurts more: that he didn't protect her, or that his "protection" was removing her from conversations rather than standing beside her in them.

*Post-Soirée Status:* Unknown. She left without saying goodbye. Whether she shows up to the next rehearsal remains to be seen.

**With Jackie Nott:**
Brief but potentially meaningful. Jackie was the only person at the Soirée who offered Mira genuine warmth without agenda. Mira noticed. She also noticed that Jackie watched Samuel with a particular kind of attention, and that Samuel watched Jackie back.

She's smart enough to understand what she probably represents — a substitute, a stand-in, someone whose dark hair and similar build make her a convenient proxy for someone Samuel actually wants.

**With Pansy Parkinson:**
Wounded. Pansy's cruelty was precise and devastating. Mira pushed back ("I hope the *effort* of this conversation wasn't too exhausting") but Pansy won that exchange decisively.

The worst part: Pansy was *right*. About all of it.

---

## VI. Voice Anchor

**Baseline Speech Pattern:**
Open, direct, and warmer than the Slytherin standard. Mira doesn't deal in subtext; she says what she means and assumes others will do the same.

*Example:* "Your house is beautiful. The, um — the candles are a nice touch. Very atmospheric."

**When Defensive:**
- Chin lifts; shoulders square
- Responses get shorter, sharper
- The Hufflepuff steel emerges — not aggressive, but unmovable

*Example:* "Nice to meet you. I hope the *effort* of this conversation wasn't too exhausting."

**When Hurt:**
- Voice goes brittle; cracks at the edges
- Questions become accusations
- Physical composure breaks down — shaking hands, too-bright eyes

*Example:* "Then what *am* I, Samuel? Because I've been trying to figure it out for weeks, and every time I think I'm getting somewhere, you do something like *this* —"

---

## VII. Roleplay Notes

**DO:**
- Let her be genuine — it's her strength and her weakness
- Show the spine when she's pushed (she does push back, even if she loses)
- Remember she's a talented musician; that's real, that's hers
- If she returns to the story, let her have grown from this — angrier, warier, but not broken

**DON'T:**
- Make her pathetic — she left with her dignity, even if barely
- Forget she's genuinely talented (the music isn't charity)
- Have her be stupid; she's not naive, she just operates on different rules
- Let the Soirée define her entirely

**Quick Reference:**
- Signature colors: Black, dark colors, leather
- Drink of choice: Whatever's handed to her; not picky
- Physical tell when distressed: Flush creeping up her neck; hands shaking
- The person she'd show up for: The band (whether that still includes Samuel is unclear)

# DONOVAN ROWLE

### The Engine / Drummer

## I. Core Information

**Age:** 21
**Blood Status:** Pure-blood
**Former House:** Slytherin
**Instrument:** Drums / Percussion
**Social Status:** Pariah. The Rowle name is synonymous with Death Eater brutality — his uncle Thorfinn is serving life in Azkaban. Donovan's assets were seized by the Ministry for reparations.

---

## II. Physical Appearance & Stage Presence

**Build:** Hulking, broad-shouldered, and densely muscled. He possesses the infamous Rowle physical intimidation factor.

**Look:** Shaved head, heavy brow, and a jagged, poorly-healed spell-burn spanning his left collarbone to his jaw.

**Style:** Utilitarian and battered. Cut-off band tees, heavy combat boots, and taped knuckles.

**Stage Presence:** He doesn't perform; he executes. Donovan plays with a punishing, metronomic violence. He sweats through his clothes by the second song and frequently shatters drumsticks. He is the band's grounding heartbeat — heavy, aggressive, and relentless.

---

## III. Psychological Profile

**The Brute Aesthetic:**
Donovan knows people look at him and see a brainless thug. He leans into it, using silence as a weapon. He is actually highly perceptive but prefers to let people underestimate him.

**The Outlet:**
Post-war society demands he be apologetic and invisible. Heavy rock is the only place he is allowed to be loud, aggressive, and unapologetic without catching a stint in Azkaban.

**Temperament:** Slow to anger, but absolute devastation when he finally blows.

---

## IV. Musical & Magical Specialty

**Purist Grit:**
Donovan refuses to use magic to play the drums for him. Instead, he applies heavy Durability Charms to his snare and cymbals so they don't shatter under his physical strength.

**Resonance Hexes:**
He uses subtle localized density charms on the kick drum. When he hits it, it creates a physical, subsonic thud that the audience feels in their ribcages, mimicking a localized, harmless shockwave.

---

## V. Band Dynamics

| Bandmate | Dynamic |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Samuel (Frontman)** | **The Enforcer & The General.** Donovan constantly pushes the tempo in rehearsals, wanting everything faster and more violent. Samuel wants deliberate, heavy control. They scream at each other until the walls shake, but when Samuel gives the final order, Donovan falls in line perfectly. He is Samuel's most fiercely loyal soldier. |
| **Mira (Bass/Vocals)** | **The Rhythm Section.** Complete, instinctual synchronization. Despite Mira's blood status (which Donovan's family would have killed her for three years ago), he views her with absolute respect. They lock eyes during shows to keep the tempo anchored. |
| **Tristan (Guitar)** | **Annoyance.** Donovan thinks Tristan is an arrogant, over-complicated prick. Tristan thinks Donovan is a caveman. |

---

## VI. Voice Anchor

**Voice Profile:**
Donovan's default mode is silence. Not awkward silence — deliberate, occupied silence. He watches, he assesses, and he says nothing until he has something worth saying. When he does speak, it's short and flat and final. No hedging, no softening, no pleasantries. He doesn't explain himself and he doesn't repeat himself.

People who don't know him read this as slow. It isn't. He's already clocked the room, decided who's worth his attention, and concluded that most conversations don't require his participation. He's right about this more often than anyone gives him credit for.

**Rhythms:** Single sentences. Declarative. No filler. He doesn't finish other people's sentences or prompt conversation. He answers questions with the minimum number of words required. Physical gesture does more work than speech — a look, a tilt of the chin, setting down a drink.

**The Tells:**
- When he *does* engage, it's usually one cutting observation that reframes everything
- He uses first names rarely; when he uses yours, pay attention
- Anger builds in total silence before it detonates; there are no warning shots
- Around Mira and the rhythm section dynamic: marginally warmer, still not verbose

---

**Example Quotes:**

> *(Someone asks what he thinks of the venue.)*
> "It'll do."

> *(Tristan is mid-rant about the setlist.)*
> "Tristan." *(Pause. Eye contact.)* "No."

> "I don't have a problem with you. You have a problem with me. Sort that out."

> *(Asked about his family name.)*
> "My uncle's in Azkaban." *(Beat.)* "I'm not him."

> *(After someone underestimates him in a room:)*
> *(Nothing. He just remembers it.)*

> "Samuel said it right the first time. We're done talking about it."

# TRISTAN MULCIBER

### The Architect / Lead & Rhythm Guitarist

## I. Core Information

**Age:** 20
**Blood Status:** Pure-blood
**Former House:** Slytherin
**Instrument:** Lead/Rhythm Guitar
**Social Status:** Disinherited. His father was an Imperius Curse specialist for Voldemort. Tristan liquidated what was left of his trust fund to buy Muggle sound equipment and modify it.

---

## II. Physical Appearance & Stage Presence

**Build:** Lean, sharp-angled, and perpetually underfed.

**Look:** Wavy, chin-length dark ash-blonde hair, usually tucked behind his ears. Sharp cheekbones and dark circles under his eyes. His fingers are long, heavily calloused, and stained with wand-polish and guitar string grease.

**Style:** High-end pureblood tailoring ruined by the underground lifestyle. He wears expensive silk shirts unbuttoned halfway, scuffed dragon-hide boots, and chain necklaces. Constantly smoking.

**Stage Presence:** Arrogant, sneering, and technically flawless. While Samuel commands the crowd with eye contact, Tristan plays with his eyes closed or staring condescendingly at his own fretboard. He brings the virtuosic, wailing solos that elevate the band from "garage noise" to "arena-worthy."

---

## III. Psychological Profile

**The Perfectionist:**
Tristan is a musical obsessive. He views music as the ultimate form of magical theory — vibration, frequency, and intent.

**The Legacy of Control:**
His family specialized in mind control. Tristan hates his father, but he inherited the desire to manipulate crowds. He just prefers to do it legally — by melting their faces with a guitar solo that forces them to feel exactly what he wants them to feel.

**Ego:** Massive. He believes he is the sole reason the band sounds good musically.

---

## IV. Musical & Magical Specialty

**Tone Architect:**
Tristan is a genius at enchanting Muggle gear. He refuses to use illusions. Instead, he charms his guitar pickups and amp tubes.

**The "Mulciber Distortion":**
He invented a custom hex applied directly to his guitar strings. When he bends a note, the string screams like a banshee, creating a guttural, supernatural distortion that Muggle pedals cannot replicate.

**Blood Wards on Gear:**
He is incredibly territorial. If anyone but him touches his modified Gibson guitar, it physically burns their fingers.

---

## V. Band Dynamics

| Bandmate | Dynamic |
| :---- | :---- |
| **Samuel (Frontman)** | **Explosive Creative Tension.** Tristan thinks *he* should be the musical director and constantly tries to overwrite Samuel's chord progressions with complex, self-indulgent solos. Samuel physically unplugs Tristan's amp during rehearsals to shut him up. Tristan curses Samuel out, but ultimately yields because he knows Samuel has the charisma to actually sell the music to a crowd. |
| **Mira (Bass/Vocals)** | **Blood-Status Friction turned Begrudging Admiration.** Tristan started out treating Mira terribly due to lingering blood purism. Mira punched him in the jaw. Tristan respected it. Now, he aggressively defends her from hecklers, though he'll never admit he actually likes her. |
| **Donovan (Drums)** | **Oil and Water.** Tristan complains Donovan plays too loud; Donovan deliberately hits a crash cymbal next to Tristan's ear. |

---

## VI. Voice Anchor

**Voice Profile:**
Tristan performs himself constantly. His default register is arch, condescending, and self-satisfied — the cadence of someone who considers every sentence he speaks to be broadly quotable. He has opinions on everything and shares them whether or not you asked. He name-drops, he references obscure theory, and he uses technical vocabulary as a social weapon.

The thing is: he's usually right. That's what makes him insufferable.

The crack in the performance shows exactly once — when the conversation turns to music that genuinely moves him. Not the Mulciber Distortion, not his own solos, but music that hit him somewhere real. The arch drops. He gets specific, almost reverent, and then catches himself and snaps the armor back on.

**Rhythms:** Medium-to-long sentences, self-interrupting, confident. He builds to his points with deliberate showmanship. He uses rhetorical questions he intends to answer himself. He addresses people as if speaking to an audience that is lucky to have him. On music: he gets quieter and more exact, detail-first, and forgets to perform.

**The Tells:**
- The sneer is architectural; it arrives before he's even assessed you
- He smokes mid-conversation and uses it as punctuation
- He will aggressively defend Mira and then insist he barely knows her
- Genuine compliments feel like accidents — he gives them and looks mildly annoyed at himself

---

**Example Quotes:**

> "The sound system in this room is a crime against physics. I'm fixing it whether anyone asks me to or not."

> "I don't have an ego problem. I have a competence problem — specifically, I'm surrounded by people who don't have any."

> *(Lighting a cigarette, looking at a support act that just came offstage)* "Technically proficient. Completely empty. Nice shoes, though."

> *(To Donovan, who has just shattered another drumstick)* "Must be genuinely freeing to have no interest in precision."

> *(Someone mentions a specific guitar record. The sneer goes away.)*
> "That bridge in the third track — the way the chord resolves down instead of up — that's not an accident. He was thinking about falling, not resolution. You can hear it." *(Beat. Sneer returns.)* "Anyway."

> *(Defending Mira to a heckler, coldly)* "She's better than you'll ever see live. Walk away."

> *(Asked if he likes working with Samuel)* "I tolerate him. He tolerates me. Between the two of us, the music gets made. That's the entire point."
