### Draco

Draco took his hand off the door and dropped to one knee beside her. He had heard that voice before. The wood between them did not make it easier to hear now, with Jackie on the floor and the sound of the impact still in his ears. He put an arm within her reach, palm upwards, then looked over his shoulder. Filippo had moved in front of the latch. Nell stood beside him, her case forgotten on the chair.

"Nobody is opening it," Draco said. He kept his voice low and quite flat. "I'll come with you."

He looked for blood before he could stop himself: her hands, the floor beneath them, the cuffs of her hoodie. There was none that he could see. He did not understand how she had gone down so suddenly, but this was not the place to ask. His own pulse was beating hard enough that he wondered what it sounded like through the door. He had brought them both down here and told Theo they were outside as though their arrival could only be good news.

Nell crouched a little way off, leaving him room. "There's a room across the landing where you can sit. I'll keep everyone else out." She looked at Jackie rather than asking Draco about her. "Have you been bitten?"

"No," he said, too quickly. Nell's eyes flicked towards him. He knew what that look meant and disliked receiving it, especially because he had just done precisely what Jackie had objected to upstairs. He turned his attention back to her and waited, keeping his offered hand still.

Behind him, Filippo spoke. "Nell. Get the blood."

"I need to speak to Viola."

"Then speak to her now." There was no raised voice, no threat Draco could point to. Filippo had placed his hand against the door above the latch, and he had not moved it when something shifted on the other side. "I will remain here. Tell her that withholding enough to keep him from walking has not kept him from reaching this door."

Nell rose. She took one look at the closed door, then picked up her case. "Don't offer him a wrist while I'm gone. Either of you." This time her eyes settled on Draco's exposed scar. He pulled his sleeve down without answering. She left the case by the stair and went up quickly; he heard her call for the Seneschal before her footsteps passed out of hearing.

Draco looked towards Filippo. For one ugly moment he wanted to refuse to leave him here as well. He had only just heard Theo say his name. He wanted to see his face, to establish with his own eyes what had happened, and now the first thing he was going to do was walk away from the door. Filippo met his gaze.

"I stay," he said. Then he turned towards the wood. "Theodore. It is Filippo. I am not coming in yet."

Draco waited for an answer. There was a faint scrape from inside, too indistinct to tell him anything useful. He made himself turn back. He could not keep looking at the door while offering to help Jackie leave it.

"I can carry you," he said quietly. "If you'd rather get up yourself, use my arm. We don't have to go past him."
