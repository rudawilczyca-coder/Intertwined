### Draco

Draco let her keep his hand where she'd brought it. His fingers curved gently against her cheek, and he moved the chair closer with his free hand, awkwardly, one leg catching on the edge of his shoe. He left it crooked rather than pull away to straighten it. There was something he could do, then. Not much, and nothing that would have occurred to him while he was sitting there asking questions, but she had taken his hand for a reason.

"All right," he said quietly. "You don't have to describe the rest."

He could not quite put her words beside the voice he had heard through the door. That voice had sounded horribly composed. Hungry, certainly, but capable of choosing a sentence, of making them listen. He had taken that for control. Across the landing, Filippo was speaking to someone who could answer him; here, Jackie had barely been able to finish telling Draco what it was like to be near Him. He looked down at their hands. The contrast made him angry, and he kept that out of his grip.

"If He can speak to you, He can hear that He's hurting you." It came out as a statement before Draco caught himself. He paused. "Can He? I don't know how much of this goes both ways."

He wanted the answer to be yes. He wanted there to be someone he could hold responsible for stopping, rather than another thing happening to Theo's body that they could only endure until it passed. But if the answer was yes, then he would have to reckon with the fact that He had kept doing it. Draco pressed his tongue against the back of his teeth and made himself leave the question there. Jackie had said she didn't know whether He couldn't or wouldn't. Asking it harder wouldn't tell them.

With his free hand, he set his wand on the mattress beside his thigh, within reach. He had to lean forward to do it without disturbing the hand she held, and stayed there afterwards, his knees close to the bed.

"I'm not going to ask you to reach back in and find out," he added. "Not now."

There were other questions: whether Theo knew, whether feeding would change it, whether leaving this building would be far enough. He let them wait. He could feel the hours without sleep in his own eyes, and he wasn't the one trying to keep somebody out of his head.

"Keep hold of me." His thumb moved once against her cheek. "As long as it helps. And if this room is still too close, we'll go farther. Filippo can come and find us."
