### Draco

Nell glanced up from the wound. "Provided there's no silver left in it." She turned the cloth in her hand, finding a clean corner, and went back to work. "I'll check before I close it." There was no reproach in the answer. Draco watched her set aside the scissors, grateful for a few seconds in which somebody in the room was occupied with a problem that had an end.

Filippo turned towards Jackie when she refused. "There may be." He stayed seated, his hand resting beside the closed file. "I have not agreed to anything. But I will not exclude taking him somewhere safe before I know what keeping him here will require." Draco pulled out the chair between them and sat down. He needed to stop gripping it. From this height, the table hid his hands, and he pressed them flat against his thighs until he could trust himself to speak.

"You've offered to take him out of his home," he said. "That is not a small thing to put on the table."

"No." Filippo looked at him. "It is not." He did not retract it. Draco had expected reassurance at least, something about temporary arrangements, but Filippo turned back towards Viola instead. The space he left after the answer made it plain that he was prepared to be disliked for this. Draco stared at the side of his face, remembering how relieved he had been to see him walk into the tent.

Viola opened the file. "If he wishes to remain in London, I require more than your confidence in his temperament. He will present himself properly. He will answer a summons when I send one. And he will make himself available when this court has work suited to his abilities." She lifted a sheet without offering it across the table. "You have found uses for those abilities yourself, Filippo. I do not imagine the principle troubles you."

"What work?" Draco asked.

"That would depend on what is required."

He felt his mouth tighten. "Then you aren't asking him to agree to work. You're asking him to agree before he's told what it is." Viola let the sheet settle. She did not look offended; he almost wished she would. "I am discussing the terms under which he may enjoy the protection of this Domain. His sire understands the distinction." Filippo's expression had not changed, but his hand moved off the table. "His sire would also like to hear Theodore's answer before continuing this discussion."

For a moment nobody spoke. Nell reached into her case for a dressing. Leandro held still while she placed it, his eyes on Viola rather than the hands at his side. Draco looked towards the door. They had been promised access twice now, and every minute spent discussing where Theo would live was another minute in which nobody who loved him had seen him.

"You said we could see him," he said.

"Yes." Viola turned towards Nell. "Can Nott be brought here?"

Nell paused with the end of the bandage between her fingers. "I would rather you went to him. He's lucid, but getting him upright was difficult enough when I last attended him." She secured the dressing and began putting her instruments away. "If you want answers, you'll get better ones without making him walk here first."

Draco's hands went still against his legs. He had known Theo was detained. He had not known he couldn't readily stand. Beside him, Filippo rose, the chair sliding back across the carpet.

Viola watched Nell close the case. "Take them down, then. Filippo, we will finish this afterwards."

Nell came around Leandro's chair towards the door. Draco stood before she reached it. "Why can't he stand?"

She stopped with her hand on the latch and looked back at him. "He hasn't enough blood."
