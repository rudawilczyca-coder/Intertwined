### Jackie

She managed to say 'Not now' to Draco. She was aware of how good vampiric hearing was and had no intention of revealing the bond to Viola's court. She took her hand off of Draco's arm and bushes both hands into her hoodie pockets. She found Theo's wedding band and pressed.

Even if she wanted to say something to The Malice, the onslaught of emotion that accompanied his presence made it impossible. So she build the barrier instead.

She could barely register the room they went through with how hard she had to focus. She saw humans and vampires and chose not to look to closely, knowing she wouldn't have liked what she saw.

She looked up when the rooms changed. She expected someone akin to Caterina. London's Prince was also a woman, although her appearance was very androgynous and only the name seemed to betray that. That's where similarities ended. Viola looked older and less feminine. She also liked Caterina's saccharine smile and the wickedness in her red eyes.

She also spoke to Jackie more like a business woman than someone who wanted to determine how her blood tasted.

"We are here on Signor de'Medici's invitation and we belong to his household. But spare me the pretense of asking questions, as if my answers matter in your world."

Suddenly a cold thought formed in Jackie's mind. If Theo would be released from Filippo and acknowledged by London's Camarilla, wouldn't that make all of them ultimately belong to Viola? She didn't like that thought one bit.

### Draco

Viola left her hand on the closed file. "Your answers matter a great deal to Theodore. He has made that abundantly clear." She said it without warmth, but without any particular satisfaction either, and Draco found himself wishing she had smiled. It would have given him something easier to dislike than this quiet examination of what they were worth to someone else. Beside Leandro, Nell opened her case. The little catches clicked one after the other while Viola turned to Filippo. "Do you acknowledge them as members of your household?"

"They are under my protection." Viola drew the file towards her, though she did not open it. "That was not what I asked." Filippo looked at the chair she had offered him, then pulled it out and sat. Draco remained standing behind its empty neighbour. He had watched men use seating arrangements at the Ministry to make other men concede things they hadn't yet discussed; he disliked recognising the same manoeuvre here while having no idea how much the concession cost. "Yes," Filippo said. "I acknowledge them. You may address your concerns to me."

Nell eased a piece of blood-stiffened fabric away from Leandro's side. He flinched and she stopped, waiting until he had settled before continuing. "Silver?" she asked. "Yes." She took a small pair of scissors from the case and cut the shirt away from the wound instead. Draco caught a glimpse of the darkened edges before her shoulder obscured them. He had held that chair steady. For a moment he was back in the tent, wanting the knife out and not wanting Leandro relieved, and he looked away towards Viola before the recollection could occupy any more of him.

"My concern is that your household appears to reside in my city while you conduct its affairs from another country," she said. "Theodore feeds here. Hunts here. Returns to the same house every morning. You have had ample opportunity to decide whether this arrangement was temporary."

"I allowed it to remain unresolved," Filippo said. "That is mine to answer for." Viola's voice had not risen. "Then answer for it. What are you offering me, Filippo? Another assurance that he is controlled? I have seen his control. It does not make him cooperative." Draco's fingers closed over the back of the empty chair. "You sent a man into our family wearing his face." Viola glanced at him. "And you returned him injured. We will discuss that as well." She turned back before Draco could answer, as though his objection had been a document placed in the wrong pile. He drew breath, then held it. Nell was still cutting the shirt. The sound was absurdly small in the pause.

Filippo laid both hands on the table. "I want to see him before I give you any undertaking concerning his residence or his conduct."

"You may see him. You may not remove him while his position remains unresolved." Viola looked past Filippo towards the two empty chairs. "Sit down. Neither of you is being asked to surrender your marriage. I am asking his sire where he intends his childe to live."

"He lives with us," Draco said. This time he did not wait for her attention. Filippo turned his head towards him, and the expression on his face made Draco's grip tighten before Filippo spoke. "I know. But if remaining in London is what prevents his release, I am prepared to take him back to Florence."
