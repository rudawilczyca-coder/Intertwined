## BOOK SEVEN: HOMECOMING & SEVERANCE (January – February 2, 2002)

---

**January 1–20: Recovery & Preparation (Florence / London)**
- Theo remained in Florence with Filippo to complete fledgling training. Jackie and Draco returned to London.
- Jackie installed a piano in Haven House's music room and fitted every window with her custom sun-proofing spell — both surprise gifts for Theo's return.

**January 21: The Threshold (London)**
- Theo arrived at Haven House after six months in Florence. Required verbal invitation to cross the threshold (vampire lore). First physical contact with Jackie and Draco in six months.
- **Triad reunion (intimate):** Jackie bit Theo during sex, drinking his blood — the Malice responded with awe rather than anger ("She marked us. She CLAIMED us"). Draco asked to be the centre — inside Jackie while Theo was inside Draco. Draco said all three France sentences for the first time, including "I know how to hold something without breaking it."
- **The proposal permission:** Jackie raised the idea of remarrying — she is legally widowed with Theo "dead." Draco accepted before she finished. Theo gave his blessing with grief beneath the joy. Rules: Draco proposes properly in February; Theo attends the wedding via Polyjuice as Eli; ceremony after dark; Jackie's Protean ring (from Draco) counts as the engagement ring.

**January 22–24: Reintegration (London)**
- Triad re-established daily rhythms. Theo learned the new house — staircases, blood-bag supply in the cellar, Jackie's annotated kitchen systems. Jackie continued Auror training; Theo and Draco navigated parallel domesticity.

**January 25–27: The Severance (London)**
- **Samuel's unannounced visit:** Samuel Carrow arrived at Haven House while Draco was at work. Asked Jackie if her grief "changing anything" meant there was hope for him. Theo, listening upstairs with vampire hearing, registered Jackie's heartbeat speeding up. The Malice surfaced; Theo revealed himself — fangs descended, crimson eyes. Threatened Samuel's life, restrained only because Jackie had asked him to be better than his instincts.
- **The explosion:** Samuel learned Theo was alive AND a vampire. Jackie detonated at Theo for refusing for six months to let her tell Samuel: "For six months I lied because YOU made me." Theo took full responsibility — admitted his failing.
- **The marking offer:** Jackie offered the Malice a compromise — let Him mark her so the question of belonging would be answered. The Malice responded with respect and agreed to discuss terms after the trip. *Conversation postponed.*
- **The Lamb and Flag meeting:** Theo met Samuel at a Muggle pub to apologise. Admitted he had heard Jackie's pulse react and "couldn't handle that some part of her wanted to." Called out Samuel's long game ("We used the same tactics. I've RUN that play").
- **Samuel's terms:** Theo must tell Pansy and Blaise before Samuel returned from the States. Samuel claimed an undefined debt against Theo: "Someday I'm going to collect."
- **Samuel's parting shot:** "If she BEGS me to... you being alive won't stop me."

**January 28: Departure (London → Atlantic)**
- Jackie left Haven House for the US trip with the Second Sons at approximately 2 AM.
- **Soul bond snap:** When the plane crossed out of London airspace mid-flight, Jackie's surviving side of the soul bond broke. She experienced it as being "hollowed out, like a tree struck by lightning that hadn't fallen yet."
- **The Week Between begins (London):** Theo and Draco began their first week alone since Theo's turning. Theo explored the empty house — found Jackie's wedding ring on the dresser (left because too risky to travel with given the Protean band and Eli), Samuel Carrow's poster on the back of her bedroom door. Draco wrote to Pansy and Blaise inviting them to dinner Tuesday with a postscript flagging there was something to discuss in person.

**January 29: The Reveal (London) / The Arrival (New Orleans)**
- **(London) Pansy and Blaise dinner:** Theo revealed himself as alive and a vampire. Pansy was vindicated — she had suspected something for months. Blaise hexed Theo with a Depulso for the years of grief. The July 2001 truth surfaced via Blaise's recollection of the Soirée: Pansy and Blaise learned Jackie had nearly slept with Samuel.
- **(London) Aftermath:** Draco confronted Theo about the July truth at the front door. Theo confirmed the full account (Jackie at Samuel's flat, nearly naked, stopped it herself, came home, told Theo the same night). Draco spiralled into "I don't deserve what I have." Theo grounded him by reaffirming the three France sentences — *"I deserve good things. I'm allowed to want. I know how to hold something without breaking it."* (originally spoken by Draco during the France Arc, August 2001) — and answering Draco's "Tell me what to do" with "We trust her."
- **(New Orleans) Arrival:** Jackie met Eli Lavigne at L'Eclipse — immediate mutual attraction between Eli and Samuel. **Silas Thorne, the Vampire Sheriff of New Orleans,** clocked Jackie immediately — smelled her snow leopard Animagus form and Theo's vampire blood on her skin. Implicit reference to "a fledgling in Florence." Issued conditional protection: "What you carry stays yours, so long as you don't make it my problem."
- **(L'Eclipse) The drug exchange:** Jackie spotted vials of *Sanguinem Mortis* — processed vampire blood — being sold. Followed the dealer; allied with Silas to investigate.
- **(Boat) Samuel's confrontation:** Samuel pressed Jackie about what Silas's words implied. He had spent two years competing with Theo without knowing Theo was a vampire. Beginning of his shift from hope to recognition.
- **(Girls' night) Mira and Jackie:** Mira asked directly whether Jackie had known Samuel was in love with her during their relationship. Jackie admitted she had known since July 2001 and had hoped Mira might make him forget. New friendship between them began here.

**January 30: The Sunken Quarter & The Reckoning**
- **(New Orleans) Marchetti's warehouse in the Bywater:** Jackie and Silas found a vampire blood processing operation — six containment cells holding captive vampires being drained, experiment logs documenting harvest rates, evidence of forced siring (creating fledglings specifically to harvest). Several captives were beyond saving; Silas granted mercy. One survivor extracted (a recently-turned woman from Atlanta).
- **The mercy kill:** A conscious vampire begged Jackie for death — could no longer remember his own name, only "a garden. My mother's garden. There were roses." Silas ended his suffering. Jackie stayed kneeling in the ash.
- **Combat:** Jackie shifted between human and Animagus mid-fight; took a cutting curse to the ribs (dark-magic damage that prevented normal healing).
- **The healing:** Jackie drank two mouthfuls of Silas's blood out of necessity. No bond formed (small quantity). His blood tasted "like Louisiana itself."
- **Silas's gifts:** A sanctuary coin granting Theo safe harbour in New Orleans under Silas's protection; promise of European contacts who owe him debts; warning that the Liverpool origin point implied either high-level corruption or someone powerful enough to ignore Camarilla law.
- **The evidence:** Shipping manifests showing European origins (Marseille, Rotterdam, **Liverpool**); a leather-bound ledger with every transaction, buyer, and supplier. Jackie promised to investigate the British side.
- **(Boat) Jackie's return:** Stumbled onto The Revenant covered in blood. Found Samuel and Eli emerging from her cabin — clearly mid-kiss. Lied about a mugging; Samuel saw through it but did not press. She asked him to stay; he held her all night against the nightmares.
- **(London) Draco's first sick day ever.** First genuine laughter of the week — Draco compared Theo's blood bags to Capri Sun, burned eggs catastrophically, placed tea Theo could not drink on the right side table "because that's where it goes."
- **(London) Theo's first London rooftop run.** Stayed north of the river per his promise to Draco. Sat by the Serpentine. The Malice articulated wanting Jackie *not to feed from* — first non-hunger want. Theo: "We're not becoming less. We're learning how to be something without her in the room." Returned an hour late; Draco was waiting.

**January 31: Recognitions**
- **(New Orleans, morning) Jackie released Samuel from her orbit.** "You're not the worst person possible for me. You're just not the right one. There's a difference." Forehead kiss. Told him to pursue Eli: "So I'll know you actually made something for yourself."
- **(L'Eclipse, evening) Second Sons performance.** Samuel sang "like a man who'd had his heart rearranged."
- **(New Orleans, night) Samuel and Eli's first night together** at Eli's grandmother's Creole townhouse. Eli: "I want to give you something good without any strings attached." Samuel slept well for the first time in years.
- **(London) Draco took the rest of the week off work.**
- **(London) The proposal conversation began.** Jackie didn't want a ring (already has the Protean). Theo challenged Draco: "You're thinking like Lucius. Jackie doesn't want a Malfoy proposal. She wants a Draco proposal." Direction identified — not claiming, but giving.
- **(London) Theo revealed the February 2000 annulment conversation** (Jackie had once asked about annulling so she could marry Draco; Theo had shut it down out of fear).
- **(London) Theo said "I love you" to Draco** — unprompted, in the library. "She's not here now. So I'm saying it again. Just to you."
- **(London, evening)** Draco played Debussy's *Rêverie*. They slept on the sofa, hands joined at 3:30 AM.

**February 1: The Tour Extension / Choosing Each Other Without Her**
- **(New Orleans) Eli announced ten additional Gulf Coast venues** (Baton Rouge, Lafayette, Houston, Austin, San Antonio). The Second Sons voted unanimously to stay. Jackie would return to London alone.
- **(London) Morning:** Draco woke on the sofa with his hand in Theo's. Theo: "You reached." Draco chose not to pull away.
- **(London) The proposal gift crystallised: a joint letter, two handwritings on one page.** They walked through Haven House and discovered Jackie's love letters everywhere — sticky notes, marginalia, sewn-in tags, a plant labelled "Night-bloomer. Flowers only open after sunset. Thought of T." The proposal would be the thing she had never had: someone writing back.
- **(London) Theo and Draco kissed on the living room sofa.** Draco crossed the empty cushion: "Tonight I'm choosing." First chosen, non-crisis kiss between them — no hunger as justification, no trauma to process, no Jackie nearby. (They had kissed before — stag party 1999, France 2001, December feeding — but always under the weight of something else.)
- **(London) Mutual non-penetrative intimacy in Theo's bedroom (Theo leads).** Slow, deliberate. Theo did not let Draco undress him — "tonight is yours." Not penetrative — that was Florence territory and London-with-Jackie territory; this was something else. The significance was the absence: Jackie on a different continent, no crisis, no hunger justifying anything. They chose each other in her absence rather than in her presence. Draco asked Theo to stay in the bed; Theo stayed.

**February 2: Departure / Composition / Return**
- **(New Orleans) Airport farewell.** Jackie hugged Samuel ("Thank you. For staying. That night.") and Eli (he reminded her that Silas always collects). Walked away without looking back. Samuel: "She's not mine. She never was, really."
- **(London, morning) Mutual intimacy continued (Draco leads).** Draco took Theo's lighter from his hand: "Not today." First time leading without Jackie present, without crisis as justification. The Malice was acknowledged: "I meant it for both of you." Still non-penetrative — they were holding the chosen-without-her register through the whole week, deliberately distinct from what they did in Florence and what they'd do once Jackie was home.
- **(London, afternoon)** Cleaned the house together — domestic preparation for Jackie's return. Draco washed; Theo dried.
- **(London) Draco composed an original melody in C minor** at the piano — built phrase by phrase, no sheet music. Theo told him to write it down; Draco notated it on the back of his Chopin score.
- **(London, evening)** Theo and Draco stood in the entry hall waiting. Theo noticed the third coat hook Jackie had installed before leaving — for a man who didn't own a coat and couldn't use the front door in daylight.
- **Jackie returned to Haven House** between 7 and 8 PM, jet-lagged, carrying the New Orleans evidence and Silas's sanctuary coin. **Soul bond restored on her side** when the plane crossed back into London airspace. End of US Trip Arc.
