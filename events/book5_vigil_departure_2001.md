## BOOK FIVE: THE VIGIL, DEPARTURE & FLORENCE SEPARATION (August – November 2001)

---

### August 11–14, 2001: The Vigil & The Waking

**August 11–14: The 72-Hour Vigil**
* Theo's body was relocated to a warded subterranean cell. Upon learning Theo remained aware during the transformation, Draco confessed his guilt over his hesitation, and Jackie broke her initial catatonia.
* Jackie and Geneviève orchestrated the legal cover-up, arranging an empty-casket funeral, the sale of Nott Manor, and a future assassination plot against Theo's attacker. Draco provided a false death confirmation to the French DMLE.
* Jackie and Draco finalized feeding logistics, with Draco extracting a promise from Jackie to remain safely outside the cell during the waking.

**August 14: The Waking**
* Theo's soul-tether reconnected one-directionally, flooding Jackie with feral hunger. Theo awoke completely starved, violently rejected blood bags, and fixated on Jackie.
* When Draco offered his wrist, Theo attacked him savagely and ignored their safe word, requiring Filippo to physically wrench him away before Draco bled out.
* Breaking her promise, Jackie entered the cell to rescue and heal Draco, narrowly trapping a lunging Theo inside as she kicked the door shut.
* Regaining partial lucidity, Theo recalled everything spoken during his 72-hour paralysis. He questioned Jackie's choice to doom him to a monstrous existence, though they ultimately reaffirmed their bond through the iron bars.

---

### August 14–17, 2001: The Hollow Week & Departure

**August 14: The Funeral & The Aftermath**
* Jackie, Draco, and the Lavigne family held an empty-casket funeral and wake for Theo. Samaya departed after sensing the deception but promising unconditional support.
* Draco visited the cell, where he reaffirmed his absolute loyalty despite Theo's fear of viewing him as prey.
* Jackie and Theo argued over her intention to confide in Samuel Carrow. Theo admitted his terror over their severed soul-bond, while Jackie demanded his trust.

**August 15: The Blood Crisis**
* After Theo violently rejected preserved blood, Jackie provoked the Malice to negotiate a feeding solution. The Malice confessed He would drain her if He fed directly.
* To make the preserved blood palatable, Jackie decided to spike the bags with her own blood.

**August 16: The Solution**
* The scent of Jackie's drawn blood drove Theo feral; he nearly breached the cell before Filippo restrained him. The spiked bags successfully stabilized Theo.
* Geneviève confronted Theo, explicitly demanding he prove worthy of Jackie's sacrifices.

**August 17: The Departure**
* Jackie donated a final blood supply for Theo's journey. Theo successfully suppressed the Malice, who violently opposed Jackie leaving.
* Jackie and Draco departed for London via Portkey. Filippo released Theo from the cell, and the two left for Florence to begin his training.

---

### September–November 2001: The Florence Separation & The Fracturing

**Timeline:** August 17 – November 2001
**Locations:** Florence (Filippo's estate) / London (Draco's flat → Haven House)

### The Correspondence Arc

Regular letters exchanged between Jackie and Theo throughout the separation. Jackie writes; Draco adds marginalia in his own handwriting. Theo's letters vary in coherence based on his state — fragmented on bad days, more composed as control improves. The Malice occasionally bleeds through in heavier handwriting, crossed-out demands, possessive declarations.

**Key Letter Developments:**
- Jackie revealed the bond partially survived — she can still feel Theo (hunger, emotions), though he feels nothing from her. She kept this secret during the waking because he was drowning in hunger.
- Theo's blood ratio steadily decreased: from one-quarter → one-fifth → one-eighth → one-tenth of Jackie's blood per bag by late October
- Filippo confirmed a December visit is possible if progress continues; Florence chosen over London because Theo needs to succeed at something before risking failure at home
- Jackie named her white raven "Hope"
- Theo adopted an orange cat named Beatrice who has no survival instincts and brings him dead mice

### Friday, September 7th — The Concert

**The Tarnished Cup, Knockturn Alley:**
- Jackie and Draco attended a Second Sons concert; Theo granted reluctant permission on condition Draco accompany her
- Samuel debuted "Someone Else's" — the song Jackie helped write the bridge for — performed as a duet with new female vocalist Mira Vance
- Mira Vance: black hair, dark-brown eyes, similar build to Jackie; sleeping with Samuel; uses "Sam" (previously reserved for Lyra and Jackie only)
- Draco clocked that the songs are about Jackie; confronted his discomfort watching her watch Samuel

**The Bathroom Incident:**
- Two men (Garrett Voss, Edmund Flint) cornered Jackie in the bathroom with intent to assault
- Samuel arrived, broke Voss's jaw, drew wand on Flint; the men fled
- Raw moment: Jackie hugged Samuel under his jacket; his confession slipped out ("She's not you")
- Jackie pulled back; deflected by asking about Mira; called Samuel her "friend"

**Aftermath:**
- Draco thanked Samuel genuinely — a shift in their dynamic
- Samuel and Draco agreed to collaborate on finding the two men

### Friday, September 14th — The Reckoning

**The Confrontation:**
- Draco and Samuel tracked down Voss and Flint at The Hanged Man in Knockturn Alley
- Samuel used physical violence; Draco disarmed Voss
- **Draco used the Cruciatus Curse on Flint** — held it for five seconds; the spell felt "natural" and he felt power rather than revulsion
- Delivered warning: forget Jackie's name, spread the word about what happens to men who corner women in Knockturn

**Aftermath:**
- Draco confessed to Samuel he'd practiced Cruciatus on spiders as a child; feared he was "just like his father"
- Samuel reframed it: the question isn't whether you're capable of darkness, but where you aim it
- They agreed never to speak of this to Jackie
- Draco and Samuel now share a secret and grudging mutual respect

### Late September–Early October — Haven House

- Jackie and Draco picked up keys to the Notting Hill residence on September 1st
- Warding extensive; Muggle furniture purchased; walls repainted the wizard way
- House named "Haven House" — Luna the Kneazle approved
- Shutters and spelled glass installed for sun-safety; one completely lightproof room prepared per Filippo's advice

### Saturday, October 13th — The Snake Soirée

**Guests:** Pansy Parkinson, Blaise Zabini, Daphne & Astoria Greengrass, Samuel Carrow, Mira Vance

**Major Revelations:**
- **Blaise's secret marriage:** Married Vittoria Rossi (Italian pure-blood) in March 2001; wife lives in Italy. Pansy and Blaise are together in everything but legal status — living in impossible limbo.
- **Daphne's hidden heroism:** During sixth year under the Carrow regime, smuggled a Muggle-born first-year to safety via stolen Floo powder. Never told anyone until tonight.
- **Astoria's confession:** Frustrated about being treated as fragile due to her blood curse — "I'm not dead yet. And I'm so tired of being treated like I already am."
- **Draco's truth:** Admitted to enjoying the violence when he and Samuel went after Voss and Flint. Confessed fear that Jackie will see the darkness his father put in him.

**Party Games:**
- Never Have I Ever revealed uncomfortable truths; Mira realized she meant nothing to Samuel
- Samuel dared Jackie to kiss Pansy — what started as a technicality deepened into a full kiss with tongue (~15 seconds)
- Pansy's reaction after: "Well. *Fuck.*"
- Astoria strategically ended the game by daring Draco outside

**The Confrontation (Deck):**
- Draco confronted Jackie about her closeness with Samuel; accused him of "playing at being a friend"
- Jackie countered: she'd followed every boundary, but Draco hadn't touched her since France. "You don't want me either."
- Draco's confession: Can't touch Jackie without feeling like he's "stealing something that belongs to" Theo. Wanting her while Theo is gone feels like betrayal.
- Jackie showed Draco the portion of Theo's letter she hadn't read to him — Theo calling him an "emotionally constipated idiot" and giving explicit permission
- **Draco reached for her; Jackie stepped back.** "If you only let yourself want me because you have Theo's explicit permission... then I'm just a thing."
- Jackie declared: No more ground rules with Samuel. Draco can explain that to Theo himself.

**Aftermath:**
- Pansy passed out in Jackie's bed
- Jackie and Draco slept separately for the first time since moving in — Draco on the fourth floor
- The Samuel restrictions were lifted by Jackie's unilateral decision

### October 15th — Draco's Letter to Theo

- Draco wrote to Theo directly, confessing everything: his failure to touch Jackie, his grief-paralysis, the lifted restrictions
- Admitted he needs to learn how to love Jackie on his own, without Theo guiding his hands
- "I don't know how to grieve him and love you at the same time"

### Late October — The Silence

- Jackie stopped writing for nearly two weeks
- Theo's letters grew increasingly desperate; asked her to write back "even if it's just a line"
- Theo walked to the edge of Filippo's wards for the first time — stood at the gate looking at Florence, breathed without incident, walked back on his own
- The Malice watched but didn't surge — marked as significant progress

### November 6th — Jackie's Return Letter

- Jackie admitted to being in "limbo" — alive but not living
- Draco has been trying (notes, flowers); she notices but says nothing because she has nothing left to give
- Visited Samuel twice; he never touched her or insinuated anything
- Kissed Pansy at the Soirée (drunk, a dare); they've become "girl-friends" since — Pansy visits with gossip and wine
- Expressed fear: maybe she's only good for being a "human seasoning packet"; maybe Draco misses Theo so much he can't be with her, and she doesn't feel that way, which means she doesn't miss Theo enough

### Saturday, November 17th — The Verdant Table

**The Date:**
- Jackie asked Draco to dinner at The Verdant Table — the same private booth where they'd celebrated her Auror acceptance with Theo in July

**The First Date Game:**
- Jackie revealed she'd written "Jackie Malfoy" in her diaries at thirteen, in various cursive styles
- Draco confessed he'd written "Jackie Taylor" in the margins of his Potions notes in fourth year, spelled so no one could read it

**The Patronus Confession:**
- Jackie revealed her happiest memory — the one she uses to cast her corporeal Patronus — is their first kiss in the gazebo at fifteen
- After their return Pansy was waiting in the living room, immediately clocked the shift in their dynamic; gave Draco unsolicited advice ("Remember what I taught you about pace") before Disapparating

**The Bedroom:**
- Draco carried Jackie upstairs; undressed her slowly as promised
- Used his mouth on her until she came screaming his name
- Both exchanged "I love you" — unprompted, genuine, without crisis forcing the words
- Jackie asked Draco to confess something he'd always wanted but was too afraid to ask
- He admitted he'd always wanted to wake up beside her and just *reach* for her — without overthinking, without permission

**The Shower:**
- Jackie initiated; Draco followed without hesitation (growth — he acted without overthinking)
- Jackie went to her knees; brought him to completion

**The Morning After:**
- Draco woke first; slow, unhurried morning intimacy — Draco inside her for the first time since France. Both came together; emotional release matching physical

**Breakfast:**
- Domestic morning in the kitchen; Jackie wearing Theo's jumper, making coffee and toast
- Draco admitted he loves both of them; still learning to say it without feeling like he's stealing something
