### Early March 2001: The Reunion

**Wednesday, March 7th — Café Meeting**

- Draco arrives twenty minutes early to a small, unassuming Muggle café — neutral ground. He's barely slept. The past nine months have carved something quieter into him; the letters evolved from stiff formality to confessions and dry observations.
- Theo arrives first, alone. He came ahead to make sure Draco wouldn't bolt. *"Old habits."* Draco: *"I wasn't going to run."* Theo: *"Wasn't sure. Figured it was better to find out before she got here."*
- Draco studies Theo — sun-streaked hair, warm skin, the settled ease of months on open horizons. *"You look well."* Theo's reply is blunt: *"You don't."*
- Jackie arrives in a green dress and a silk scarf Theo bought her in Istanbul, sprayed lightly with his cologne. She orders a matcha latte and an americano, sits on Theo's right. Her hands tremble; she folds them in her lap.
- *"Hi, Draco. Thank you for meeting us."*
- Draco notices her shorter hair — cut in Marrakesh by a witch with silver hands. Theo shares the story; the easy bickering between him and Jackie makes Draco's mouth almost curve.
- Draco mentions keeping the blue tile from Lisbon on his desk at the Ministry. *"Luna hasn't knocked it off yet. A minor miracle."* — his way of saying *I kept it. I read every letter.*
- Jackie asks if Luna is his only company. Draco answers simply: *"Yes."* No one else. The letters had been enough.
- Jackie lights up talking about the world trip — Japan, Lisbon, the Pillars of the Unreturned. Theo climbed a cursed pillar and Jackie had to Incarcerous him. They discuss books; Jackie mentions a fae romance series and murmurs *"wingspan matters"* into her glass, flushing. Draco's knee bumps hers under the table. She pulls away on instinct — then deliberately slides back.
- Draco pays the bill over Theo's attempt. He offers the Janus Thickey Ward — Tuesdays and Thursdays, Healer Pye — then leaves without looking back, giving them space.
- Jackie fights the urge to grab his wrist and ask him to stay.
- Theo, dry: *"Well. That was… civil."* Then, seriously: *"He's different. Not softer. Just… quieter about the sharp edges."*
- Jackie admits she needs to keep her heart on a tight leash until she's sure Draco is past the old fracture points. Theo concedes the volunteering — but won't go himself. *"That's yours. And his. I'll be here when you get back."*
- Jackie: *"If it goes sideways… then you have me. And I have you. That will never change."*
- They Apparate home. Sex on the couch — the act reclaiming the Manor as theirs again after nine empty months.

**Wednesday Afternoon — Settling Back In**

- Jackie reads on the couch; Theo sprawls beside her. She mentions Samuel's Knockturn gig on Saturday, wants to invite Anne and Caroline. Theo insists on coming — territorial about Samuel. *"I don't need them to like me. I just need them to understand that you're mine."* Jackie pushes back: *"They're my friends… And if you can't behave around them, I will wipe the dueling mats with your face."* Theo concedes.
- Jackie raises Octavia — asks whether Theo wants to visit her grave now that they're back. The comfortable ease evaporates. Theo's response is guarded and bitter. Octavia died when he was young; what's left of her is a headstone and the echo of a childhood he never talks about. *"It's always there. The past. You don't have to dredge it. It just… floats."*
- Jackie wraps her arms around him from behind. *"I'm sorry, love. I didn't mean to dredge up the past."*
- Evening passes in domestic quiet. When Theo moves toward his old bedroom out of habit, Jackie catches his wrist and redirects him to hers. Sleeping alone is a relic.

**Thursday — Rome**

- The Colosseum, Circus Maximus (twelve abraxani in aerial loops — Jackie wins fifty Galleons on Vento d'Oro). All three agree, earnestly and without reservation, to move in together once the Manor sells. Jackie cries happy tears over carbonara.
- Theo kisses Draco's cheek for an Instax photo — capturing Draco's shock in perfect detail.
- Outside a café: Theo flirts with a barista. Draco confronts him with jealousy that surprises them all — not on Jackie's behalf, but his own.

**Thursday — Theo and Draco at the Villa**

- Theo: *"She's happy. Really happy. You're good for her. For us."* Draco: *"I didn't come back for the 'us' of it. I came back for her, and somehow… found you."*

**Thursday Evening — Beach Date (Jackie & Draco)**

- Jackie confesses her fear: *"I'm terrified this is just a 'Tuscan charm.'"*
- Draco admits he came to Italy expecting it to fail — wanting proof the three of them was impossible. *"But it didn't fail. And I wasn't right."*

**Thursday Night**

### Friday — Florence & The Dueling Ring

**Friday Morning — The Train**

- Draco wakes in Jackie's room, blushes crimson when he sees Theo, and flees upstairs to shower.
- On the train, Theo tells Draco plainly: if someone challenges him, he's accepting. Draco: *"What happens to her? To us?"* Theo: *"For a long while, there was no 'us.' There was Jackie. And there was me."*
- Fragile truce: Theo will fight smart, not seek trouble, but won't apologize for how he handles it.

**Friday — Florence & The Underground Ring**

- Sightseeing: Duomo, Uffizi Gallery, Ponte Vecchio. Dinner at an elite restaurant. Then the ring.
- Entry via Samuel Carrow's enchanted compass. Underground cavern — bar floor above, warded dueling circle below. Half-giant doorman. Minimum bet: 100 Galleons.
- Jackie encounters **Filippo de' Medici** — vampire, ring owner. He uses supernatural allure to entrance her; Theo senses her fear through the bond.
- Filippo's wager: three rounds against his champion. If Theo survives without losing control, freedom and a gift. If he loses control, he becomes Filippo's permanent "pet." Theo accepts. Jackie attempts to sacrifice herself; Filippo refuses.
- Round one: Theo loses, fighting at a fraction of his ability. Round two: Theo wins, control severely fraying. Round three: Theo integrates rather than suppresses his darkness — fights with full, governed intensity, wins decisively, offers his hand to the fallen champion.
- Filippo's two gifts: a glass vial of vitrified ancient blood (converts the one-sided soul tether into a reciprocal bond) and an enchanted obsidian tattoo (The Butcher's Eye). Theo chooses the vial. Hands the decision to Jackie. She accepts with *not now* rather than *no.*
- Filippo hosts them overnight. Jackie and Filippo speak privately for two hours — he shares the story of Elisabetta, the human woman he loved and lost in 1923. Jackie offers friendship and correspondence; he accepts.
- Dawn departure. Saturday designated as full rest. Sunday return to London by plane.
