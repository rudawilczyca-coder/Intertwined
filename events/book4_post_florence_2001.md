### April 23–30, 2001: Post-Florence

**Monday, April 23, 2001**
- Jackie drinks Filippo's blood vial. Soul tether now bidirectional; she feels Theo's emotions as he feels hers
- Bond has emerging telepathic dimension (directed thoughts, involuntary bleed-through)
- Theo admits he's known 8 months that Samuel Carrow is in love with Jackie; song "Between the Lines" written about her
- Theo admits possessiveness extends to eliminating people Jackie turns to outside him/Draco. No apology.
- Jackie asked Theo directly about the night with Draco in Tuscany. Theo admitted: *"It was the first time someone gave me something that size willingly, and I don't know what to do with it."*
- Theo confirmed he knew about Jackie's contraceptive potion; considers it entirely her decision.

**Tuesday–Wednesday, April 24–25, 2001**
- Jackie confesses drinking blood vial to Draco; he absorbs hurt, doesn't leave
- Draco reveals Howler from Narcissa, her illness, Malfoy financial crisis
- Theo's nightmare bleeds through bond at 3am; all three negotiate lucid dreaming solution
- Jackie achieves stable Anchor State in Occlumency; **modifies her wedding ring with a needle-point protrusion on the inner band as her pain anchor for meditation focus**
- Glimpses Mind Construct (forest sanctuary, iron gates depicting panther and snake)
- **Memory exchange experiment:** Jackie sends Theo a memory of them together in Italy; he sends back his favorite memory of her — *the dueling hall confrontation in the Highlands, January 2000, the moment she commanded him to let go and he obeyed.* Not an intimate memory; the one he kept because it was the first time someone took his control by force and he would have surrendered it willingly.
- Bond feedback loop during sex = both feel pleasure simultaneously
- Jackie has sex with Theo in Draco's bed; tells Draco directly afterward; he processes, accepts apology, confirms not leaving

**Sunday, April 29, 2001: Taylor Estate Tea**
- Cyrus admits: betrothal contract designed to dispose of Jackie before permanent wolf transformation — calls her "expendable"
- Cyrus breaks: loved Amalthea "so completely" that watching her disappear nearly destroyed him; coldness toward Jackie = self-protection
- Jackie rejects excuse; announces collecting belongings, visiting mother
- **Poisoned invitation:** Jackie lied to Cyrus — no intention of going into woods; giving Amalthea chance for revenge
- Jackie reveals plan: if Cyrus dies, she inherits, will anonymously transfer Taylor gold to Draco's vault

**Sunday Evening: Memory Exchange**
- Jackie and Theo exchange childhood memories/diary entries
- Theo's childhood: Cassius took him to watch Muggles hunted (age 7); tried to run away (age 9); house-elf Mint who comforted him was freed by Cassius when Theo was 11 — she froze to death that winter
- Jackie's childhood: first uncontrolled transformation triggered by emotional distress (age 15), terror about curse progressing

**Monday, April 30, 2001: Conservation**
- Cyrus entered woods after sunset; hasn't returned
- Jackie and Theo trek to meet Amalthea (pony-sized wolf, heterochromatic eyes matching Jackie's)
- Amalthea confirms via blinks: Cyrus alive, pack holding him, she intends to "take her time" before ending him
- Theo kneels, addresses Amalthea directly; she mock-lunges then licks his cheek (acceptance)
- Amalthea presses nose to both their chests (blessing)

### August 4–11, 2001: The France Arc (Lavigne Luminary)

**Location:** Château de la Roche-Sanglant, Lavigne family estate, France

**Purpose:** Jackie, Theo, and Draco traveled for the annual Lavigne Luminary, hosted by Jackie's maternal grandmother Geneviève Lavigne. The triad was formally presented to French wizarding society as a unit — the first public acknowledgment of their polyamorous arrangement to European pureblood society.

**Days 1–4 (August 4–7): Arrival & Settling In**
- Formal introduction to Lavigne family and Château
- Jackie reunited with cousins: Vivienne (23, heir), Bastien (19), Elodie (17), and the American cousins Cole (26), Eli (23), and Sloane (19)
- Samaya present for inheritance document signings
- Filippo de' Medici arrived August 8th, having arranged his own invitation through longstanding Lavigne connections

**Day 5 (August 8): The Dueling Tournament**
- Lavigne family tournament held; Jackie won
- Draco defeated Samaya; demanded Jackie fight him properly
- Theo's "God of Malice" fully surfaced during final match (eyes went dark)
- Colette Mercier (servant) fell into pool; Draco rescued her and gave her first kiss as kindness
- **Evening:** Jackie revealed the Florence secret — she had offered to sign Filippo's contract to save Theo's life during the dueling ring encounter. Filippo refused because he didn't want a desperate offer.
- **Night:** Theo explained the Malice to Jackie — a separate internal presence she inadvertently gave more agency by naming it in April 2001

**Day 6 (August 9): The Solar & The Shattering**
- **Morning:** Geneviève summoned the triad to the Solar for inheritance document signing
- **Geneviève's condition:** Offered to purchase Nott Manor (enough to afford London townhouse) in exchange for one truth from Theo — something he had told no one
- **Theo's confession:** In September 1999, he received a warning (Eirik Fenholt's name) that Jackie was being watched. He did nothing because he was nursing wounded pride over being sent away. Fenholt kidnapped Jackie the night after Theo returned.
- Jackie's shields slammed shut; she fled without speaking
- Samaya heard everything; looked at Theo with cold horror
- **Morning continued:** Jackie suffered panic attack in east gallery; Eli grounded her
- **Afternoon:** Jackie confronted Draco; admitted she didn't know if she could trust Theo again
- **Draco's declaration:** "You are my home, Jackie Nott... I will burn down every other structure in my life before I let you stand alone in the wreckage."
- **Afternoon continued:** Jackie toured the Oracle's Vaults with Filippo; he revealed he suppresses his allure entirely around her. She hugged him for the first time. He confessed: "I want to matter to you."
- **Evening:** Jackie returned wearing Filippo's jacket; returned it deliberately at dinner (scandal)
- **Night:** Jackie retreated to secondary bedroom; shields remained up

**Day 7 (August 10): The Fracture & The Choice**
- **Morning:** Jackie still not speaking to Theo beyond necessity
- Luminary gowns revealed: Jackie in midnight-blue-to-gold constellation gown; Draco in luminous white celestial suit; Theo in black-and-gold shadow-and-flame suit — a deliberate matched set orchestrated by Geneviève
- **Afternoon:** Jackie went to Filippo's subterranean quarters; told him everything about Theo's lies, the Fenholt secret, the rape, all of it
- **Evening:** Private dinner with Filippo in the gardens after dark
- **Jackie offered Filippo her blood** — as a gift, freely given, because she recognized his loneliness
- Filippo informed her about the venom's aphrodisiac effects before feeding; she consented with full knowledge
- Both acknowledged "in another life" before parting
- **Night:** Jackie returned to suite carrying venom's effects; demanded the Malice instead of Theo
- **The Malice emerged:** Claimed Jackie violently and absolutely; she chose Him because His obsessive possession was the only honest thing left in the marriage
- Jackie agreed to stay — not for Theo, but for the Malice, who would never let her leave
- She slept in the main bed between them for the first time since the Solar

**Day 8 (August 11): The Lavigne Luminary**
- **Morning:** Draco woke to find Jackie covered in bruises and bite marks from the Malice; healed her for the Luminary
- Tether reopened for the first time since the Solar
- **Afternoon:** Formal receiving line; Draco maintained flawless Malfoy mask despite recognition as Lucius's son
- Samaya cold toward Theo, warm toward Jackie
- Filippo acknowledged Jackie with charged eye contact; kept deliberate distance
- **Evening — The Luminary Ball:**
  - The triad entered together in their celestial outfits; genuine moment of connection
  - Jackie and Draco opened the dance floor with the traditional waltz
  - Vivienne learned about chaperoned dance with Henri Delacroix (Geneviève's orchestrated surprise)
- **THE ATTACK:** Mathieu Vauquelin (brother of a girl killed by Lucius Malfoy during the war, with Draco present) infiltrated the Luminary
  - Draco recognized him and *hesitated* — a fraction of a second of trauma-paralysis
  - **Theo stepped into the curse's path**
  - Dark curse hit Theo in the chest; he collapsed on the ballroom floor
- **Jackie's decision:** Filippo offered his blood to heal the catastrophic injury, warning that if the damage outpaced the healing, Theo would turn
  - Jackie commanded: "Give him your blood."
  - **The blood did not heal fast enough. Theo's heart stopped.**
  - **Theodore Nott is now turning into a vampire.** 72 hours until he wakes.

**Relationship Status — End of France Arc:**

- **Jackie/Theo:** Trust shattered by Fenholt revelation; rebuilt through the Malice's absolute possession. Jackie stayed not because she forgave Theo, but because the Malice would never let her leave. Theo is now undergoing vampiric transformation; outcome unknown.
- **Jackie/Draco:** Deepened significantly; he declared he would choose her over everything. He carries the guilt of hesitating — the parallel to Theo's Fenholt failure. Now must face that his hesitation led to Theo's transformation.
- **Theo/Draco:** Draco declared love for Theo aloud during the France arc. Now must reckon with what Theo will become when he wakes.
- **Jackie/Filippo:** Friendship solidified and made physical (feeding). Both acknowledged "in another life" — the door that cannot be opened. Filippo gave his blood to save Theo despite the calculus that letting him die would remove an obstacle.

**Major Reveals & New Canon:**
1. **The Fenholt Secret:** Theo had warning about Jackie's kidnapper and did nothing out of wounded pride
2. **The God of Malice:** Named and given agency; separate internal presence that Jackie inadvertently empowered; she chose Him over Theo
3. **Filippo's Feelings:** Confessed he wants to matter to Jackie; suppresses allure entirely around her; would not have refused her in another life
4. **Geneviève's Power:** Orchestrated the entire week; purchased Nott Manor; extracted Theo's darkest secret; publicly claimed the triad
5. **The Vauquelin Attack:** Draco's war guilt manifested; his hesitation led to Theo taking the curse
6. **Theo's Transformation:** Now turning into a vampire; will wake in 72 hours in frenzy
