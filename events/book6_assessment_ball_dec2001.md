## BOOK SIX: THE ASSESSMENT & THE BALL (December 2001)

---

**December 7–8: The Assessment & The Ruling (Florence)**
- Jackie and Draco arrived in Florence. Despite overwhelming hunger, Theo successfully released Jackie without feeding, relying on Draco as an anchor.
- Camarilla Archon Dante della Rovere interrupted the visit to assess Theo. Dante ruled Theo was required to publicly feed from Jackie at the upcoming New Year's Eve ball in Rome.

**December 10–23: Trials & Correspondence (Florence / London)**
- Theo informed Jackie and Draco of the ruling via letter, triggering severe emotional fallout. Jackie contemplated extreme measures to spare Theo from becoming a monster.
- Under Dante's supervision, Theo endured escalating trials, achieving a breakthrough by stopping an extreme feeding unprompted. Theo realized the Malice's hunger for Jackie was rooted in terror of abandonment. Dante departed.

**December 24–25: The Christmas Reunion (Florence)**
- Jackie and Draco returned to Florence. Jackie tested Theo's restraint by carrying Draco's scent, which Theo withstood.
- Jackie challenged Theo's claim that the Malice was tamed, wounding the Malice by hesitating to accept Him.
- For Christmas, Jackie revealed she had invented a custom sun-spell to give Theo light. Filippo admitted his fear of eventually watching Jackie die.
- Jackie gave Theo a set of chisels and a block of wood; she gave Draco wizarding chess handbook with her annotations.

**December 26: The First Feeding (Florence)**
- Theo asked Draco to feed him voluntarily — to replace the August trauma with a chosen experience. Jackie excused herself; the scene needed to happen without her. Filippo was three rooms away as a failsafe.
- Theo bit Draco at the throat, controlled and brief; the venom-aphrodisiac took effect. Theo stopped on his own without sire-bond intervention.
- **First penetrative sex between Theo and Draco.** Theo led, slow and careful. Draco: "I want you inside me." The Malice was present but watchful, not pushing — *"He tastes good. Not like her, but... different good."*
- Draco confirmed afterward he had felt "chosen" rather than hunted. Theo: "I wanted you more than I wanted the blood."

**December 27: The Confrontation (Florence)**
- The morning after the first feeding, Draco confirmed he had felt "chosen" rather than hunted.
- Jackie executed a hidden plan and confronted Theo's inner "God of Malice" in a brutal fight. She proved she was not prey, forcing the Malice to respect her and agree to mutual terms.

**December 28–29: Relocation & Trauma (Rome)**
- The triad and Filippo traveled to Rome's nocturnal Hotel Obscura. Filippo warned Jackie that fledglings rarely maintained human attachments.
- Following a visit to Caterina's villa, Draco was traumatized by the allure of the feeding. Jackie used her own history of assault to ground him.

**December 30–31: The Test & The Assessment (Rome)**
- To guarantee Theo could stop feeding publicly, the triad staged a controlled test with Jackie as donor and Draco as failsafe. The test revealed Jackie was the unstable variable, not Theo.
- Theo faced his formal Camarilla assessment at Caterina's New Year's Eve ball. The event culminated in a successful public feeding where established terms held. They survived the assessment.
