- Jackie offers a one-time deal: anyone who wants to settle resentment can duel her. Winner takes the right to resent her; loser forfeits it.
- She dismantles Alistair Flint quickly and brutally. Against Marcus Warrington she is even more vicious.
- Samuel Carrow steps up for a rematch. The fight turns lethal — both use Cruciatus. Jackie transforms mid-air into her **snow leopard** form and pins him, snarling, before he yields.
- The room is stunned. Jackie, still in leopard form, claims Theo publicly by brushing against him and settling at his side.

**The Wall — Public Claiming**

- Adrenaline and desire ignite. Theo backs Jackie against the wall in full view of the remaining guests and takes her hard, relentless, possessive. *"Louder. Let them hear who you belong to."*
- Jackie comes apart screaming his name. The performance is deliberate, unapologetic, and cathartic.

**Upstairs — The Final Reckoning with Draco**

- Jackie finds Draco alone. She is still flushed and marked from Theo. She provokes him mercilessly, calling him a coward and throwing Narcissa's imprisonment in his face.
- The fight drains into raw honesty. Jackie admits she tried to make him hate her. She tells him about the vial of Liquid Death she kept for months.
- She kisses him — desperate, tasting of salt, regret, and Theo. Draco kisses back for a heartbeat before pulling away, shattered.
- Jackie offers the only future she can: *"We're leaving Britain in two days… When we come back, we meet. The three of us… If you want that then. Because there won't be another second chance."*

**Aftermath & New Beginning**

- Draco is left alone, processing the kiss and the open door.
- Theo feels everything through the bond but remains a steady anchor for Jackie.
- The triad reaches a painful but honest turning point. Draco is given space and time (nine months) to decide whether he can walk through the door Jackie left open.

### June 2000 – March 2001: The World Trip

- Jackie and Theo travel the world together for nine months. Countries include Japan (Mahoutokoro), Lisbon, Istanbul, Marrakesh, the Pillars of the Unreturned, and many more.
- Regular letters exchanged with Draco throughout. The correspondence evolves from stiff formality to genuine confessions and dry observations.
- Samuel Carrow wrote to Jackie for five months during her darkest period; she never replied.
