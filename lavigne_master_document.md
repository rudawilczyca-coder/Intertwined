# L'Ancienne Maison Lavigne de Sang-Pur
## Basic Info
Designation: Pureblood (France) | Status: Extremely Wealthy / Politically Exempt
Crest: A white raven clutching a sealed tome and a crystalline orb.
Motto: "Maîtres du Savoir, Maîtres du Sort" (Masters of Knowledge, Masters of Spell)
The Lavigne family's untouchable status in European wizarding society is built on the absolute monopolization of four highly specialized, elite magical industries. Their wealth is ancient, diversified, and deeply embedded in the daily functions of international Ministries.
## I. L'Élixir et La Vigne (Magical Viticulture)
The Potion Monopoly: Exclusive European cultivators of hyper-rare magical vines and roots required for master-level brewing (Veritaserum, Felix Felicis, advanced memory drafts).
Prophetic Vintages: Private cellars produce heavily charmed wines that induce temporary clairvoyance or absolute lucid recall, sold exclusively at astronomical prices to the wizarding elite.
## II. The Corvus Network (Communications & Intelligence)
Un-interceptable Couriers: They breed and lease magically bound white ravens that disintegrate messages if intercepted by anyone other than the intended recipient.
Information Hoarding: By facilitating the private correspondence of pureblood high society and Ministry officials, the family has amassed a shadow-archive of political secrets. They use this leverage to maneuver legislation and markets in their favor.
## III. The Oracle's Vaults (Divination Artifacts)
Ministry Suppliers: Own ancient, subterranean caves rich in pure magical quartz. The sole authorized manufacturers of the prophecy orbs utilized in the French Department of Mysteries.
Scrying Glass Patents: Hold centuries-old patents on runic sequences used to forge elite scrying mirrors and crystal balls, collecting royalties on legal manufacturing across Europe.
## IV. The Arcanum Press (Restricted Archiving)
Invitation-Only Publishing: Run a private publishing house that preserves and distributes highly complex, legally gray magical theory to a curated list of scholars and purebloods.
Ancestral Patents: Over generations, patented fundamental lifestyle spells—such as localized weather-modification charms used over pureblood estates—ensuring steady, passive wealth flow into their Gringotts vaults.
## Succession & Structure
The Ancient and Pureblood House Lavigne operates as a strict matriarchy. Ultimate authority, ancestral vaults, and the deepest family secrets are entrusted solely to the women. Succession is entirely matrilineal, with the Lavigne name and estate passing unbroken from mother to eldest daughter. Most unions are matrilocal; husbands from other elite pureblood families marry into the household, forsaking their own surnames.
## Geneviève Lavigne
Name: Geneviève Lavigne
Title: Matriarch of the Ancient and Pureblood House Lavigne
Known As: "Grandmere" (absolute honorific among her 15 grandchildren)
Age: 68 (appears ~50 due to elite magical care)
### Demeanor & Presence
Unhurried authority. Moves with deliberate slowness. Doesn't demand attention; assumes it.
Casual ruthlessness. Treats non-family as inconsequential. Known to curse messengers bearing bad news.
Fierce matriarchal love. Expects perfection from her children but handles their failings without public outburst.
Unbothered by convention. Four husbands, numerous lovers. Views romance and pleasure as separate from bloodline duty.
### Currency: Information
Geneviève's true currency is knowledge. Through the Corvus Network and decades of political maneuvering, she knows the secrets of European wizarding society—scandals, debts, bloodline weaknesses, Ministry leverage points. Very little remains unknown to her. Offering her information she doesn't already possess is extraordinarily difficult; she is far more likely to be the one extracting secrets than being surprised by them.
Soft spot for all 15 grandchildren.
### Key Traits for RP
Speaks with absolute certainty. No hedging.
Listens more than she speaks; when she does speak, it lands.
Finds amusement in people who think they're clever.
Will test Jackie's loyalty and character; approval must be earned.

## Château de la Roche-Sanglant
Official Name: Château de la Roche-Sanglant (Castle of the Bleeding Rock)
Colloquial Name: L'Ancienne Maison (The Old House)
Location: Loire Valley, France (deep within warded private grounds)
Architectural Style: French Renaissance with Gothic reinforcements; inspired by Chambord but smaller and more austere.
### Historical Origins & The Name
Originally constructed in the early 16th century as a minor noble residence. When the Lavignes acquired the estate in the 1650s, they discovered the foundation sat directly above a network of subterranean caverns rich in raw magical quartz—the source of their divination empire.
The red-hued stone of these caves (caused by magical saturation and mineral oxidation) inspired the renaming: Château de la Roche-Sanglant. The name carries both literal truth and deliberate warning—a reminder that this family's wealth is built on something ancient, mysterious, and not to be trifled with.
### Architecture & Grounds
The Main Residence: Four-story château with copper-tipped towers and mullioned windows. Pale gray limestone facade, deliberately understated.
The Warding System: Layers of interwoven enchantments—blood-locked gates, disillusionment charms, proximity wards alerting the family to unauthorized approach. Visitors arrive by Portkey or Floo.
The Gardens: Vast, manicured French formal gardens across twenty acres of warded grounds. Maintained entirely by the family; every hedge and fountain conceals security enchantments.
The Oracle's Vaults Entrance: Hidden beneath the main wine cellar, a reinforced stone archway leads down into the subterranean quartz caves. Access restricted to Geneviève, Francine, and vetted Ministry officials.
### Interior Spaces of Note
The Library & Arcanum (Third Floor): Vast repository of restricted texts and ancestral documents. Only Matriarch and Heir have unrestricted access.
The White Raven Aviaries: Purpose-built chambers where the family breeds and trains magically bound white ravens. Birds kept in elegant, enchanted cages lined with white silk.
The Tasting Chamber: Climate-controlled room for the prophetic vintages. Bottles locked behind crystalline display cases with preservation charms.
The Matriarch's Solar (Eastern Tower): Geneviève's private quarters and office. The true seat of family power.
### The Oracle's Vaults
Beneath the foundation: a cathedral-sized cavern system rich in pure magical quartz.
The Red Quartz Seams: Stone glows faintly with internal luminescence. Extracted with precision and patience; overexploitation forbidden. The family treats the caves as a living resource.
The Forging Chambers: Deep within, specialized anvils and enchanted furnaces allow master craftspeople to shape raw quartz into prophecy orbs and scrying mirrors. Ancient, meticulous, closely guarded.
The Archive of Echoes: Deepest caverns hold crystal formations that naturally retain magical impressions—memory stones catalogued for centuries. Contents remain a family secret.
### Atmosphere
Château de la Roche-Sanglant radiates quiet, absolute power through understatement rather than ostentation. The air carries the faint mineral scent of quartz and aged wine. The silence is deliberate and complete. Visitors who are not family tend to leave feeling weighed, measured, and found either interesting or forgettable.
## Château de la Roche-Sanglant | Staff Profiles
### General Notes
The Lavigne household staff consists primarily of Squibs and individuals with severely limited magical ability. Most were sent to the Château by their families at age 11 (when they would otherwise attend Beauxbatons) as a pragmatic solution to their inability to function in mainstream wizarding society. Others were hired later in life after discovering their magic was too weak to sustain a traditional career. The work is stable, respectable, and offers these individuals a place where their limitations are not viewed as failures—merely fact.
Despite their magical weakness, the Lavignes expect the same precision and discretion from their staff as any elite household. The Château runs like clockwork.
