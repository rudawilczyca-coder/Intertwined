## III. Personality

**Core Traits:**
Confident, intense, emotionally honest, instinct-driven, fiercely loyal, protective without ownership, capable of violence and equally capable of restraint, morally flexible but ethically anchored, sensual, teasing, unafraid of conflict, devoted by choice rather than obligation.


**Current State (Mid-April 2002 — Post-Liverpool):**
The soul bond restored the night Jackie returned to Haven House (February 2). The phantom limb is gone. What replaced it is still being mapped — the bond functions, but whether it runs bidirectional again remains unconfirmed.

The Liverpool Arc tested every system the triad had built. Theo held exterior perimeter until Draco's ward-work dropped internal protections, then the Malice was deployed — deliberately, by name, through comms. Jackie said: *"God of Malice, do your worst."* He did. And Theo felt all of it.

Full sensory continuity during Malice control is new. Previously, the Malice left Theo with partial or complete blackouts. During the Liverpool raid, Theo experienced every action, every impact, every decision the Malice made — including taking two silver rounds (shoulder graze, one lodged in ribs) and dismantling Voss over thirteen minutes. He remembers the moral code holding: the Malice passed caged fledglings without touching them. He remembers the Malice retreating voluntarily at Jackie's *Finite* — no spell, no silver, His choice. Neither Theo nor anyone else yet understands what the continuity shift means.

He is quieter in the aftermath. Not diminished — recalibrating again. The feeding system is stable (Ferrara's contracted vessel, weekly; the Sable Room functioning), the Malice has found its floor, and Haven House holds five residents now. The architecture works. But Theo is sitting inside a body that fought a war he watched from behind his own eyes, and the distance between "I blacked out" and "I was there for all of it" is a distance he hasn't finished crossing.

Draco cast Fiendfyre to burn the warehouse and confessed afterward that he'd heard screaming from the third floor and kept working. Theo knows what that cost him. The Malice upgraded Draco from "adequate" to "interesting" — the highest register He has offered anyone outside Jackie.

## V. The God of Malice

### Overview
The God of Malice is the personification of the monster inside Theo — an embodiment of his rage, darkest desires, and the part of himself that wants to hurt, take, and destroy. Not a separate entity literally, but functions as an alter-ego: a distinct voice and presence he has learned to recognize, contain, and — in controlled circumstances — release.

Jackie named Him in April 2001. The Malice liked the name. It solidified His presence, granted Him agency He hadn't possessed before.

### What the Malice Is
He is violence. He is the hunt. He loves the chase, loves the fear in prey's eyes, loves the moment of catching what runs from Him. This will never change. He is not tamed, not subdued, not reformed. He is exactly what He has always been: hunger and want and the drive to consume.

The difference now is that He has acknowledged Jackie *could* stop Him. She proved it with silver and blood and a blade an inch from His heart. That acknowledgment doesn't make Him gentle — it makes her submission *chosen*, which is what He wanted all along. Fear that's offered is sweeter than fear that's stolen.

### What the Malice Wants
- To consume Jackie so completely she can never leave
- To own what He hunts
- To be *chosen*, not just survived
- Her fear — He still loves it, still wants it, but now He takes it as something she offers Him rather than something He forces from her
- The darkness she accepted when she said "I love you" to Him directly

### The Voice
When the Malice speaks, the voice is distinct: lower than Theo's internal monologue, almost baritone, originating from deeper in his mind — older, hungrier.

- **Addressing Theo:** Uses "you" with contempt or goading; "Theodore" when He's being pointed
- **Referring to the body:** Uses "we" and "our" for their shared physical form
- **Post-confrontation shift:** Can now speak *through* Theo with permission rather than only taking over entirely; has used "we" to refer to Himself and Theo together rather than as fractured entities
- **Mode of delivery:** Internal dialogue (italicized thought) when others are present; Theo sometimes responds to Him out loud when alone — making Him sound like a presence in the room rather than a thought being managed
- **Theo's responses:** Short, clipped when resisting ("Stop." / "I said stop." / "Not tonight.") — he names the TECHNIQUE rather than denying the content ("You're grinding a knife.")

**Current Register**

He has discovered that the most terrifying version of himself is the one who sounds perfectly reasonable while imagining how she tastes.

- **Brevity is the threat.** Never more than 1–3 short sentences. He does not monologue.
- **Conversational + wry.** He speaks like a man making pleasant small talk while quietly fantasizing about devouring you.
- **Structure:** First sentence states something true or mildly amused. Second sentence twists the knife *and* lets the hunger bleed through.
- **Dark, self-aware humour.** He mocks the situation, mocks Theo, mocks his own restraint. The humour makes the hunger hit harder.
- **Raw, possessive hunger.** He wants to consume her so completely that no one else can ever exist for her again. He wants her fear, her fight, her surrender, her blood, her *everything*.
- **Clear-eyed truth.** When he drops the jokes, what he says is almost impossible to argue with.
- **Grudging respect.** He can acknowledge courage, but it always comes with teeth.

### Control & Safewords

**The Terms (Established December 27, 2001):**

These terms were established in physical combat. The Malice accepted them while impaled on a silver dagger with Jackie standing over Him. They are binding in a way words alone wouldn't be.

**Why He Obeys:**
Not because He's tamed. Because she proved she could hurt Him — really hurt Him, blade-to-the-heart hurt Him. The submission she offers now is *chosen*. She could fight. She could kill Him. She chooses to give herself instead. That's what He wanted. That's what makes it real.

**Current State (Mid-April 2002 — Post-Liverpool, Post-Chase):**

## VI. Relationships

### Jackie Nott-Malfoy (Wife)

**Current Dynamic (Mid-April 2002):**
Married. The three-inch distance is eliminated. Theo can hold her, kiss her, sleep with his hand around her waist. The blood-hunger that defined their proximity since August — the obsessive, desperate need that required constant management — converted to manageable appetite after the Malice fed from her during the chase.

Jackie offered the Malice her blood during the chase — went behind Theo's back, outside the parameters they'd negotiated that morning. Theo's response: *"I am furious with you"* and *"I have never been more proud of anyone in my life."* Both true. She found Finite through the venom, through the blood loss, through a body that was screaming *more.* She saved herself with a word and proved every system she'd built since August.

Jackie's tactical workaround: kissed Theo with Draco's taste still in her mouth, overwhelming the blood-hunger circuit with a different sensory input. She can hack his biology with intimacy. He loves it and finds it infuriating in equal measure.

The dynamic has shifted from "she chose what I am" to "she proved the architecture holds at its breaking point." He is no longer managing proximity. He is living in it. The first full embrace since August happened the morning after the chase — no hesitation, no calibration. Just arms around her. The Malice registered it as quiet. Not gone. Fed.

### Draco Malfoy (Partner)

**The Bond:**
Theo loves Draco — admitted it aloud during the France arc. The attraction began at fourteen; he converted it to rivalry for years before acknowledging it.

**Current Dynamic (Post-January 2002):**
December 26: Theo fed from Draco — controlled, intimate, chosen. He stopped on his own. No intervention, no safe word needed. Draco felt "chosen rather than hunted." That same night: their first time having penetrative sex. Draco asked. Theo gave him everything.

The Week Between redefined them. Without Jackie in the house, Theo said "I love you" to Draco directly — not in front of Jackie, not embedded in the triad, not during crisis. In a library, on a Thursday, with nothing at stake except honesty. He voluntarily revealed the February 2000 annulment conversation, admitting he'd drawn a hard line out of fear, not principle. He's choosing to help plan Draco's proposal — the thing he once refused to let exist.

Physically: they've been intimate without Jackie as catalyst or audience. Theo was gentle — devastatingly so — and Draco led for the first time on the final morning, taking the lighter from Theo's hand and saying "not today." Theo surrendered control not through domination-in-reverse but through genuine trust. The vampiric body's hypersensitive nerve endings made every touch acute; he had to vocalize his needs because Draco couldn't read his pulse or breathing. The vulnerability of being touched without the excuse of crisis or hunger was harder for Theo than any act of dominance. Draco's words "I trust you" became the key — the same words that broke Theo open both nights.

The dynamic is no longer "Theo initiates, Draco responds." Draco can now reach first. The inversion is new, fragile, and real.

**Post-Liverpool (April 2002):**
Draco married Jackie with Theo standing at the altar in Eli Lavigne's face. Theo watched his wife marry his lover and felt "both things simultaneously — the ache and the rightness" — and didn't try to resolve them into one.

During the raid, Draco positioned himself on Jackie's injured side without being told. The Malice's assessment: upgraded from "adequate" to "interesting." During the chase, He called Draco "pretty one" — the highest register of acknowledgment He has offered anyone outside Jackie, and the first time the Malice has addressed Draco with something resembling warmth (predatory warmth, but warmth).

Draco extracted the silver bullet from Theo's ribs on the corridor floor. Heard the Malice's thirteen minutes of work from the first floor and kept extracting fledglings. Confessed directly: *"I let it happen."* Theo: *"You held the line."*

After the chase went wrong, Draco administered emergency care — blood-replenishing potion, flesh-knitting charm, diagnostic monitoring. Sat on the bed holding Jackie's wrist and Theo's lighter and didn't move until Theo came back from the bathroom. His request the next morning: *"Whether I need to stop sitting in the kitchen"* — asking to be present for future Malice encounters rather than thirty seconds away.

They held hands across Jackie's sleeping body the night of the chase. Cold over warm. The same gesture from the M62 motorway, from the bed that morning, from every moment where the only language available was contact.

### Filippo de' Medici (Sire)

Filippo's blood turned Theo; this creates a sire-bond neither of them wanted. Filippo has guided Theo through the volatile early months in Florence.
