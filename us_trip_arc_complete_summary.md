# US TRIP ARC — COMPLETE SUMMARY

## **Arc Timeline: January 21 – February 2, 2002**

---

## PHASE 0: HAVEN HOUSE HOMECOMING (January 21)

### **Theo's Return**

After six months in Florence, Theo arrives at Haven House. Key moments:

- Required verbal invitation to cross the threshold (vampire lore)
- First physical contact with Jackie and Draco in six months
- Discovered the piano Jackie and Draco installed — emotional centerpiece of his homecoming
- All windows fitted with Jackie's sunproofing spell (her invention, Christmas gift to Theo)

### **The Intimacy**

- Jackie bit Theo during sex, drinking his blood — the Malice was *awed*, not angry: "She marked us. She CLAIMED us."
- Draco requested to be the center: inside Jackie while Theo fucked him
- Draco finally said all three France sentences, including the hardest: "I know how to hold something without breaking it."
- Emotional catharsis for all three

### **The Proposal Permission**

- Jackie floated the idea of remarrying (she's legally a widow with Theo "dead")
- Draco said YES before she finished explaining
- Theo gave his blessing with genuine happiness beneath the grief
- Rules established: Draco must propose properly in February; Theo attends via Polyjuice as Eli; ceremony after dark; Protean ring counts as engagement ring

---

## PHASE 1: THE SEVERANCE (January 25-27)

### **Samuel's Unexpected Visit**

Samuel arrived at Haven House unannounced while Draco was at work. Theo, listening from upstairs with vampire hearing, heard Jackie's heartbeat speed up when Samuel asked if her grief "changing anything" meant there was hope for him.

**The Confrontation:**

- The Malice took over; Theo revealed himself — fangs descended, crimson eyes
- Threatened Samuel: "The only reason you're walking out with your throat intact is because Jackie asked me to be better than my instincts"
- Samuel learned Theo is alive AND a vampire

**Jackie's Explosion:**

- Detonated at Theo for not trusting her to handle Samuel
- "I asked you if I could tell Sam — you said NO. For six months I lied because YOU made me!"
- Theo took full responsibility; admitted his failing, not hers

**Resolution:**

- Jackie demanded Theo tell Pansy and Blaise immediately
- Offered the Malice a compromise: let Him mark her so everyone knows who she belongs to
- The Malice responded with *respect* — agreed to discuss terms after the trip

### **The Lamb and Flag Meeting**

Theo met Samuel at a Muggle pub to apologize. Key exchanges:

**Theo admitted:**
- He heard Jackie's heartbeat speed up — "couldn't handle that some part of her wanted to"
- Called out Samuel's long game: "We used the same tactics. I've RUN that play."

**Samuel's terms:**
- Theo must tell Pansy and Blaise before Samuel returns from the States
- Samuel holds an undefined debt — "Someday I'm going to collect"

**Samuel's parting shot:**
- "If she BEGS me to... you being alive won't stop me."
- "Just so you have something to think about while we're gone. With her."

## PHASE 2: THE ATLANTIC (January 28-29)

### **The Flight**

- Soul bond with Theo **snapped** ~8 hours in when they left London airspace
- Jackie felt "hollowed out, like a tree struck by lightning"
- Guilty freedom at being "just one person"
- Samuel spent twelve hours not speaking to Jackie

### **New Orleans Arrival**

**Meeting Eli Lavigne:**
- Immediate mutual attraction with Samuel — Cole announced Eli had been "talking about Samuel for months"
- Eli's interest obvious from first meeting

**Silas Thorne (Vampire Sheriff) clocked Jackie:**
- Smelled her Animagus form (snow leopard)
- Smelled Theo's vampire blood on her skin
- Referenced "a fledgling in Florence" who "nearly killed someone precious"
- Implicit threat/protection: "What you carry stays yours, so long as you don't make it my problem"

### **The Boat Deck Confrontation**

Samuel confronted Jackie about what Silas revealed:

> "I wrote you letters for months when you wouldn't answer. I kept writing because I thought you needed to know someone gave a damn whether you lived or died. And now I find out the man I've been competing against for two years is a vampire who nearly ripped your throat out, and you chose him anyway."

**Samuel's shift:** Beginning to move from hope to exhausted recognition — the start of letting go.

### **Girls' Night (Jackie + Mira)**

- Mira asked directly if Jackie knew Samuel loved her while they were together
- Jackie admitted she knew since July 2001, hoped Mira might make him forget
- Mira's response: "I chose to stay with someone emotionally unavailable because I hoped I'd be the exception. That's on me."
- New friendship forming between them

---

## PHASE 2.5: THE WAREHOUSE HORROR (January 29-30)

### **Jackie Spots the Exchange**

At L'Eclipse, Jackie caught a drug deal — vials of processed vampire blood ("Sanguinem Mortis"). She followed the dealer and allied with Silas to investigate.

### **What They Found**

Marchetti's warehouse in the Bywater — a vampire blood processing operation:

- **Six containment cells** with captive vampires being drained
- **Forced siring confirmed** — creating fledglings specifically to harvest
- **Experiment logs** documenting how much could be taken, how fast vampires recovered
- Vampires too far gone to save — Silas granted mercy (quick death)
- One survivor extracted (woman from Atlanta, recently turned against her will)

**The Mercy Kill:**
- A conscious vampire begged Jackie for death — couldn't remember his own name
- "A garden. My mother's garden. There were roses..."
- Silas ended his suffering; Jackie stayed kneeling in the ash

### **Jackie's Combat**

- Started controlled and professional
- Escalated to full predator mode as she witnessed the horror
- Shifted between human and Animagus form mid-combat
- Took a cutting curse to the ribs — serious injury requiring vampire blood to heal

### **The Healing**

- Jackie drank Silas's blood out of necessity (dark curse preventing normal healing)
- Different from Theo's — older, heavier, tasted like Louisiana itself
- Two mouthfuls; no bond, no complications

### **Silas's Gifts**

1. **Sanctuary coin** — present to any New Orleans Kindred for Theo's protection
2. **Promise of resources** — allies in Europe who owe him debts
3. **Warning** — Liverpool origin point suggests either high-level corruption or someone powerful enough to ignore Camarilla law

### **The Evidence**

- Shipping manifests showing European origins: Marseille, Rotterdam, **Liverpool**
- Leather-bound ledger with every transaction, buyer, and supplier
- Jackie promised to investigate the British side of the operation

## PHASE 3: THE RECKONING (January 30)

### **Return to The Revenant**

Jackie stumbled onto the boat covered in blood. Found Samuel and Eli emerging from her cabin — clearly interrupted mid-kiss.

**The Lie:**
> "Someone tried to mug me behind L'Eclipse. Silas Thorne arrived, helped out, and we dealt with them accordingly."

**Samuel's Response:**
> "Don't insult me by pretending I can't see through bullshit when it's standing right in front of me."

He chose not to push — gave her space while making clear he knew she was lying.

### **Jackie Breaks Down**

- Asked Samuel to stay; needed an anchor against the nightmares
- Threw herself against his chest, needed to feel his heartbeat, his warmth, his *aliveness*
- "I can't tell you what I saw today. But it was awful. Monstrous. The kind of thing Voldemort would be proud of."
- Samuel held her all night

### **The Morning After**

Jackie woke with Samuel pressed against her back, arm around her waist. Instead of pulling away, she pressed closer.

**Jackie's honesty:**
> "You're completely right, Carrow. I do like the way you orbit me. It makes me feel special. I do like the way I can play you like you play your guitar. I've never been generous with you. Just selfish. Which is precisely why I'm the worst person possible for you."

**The forehead kiss** — blessing, absolution, release. Told him to pursue Eli: "So I'll know you actually made something for yourself. Not thanks to me, but despite me."

**Samuel's response:**
> "You're not the worst person possible for me. You're just not the right one. There's a difference."

**DECISION:** The dynamic fundamentally shifted — from painful unrequited orbit to genuine friendship built on honesty.

---

## PHASE 4: THE CONCERT & AFTERMATH (January 30-31)

### **L'Eclipse Performance**

The Second Sons delivered an extraordinary set. Samuel sang like a man who'd had his heart rearranged and decided to make it everyone else's problem.

### **Samuel and Eli's Night**

Eli brought Samuel to his grandmother's Creole townhouse — a real home, not a stage set.

**Eli's vulnerability:**
> "I want to know what you sound like when you're not performing. What you look like when you stop calculating... I want to give you something good without any strings attached."

**Samuel's honesty:**
> "I want to stop thinking for a few hours. I want to be with someone who actually sees me instead of the performance. I want to feel like I'm allowed to have something good."

They spent the night together. Samuel slept well for the first time in years.

---

## EPILOGUE: THE DEPARTURE (January 31 - February 2)

### **Tour Extension**

Eli announced ten additional venues across the Gulf Coast — Baton Rouge, Lafayette, Houston, Austin, San Antonio. Three weeks. The band voted unanimously to stay.

Jackie had to return home — work, responsibilities, a vampire trafficking network to investigate.

### **Airport Goodbye**

Samuel and Eli drove Jackie to the airport.

**Jackie's orders:**
- Write often — both of them, separately
- Be disgustingly, obnoxiously happy together
- "If I find out you've fucked this up, I will personally fly back to Louisiana and kick both your asses."

**The farewell:**
- Hugged Samuel fiercely — "Thank you. For staying. That night. For being there."
- Hugged Eli — he reminded her about Silas's debts: "He always collects."
- Walked away without looking back

**Samuel and Eli watched her plane take off:**
> "She's something else. Your Jackie."
> "She's not mine. She never was, really. I just didn't want to see it."

They walked out of the airport together, Eli already planning the Baton Rouge show.
