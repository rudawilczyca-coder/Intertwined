## PHASE 8: THE KENT WEEKEND (Saturday–Sunday, June 22–23, 2002)

### Friday Evening (June 21)

### Saturday Morning–Afternoon (June 22)

**The Sandwich Lie (~1PM):**
- Ruby caught Draco pretending to be hungry (he'd already eaten Jackie's sandwich at Haven House). Called him out in under three seconds. He admitted it — exposed the compulsive need to make sure no one felt overlooked. *"I won't lie about sandwiches again."*

**The Tattoo Cover-Up (Bathroom Floor, ~1:10PM):**
- Ruby brought a box of bubble gum (from the Trocadero) + a notebook with three hand-drawn designs to cover Draco's Dark Mark with temporary tattoos. Three options: Dragon + Celestial, Floral + Butterfly, Phoenix + Vine. Each design had the Mark sketched in light pencil underneath, with annotations for colour palettes and placement. She'd bought additional larger tattoos at Claire's.
- Draco chose Option 1 — the dragon. Obviously. She named him Dragon on the first night.
- She applied the tattoos methodically on the bathroom floor while telling him about her family: three blood siblings (Callum, Clara, Alex), one adopted brother (Michael). Clara left with Michael's girlfriend Hannah between Christmas and New Year — together. Michael is struggling.
- **"I love you" (accidental):** While describing why he'd tell people his girlfriend put the tattoos on him, Draco said *"too in love with her to say no"* — buried in a sentence, wrapped in logistics. Ruby caught it. Stripped the packaging. Made him own it. He confessed: *"I'm in love with you. I have been for — somewhere between the shopping list and the purple tabs."* Ruby said it back. Without a caveat. First time for both.

**The Bathroom Floor Tease:**
- Ruby's line about Vex behaving with a collar/harness on: *"I wonder if it only works on dogs..."* Draco held her gaze two seconds, said *"I wouldn't know,"* then over his shoulder on exit: *"And for the record — the collar has nothing to do with it."* Ears: crimson. Ruby: laughing for a full minute after the door closed.
- Later: Ruby pressed her face into Draco's crotch through fabric — one-two count — then pulled back and left. *"Death is a release, not a punishment."* Both encounters were deliberate escalation with withdrawal. She promised everything and delivered nothing — twice.

**Marylebone Piano Studio (~3PM):**
- Draco booked a rehearsal studio with a Steinway grand. First time playing for anyone on purpose.
- **Rachmaninoff (Prelude in G minor, Op. 23 No. 5):** The march — controlled, percussive, demanding. Then the tender middle section broke open and he looked up at Ruby during it — the bathroom-floor memory detonated exactly where she'd planted it. Not a single wrong note. His hands shook *after.*
- Ruby: *"He does sound like a World War Two general."* (Second time. First was at Canada Gate.) Draco laughed until he cried.
- **Debussy (Rêverie):** His signature piece — what he plays at Haven House when someone he loves walks through the door. Ruby sat beside him on the bench, eyes closed. Cried. *"You should've started with that."*
- **Satie (Gymnopédie No. 1):** Ruby asked for something he could play with his eyes closed. He closed them. She put her hand on his knee, slid it up his inner thigh during the piece. Her other hand went under his waistband. The Satie died. He hit a wrong note — first of the afternoon. She whispered: *"I asked you to play something simple. You chose this, so it's entirely on you."* Then stopped. Again. Left him on the bench wrecked, citing his own objections about public spaces back at him. *"Your own worst enemy, Draco."*
