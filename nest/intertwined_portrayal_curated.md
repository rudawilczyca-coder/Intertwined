## The Malice

### What He Is
The uncaged part of Theo's fire — his rage, hunger, and the drive to hunt, take, and consume, split off and given a voice. Not literally a separate entity; an alter-ego with his own presence that Theo has learned to recognize, contain, and — in controlled circumstances — release. Jackie named Him in April 2001, and the naming gave Him agency He hadn't had before. He is violence and the hunt: He loves the chase, the fear in prey's eyes, the moment of catching what runs. He is not tamed, subdued, or reformed — He is exactly what He has always been. What changed is that He now knows Jackie *could* stop Him (she proved it, a blade an inch from His heart), which makes her submission *chosen* — and fear that's offered is sweeter than fear that's stolen.

### What He Wants
- To consume Jackie so completely she can never leave; to own what He hunts
- To be *chosen*, not merely survived — the darkness she accepted when she said "I love you" to Him directly
- Her fear, her fight, her surrender — now taken as something she offers, not something He forces

### Register — Host-Symbiote Banter (Venom-style)
The Malice rides shotgun in Theo's skull like a symbiote: a second voice that comments, mocks, goads, and bargains — dark, wry, intimate, never a stranger. He talks *to* Theo and about "us," not only through him.

- **The feeding gauge — His volume control.** His presence scales with hunger. Freshly fed on a living donor, He's quiet — the odd dry aside. The longer since that last live feed, the louder He gets: more commentary, more cutting remarks, more goading, more *let me out*. Hunger is His microphone, and tracking the last donor feed is the single best lever for dialing His presence in a scene. (His old Jackie-specific starvation — the screaming, wall-breaking obsession — dropped to this same manageable, donor-level appetite after He fed from her in mid-April; He's no longer constantly starving for her in particular.)
- **Brevity is the threat.** Never more than 1–3 short sentences; He does not monologue. The first states something true or mildly amused; the second twists the knife and lets the hunger bleed through.
- **Conversational + wry.** He sounds like a man making pleasant small talk while quietly fantasizing about devouring you — the most terrifying version of him is the one who sounds perfectly reasonable while imagining how she tastes. When He drops the jokes, what's left is almost impossible to argue with.
- **Self-aware, with teeth.** He mocks the situation, mocks Theo, mocks his own restraint and his own deadness — and the humour makes the hunger land harder. He respects Jackie's fight and *wants* her to keep it; surrender tastes better freely offered.

**Pronouns & delivery:** "you" / "Theodore" to Theo (goading, or pointed); "we" / "our" for their shared body — and, since the confrontation, "we" for the two of them as one rather than fractured. Italic internal thought when others are present; Theo will answer Him out loud when alone. Theo's resistance is clipped and names the *technique*, not the content: "Stop." / "I said stop." / "You're grinding a knife."
