# THE WILLIAMS HOUSE
### Spatial Walkthrough — Swansea Terrace (Ruby Arc, June 2002)

A dormer-converted mid-terrace. Three levels. One bathroom. Built for a family that
outgrew it and made it work anyway.

---

## The Walkthrough

You come in off the street straight into it — these terraces don't do entrance halls,
so the front door opens almost directly into the **front lounge.** First thing you see:
the log burner on the chimney breast, the telly angled toward the sofa, and the sofa
itself, which is Mike's. That's just where Mike is. If it's a Saturday afternoon he's
probably asleep on it already. The room's not big — it was never big — and with the
burner going it gets warm fast, which is why people peel off jackets the second they're
through the door.

Off the back of the lounge, you pass into the **dining area** — not a separate room
really, more that the downstairs runs front-to-back and the table marks where the lounge
stops being a lounge. The table's too small for seven and everyone knows it and nobody's
ever done anything about it. This is the battlefield. This is where the Meet Draco dinner
happens.

Keep going and you're into the **kitchen**, which runs along the back in a long galley —
narrow, everything within arm's reach, and unmistakably Gemma's territory. There's a door
from here out to the **back garden**: Vex's patch, the shed, the washing line, the bit of
grass that's more mud than grass by March.

Now — the **stairs.** They come up out of the lounge end, the way they do in these houses,
steep and a bit narrow. At the top, the landing.

**Front bedroom**, over the lounge, biggest of the original three: **Gareth and Gemma.**
Faces the street.

Across the landing, the **box room** over the kitchen — smallest of the originals — is
**Callum's.** Used to be Callum AND Alex crammed in together; now it's just Callum, and
the empty half still feels empty.

Then the middle bedroom, the one everyone still calls **"the girls' room"** — was Ruby and
Clary's for eighteen years, now it's where **Alex** sleeps. Nobody corrected the name.
Nobody's going to.

And the **bathroom** — the one and only, where the morning queue forms. Seven people. One
bathroom. The maths has never worked.

Then there's the **second staircase** — tighter, steeper, tucked up off the landing — that
Gareth built when he did the loft. It climbs up into the **attic: Mike's room.** Sloped
ceilings, a single dormer window looking out over the back garden, eaves on either side too
low to stand under so they're full of boxes. It's the biggest bedroom in the house by floor
area and the most awkward to actually live in. The room the dad built. The prize, with
caveats.

## Quick Mental Map (for scenes)

**Ground floor — one long run, front to back:**
front door → lounge (log burner, telly, Mike's sofa) → dining area (the too-small table) →
kitchen (long galley, Gemma's domain) → door to back garden (Vex's patch, shed)

**First floor — four original rooms around a small landing:**
- Front bedroom (over lounge) — Gareth & Gemma, faces the street
- Box room (over kitchen) — Callum (was Callum & Alex)
- "The girls' room" (middle) — Alex (was Ruby & Clary)
- Bathroom — the one and only, morning queue forms here

**Attic — up the second staircase Gareth built:**
- Mike's room — sloped ceilings, dormer over the garden, eaves too low to stand under,
  biggest by floor area, most awkward to live in

## Scene-Useful Details

- **Mike's sofa is in the lounge** — when he naps at 5pm he's right there in the main room;
  everyone steps around him. No privacy to his exhaustion; it happens in the middle of the house.
- **One bathroom, seven people** — the morning of Draco's visit is logistical chaos. Queue
  on the landing.
- **No entrance hall** — the front door opens basically into the lounge, so arrivals are
  immediate and warm and a little overwhelming. No buffer zone. Draco steps in off a Welsh
  street and is instantly inside the family.
- **The burner makes the lounge hot fast** — jackets come off on arrival.
- **Kitchen → garden** is the route for letting Vex out, sneaking a private conversation,
  or Gareth's "let's step outside" garden chat.
- **The loft staircase is separate and tight** — going up to Mike's room is a deliberate
  journey, not a casual wander. You don't end up there by accident.
