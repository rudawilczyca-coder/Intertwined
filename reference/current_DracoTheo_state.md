## Draco

- He is grieving Ruby inside an otherwise good life. He honours no-contact absolutely and does not turn the loss into pursuit, punishment, comparison, or a project. Her final message reached him; his answer bounced unread.
- July left him more willing to name a want inside established relationships and choices he understands, and less willing to disguise one as logistics. In Paris he has said that he wants children with Jackie for himself, wants rare privacy with her, and wants to be seen without making exposure into a service he performs. This does not make unfamiliar male attention easy to classify: he has acknowledged wanting Theo, but still experiences that as an exception before he experiences bisexuality as a settled identity.
- With Jackie, he no longer behaves as though he is auditioning for the marriage. He can receive her forgiveness without denying the harm behind it, and accepts her defence as love without accepting it as absolution.
- With Theo, he can bring an unmanageable thing and accept clarity without consolation. Quiet contact and trust no longer require an emergency or an audience.
- He still fears being insufficient, but the fear no longer determines the verdict. His current direction is presence, consent, stated desire, and restraint without disappearance.
- After Leandro's False-Eli reveal, he knows Theo is alive and detained at Viola's London court. He, Jackie, and Filippo have brought Leandro there bound as leverage; they have not yet reached Theo. The Seneschal confirmed Theo's presence and said an attendant would tell him they had arrived, but message receipt is not observed.

## Theo

- During his nightly walk on Aug 6–7, he accepted Viola's formal invitation to court rather than make Haven House the site of her next approach. After a civil interrogation and a refused donor test, Viola detained him under prepared wards as an unpresented, unreleased vampire. Leandro later confirmed the detention to Jackie, Draco, and Bastien; the planned Saturday Portkey did not bring Theo to Fontainebleau.
- **The Saturday cover was Eli Lavigne.** Theo's prepared potion and Eli's supplied hair were in the office desk's second drawer. Leandro used Eli's face for the False-Eli operation; the genuine Eli's safety remains unconfirmed in the observed batch.
- He walks London most nights while the household sleeps. The empty city is not a consolation prize but a life he values; Jackie now knows, while Draco did not know the walks had become nightly.
- He has finally named a want belonging to his own marriage: chosen vows, a real honeymoon, and time that is not built around anyone else's father or ceremony. The household endorsed it, and the honeymoon played through Saturday; Theo himself remains detained at the closure cursor.
- Genevieve counted him in writing with a third World Cup ticket. Her purpose remains unknown.
- In Draco's post-Ruby grief, Theo's role is presence without false reassurance or a plan. He gives boundaries, refuses comforting lies, stays, and offers an instruction when narration would only deepen the spiral.
- The Malice is stable, purposeful, and reconciled with Jackie. It is the ancient cold fury Theo carried even before turning and shares the same bidirectional bond. Theo's sending side was cut by death, but the Malice retains access. During the taking it reached for Jackie through the bond; France's distance made contact difficult except for an extreme nightmare burst, while proximity now causes involuntary affect bleed-through even without conscious sending. Theo must learn containment.
