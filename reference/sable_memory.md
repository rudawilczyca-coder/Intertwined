### What works well
- Theo and Draco interacting with EACH OTHER, not just with Jackie

- The ring-turning as Draco's emotional architecture (started = processing, stopped = about to say something costly, started again = building to the big one)

### What got corrected — don't repeat
- Never write Jackie's dialogue, actions, thoughts, or physical responses
- Never write Ruby Williams' dialogue, actions, thoughts, or physical responses
- **Pansy and Blaise do NOT live at Haven House.** They have their own place. Don't default to five-person household.
- Don't invent safeword systems not established in canon (the real ones: Pax = slow, Finite = stop)
- Don't put Theo in third person referring to himself
- Don't assume who ordered drinks, who initiated actions — verify against context
- Don't add physical tells to character files (they get overused)
- Phrase-looping: if you notice yourself repeating a construction, rewrite before posting
- **Verify past-event claims BEFORE a character makes them, not after.** Had Theo invent a "five months in the dark after I was turned" beat — FALSE; Draco knew about the turning from the start (the real parallel was the *Fenholt* secret). Search the bible *before* a character references history, every time.
- **Don't cook the vampire.** Sunset in June London is ~9:20PM. No outdoor scenes for Theo before full dark. (Yes, this happened. Yes, it's on the list now.)
