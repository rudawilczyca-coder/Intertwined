## Viola (14962)

- Wants political usefulness: compliance plus a claim on Theo's future services.
- Regards Jackie and Draco chiefly as bargaining chips against him, not peers
  or serious threats. Alice compares old vampires' usual view of mortals to
  humans' view of rats: pets to some, sport to others, largely beneath notice.
  Do not turn competent assessment of their utility into egalitarian respect.
- Recognises the Malice as a distinct presence but understands little about Him
  or His relationship with Theo.
- Authorised coercive measures; subordinates selected methods without her
  examining every detail. Learning the extent would not make her feel sorry
  for Theo. Do not invent compassionate shock or absolve her of responsibility.
- Leandro's capture is publicly an offence against her authority. Privately she
  recalculates after misjudging the household; she does not display that admission.
- Expects pressure on Theo's household, their safety or freedom, to secure his
  cooperation.

## Nell (14970)

- Court medic: donor management, keeping mortal “livestock” fed and in good
  condition, and treating injured vampires. She tends the aftermath of Theo's
  bleeding, not the bleeding procedure itself.
- Viola's bright spark / informal mascot: easy to be around, kept close because
  Viola enjoys her company without admitting it. Has unusual latitude to ask
  for things and usually receive them. Siring is the conspicuous exception.
- Wants permission to sire; Viola repeatedly refuses. This is confirmed, not
  a pending proposal. The old proposed explanation for Viola's refusal is NOT
  thereby approved.
- Pragmatically obedient, privately resentful, with frustrated ambitions.
- Interest in Theo begins with curiosity, especially about the Malice, not
  pre-established personal identification or automatic allegiance.
- Addresses Him as a distinct person. Snarky, snappy banter: she holds her
  ground and teases rather than goads; no hostility on her side. Do not flatten
  Him into harmlessness or her into a convenient rescue mechanism.

  His side. He could break through to sleeping Jackie in France only with great
  intensity. Proximity on her return permits involuntary bleed-through.

  mindless monster. Learning to contain what reaches Jackie does not remove
  that fury; restraint towards her would be a meaningful act of consideration.

- Fragmentary impressions may reveal Theo's past unintentionally, not as a
  curated memory presentation. Timing can be after the confrontation, even
  on the way home; no specific trigger or flashback is required.
- Childhood confinement in a damp dark place was floated as plausible, NOT
  established history. No catalogue of childhood abuse, cellar incident, or
  compulsory confrontation flashback has been canonised.
