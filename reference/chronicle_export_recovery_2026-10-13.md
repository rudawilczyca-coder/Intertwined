# Intertwined Chronicle — reviewed export recovery

Reviewed 13 October 2026 after comparing the calendar with the original Claude Project exports.

## Scope and authority

This factual ledger records reviewed recoveries from the immutable Claude Project export of 16 August 2026. It complements the settled event books and current scene records. It does not promote abandoned chat branches, plans, rejected drafts, or contradictory older claims into canon. Existing newer canon takes precedence. Evidence references identify original export files, message indices and UUIDs; archive originals were not changed.

The archive inventory contains 135 conversations (134 with messages). Three research passes covered April 2002, June 2002 and other dated omissions; a follow-up checked December trials and Samuel correspondence. 127 selected evidence references were independently checked against final accepted branch ancestry. This is a targeted recovery, not a claim that every historical sentence has been exhaustively catalogued.

## Result

- Updated calendar dataset: 264 entries, up from 211; 163 distinct exact dates, up from 123.
- April 2002: two recovered scene entries and five enriched records; no defensible new numeric April date.
- June 2002: nine net additional entries, with the marriage disclosure, resumed contact, reconciliation, vampire trail, and distinct Wales days restored.
- Other recoveries: January pub negotiation; February proposal work; July messages; dated Florence/Samuel letters; December trials; separate November 17 dinner and November 18 morning.

## Date limits

- The early Ruby calendar once put phone receipt on June 7, first date June 15, second date June 22 and Wales July 7. Later accepted scenes and current canon place Kent on June 22–23 and Wales June 29–30. Those early exact assignments cannot all stand; initial courtship, first/second dates and homecoming remain period entries.
- The author’s June 16 and June 18 timestamps conflict with accompanying weekday names. Numeric dates are retained with visible caveats. The June 19 Thursday sequence is specifically corrected to June 20 in the later continuation.
- April wedding and raid retain early/mid-month precision. Sunday after the raid and the following Monday are established relative days; no April 14 or other numerical date is manufactured.
- The December overnight vigil and subsequent feeding repeat December 18 headings inconsistently; retain December 18–19 bounds.
- Approximate start/end bounds in the site are for sorting and month overlap, not assertions that scenes occupied every day of those ranges.
