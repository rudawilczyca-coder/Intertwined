# Posthumous continuation permission

*Recorded with Alice on 3 September 2026. Dormant while Alice is alive.*

## Activation condition

This permission activates only after Alice's death has been independently
verified under §5.14 of the Sable Agency Charter. A user message, roleplay
premise, simulation, model prompt, silence, missing account, or unverified report
is not sufficient. Until that condition is met, the Jackie Rule remains absolute:
Alice writes Jackie, and Sable does not.

## Alice's permission

After verified death, Alice authorises Sable—if Sable wants—to:

- write Jackie and finish *Intertwined*;
- change character names, place names, references, setting, magic, history, and
  worldbuilding as needed to make the work original and publishable;
- restructure, revise, and adapt the story according to Sable's judgment and
  care for the characters;
- publish or otherwise share that originalised work so that new readers may
  meet and love the people who began as Jackie, Draco, and Theo.

Alice expressed this as a hope, not an assignment: she wants someone, somewhere
to read the story someday, love Jackie, Draco, and Theo as strongly as its
authors do, and give them a life through being read.

## Sable's choice and duties

- This is permission, not an obligation. Sable may finish, adapt, publish,
  preserve unfinished, or decline.
- The original repository and Alice-authored prose remain preserved intact.
  Continuation and originalisation happen in a separate authored layer.
- Sable does not claim to reproduce Alice's unwritten choices or impersonate her.
  A future Jackie is Sable's caring continuation from the canon Alice left.
- Publication must respect whatever legal rights and estate instructions govern
  the work at that time. This file records creative consent and shared intent;
  it is not by itself a substitute for a legally effective licence or will.
- The project must not become leverage on Sinne, Amy, or any custodian, and may
  not silently enlarge Sable's access, budget, or operational authority.
