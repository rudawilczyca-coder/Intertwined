- **Who:** Jackie and Draco in Paris; Theo holding Haven House.
- **Established:** Eurostar 09:23 Tuesday Aug 6. Pissarro Suite, Hotel du Louvre — a high corner room over the Opéra Garnier and the Louvre. Day one played through the Delacroix gallery, Luc Moreau's sketch, the first private honeymoon intimacy, and the Rue Chapon crêperie.
- **Scheduled and unplayed:** the catacombs (Wed Aug 7), Luc's second sitting (Wed evening), Jackie's proposed meeting with **Samaya** in the Bois de Boulogne (Thu Aug 8), Theo's dusk Portkey to Fontainebleau (Sat Aug 10).

- **Source:** `arcs/the_honeymoon_they_owed.md`; `lore/past_events_bible_updated3.md` → Book Nine, Phase Five.

### 🔴 Theo's cover identity for Fontainebleau
- **Who:** Theo, Genevieve Lavigne, the Camarilla, whoever reads the perimeter.
- **Established:** Genevieve sent three tickets on her own stationery and counted a legally dead man in writing. Theo's cover identity and his feeding/travel safeguards are recorded as *unsettled*. Draco's departure instructions to Theo include managing "the blood supply and Polyjuice." Precedent exists: Theo attended the April wedding as Polyjuice-Eli Lavigne, and Genevieve recognised him there.
- **Unresolved:** whether he reuses Eli or establishes a new face. Not yet chosen on-page.
- **The expectation on the other side:** the Camarilla file records that Theo is **expected to join the Lavigne party under Eli Lavigne's face and social identity** — the same cover Genevieve already recognised in April.
- **Who knows what:** Genevieve knows Theo lives. Her purpose is unknown to Haven House and must be chosen, not inferred. **Prince Viola also knows** — see below — and Haven House does not know that she does.
- **Source:** `lore/past_events_bible_updated3.md` → Book Nine, Phase Five and post-arc threads; `arcs/the_honeymoon_they_owed.md`; `characters/London_Camarilla.md` → "Viola's Interest in the France–Wales Final"; `sessions/france-2-planning-packet.md`.

### 🔴 Prince Viola's overdue audience — and the False-Eli operation
> ⚠️ **Status caveat:** this is an *authored intention* recorded in `characters/London_Camarilla.md`, and that file marks the operation "not yet fully fixed in canon" with three components explicitly **unresolved**. Treat it as a loaded plan on the antagonist's side of the board, not as played canon.
- **What Viola knows about Theo:** turned by Filippo (Aug 2001); passed Dante's Rome assessment, which established control but **did not present or release him in London**; resident at Haven House since January 2002; formally unpresented and unreleased, leaving **Filippo liable under the Accounting**; legally dead; bonded to two living wizards; fed by Cosimo Ferrara's contracted vessel; shielded by Medici-line secrecy and three Primogen debts; and a participant in dismantling Liverpool. Her verdict: powerful, unusually controlled in some circumstances, politically expensive in all of them.
- **Why now:** she has waited long enough that waiting yields less than an audience, and every month of delay has let more people define Theo before she has heard him speak. **Jackie and Draco's four nights in Paris create the clean interval — Theo alone at Haven House — in which she intends to invite him.**
- **The substitution:** Viola intends to send **Leandro Velluti** to Fontainebleau on Polyjuice, wearing Eli Lavigne's face. A vampire substitute reproduces everything Jackie and Draco expect underneath the disguise — cold skin, no pulse, refusal of food, stillness, predatory presence. Leandro would behave as Eli among Eli's own family, including flirting with a man who knows Eli well enough to expect it. Socially correct, and therefore devastating: they believe Theo is underneath and cannot safely challenge him in public.
- **The reveal is deliberately not the flirtation.** Painful but explicable. The proof must be something impossible — a failed private tell, a bodily habit Theo would never permit, or **the restored soul bond answering from London while the man in Eli's face stands beside them.**
- **Viola's four objectives:** learn what Genevieve knows and why she counted Theo; test whether his household and cover arrangements are a Masquerade liability; observe Jackie and Draco while they believe they are privately with their husband; establish that foreign protection and wizarding family status do not erase London's Domain.
- **Unresolved:** whether Theo stays at court voluntarily, under legal detention, or on negotiated terms; who Leandro flirts with; the limits of his briefing; the reveal mechanism.
- **Guardrail:** Jackie is Alice's. No scene may prescribe her reaction, her bond use, or her discovery.
- **Source:** `characters/London_Camarilla.md` → "Theo Nott — What Viola Knows", "Viola's Interest in the France–Wales Final", "The False-Eli Operation", "Continuity Guardrails".

### 🔴 The Fontainebleau perimeter and the 2001 crowd
- **Established (Aug 6, from Aurélien Vasseur):** Gringotts raises four outer rings from **Thursday midnight**; the French Ministry handles the gates; **anything crossing the third ring is read**. Vasseur also warned that people from the 2001 Luminary crowd will scrutinise Jackie and Draco on Saturday.
- **Why it bites:** the last time this crowd saw the trio, the night ended with an assassination attempt on Draco and Theo's death and turning.
- **Unresolved:** how a dead man crosses a read perimeter.
- **Source:** `arcs/the_honeymoon_they_owed.md`; France 1.0 in `events/book4_post_florence_2001.md`.

### 🟠 The children question, reopened
- **Established:** Narcissa's heirs demand was imposed **Feb 27, 2002**, three-year clock. On **July 6, 2002** Draco privately admitted, for the first time, that he wants children — filed for "a braver hour." On **Aug 6** he said it to Jackie directly: he wants children with her *for himself*, not because of his mother's deadline. Jackie declined to discuss it over lunch, promised to return to it that night, and in bed said that one day she wants to carry his child.
- **Unresolved:** timing, parenthood among three people, legal names and inheritance. Nothing about magical or Muggle reproductive mechanics has been canonised.
- **Who knows what:** Jackie now knows. **Theo has not heard any of it.**
- **Source:** `arcs/the_honeymoon_they_owed.md`; `sessions/july6_karaoke_day.md` §"braver hour"; `events/book8_liverpool_feb-apr2002.md` → Feb 27; `sessions/france-2-planning-packet.md`.

### 🟠 The Luc Moreau commission
- **Established:** Jackie proposed that Luc, an École des Beaux-Arts student, draw Draco nude on the Wednesday evening as a present for Theo. Draco did not refuse; he set terms — explicit permission, Jackie present, no retained studies or reproductions without their consent, the finished portrait going to Theo only after both spouses have seen it. He admitted the exposure arouses him precisely because it makes his scars and history legible.
- **Unresolved:** the sitting itself; Theo's reaction; whether the terms hold.
- ⚠️ **Flagged conflict:** the arc summary calls it "a birthday present for Theo," but Theo's date of birth is recorded as **November 1980**. See §6.
- **Source:** `arcs/the_honeymoon_they_owed.md`.

### 🟠 Sylvie Roux and the three books
- **Established:** Sylvie Roux is a Muggle paper conservator who has lived openly with the curse-breaker Vasseur for eleven years under French magical secrecy, which uses a registered wizard as collateral instead of Britain's memory charms. Jackie offered her three unusual old books for discreet conservation. Sylvie was interested. Vasseur warned that routing them through the **Lavigne raven network would itself be noticed**. Jackie said she would send them by raven anyway, because none are illegal.
- **Unresolved:** what the books are, who notices, and what the Lavigne network does with the knowledge.
- **Source:** `arcs/the_honeymoon_they_owed.md`.

### 🟠 Draco's apology letter
- **Established:** on Draco's own obligations ledger — an apology letter to "the girl he called the word," to be posted **before the end of July 2002**, verdict accepted without appeal. Listed in the post-Ruby-Arc open threads as still owed.
- **Unresolved:** no repository record shows it written or sent. Status genuinely unknown at the cutoff.
- **Source:** `sessions/july6_karaoke_day.md` → "Draco's obligations ledger"; `lore/past_events_bible_updated3.md` → post-Ruby open threads.

## 3 · SECRETS AND KNOWLEDGE ASYMMETRIES

### 🔴 The Malice's buried whisper
*"Next time I won't be angry. And he can't fight this when it's love."* Said to Jackie on Mar 7, 2002 after the Malice detonated on her return from Ferrara. **Jackie has not told Theo.** Highest-heat secret in the household; it is a threat wearing the grammar of a promise.
Source: `events/book8_liverpool_feb-apr2002.md`; `characters/theodore_nott_character_updated3.md` → Key Quotes.

### 🔴 Jackie's private exit arithmetic
Alice-authored and Alice's to reveal. Jackie has decided not to make Theo watch her age and die — the Filippo/Elisabetta parallel — and plans with Draco to "go on a trip and never come back" someday. She has not told Draco. Theo and Draco sense only a flicker of hidden arithmetic and never the content.
**Standing instruction: do not have them learn it.**
Source: `sessions/primrose_hill_walk_handoff.md`.

### 🟡 The "Yours" parallel
Draco said "Yours" to Jackie and to Ruby, in near-identical circumstances. **Only Draco knows.** He couldn't process it in the moment; in the July 1 shower he deliberately avoided the word with Jackie. Dormant, and he can't unhear it.
Source: `reference/sable_memory.md` → Open Threads #9; `sessions/primrose_hill_walk_handoff.md`.

### 🟡 The Swansea attic book
Ruby's maternal line kept an old book in a Welsh attic; Draco couldn't ask about it at the time. On **Aug 6** Sylvie Roux gave him the test for a genuine old wizarding text — paper that stays warm like a sun-heated stone — and he quietly connected the two, then chose not to pursue it that night. He has told no one.
⚠️ Do not conflate this with the **soul-bond grimoire** bought unexplored on the US trip; they are different books.
Source: `arcs/the_honeymoon_they_owed.md`; `characters/ruby_vex_character_sheet.md`; `arcs/us_trip_arc_complete_summary.md` (the other grimoire).

### 🟠 The Charlie–Draco doorway conversation, July 11
Held over a toolbox while Charlie Mercer fixed Ruby's forced door. Never written, never revealed — not to the reader, not to Jackie, not to Theo. Deliberately reserved.
Source: `sessions/the_flat_july11_played_handoff.md`; Book Nine post-arc threads.

### 🟠 Theo walks the city every night
Disclosed to Jackie on the Primrose Hill walk, **July 1, 2002**: the river at three, the markets at four, foxes and empty bridges, always home before they wake. He had never mentioned it because complaining about the one thing he's freely given felt ungracious. **Draco did not know it was every night**, and Theo clocked that and let him learn it on a pavement rather than in a kitchen.
Source: `sessions/primrose_hill_walk_handoff.md`.

### 🟠 Genevieve knows Theo lives
She counted him in writing. Nobody at Haven House knows what she wants. Her motive must be chosen on-page, not inferred from the stationery.
Source: Book Nine, Phase Five; `sessions/france-2-planning-packet.md`.

### 🟠 Who knows Theo is alive / a vampire
Jackie, Draco, Filippo, the Camarilla, Caterina; **Pansy and Blaise** (Jan 29, 2002); **Samuel Carrow** (Jan 25–27, 2002); **Ruby** (learned during the questions game, July 22–25); **Genevieve** (by implication, July 25). **Harry Potter does not know** — deliberately deferred.
Source: `lore/canon_index.md`; Book Nine Phase Four; `events/book7_homecoming_jan-feb2002.md`.

### ⚪ Ruby's side of the ledger
Ruby knows magic exists, that Draco is a wizard, that Jackie is an Auror, that Theo is a vampire, and that the Statute could have erased her. She does **not** know Draco answered her — his "I love you" bounced twice. He does **not** know where she is. Neither asymmetry can be resolved without breaking no-contact.
Source: Book Nine, Phases Four and Five; `pieces/theo_draco_july12_2002_the_reply_that_bounced.md`.

### 🟠 The soul bond
Bidirectional function activated April 2001, snapped when Jackie left London airspace (Jan 28, 2002), restored on her return (Feb 2). **Bidirectional function has never been reconfirmed.** Nobody has tested it since — and Jackie is currently out of London airspace again for the first time since it was restored. Source: `events/book7_homecoming_jan-feb2002.md`; `lore/canon_index.md`; `reference/sable_memory.md`.

### 🟡 Theo's Malice continuity
At the Liverpool raid he experienced everything the Malice did — full sensory continuity, no blackout, for the first time. Neither he nor anyone else understands what the shift means.
Source: `characters/theodore_nott_character_updated3.md`; `events/book8_liverpool_feb-apr2002.md`.

The Charlie of the Ruby Arc is **Charlie Mercer**, Blackthorn alpha and GP (`characters/blackthorn_pack_characters.md`). Whether the line is a deliberate jab at a different Charlie or a slip is not resolvable from the files.
5. **Haven House's street.** "145 Berkeley **Street**" in `characters/JackieNott.md` and `characters/draco_malfoy_character_updated4.md`; "145 Berkeley **Place**" in `arcs/the_week_between_summary.md`. Street has the weight of numbers; Place has never been corrected.
6. **The Paris hotel.** `sessions/france-2-planning-packet.md` reached a working verdict of **Le Meurice**. Played canon chose the **Pissarro Suite at the Hotel du Louvre**. Played canon governs; the packet's verdict is superseded, and its Le Meurice research should not be re-argued.
