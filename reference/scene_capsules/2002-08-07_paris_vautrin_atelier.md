# SCENE CANON CAPSULE — Wednesday, 7 August 2002, Maison Vautrin

> Direct scene context for SillyTavern. Factual ledger only—not a suggested
> emotional arc. This supersedes the dawn capsule for the current atelier scene.

## Clock and place

- Wednesday, 7 August 2002, mid-morning.
- Maison Vautrin, a hidden magical atelier behind Galerie de Montpensier near
  the Palais-Royal. Its Muggle-facing shop appears to restore historic textiles;
  the expanded atelier beyond the concealed entrance is fully magical.

## Present

- Jackie and Draco are selecting formal clothes for Friday's World Cup
  Diplomatic Reception.
- Madame Vautrin and two atelier assistants are present.
- The current fitting is Jackie's. She is on the dais wearing the storm-grey
  gauze and blackened-armour design. Do not begin Draco's fitting or move to a
  new social hinge until Jackie answers or explicitly yields the moment.

## Immediate visible beat

- Jackie has accepted the bold design provisionally, praised Vautrin's speed,
  then told Vautrin that Draco's colours are charcoal through dove-grey and
  white—not black. She teased that he may have copied someone he admires.
- Draco reacted by naming Theodore and denying that Theodore owns black.
- Madame Vautrin may answer Jackie and Draco from what has happened in the room.
  Keep the social focus available to Jackie rather than converting her comment
  into a long independent Draco/Vautrin fitting sequence.

## NPC knowledge boundaries

- Vautrin met Draco once at a 2001 trade reception. She may remember his public
  professional role, manner, and clothing. This does not grant intimacy.
- Vautrin now heard Theodore's name from Draco. She may know only whatever
  limited public account she plausibly remembers; she does not know Theo is
  alive, the real household structure, or private relationship dynamics.
- Vautrin and the assistants do **not** know Luc Moreau, the nude portrait
  appointment, what happened in the Pissarro Suite, Draco's attraction or
  sexuality, Jackie's nightmare, Ruby, or Theo's current situation. Do not
  invent a phone call, friendship, client link, gossip route, or lucky guess
  that reveals any of these facts.
- They know the couple need formalwear for an official reception because Draco
  told Vautrin the practical purpose of the visit. They have not seen the
  invitation unless Jackie or Draco shows it.
- Strangers may notice clothing, posture, accent, public names, and behaviour in
  this room. They cannot accurately infer private history from those surfaces.

## Muggle / magical boundary

- Maison Vautrin's interior is magical; enchanted cloth, mirrors, mannequins,
  tailoring spells, and warded formalwear are appropriate here.
- The Hôtel du Louvre and its staff are Muggle. Hotel staff provide ordinary
  French/Muggle newspapers and do not know wizarding institutions. A wizarding
  newspaper or magical object can enter the hotel only if Jackie or Draco brings
  or separately obtains it from a magical source.
- Magic used outside the concealed atelier must respect the Statute of Secrecy.

## Hidden state

- Theo was taken from Haven House during the night and is detained by Prince
  Viola's court. Jackie and Draco do not know. Nobody in the atelier knows.
- Luc Moreau's 20:00 sitting remains a private appointment known only to Jackie,
  Draco, and Luc.
