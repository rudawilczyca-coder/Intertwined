- Bastien greeted Jackie outside, completed a warm handshake with Draco, and departed with a crate of Friday's empty bottles for the hospitality collection point.
- Jackie and Draco ate lunch with Bastien and Margaux after leaving the magically expanded Lavigne lounge. Élodie remained at the tent. Geneviève and Vivienne are at tea with the Welsh representation captain's aunt over possible mining shares; Francine remained at the château to oversee the quartz interests.
- During lunch, Jackie said Eli is expected at dusk and that she and Draco will collect him from the north Portkey field before meeting the others in the family box. Beneath those ordinary logistics she aches for Theo, but Bastien and Margaux know only Eli's cover identity. Draco privately reassured her: “Soon.”
- After lunch, Jackie wagered thirty Galleons that she could get closer to the Common Welsh Green's cage than Bastien. They explicitly agreed that neither contestant could cross the containment wards. Draco objected, then followed with Jackie's stuffed dragon and tiny smoke-breathing figurine in his arms while Margaux accompanied them.
- At the enclosure, Bastien approached first, stopped close enough to provoke the dragon's attention without crossing the ward, and marked his nearest point in the grass. Jackie handed Draco both dragons, transformed into her snow-leopard Animagus form, crossed the containment ward, and sprinted around the cage while the Welsh Green pursued her with fire. She escaped back through the ward with only the fur at the tip of her tail singed.
- Jackie transformed back, laughing and exhilarated, and declared that she had crossed the ward and outrun a dragon. Bastien immediately disqualified her because crossing the ward broke the wager's agreed rule, then took responsibility for the bet when a handler arrived. Draco verified that Jackie was alive and unburned, pulled her against him, and—still shaking with fear beneath his anger—said: “You do not get to ask me to admire this yet.”
- Jackie and Draco left Bastien and Margaux handling the ward-breach fallout. Jackie's claim that dying happy would be a good end, like Theo's death protecting Draco, struck Draco's deepest guilt. Jackie apologised for the comparison and disclosed the prognosis history she had never given him in numbers: twenty-five, then twenty-two, then only two years by their final Hogwarts year—enough for Cyrus to marry her, extract a child, and let her die. Her tiny dragon broke in her hand; Draco repaired the clay, but the animation charm was gone, and Jackie threw the lifeless figurine away.
- Jackie explained that she had promised herself the years recovered from the curse would contain actual experience rather than mere survival. Draco conceded that he had misunderstood, affirmed that her unexpected years belong to her, and distinguished autonomy from concealing a risk in which she had made him an unwitting participant. He recalled telling her at the forest edge to “run fast—but come back to me,” and said he meant both halves. Jackie promised to come back, called the dragon run a calculated ninety-percent survival risk, and anticipated Theo's fury. Draco accepted the promise but not the odds, insisted Jackie must tell Theo the full story herself, and ended by reminding her that crossing the ward disqualified her: “You owe Bastien thirty Galleons.” This is the live endpoint; Jackie and Draco are walking together through the promenade, away from the enclosure. Jackie's response is unwritten.
