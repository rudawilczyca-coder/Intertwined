# Saturday — Fontainebleau

## Live working scene
- Saturday, 10 August 2002, afternoon. France–Wales World Cup Final.

- Opening is a live working draft, not yet archived played canon. The earlier rushed opening was withdrawn and replaced in full.
- Jackie and Draco crossed the first Muggle-repelling ward and the second ticket ward, descended through the hospitality village, and reached the Lavigne tent. Later, Jackie, Draco, Pansy, and False Eli crossed the third reading ring successfully on their way into the stadium.
- Bastien greeted Jackie outside, completed a warm handshake with Draco, and departed with a crate of Friday's empty bottles for the hospitality collection point.
- Jackie and Draco ate lunch with Bastien and Margaux after leaving the magically expanded Lavigne lounge. Élodie remained at the tent. Geneviève and Vivienne are at tea with the Welsh representation captain's aunt over possible mining shares; Francine remained at the château to oversee the quartz interests.
- During lunch, Jackie said Eli is expected at dusk and that she and Draco will collect him from the north Portkey field before meeting the others in the family box. Beneath those ordinary logistics she aches for Theo, but Bastien and Margaux know only Eli's cover identity. Draco privately reassured her: “Soon.”
- After lunch, Jackie wagered thirty Galleons that she could get closer to the Common Welsh Green's cage than Bastien. They explicitly agreed that neither contestant could cross the containment wards. Draco objected, then followed with Jackie's stuffed dragon and tiny smoke-breathing figurine in his arms while Margaux accompanied them.
- At the enclosure, Bastien approached first, stopped close enough to provoke the dragon's attention without crossing the ward, and marked his nearest point in the grass. Jackie handed Draco both dragons, transformed into her snow-leopard Animagus form, crossed the containment ward, and sprinted around the cage while the Welsh Green pursued her with fire. She escaped back through the ward with only the fur at the tip of her tail singed.
- Jackie transformed back, laughing and exhilarated, and declared that she had crossed the ward and outrun a dragon. Bastien immediately disqualified her because crossing the ward broke the wager's agreed rule, then took responsibility for the bet when a handler arrived. Draco verified that Jackie was alive and unburned, pulled her against him, and—still shaking with fear beneath his anger—said: “You do not get to ask me to admire this yet.”
- Jackie and Draco left Bastien and Margaux handling the ward-breach fallout. Jackie's claim that dying happy would be a good end, like Theo's death protecting Draco, struck Draco's deepest guilt. Jackie apologised for the comparison and disclosed the prognosis history she had never given him in numbers: twenty-five, then twenty-two, then only two years by their final Hogwarts year—enough for Cyrus to marry her, extract a child, and let her die. Her tiny dragon broke in her hand; Draco repaired the clay, but the animation charm was gone, and Jackie threw the lifeless figurine away.
- Jackie explained that she had promised herself the years recovered from the curse would contain actual experience rather than mere survival. Draco conceded that he had misunderstood, affirmed that her unexpected years belong to her, and distinguished autonomy from concealing a risk in which she had made him an unwitting participant. He recalled telling her at the forest edge to “run fast—but come back to me,” and said he meant both halves. Jackie promised to come back, called the dragon run a calculated ninety-percent survival risk, and anticipated Theo's fury. Draco accepted the promise but not the odds, insisted Jackie must tell Theo the full story herself, and reminded her that crossing the ward disqualified her: “You owe Bastien thirty Galleons.”
- Draco agreed Jackie could postpone telling Theo until they returned home so the World Cup reunion would not become an immediate disciplinary hearing. He rejected her claim that the leopard was a separate legal person, identified Theo's conflict of interest as her proposed counsel, and ordered her to pay Bastien.
- Jackie paid Bastien after he and Margaux returned to the tent. Margaux supplied a Snitch-Shooter cover story for the wager. Genevieve, Vivienne, and Margaux left for the family box at 18:00; Pansy arrived at 18:15 after fighting the Apparition crowds.
- Pansy is established as living at the Lavigne château and working as Genevieve's Private Secretary and British Liaison. She knows the Lavigne family well. She told Jackie that Blaise visited the previous Sunday but slept alone in the blue guest room: Pansy ended their affair when she moved to France and will not resume it unless he chooses her openly and accepts the consequences. Jackie jokingly suggested Bastien as another fish in the sea; Pansy and Bastien's exchange developed genuine, if wary, flirtation.
- Jackie, Draco, and Pansy went to the north Portkey field and met the man they believe is Theo under Eli Lavigne's Polyjuice cover. He is actually Leandro, sent by Viola's court while Theo remains detained in London. Leandro reproduced Eli's social manner and Theo's private physical cues convincingly enough to pass Jackie, Draco, and Pansy's first scrutiny. He passed the third security ring with the court-prepared identity and credentials.
- Inside the World Cup stadium, the pitch has three golden goal hoops at each end. The accepted staircase rewrite has the group meet Harry, Hermione, Ginny, and Ron: Harry greets Jackie warmly and does not suspect Theo survived or that Eli is false; Hermione and Draco use first names after the previous evening's apology and duel; and the old hostility between Pansy/Draco and Ron/Ginny remains specific rather than becoming a quip exchange. Pansy introduces False Eli, whose cover remains convincing. During the match, Pansy notices that False Eli is convincingly comfortable with Laurent and asks whether he seems all right; Jackie insists he is simply acting like Eli, while Draco's unease remains unresolved. Wales wins 210–130 after the Welsh Seeker takes the Snitch. Draco celebrates with Eli, then says he would rather return to the tent than stay for the presentation: “Unless either of you wants to watch it.”
- The Welsh scarves help Jackie, Draco, Pansy, Bastien, and False Eli move through the celebrating crowd back to the Lavigne tent. Élodie asks that the tent be closed because the noise agitates its fabric, then withdraws down the corridor. Jackie asks False Eli whether the crowd troubled him. He answers safely that he prepared before leaving and that it was only noise.
- Draco tests him with a false premise from the private July 26 Haven House library scene: “When I said I didn't know how to lose someone who was still alive, you kissed me.” No kiss occurred. Leandro accepts and intensifies the invention—“You kissed me first”—and reaches for Draco's jaw. Draco catches his wrist, says the kiss never happened, draws his wand, and identifies him as not Theodore.
- Leandro pivots immediately into Eli's public identity and the family setting. He moves toward Bastien and the tent opening, loudly reframing the spouses as asking Eli to indulge grief for their dead husband and Draco as inventing a kiss and reaching for him. Pansy stands in the path to the flap without drawing her wand. Leandro's raised voice can carry down Élodie's corridor. Bastien has heard the Theodore/impersonation claim and turns to Jackie with: “Theodore is dead.” The next turn begins on Jackie's response; Leandro's social countermove is active and physical containment remains unresolved.
- Jackie silently draws her wand and casts *Incarcerous*. Leandro nearly reaches Bastien before the ropes catch him and drop him face-first on the carpet. Jackie tells Bastien that Theodore is “not exactly” dead and that he should sit. Leandro flexes once against the ropes; they hold, but visibly strain. Draco keeps his wand trained on him while Pansy draws hers and moves beside Bastien.
- Leandro identifies himself as Leandro Velluti in service to Prince Viola of London. He confirms that Viola holds Theodore at court after Theo refused to remain for formal presentation, and that Theo was alive and still refusing her when Leandro left London. He admits that the False-Eli operation was intended to keep Jackie and Draco deceived while Viola decided what to do with Theo. Draco tells Bastien the concealed history: Theo died in August 2001, woke three days later as a vampire, and has been alive since. Bastien finally sits.
- Jackie refuses to untie Leandro in a tent full of her family, binds his chair with the existing ropes, and orders the interrogation moved to her and Draco's bedroom so it can be muffled away from Élodie, the entrance, and any returning relative. Pansy places and reinforces *Muffliato* at the bedroom door; Draco floats the occupied chair inside; Bastien follows and refuses to wait outside. The ropes still hold but are not proven sufficient for an ancient vampire. Draco and Pansy remain armed.
- Leandro says Viola seeks neither money nor blood. He explains that Theo has resided in Viola's Domain since January without presenting himself, that Rome's control assessment could not recognise him in London, that Filippo remains answerable for an unreleased fledgling, and that the household's arrangements exist outside Viola's permission. He discloses that Geneviève's three tickets counted a legally dead, unpresented vampire as family and that the False-Eli operation was surveillance as well as concealment. Leandro cannot promise terms Viola has not named or guarantee Theo's release; he can carry a request for an audience, explain Viola's jurisdictional grievance, and tell her that Theo's family understood the message. His demand to be untied was a test of whether fear made Jackie careless, which she passed. The next turn begins with Draco's wand pressed to Leandro's borrowed chest and Leandro telling Jackie to ask him for entrance to Viola's court without pretending Viola has already decided the price.
