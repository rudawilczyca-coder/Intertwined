Draco conceded that he had misunderstood, affirmed that her unexpected years belong to her, and distinguished autonomy from concealing a risk in which she had made him an unwitting participant. Jackie promised to come back, called the dragon run a calculated ninety-percent survival risk, and anticipated Theo's fury. Draco accepted the promise but not the odds, insisted Jackie must tell Theo the full story herself, and reminded her that crossing the ward disqualified her: “You owe Bastien thirty Galleons.”
