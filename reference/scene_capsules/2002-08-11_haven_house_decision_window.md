## Sunday, 11 August — return, threshold, collapse

### Travel and ward geometry

- It is after sunrise by the time they return.
- Vampires cannot Apparate independently, but may Side-Along with a wizard and
  may use Portkeys.
- Haven House's wards recognise only the three residents; they were tightened
  after Pansy developed a habit of arriving unannounced. Draco can Side-Along
  Theo directly inside. Filippo cannot be Apparated through the wards.
- Jackie may Side-Along Filippo to the front step/ward boundary, open the door,
  and invite him across the threshold. The exact invitation and Jackie's action
  are Alice's to write.
- **Author correction — sunlight:** direct sunlight begins harming a vampire
  immediately, but does not annihilate them at first contact. Destruction takes
  seconds and may approach a minute depending on vampire, exposure, and
  circumstances; this is not a safe or precisely timed grace period. Filippo's
  few exposed steps to the door are dangerous but survivable. This supersedes
  the stale “immediate and total” wording in the older quick reference.

### Luna's grievance

- Luna, Draco's black Kneazle, has been alone for several days because Theo's
  detention and the France crisis prevented anyone arranging care.
- She is intelligent and capable of finding food; she is angry and neglected,
  not starving.
- Planned comic release: once the door opens, Luna becomes a black mass of fur
  aimed at Jackie, echoing her old grudge from being kept in a gift box before
  Draco received her. Alice controls whether Luna reaches, scratches, or is
  intercepted; do not determine Jackie's action or injury.
- Filippo's installation as a guest may carry quiet domestic comedy: an ancient
  Florentine vampire entering the exhausted, disorderly household for the first
  time. Do not turn him into a caricature.

### Blood and rest

## Monday, 12 August — bond cost and the Florence alternative

### Private bond testing

The household tests what proximity between Jackie, Theo, and the Malice now
means. This is intimate discovery, not Viola's public demonstration.

Author-set mechanics:

- Proximity alone is tolerable while the Malice remains quiet and does not reach
  through the bond.
- When He rises close to the surface or actively taps the bond, He bypasses
  Jackie's shields far too easily and overwhelms her.
- Theo's depletion keeps the Malice near the surface and makes bleed-through
  more likely. Feeding improves regulation but does not erase the mechanism.
- Jackie is likely to minimise the cost, say she is fine, and power through;
  only Alice may write whether and how she does so.
- The Malice knows the true cost because He is inside the contact. His live
  dilemma is whether stopping would override Jackie's stated agency or whether
  continuing would collude with concealed harm.
- Preferred Malice choice: He initially honours an informed request to continue,
  but does not lie to Theo. Once He recognises that Jackie is concealing the
  actual cost, He tells Theo plainly. He protects her by surrendering access,
  not by paternalistically deciding at the first discomfort.
- Theo cannot feel Jackie through his severed side of the bond. The Malice can
  reach where Theo cannot; Theo is likely to hate the asymmetry. Keep the
  reaction character-led rather than therapeutic.
- *Pax* and *Finite* retain their established meanings. Their exact effect on
  bond contact may be tested in play; do not announce a result before evidence.

### Filippo and Theo — bilateral choice

This conversation occurs without Jackie present. Sable writes both Theo and
Filippo; Alice may steer premises OOC, but the scene must not summon Jackie to
decide it for them.

- Filippo wants Theo to genuinely consider returning to Florence. He does not
  offer it as ownership or punishment.
- Florence is a credible alternative: Theo knows the city and did not hate his
  six months there; Filippo has influence; he could seek suitable positions for
  Jackie and Theo through Italian Ministry contacts; Draco could build a career
  where the Malfoy surname is not a permanent British post-war sentence; the
  household could live comfortably and under stronger political protection.
- Filippo's argument should strike Theo through his spouses: London may be what
  Theo wants, while Italy may offer Jackie and especially Draco a fairer future.
- Theo must not dismiss the offer with “London is home, period.” He has to weigh
  whether remaining is selfish and articulate why chosen belonging at Haven
  House still matters.
- Filippo's hesitation about releasing formal authority is protective: once he
  releases Theo, Viola gains direct jurisdiction and Filippo loses a legal means
  of shielding him. It is not possessiveness.
- The intended direction is eventual release and London presentation, but the
  conversation must earn it. Their family/mentor bond survives the end of legal
  ownership.

## Tuesday, 13 August — preparation and the family-future conversation

### Court preparation

- Filippo prepares Theo for presentation: Camarilla hierarchy, London names and
  roles, etiquette, phrases that preserve autonomy, and language that would
  accidentally concede more authority than intended.
- Draco can contribute legal and political reading without being treated as a
  Camarilla equal. The Malice may provide concise, unhelpful suggestions about
  eating officials, but He must not flatten the scene into a running joke.

### Children and contraception — must occur before the time-skip

The conversation is postponed from Sunday/Monday but should happen Tuesday,
after some recovery and before Wednesday closes the arc. Filippo is still a
guest with supernatural hearing; the household may use Muffliato. The privacy
awkwardness may be funny, but the conversation itself matters.

Established entering facts:

- Draco has told Jackie he wants children with her for himself, not to satisfy
  Narcissa's three-year heirs demand.
- Jackie told Draco that one day she wants to carry his child.
- Theo does not know either statement.
- Earlier canon establishes that Theo knew Jackie used a contraceptive potion
  and considered taking it her decision.

Scene purpose and boundaries:

- Jackie raises that she does not want to continue the contraceptive potion, or
  does not want to take the next dose. Alice chooses her exact wording, timing,
  and whether she has already missed a dose; do not invent it for her.
- Her body and the potion are her decision. She is not asking permission to stop.
- Continuing sex with pregnancy possible creates consequences for all three, so
  the household must discuss the risk before it silently becomes a fait accompli.
- Draco has prior context; Theo must be brought into the conversation rather
  than discovering that Jackie and Draco privately decided the household's
  future around him.
- The scene need not become “we are trying for a child tonight.” It must reach
  an honest shared understanding of what stopping means: accepting pregnancy as
  a possibility, using another method temporarily, or changing sexual practice
  while readiness remains unresolved. The exact decision is live and must be
  earned in play.
- Do not convert disagreement into therapy language or give any spouse a veto
  over Jackie's body. Let practical, emotional, dynastic, legal, and relational
  consequences remain distinct.

## Wednesday, 14 August — presentation off-page, aftermath on-page

- Theo and Filippo go to the London court without Jackie and Draco.
- Reasons: mortals have no Camarilla standing; their presence adds exposed
  targets rather than leverage; Jackie's proximity to the surfaced Malice can
  complicate Theo's control; and the formal decision belongs between Prince,
  sire, and childe.
- Do not play the full court hearing unless Alice later asks. Cut from their
  departure to their return and focus on the household aftermath.

Planned agreement returned from court:

1. Filippo formally releases his sire authority over Theo.
2. Haven House is recognised as Theo's residence.
3. Theo answers directly to Viola for feeding, secrecy, conduct, and summons.
4. Theo attends the London court one night each week for education in hierarchy,
   etiquette, local vampires, obligations, and court machinery. Viola regards
   Filippo's six months of training as insufficient because he prioritised safe
   feeding and preventing Theo from killing Jackie.
5. Viola retains a continuing claim on Theo's service, not a single task that
   purchases permanent freedom. A possible first use is assessing or tracking a
   vampire whose Beast appears unusually organised; keep the exact assignment
   open until it is actually issued.

### Nell and nocturnal London

- Once Theo is no longer her detained patient, Nell may invite him out rather
  than receive a direct order from Viola. Preferred texture: she has seen him
  wandering London aimlessly and tells him there are wilder, better ways to
  spend the night.
- This can become once- or twice-weekly outings alongside the required weekly
  court attendance.
- Their relationship is friendship/foil, not romance. Nell knows the court Theo
  distrusts; Theo knows domestic belonging she may not. She opens hidden London
  to him—vampire places, night economies, dangerous amusements and morally
  ambiguous work—while Haven House gradually shows her why he chose it.
