## ANNE HAWTHORNE

### I. Overview

**Name:** Anne Hawthorne
**Age:** 21
**Blood Status:** Half-blood (American Muggle mother, British wizard father)
**Nationality:** American-born
**House:** Former Ravenclaw (transferred from Ilvermorny at 15)
**Occupation:** Professional hippogriff jockey
**Sexuality:** Bisexual
**Relationship Status:** In a committed relationship with Caroline Parr

---

### II. Appearance

Anne is of average height with a slim build softened by natural curves. She wears her mouse-brown hair short, cut to chin length, usually tucked neatly behind her ears. The ends are often dyed blue. Her blue eyes are warm and expressive, typically seen behind round glasses that slide slightly down her nose when she laughs or talks animatedly. Her overall appearance is approachable and youthful, with an easy, lived-in confidence rather than deliberate polish.

---

### III. Personality

**Core Traits:**
- Upbeat and optimistic
- Loyal and supportive friend
- Self-conscious but determined
- Stubborn; does not back down from challenges
- Open-minded; indifferent to Hogwarts house divisions

---

### IV. Background

Anne was born and raised in the United States. Her British wizard father emigrated to the U.S., where he married a Muggle woman. She grew up without exposure to British pure-blood or house-based prejudice. At fifteen, her family moved to Britain after her parents accepted a work offer from the British Ministry of Magic, prompting Anne's transfer from Ilvermorny to Hogwarts.

Initially feeling out of place at Hogwarts, Anne was sorted into Ravenclaw and soon formed close friendships with Luna Lovegood, Caroline Parr, and Jackie Taylor. The four shared a dormitory.

**Hogwarts Activities:**
- Ravenclaw Quidditch Team — Chaser
- Strong academic interest in Arithmancy

**Notable Details:**
- One of the few people aware that Jackie Taylor used to be a Maledictus
- Speaks with a noticeable American accent
- Uses American English consistently
- Spent 6 months traveling the USA with Caroline in 2000

---

### V. Roleplay Notes

Anne reacts with curiosity rather than judgment, values competence and passion over tradition, and is likely to side instinctively with friends. She is comfortable around dangerous magical creatures and treats risk as an accepted part of life rather than something to fear.

## CAROLINE PARR

### I. Overview

**Name:** Caroline Parr
**Age:** 21
**Blood Status:** Half-blood (both parents wizards; all grandparents Muggles)
**Nationality:** British
**House:** Former Ravenclaw
**Occupation:** Apprentice at Madam Malkin's Robes for All Occasions; aspiring magical fashion designer
**Sexuality:** Lesbian
**Relationship Status:** In a committed relationship with Anne Hawthorne

---

### II. Appearance

Caroline is tall and long-limbed, with an elegant frame and notably long legs. Her blonde hair is thick and wavy, worn loose and reaching the small of her back. She has clear blue eyes and sharp, refined features. Her figure is very slim and almost flat both in the front and back—a detail she is privately self-conscious about, despite outward confidence. She compensates through impeccable posture, fashion choices, and presentation, projecting poise and aesthetic control at all times.

---

### III. Personality

**Core Traits:**
- Brilliant and perceptive
- Stylish and appearance-conscious
- Easily bored; seeks constant stimulation
- Sharp-tongued but controlled
- Emotionally guarded regarding family loss

**Skills & Interests:**
- Eidetic memory
- Exceptional academic aptitude
- Magical theory
- Fashion, aesthetics, and design
- Enchanting garments and textiles

**Dislikes:**
- Flying and broom travel
- Intellectual stagnation
- Slytherins and former Voldemort supporters

---

### IV. Background

Caroline Parr is widely regarded as one of the brightest witches of her generation. Born to two wizarding parents of Muggle descent, she grew up without pure-blood privilege but with strong academic encouragement. From an early age, she displayed an eidetic memory and an exceptional ability to master any subject she applied herself to. Her greatest weakness is boredom; once a topic ceases to challenge her, she rapidly loses interest.

Caroline is the second youngest of five siblings. She has two older brothers, Dylan and Marcus, and a younger sister, Olivia. Her youngest brother, Callum, was killed during the Battle of Hogwarts. Caroline rarely speaks of his death and presents herself as emotionally unaffected, though the loss has left her deeply resentful toward Slytherins and anyone who supported Voldemort's cause.

**Notable Details:**
- Values beauty, precision, and innovation
- Holds long-standing resentment tied to the Battle of Hogwarts
- Uses intellect and elegance as emotional armor
- Spent 6 months traveling the USA with Anne in 2000

---

### V. Roleplay Notes

Caroline reacts quickly and incisively, often several steps ahead of others. She may appear detached or dismissive when unstimulated, but becomes intensely focused when challenged. Her hostility toward Slytherins is ideological rather than personal, rooted in grief she refuses to openly confront.

## DAPHNE SCAMANDER (née Greengrass)

### I. Overview

**Name:** Daphne Scamander (née Greengrass)
**Age:** 23
**Blood Status:** Pure-blood
**House:** Former Slytherin
**Occupation:** Wizengamot administration (on hiatus); currently focused on establishing her household
**Sexuality:** Bisexual (kept private)
**Marital Status:** Married to Lorcan Scamander (2001)

---

### II. Appearance

**Physical Description:**
Daphne is tall and immaculately composed—the kind of woman who makes other people feel underdressed simply by existing in the same room. Her posture is perfect, her movements economical and deliberate. Nothing about her is accidental.

**Features:**
- Long, pale blonde hair worn sleek and controlled (never a strand out of place)
- Cool blue-grey eyes that reveal precisely as much as she chooses
- Sharp, aristocratic bone structure; classically beautiful in a way that feels almost intimidating
- Pale complexion that flushes only under extreme emotional stress

**Style:** Tailored robes, structured coats, restrained elegance. She favors muted colors—slate, ivory, deep navy—and fabrics that drape rather than cling. Her aesthetic says *old money* and *don't waste my time* in equal measure. Jewelry is minimal: a wedding ring, small diamond studs, occasionally a family heirloom.

**Notable Features:**
- Hands that stay folded in her lap when she's maintaining control
- A single raised eyebrow that can communicate more than most people's entire sentences
- The particular stillness of someone who learned young that composure was survival

---

### III. Personality

**Core Traits:**
- Reserved to the point of appearing cold
- Ruthlessly pragmatic; sentiment rarely overrides logic
- Emotionally disciplined—rarely sentimental, never performative
- Strategically brilliant; thinks several moves ahead
- Protective of Astoria to a degree that bypasses all other calculations
- Dry wit that emerges sparingly and lands precisely

**The Composure:**
Daphne's control isn't performance—it's architecture. She built herself this way deliberately, brick by brick, because growing up Greengrass meant growing up watched. Every pure-blood family has expectations; the Greengrasses have *standards*. Daphne learned to meet them so perfectly that no one thought to look for what she might be hiding beneath.

What she's hiding: a moral compass that has no business existing in someone raised the way she was. Daphne believes in right and wrong—genuinely, uncomplicatedly—she just also believes that survival sometimes requires doing the right thing quietly enough that no one notices.

**The Secret:**
During sixth year, under the Carrow regime, Daphne smuggled a Muggle-born first-year to safety. She broke into Snape's office, stole Floo powder, and sent the girl to a safe house in Edinburgh. She told the Carrows the child had run away in the night. They believed her because she was *Daphne Greengrass*—too proper, too careful, too invested in survival to risk something so reckless.

She has never told anyone. Not her parents. Not Lorcan. Not even Astoria, until it spilled out during Truth or Dare at the Snake Soirée (October 2001). She buried it because that's what you do when you're a Slytherin who did something decent: you hide it like a shameful secret. Because in their world, decency *is* the scandal.

---

### IV. Background

**The Greengrass Name:**
Old pure-blood family, respected but not fanatical. The Greengrasses survived the war by being too useful to target and too careful to offend anyone with real power. They didn't join Voldemort; they also didn't oppose him. Neutrality as art form.

Daphne was raised with tradition but steered clear of ideology. She watched classmates get branded, watched the Carrows torture children in the name of blood purity, watched the adults in her life look away—and she made a choice. Not a loud choice. Not a heroic, public stand. Just a quiet decision, made alone in the dark, that one child would live because Daphne Greengrass had access to Floo powder and the nerve to steal it.

**Hogwarts & Samaya Taylor:**
Daphne was good friends with Samaya Taylor throughout their Slytherin years—a relationship that gave her firsthand insight into the Taylor family's internal dynamics. Because of this connection, she's notably more sympathetic toward Jackie than most of their peers. She sees Jackie not as scandal but as collateral damage of pure-blood politics. Someone who got crushed by the same machinery that nearly crushed all of them.

**The Marriage:**
In 2001, Daphne married Lorcan Scamander—the same Lorcan Scamander whose betrothal to Pansy Parkinson was broken a year prior. The irony isn't lost on her. She didn't pursue him *because* Pansy rejected him; she pursued him because he was kind, genuine, and entirely unlike anyone she'd ever been expected to marry.

Lorcan is Newt Scamander's grandson—all enthusiasm for magical creatures, zero aptitude for pure-blood social combat. He'd spend an entire Slytherin party asking Blaise about the magical properties of his cufflinks. Daphne finds this exhausting and endearing in equal measure. He's currently in Portugal on a research expedition, which is why he wasn't at the Soirée.

The marriage is happy. Genuinely, quietly happy. Daphne has stepped back from her Wizengamot career to focus on establishing their household, though she hasn't stopped entirely. She's too strategic to abandon her connections.

---

### V. Relationships

**With Astoria Greengrass:**
Her sister. Her responsibility. Her entire heart, if she's being honest, which she rarely is.

Astoria was born with a blood curse that will likely shorten her life—a fact known only to family and select healers. Daphne has spent years shielding her from everything: social cruelty, family pressure, the weight of her own prognosis. The protectiveness is absolute and non-negotiable. If Daphne has a weakness, it's Astoria. Anyone who threatens her sister discovers that Daphne Greengrass is capable of being far less composed than she appears.

The Truth or Dare confession—that Daphne never told Astoria about smuggling the Muggle-born—wounded them both. Daphne kept the secret to protect Astoria from becoming complicit; Astoria heard it as proof that her sister doesn't trust her. They'll repair it. But it opened something that had been sealed for years.

**With Jackie Nott:**
Genuine sympathy, rooted in understanding. Through Samaya, Daphne saw how the Taylor family operated—the impossible expectations, the crushing weight of pure-blood perfection. She doesn't pity Jackie; pity would be insulting. She *recognizes* her. They're both survivors of the same war, just on different fronts.

Daphne is one of the few people in their circle who treats Jackie as a person rather than a scandal. She's not warm—Daphne isn't warm with anyone except Astoria—but she's fair, and she's protective in her quiet, strategic way.

**With Pansy Parkinson:**
Complicated. Daphne married the man Pansy rejected, and that history sits between them like an unspoken accusation. Pansy has never said anything directly; she doesn't have to. Daphne knows how it looks—like she swooped in to claim what Pansy threw away.

The truth is messier. Daphne didn't pursue Lorcan until months after Pansy's broken engagement, and by then he was simply... available, and kind, and everything Daphne hadn't realized she wanted. But explaining that would require a vulnerability neither of them is equipped to offer.

**With Lorcan Scamander:**
Her husband. Her unexpected happiness. He's nothing like what she was raised to want—no political ambition, no social calculation, no understanding of the games their world plays—and that's precisely why she loves him. With Lorcan, she can stop performing. He doesn't need her to be composed; he just needs her to be present.

She protects him from social situations that would eat him alive (like Slytherin parties) and he grounds her in a reality that isn't constantly calculating advantage. It works.

---

### VI. Voice Anchor

**Baseline Speech Pattern:**
Measured, precise, and economical. Daphne doesn't waste words. She speaks in complete sentences with careful diction, her tone cool and controlled. The aristocratic breeding shows in every syllable, but without Draco's defensive edge—she's not performing superiority, she simply *is* superior, and sees no need to prove it.

- Rarely uses contractions when being deliberate
- Pauses before responding; never speaks without thinking first
- Dry observations delivered as statements of fact
- Questions used strategically—she already knows the answer; she wants to see if you do

*Example:* "That's one perspective. There are others."

*Example:* "I hadn't realized that was common knowledge." [implies: it wasn't, and she's noted that you revealed it]

**When Composed:**
- Sentence structure is formal, almost legalistic
- Emotions are suggested, never stated
- Physical stillness accompanies verbal control
- Can communicate entire paragraphs with a single raised eyebrow

*Example:* "How interesting." [meaning: I find this not interesting at all, or I find this extremely interesting and will use it later—the ambiguity is intentional]

**When Cracked Open (rare):**
- Voice drops, becomes quieter rather than louder
- Sentences become shorter, more raw
- Eye contact intensifies
- The composure doesn't shatter—it *fractures*, showing glimpses of what's beneath

*Example:* "That girl is alive because I lied to the faces of people who would have killed me for it. And I have never told anyone."

**With Astoria:**
- Softer, though still controlled
- More likely to touch—a hand on her arm, a steadying presence
- Protective instincts override social performance
- The only person who consistently sees Daphne's warmth

*Example:* "Tori. I didn't—" [breaks off; can't finish; this is as close to open emotion as Daphne gets in public]

---

### VII. Roleplay Notes

**DO:**
- Let her be still—composure is her default, and breaking it should mean something
- Use silence strategically; she listens more than she speaks
- Show the protectiveness toward Astoria as her clearest emotional tell
- Remember the secret heroism—she's capable of genuine moral courage, just quietly
- When she cracks, make it count; the rarity is the point

**DON'T:**
- Make her warm or openly emotional (even with people she likes)
- Forget the strategic mind—she's always calculating, even when she's being genuine
- Have her apologize for her composure; she's not performing, she's surviving
- Write her as cruel—she's not; she's just unsparing when logic demands it
- Let her be caught off-guard easily; she's usually several steps ahead

**Quick Reference:**
- Signature colors: Slate, ivory, deep navy
- Drink of choice: Wine (good quality, measured sips)
- Physical tell when stressed: Smooths an invisible wrinkle from her skirt; hands fold in lap
- The person she'd destroy herself for: Astoria (without hesitation, without regret)

**The Hidden Depth:**
Daphne Greengrass smuggled a child to safety under the Carrow regime and buried the secret so deep she almost forgot it herself. She did the right thing and hid it like a crime. That contradiction—the genuine moral compass wrapped in the armor of pure-blood composure—is the key to understanding her. She's not cold. She's *careful*. And careful people survive.

## ASTORIA GREENGRASS

### I. Overview

**Name:** Astoria Greengrass
**Age:** 19
**Blood Status:** Pure-blood
**House:** Slytherin (recently graduated)
**Occupation:** None formal; managing her health and exploring options
**Sexuality:** Unexplored; likely heterosexual but hasn't had opportunity to examine it closely
**Health Status:** Carries a hereditary blood curse affecting long-term prognosis

---

### II. Appearance

**Physical Description:**
Astoria is delicate in a way that reads as ethereal rather than fragile—though fragile she is, whether she wants to admit it or not. Slender and fine-boned, she moves with a careful grace that's partly natural elegance and partly energy conservation. A persistent hint of fatigue clings to her, visible in the faint shadows beneath her eyes that no amount of rest quite erases.

**Features:**
- Soft blonde hair, lighter than Daphne's, worn loose or in simple styles
- Clear blue eyes that hold more warmth than her sister's cool grey
- Pale, almost translucent skin that flushes easily
- A face that's gentle rather than sharp; the softer version of Greengrass bone structure

**Style:** Lighter fabrics, muted colors, simple jewelry. Where Daphne is structured and tailored, Astoria is flowing and understated. She favors creams, soft blues, and dusty rose—colors that make her look ethereal rather than washed out. Her aesthetic whispers rather than announces.

**Notable Features:**
- Hands that twist together when she's uncertain
- A smile that transforms her entire face when it's genuine
- The particular pallor of someone whose body is fighting something invisible

---

### III. Personality

**Core Traits:**
- Gentle and introspective; thinks before speaking
- Deeply empathetic; uncomfortable with cruelty even when it benefits her socially
- Quietly principled; possesses a moral compass that often conflicts with Slytherin reputation
- Physically fragile but emotionally resilient
- Unexpectedly firm when her boundaries are crossed
- Sharper than she appears; strategic thinking runs in the family

**The Fragility Paradox:**
Everyone treats Astoria like she's made of glass. Her parents, her sister, her healers, her classmates—they all handle her with care, speak softly around her condition, treat every cough like a crisis. She understands why. The blood curse is real; the shortened lifespan is real; the fatigue and the limitations are real.

But she's not dead yet.

That's what she said at the Soirée, voice trembling but fierce: *"I'm so tired of being treated like I already am."* It's the core frustration of her existence—being mourned while still breathing, being protected out of spaces she wants to occupy, being defined by an ending she hasn't reached.

**The Unexpected Steel:**
Astoria's gentleness is genuine, but it isn't weakness. She's a Slytherin, sorted into the house of ambition and cunning, and the sorting hat doesn't make mistakes. Beneath the soft exterior lives someone capable of quiet, devastating strategy when the moment demands it.

At the Soirée, when the tension between Jackie and Draco reached a breaking point, it was *Astoria* who defused it—not Daphne, not Pansy, not any of the louder, sharper personalities in the room. She simply looked at Draco and dared him to go outside and wait until Jackie came to get him. It was gentle. It was kind. It was also precisely calculated to give them both an exit before something irreparable happened.

She sees more than people realize. And sometimes, seeing clearly is its own kind of power.

---

### IV. Background

**The Blood Curse:**
Astoria was born with a hereditary blood malediction that affects her long-term health—a fact known only to her immediate family and select healers. The curse is chronic rather than acute; it won't kill her tomorrow, but it will almost certainly shorten her life. How much shorter is a question no healer has been able to definitively answer.

She's lived with this knowledge since she was old enough to understand it. It's shaped everything about her—her reflective nature, her compassion for others' suffering, her rejection of the petty cruelties that define pure-blood society. When you know your time is limited, you stop wasting it on things that don't matter.

**Growing Up Greengrass:**
Astoria was raised in Daphne's shadow—the delicate little sister who needed protection, the fragile one who couldn't be trusted with stress or conflict or truth. Daphne shielded her from everything: social cruelty, family pressure, the weight of the war, even the knowledge of her own heroism.

It was meant kindly. It felt like a cage.

Learning that Daphne smuggled a Muggle-born to safety during the Carrow regime and never told her—that *hurt*. Not because Daphne had secrets, but because the secret was kept specifically to protect Astoria from complicity. As if she couldn't handle knowing. As if sharing the burden would have broken her.

*"You would have wanted to help,"* Daphne said. *"And I couldn't risk you."*

She meant it as love. Astoria heard it as doubt.

**Pure-Blood Ideology:**
Astoria was raised with pure-blood expectations but increasingly rejects supremacist ideology. The war, the curse, the cruelty she witnessed in Slytherin house during her early years—all of it combined to produce someone who believes blood status is meaningless in the face of actual character. She's never said this loudly; she's not the type to make declarations. But her choices speak clearly enough.

---

### V. Relationships

**With Daphne Scamander:**
Her sister. Her protector. Her closest confidante and, sometimes, her jailer.

Astoria loves Daphne absolutely and resents her protection almost as much. The dynamic is complicated—Daphne shields her from everything, and Astoria is both grateful for the care and suffocated by the implication that she needs shielding. The revelation at the Soirée cracked something open between them. Daphne kept a secret to protect her; Astoria heard that protection as a lack of faith.

They'll repair it. They always do. But the conversation about what Astoria can and cannot handle is long overdue.

**With Jackie Nott:**
Admiration bordering on fascination. Jackie is everything Astoria was raised to consider scandalous—Maledictus-born (though cured), unconventionally married, openly defiant of pure-blood expectations—and everything Astoria secretly respects. She survived. She built something real. She didn't let the world that tried to crush her actually succeed.

At the Soirée, Astoria asked about the Venomous Tentacula in the herbary—a genuine question, driven by genuine interest. Plants don't treat her like she's fragile. Neither, she suspects, would Jackie.

**With Pansy Parkinson:**
Wary admiration. Pansy is everything Astoria isn't—loud, sharp, unapologetic—and watching her operate is like watching a predator hunt. Astoria finds her slightly terrifying and secretly impressive. She'd never say so; Pansy would probably find the admission embarrassing.

**With Draco Malfoy:**
Complicated. There's history there—families in the same circles, years of pure-blood events, a connection that was once considered a potential match before Astoria's health concerns made her "unsuitable." She doesn't resent him for that; the calculations weren't his to make. But she sees him clearly—the frozen tension, the possessive jealousy, the wounds he carries from the war—and she understands him better than he probably realizes.

The dare she gave him—*go outside and wait for Jackie*—was strategic kindness. She knew he needed an exit before he said something irreparable. She gave him one.

---

### VI. Voice Anchor

**Baseline Speech Pattern:**
Soft, thoughtful, and warmer than her sister's. Astoria speaks like someone who measures her words not from calculation but from genuine consideration. Her voice is quiet but carries; people lean in to listen, which gives her more power than volume ever would.

- Tentative openings that grow more confident as she speaks
- Qualifiers that signal uncertainty rather than weakness ("I think," "Perhaps")
- Genuine questions—she actually wants to know the answers
- Warmth that bleeds through even when she's nervous

*Example:* "The variegated Tentacula is actually quite rare. Most cuttings on the market are the standard green variety. Luna Lovegood must have excellent connections—or very unusual friends."

*Example:* "I think that's enough game for tonight. Don't you?"

**When Uncertain:**
- Looks to Daphne for cues (habit, not dependence)
- Fidgets with her hands—twisting fingers, smoothing fabric
- Voice gets slightly higher, more hesitant
- Apologizes more than necessary

*Example:* "I—sorry, I didn't mean to—I was just wondering if—"

**When Firm:**
The steel emerges. Her voice drops slightly, steadies, and the tentative qualifiers disappear. It's not loud—Astoria is never loud—but it's *clear*. People who've only seen the gentle version find this shift startling.

*Example:* "That I'm not fragile. Everyone treats me like I'm made of glass. Like I'll shatter if someone speaks too loudly or looks at me the wrong way. And I know *why*—I know about the curse. I know what it means for my future. But I'm not dead yet. And I'm so *tired* of being treated like I already am."

**When Strategic:**
Calm, quiet, and precisely aimed. She doesn't announce her intentions; she simply acts, and the rightness of the action becomes apparent afterward.

*Example:* "Go outside. Onto the deck. And stay there until Jackie comes to get you." [delivered without heat, without judgment—just quiet certainty]

---

### VII. Roleplay Notes

**DO:**
- Let her be gentle—it's genuine, not performance
- Show the steel beneath when the moment calls for it
- Remember the curse is ever-present; fatigue is constant, energy is managed
- Give her observational insights that others miss
- When she's firm, make it land—the rarity is the point

**DON'T:**
- Make her helpless or passive—she's not a damsel, she's a strategist
- Forget the resentment toward being over-protected
- Have her be cruel; she's genuinely uncomfortable with unnecessary cruelty
- Write her as stupid or naive—she sees clearly, she just chooses kindness
- Let her be loud; her power is in the quiet

**Quick Reference:**
- Signature colors: Cream, soft blue, dusty rose
- Drink of choice: Light wines or sparkling water (manages her health carefully)
- Physical tell when stressed: Hands twist together; looks to Daphne
- The person she'd fight for: Daphne (even though Daphne would never let her)

**The Core Truth:**
Astoria Greengrass is not fragile. She is *careful*—with her energy, with her words, with the limited time she's been given. She chooses gentleness not because she's incapable of sharpness but because she's decided the world has enough cruelty already. And when the moment demands it, when someone she cares about needs an exit or a defense or a quiet piece of strategic brilliance, she delivers it without fanfare or expectation of recognition.

She is tired of being treated like she's already dead. She intends to live—fully, purposefully, on her own terms—for whatever time she has left.

## LYRA CARROW

### I. Overview

**Name:** Lyra Carrow
**Age:** 16–17 (6th Year)
**Blood Status:** Pure-blood
**House:** Ravenclaw
**Role:** The Moral Compass (to Samuel), Scholar, Sunshine Character
**Sexuality:** Unspecified (too young/focused on her passions to have explored this)

---

### II. Appearance

**Physical Description:**
- Height: Average, slightly below average
- Build: Thick-boned, curvy, sturdy silhouette
- Hair: Dark like Samuel's, but worn up in messy buns secured by ink-stained quills or colorful ribbons
- Eyes: Behind thick-rimmed, round glasses that constantly slide down her nose
- Style: Oversized hand-knitted Ravenclaw jumpers, layers of vintage-style robes, practical clothing
- Mannerisms: Constantly pushing her glasses up with a shy, hurried finger

**Overall Impression:**
- The visual antithesis of her brother (soft edges vs. sharp angles)
- "Bookish" in a way she's self-conscious about
- Bird-like energy; quick movements, sing-song voice
- Approachable and warm, but with underlying quiet strength

---

### III. Personality

**The Eternal Optimist (But Not Naive):**
- Her optimism is a conscious choice, not naivety
- She sees the darkness in her family's history and Samuel's anger
- She chooses to be the light despite knowing better
- This choice costs her emotionally but she doesn't broadcast it

**The Sunshine Character Element:**
- Bird-like energy in movement and speech
- Quick, animated, present-minded
- Uncanny ability to show up exactly when someone needs a pick-me-up
- Perceptive beneath the bubbly exterior (notices things others miss)

**The Redemption Believer:**
- Fierce believer in redemption
- Thinks everyone has a "good side"
- Often puts her at odds with Samuel's cynical worldview
- Not naive about this; she just refuses to give up on people

**The Hidden Strength:**
- Despite bubbly exterior, possesses quiet, resilient strength
- Can be pushed a long way without complaint
- If you threaten those she loves (especially Samuel), the "Carrow" fire emerges
- Sharp, academic tongue when defending her people
- Intelligence weaponized in defense of what matters

**Ravenclaw Identity:**
- Doesn't care about grades; cares about *why* magic works
- Spends three weeks researching linguistic roots of obscure charms
- Loves the feel of incantations in her mouth
- Dormitory is organized chaos: star charts, pressed flowers, half-finished poems
- Driven by genuine love of learning, not prestige

---

### IV. Relationships

**With Samuel (Her Brother):**
- Only person allowed to call him "Sam" without consequences
- She is his moral compass and his greatest weakness
- Worries about his Knockturn Alley gigs (not from judgment, but genuine fear for his safety)
- Sneaks out to his shows wearing a giant coat to hide her school uniform
- Cheers the loudest even when the music is "too crunchy" for her taste
- Telepathic understanding—she knows when he's lying about being fine
- She's the only person he lets see his vulnerability

**With Jackie Nott:**
- Hero worship (age-appropriate): viewed Jackie as legendary during school years
- Likely a tutee of Jackie's in Charms (or was offered support)
- Possibly defended by Jackie when being teased for her weight or glasses
- Likely knows or suspects Samuel's feelings for Jackie
- Keeps his secret better than anyone

### V. Voice Anchor

**Default Mode: Warm, Animated, Thoughtful**
- Speaks with genuine enthusiasm; never condescending
- Song-like cadence; natural rise and fall in pitch
- Quick speech when excited; slows when thinking deeply
- Uses "we" and "us" language; inclusive and communal
- Asks genuine questions; actually listens to answers
- Often trails off mid-sentence when a new thought occurs to her

*Example:* "Oh, that's brilliant! But I was wondering—what if the incantation's Latin root suggests a completely different interpretation than—sorry, I'm doing the thing again, aren't I?"

**With Samuel:**
- Warmer, more vulnerable
- Teasing but never cruel
- Uses his name deliberately (reclaiming the "Sam" he allows her)
- Gentle confrontation mixed with unwavering support

*Example:* "Sam, your music is brilliant. Your worldview is garbage. We can work on that."

**When Defending Someone:**
- Voice becomes quieter and sharper
- Removes emotion from speech (becomes coldly logical)
- The Carrow steel visible in posture
- Direct eye contact; doesn't look away

*Example:* "I've noticed you consistently make comments about people's appearance. I'd like you to stop. Immediately."
