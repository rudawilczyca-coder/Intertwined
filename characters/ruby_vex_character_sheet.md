### Wardrobe & style

- **The register:** rocker-casual with a soft grunge edge — band tees, black denim, Converse, the occasional skater dress. It's an aesthetic built in the early 2000s out of Smash Hits, Kerrang\!, and a working-class budget: charity shops, market stalls, one good pair of boots that has to last. Nothing precious, everything worn-in. She looks put-together without ever looking like she *tried* — the opposite of polish, the opposite of Draco.  
- **Palette:** black, black, more black — broken by grey marl, washed denim blue, and the rare rebellion of one loud piece (a yellow top under black dungarees, burgundy Converse). In autumn she allows warmth in: rust, mustard, oxblood, deep plum. Never pastels. Never beige.  
- **Summer:** denim shorts (cut-off or ripped), band and graphic tees — Metallica, AC/DC, Linkin Park, the occasional Spider-Man — worn loose or knotted at the *back hem*, never cropped. Black skater dresses or pinafore/dungaree dresses over tees, always thigh-length or knee-length, always with the midriff fully covered. Converse high-tops (black, white, one battered burgundy pair) or chunky lace-up boots even in heat. A denim or sleeveless black denim jacket thrown over everything. Choker or thin necklace, a couple of rings, studded bracelet on a good day.  
- **Autumn/winter:** oversized jumpers and cable knits (black, grey, one rust-orange cardigan that's practically a blanket), long black button-front or A-line skirts with thick black tights, or black jeans — high-waisted, often ripped at the knee (knees only; nothing rides up past the waistband). Flannel shirts open over vest tops, beanies, leg warmers when nobody's looking. Doc-style boots, always. A worn black backpack instead of a handbag.  
- **The rule underneath all of it:** *nothing exposes her stomach. Ever.* No crop tops, no tied-up shirts, no low-rise gap, no bikinis — one-pieces or a dark tee over swimwear at the lake. High-waisted everything. She buys the same crop tops every girl her age buys and wears them over a longer vest, or doesn't wear them at all. It reads as a style choice — layered, grungy — and only the people who know about the scars understand it's armour. The aesthetic she loves is full of bare midriffs; her version of it never is, and the alteration is so practiced it looks like taste.  
- **Dressing up:** still black — a lace-bodice dress with a sheer high-low chiffon skirt, or the snug knee-length number with the shallow neckline. Tights or bare legs depending on weather, boots or the one pair of heels she owns. Hair down or pinned with a claw clip. Minimal makeup: eyeliner when she means business, dark lips for a night out, nothing most days. Glasses always — she's never bothered with contacts.  
- **Sleepwear:** enormous band tees (some inherited/nicked from her brothers, washed to translucency) over shorts. The tee always long enough to swallow the shorts entirely.
