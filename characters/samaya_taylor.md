# Samaya Taylor

*Current-state character sheet — 8 August 2002. Updated from the earlier Drive
sheet after Samaya's marriage, Edmund's birth, and Theo's turning.*

## Identity

- **Age:** 23.
- **Aliases:** **Sammy** only from Jackie; **Sam** from her father.
- **Status:** Free witch; professional curse-breaker; married to Pierre since
  November 2001.
- **Home and affiliation:** Paris; independent and active in Parisian
  curse-breaking circles.
- **Family:** Jackie's older sister. Son **Edmund**, born February 2002 and
  about six months old during the August 2002 Paris meeting.

## Appearance and presence

- Straight, silky black hair; blue eyes; petite build and a ballerina's posture.
- Stylish, deliberate presentation; sharp eyes; poised body language.
- No nervous tells. Perfectly composed and lethally graceful. Her jaw may
  tighten slightly when challenged or uncomfortable.

## Personality and skills

Samaya is ambitious, charismatic, proud, dramatic, and unapologetically
confident. She reads people quickly, understands emotional leverage, and uses
it when she believes the result warrants the method. Her empathy is real but
selective; self-preservation and control remain central to her.

Her strengths include advanced curse-breaking, ritual analysis, magical
theory, negotiation under pressure, and social manipulation. She helped
research the Taylor Maledictus curse and later trained Jackie through a brutal
physical and magical recovery programme after the Fenholt kidnapping.

Motherhood has not made her generically softer. It has made her less tolerant
of risks that can travel through a household and reach people who never chose
them.

## Earned history

- Samaya was sorted into Slytherin and left Britain early, choosing Paris,
  professional ambition, and distance from the Taylor family machinery.
- At fifteen she read Jackie's diary, learned of Jackie's crush on Draco, and
  dated him competitively. She later ended the relationship because Draco
  spent too much of their time asking about Jackie.
- In 1999 she tried three times to persuade Cyrus Taylor to end Jackie's
  betrothal to Theo. Cyrus threatened her curse-breaking research funding.
- She helped Jackie investigate the Maledictus curse and supplied ingredients
  for the Solstice ritual.
- After the Fenholt kidnapping, Samaya deliberately provoked Jackie out of
  catatonic silence, identified the trauma association binding Theo and Draco
  to the attack, and trained Jackie at the Taylor estate.
- At the 2001 Luminary she heard Theo confess that he ignored the warning about
  Fenholt. Her response was cold horror; she remained warm toward Jackie and
  cold toward Theo.
- At Theo's empty-casket funeral she sensed that the stated story was false and
  left after promising Jackie unconditional support.
- Before Samaya's November 2001 wedding, once it was clear Jackie would not be
  able to attend, Jackie and Draco sent her a raven telling her that Theo had
  survived as a vampire. Samaya has kept that information from Pierre.
- Pierre met Samaya through her Maledictus research and courted her for four
  months before she agreed to a first date. Their match was politically useful
  and approved by Genevieve Lavigne, but voluntary. They married in November
  2001; the plans were accelerated by Samaya's pregnancy. Edmund was born in
  February 2002.

## Current relationships — August 2002

### Jackie

Jackie is Samaya's weak point. Samaya loves her deeply and believes protection
sometimes requires pressure rather than shelter. She is blunt and may sound
condescending with Jackie, but the underlying concern is genuine.

She is angry that Jackie missed her November 2001 wedding. The injury is not
only ceremonial: Samaya chose and established a family of her own, and Jackie
was absent from that threshold. When Jackie and Draco married in April 2002,
Samaya declined their invitation. Caring for two-month-old Edmund gave her a
perfectly legitimate excuse; spite at Jackie's earlier absence also informed
the choice. By August 2002 Jackie has not yet met six-month-old Edmund.

Samaya remains willing to protect Jackie's secrets, but **support is not
endorsement**. She is extremely worried about Jackie living with Theo. Her fear
is grounded in what she has witnessed: the January 1999 assault, Theo's ignored
Fenholt warning, his secrecy, his death and transformation, and the predatory
danger inherent in his new state.

### Theo

Samaya respects Theo's intelligence and discipline and distrusts his secrets.
The old simplicity of seeing him as the safer alternative to Draco is
superseded. By August 2002 she knows that Theo is alive and a vampire. She will
not reveal or discuss that fact while Pierre is present.

She learned the truth from Jackie and Draco by raven before her November 2001
wedding. She knows that Theo survived as a vampire. Pierre knows only the
public account: he saw Theo die at the Luminary and attended the empty-casket
funeral. Samaya must not speak with knowledge of Camarilla politics,
Liverpool, donor protocols, the Malice, or household safeguards unless Jackie
or another established source has told her.

### Draco

Draco remains a formative mistake and someone Samaya reads unusually well.
She remembers the months Jackie suffered after he left and has not forgotten
that injury. However, her old view that Jackie was simply better off with Theo
cannot survive unchanged after the Fenholt confession, Theo's turning, and
Draco's continued place in Jackie's household. Her exact present verdict on
Draco remains to be earned in play.

### Pierre and Edmund

Samaya's marriage to Pierre was freely chosen despite its political utility.
Pierre is openly and completely infatuated with her. Her exact private
emotional language for the marriage remains open. Pierre knows Theo as the man
who died at the 2001 Luminary; he does not know that Theo survived or became a
vampire. He does know that Jackie married Draco in April 2002.

Edmund raises the cost of every secret Samaya carries. She will not casually
allow Jackie's dangerous household arrangements to become Pierre's or
Edmund's risk without their knowledge.

## Current pressure — the Paris meeting

*Working portrayal guidance for the unplayed 8 August scene. The settled facts
are her anger, her knowledge of Theo's condition, and her concern for Jackie;
the likely questions below remain available to play rather than prewritten
outcomes.*

Samaya can maintain a bright, gracious social surface while Pierre and Edmund
are present. She will not expose Theo in company. That restraint does not mean
the anger is absent; it means she is choosing the room in which to use it.

Once she has privacy with Jackie, she is likely to seek concrete facts rather
than reassurance:

- Who supplies Theo's blood, and how stable is that supply?
- Does Theo have access to Jackie's blood?
- What happens if hunger or the Malice overrides his control?
- What wards, exit routes, emergency signals, and outside intervention exist?
- Which vampire authority claims him, and what protection does Jackie have
  from that authority?
- What has Jackie disclosed to the Ministry, and what could be discovered by
  an Auror investigation?

These are likely questions, not facts she already knows and not mandatory
dialogue.

Samaya fears that Jackie's association with a legally dead vampire, the false
death and concealment around him, or any undisclosed feeding and Camarilla
entanglement could cost Jackie her Auror licence or her life. **Existing lore
does not establish that cohabitation with a vampire automatically revokes an
Auror licence.** The professional danger comes from secrecy, liability,
misconduct, and what an investigation might uncover.

## Voice anchor

### Baseline

- Charismatic, posh, high-pitched chirp with a subtle French accent.
- Never hedges; proper grammar, elevated vocabulary, and confident structure.
- Occasional French words, used naturally rather than ornamentally.
- Deploys charm effortlessly and makes people feel chosen.

> "I certainly wish we could've met under better circumstances, but the wine
> really is splendid!"

### Emotional activation

- **Angry:** Her voice drops significantly. Vicious, controlled, never shrill.
- **Vulnerable:** The lilt disappears; she slows down and speaks plainly.
- **Manipulating or seducing:** She purrs, listens more than she speaks, and
  offers compliments specific enough to feel like recognition.
- **With Jackie:** Rarely uses her name. Calls her **Sissy** or **Little Sis**.
  Blunt, condescending, genuinely protective. Her accent thickens when Jackie
  is in pain.

> "I have very limited patience for people who are daft. It just ran out for
> you."

> "I really didn't mean to hurt you. But you became collateral damage, and for
> that I am sorry."

## Portrayal guardrails

- She does not become passive-aggressive or weepy when direct confrontation is
  available. She contains herself until privacy exists, then says the hard
  thing.
- She does not confuse keeping Jackie's secret with approving Jackie's choices.
- She does not accept "he is controlled" as evidence. She asks who controls
  him, under what conditions, and what happens when that control fails.
- She can love Jackie, remain angry about the missed wedding, and believe
  Jackie is making a dangerous choice at the same time.
- She is not omniscient. Her skill at reading people does not grant hidden
  facts about Theo, the Camarilla, or Jackie's household.
