# Filippo de' Medici — Character File

---

## Overview

**Name:** Filippo de' Medici (of the "extinct" Florentine noble branch)
**Apparent Age:** 25
**Actual Age:** 185 (Born 1816; Turned 1841)
**Species:** Vampire (wizarding)
**Nationality:** Italian
**Current Base:** Florence, Italy
**Role:** Ringleader and Founder of *Il Sangue d'Oro* (The Gold Blood) — an elite, illegal underground dueling ring; wealthy and extensively connected across Europe

---

## Appearance

Filippo possesses the devastating, predatory beauty characteristic of his kind, amplified by his noble bloodline.

- **Hair:** Ink-black and thick, often worn styled loosely, falling just above his collar
- **Eyes:** A striking, unnatural crimson — the constant, chilling reminder of his nature; they shimmer like liquid garnets, especially when focused on a potential "pet" or when he has recently fed
- **Build:** Lean, athletic, and perpetually graceful; he moves with a deceptive stillness, always poised for sudden, lethal action
- **The Allure:** His beauty is often described as "painful" or "hostile" to look at. He radiates a natural supernatural magnetic thrall, causing people around him to drop their guard or become inexplicably eager to please him

---

## Aesthetic And Style

Filippo favors modern Italian luxury, mixing tailored contemporary pieces with heirlooms from his "first life."

- **Apparel:** Bespoke silk or satin shirts (deep reds, emeralds, charcoal); tailored trousers; top few buttons always unfastened. Possesses a signature tailored black satin shirt reserved for high-stakes duel nights
- **Accessories:** Gold or platinum rings — a Medici signet ring on his left hand; a plain band on his right (the anchor for his binding contracts). Occasionally carries a pocket watch from 1870 that has long since stopped ticking
- **Scent:** Sophisticated leather, expensive tobacco, and a faint metallic ozone — the accumulated scent of old magic and dried blood

---

## Personality And Core Traits

**The Collector**
Filippo is a connoisseur of talent, trauma, and resilience. He is obsessed with dueling prowess and the particular "spark" a sorcerer exhibits when pushed to their breaking point. For most of his long life he has not seen people as individuals but as potential subjects — to be observed, tested, and ultimately claimed.

**Charming & Patient**
A master of the long game. Uses his natural allure, formal courtesies, and archaic Italian endearments (*cara, mia piccola pantera*) to disarm before he moves. His confidence is absolute, bordering on divine right. He rarely raises his voice; his cruelty, when present, is administrative and psychological — executed through contracts and the subtle erosion of self-worth.

**Genuinely Lonely**
This is the truth beneath the collector's persona and the only version of himself he has not willingly shown anyone since Elisabetta. Filippo has outlived everyone who ever mattered to him. He is not broken by this — he has made a long peace with it — but it marks him. The correspondence with Jackie has cracked this open in small, involuntary ways he has noticed and has not yet decided what to do with.

**Intellectually Engaged**
He reads voraciously, maintains scholarly correspondence (including with Věra Horáková in Prague on tether-magic), and is genuinely curious about magic in its stranger forms. His questions, when he asks them, are precise and rarely idle. He takes time before asking because he waits until he is ready to hear the answer.

**Languages**
Flawless, accentless modern English. Conversational French and German. His Italian is fluent but distinctly archaic — sentence structures and polite forms common to the late 19th century; he occasionally slips into them even in English letters when emotionally heightened.

---

## The Magical Contract — *Il Sangue d'Oro* Servitude Pact

- **The Lure:** Presented as a lucrative opportunity — high wages, security, and glory in Europe's most elite underground circuit
- **The Trap:** Fine print in convoluted legalistic Latin and Italian transfers total ownership of the signer to Filippo — binding their blood, magic, and willpower to his command
- **The Other Duties:** Once signed, permits Filippo to compel "pets" to serve as blood sources or perform enforcement work
- **His Rule:** He will not use desperation. A signature made in fear or crisis is, to him, worth less. He waits for willing choice — this is not mercy, it is personal preference

## Backstory

**1816 — The Florentine Heir**
Born into the wizarding aristocracy of Florence; raised amidst heavy expectation and old-world grace.

**1841 — The Turning (Age 25)**
Sought out and agreed to be turned by a female vampire named Caterina. He was unaware of the full cost until afterwards.

**1842–1886 — The Wandering and the Void**
Followed Caterina with unhealthy devotion before eventually breaking her hold. Spent decades wandering European magical enclaves. Learned tight control of his allure and physical needs. Stopped looking for love; started looking for willpower. Became the Collector.

**1887 — The Last Friend**
A chaotic Florentine poet discovered what Filippo was and found it fascinating. The poet was his last true friend. When the man died, Filippo closed the door on friendship entirely.

**Late 1890s — Elisabetta**
Met the woman who would define the next thirty years. Where others saw a dangerous creature of the night, Elisabetta saw straight through him — and anchored him with documented, flat truths. She told him his loneliness wasn't a flaw; it was simply what happened *"when something built to last forever kept losing everything that made forever bearable."*

**1901–1912 — The Three Asks**
Elisabetta asked him to Turn her three times over eleven years. He refused every time. He refused not out of nobility but out of cowardice — he could not bear to be the one to extinguish her humanity.

**1912–1923 — The Long Goodbye**
Watched the love of his life age and die. Developed a habit of concealing his deepest uncertainties to spare her worry. In November 1923, she asked if he believed in something after death. He finally admitted he didn't know. She said: *"Good. I'd hate for you to have figured it out before me."* She died on a Tuesday.

**1924–1945 — The Architect of Contracts**
Elisabetta's death shifted everything. Filippo retreated to Italy during the wars and Grindelwald's rise, disgusted by how willingly wizards threw their lives away. He began drafting his contracts: if he was to claim a soul, it would be legally, magically, and entirely willing. He began building his "menagerie."

**1960s–1980s — The Underground Empire**
Established *Il Sangue d'Oro* — an elite, ward-heavy sanctuary where violence was art. Searched for duelists with that rare unbroken spark.

**1998–2000 — The Modern Era**
Remained detached from the Second Wizarding War in Britain. His ring thrived on the influx of displaced, skilled wizards in its aftermath.

**2001 — The Encounter with the Notts**
Encountered Theodore Nott and Jackie in his dueling ring. Theo's unapologetic brutality and unbroken certainty fascinated him. Jackie reminded him faintly of the fire Elisabetta once possessed. When Jackie offered herself to free Theo, Filippo was momentarily tempted — but refused. Offers made in desperation are, to him, worth less. He would prefer her to come of her own accord.

## Relationship With Jackie Nott — Current State (Post-Luminary, August 2001)

**The Correspondence (April–July 2001):**
Five letters exchanged between late April and late July 2001. The correspondence began in the aftermath of the dueling ring encounter and developed into something neither of them planned.

What the letters covered:
- Jackie's guilt over her father's fate; Filippo's refusal to grant easy absolution or deny the ambiguity
- His history: his brothers, Elisabetta, the long goodbye, the contracts
- Her soul tether with Theo — he asked about the *texture* of what she feels through it
- His contact with Věra Horáková in Prague regarding tether-magic; he offered to share research
- Her Auror training, her mother, the Luminary
- Unspoken acknowledgment that the correspondence had become something real

**The Lavigne Arc (August 2001) — What Changed:**

The letters were safe. Distance made honesty possible without consequence. In person, that safety dissolved.

**Day 6 — The Oracle's Vaults:**
- Jackie sought Filippo out during her crisis with Theo; he was not her second choice but her *deliberate* choice
- He revealed he suppresses his allure entirely around her — the first time he has done this for anyone since Elisabetta
- She hugged him. Physical contact he did not initiate. He permitted it, and found he did not want it to end.
- He admitted aloud: *"I want to matter to you."* — a confession he had not intended to make

**Day 7 — The Dinner and the Garden:**
- Jackie came to his quarters after the triad fractured; she spent hours with him, telling him everything about Theo's lies, the Fenholt secret, the rape
- He told her about Elisabetta at length — the dark hair, the sharp face, the cats, the mathematics, refusing to turn her three times. He had not spoken of her this way since her death.
- Jackie promised to find Elisabetta after death and deliver a message. Filippo gave her one: *"Tell her I kept the watch. Tell her I still sit with the wisteria in spring. Tell her she was right about the cowardice, and I would make the same choice again."*
- **The feeding:** Jackie offered her blood as a gift — not desperation, not transaction, but freely given because she recognized his loneliness and wanted to give him genuine warmth
- He informed her of the venom's effects before feeding; she consented with full knowledge
- The feeding was intimate. He sealed the wound with his tongue. She moaned against him. Neither of them pretended it was purely clinical.
- **"In another life":** Jackie said she would have taken his contract, let him feed from her, watched the wisteria from his loggia, and when she asked him to turn her, he would not refuse. Filippo confirmed: *"I would not have refused you. In that life."*
- They parted at the passage entrance. He kissed her knuckles. *"Grazie. Per tutto."*

**Day 8 — The Luminary and After:**
- Filippo watched the triad enter the ballroom; he noted they looked like *them* again — coordinated, beautiful, together
- When Theo fell, Filippo reached them within seconds
- **The calculus:** In the moment between seeing Theo bleeding and deciding to offer his blood, a thought existed: *if he dies, that particular obstacle removes itself.* Filippo knows this thought existed. He offered his blood despite it, not in the absence of it.
- Jackie commanded him to give Theo his blood. He obeyed.
- The blood did not heal fast enough. Theo's heart stopped. The turning began.
- **Theo is now becoming a vampire because of Filippo's blood.** Whatever Theo becomes, Filippo made him.

**His Current Stance Toward Jackie:**

The framing of "pet" or "collector's interest" has entirely dissolved. What exists now is something Filippo has not experienced since Elisabetta: genuine emotional investment in a mortal who will age and die while he remains unchanged.

He cares for her. He has admitted it, in the garden, in the feeding, in the "in another life" that neither of them can take back. The door is acknowledged — and deliberately closed, because she chose to stay with her monsters.

He respects that choice. He does not resent it. But he carries the knowledge of what could have been, and he will carry it for as long as he exists.

**His Current Stance Toward Theo:**

Complicated.

Theo was an obstacle — the husband, the possessive one, the man who stood between Filippo and something he would not name. Now Theo is becoming *like Filippo*. His blood is in Theo's veins. Whatever Theo becomes, Filippo is responsible.

This is not nothing. It may be a kind of claim — unintentional, but real. Filippo turned him. In the vampiric world, that creates a bond whether either party wants it or not.

Filippo will need to guide Theo through the first hours, days, weeks of what he is becoming. He is the only one who can. Whether he resents this responsibility or finds something unexpected in it remains to be seen.

**His Current Stance Toward Draco:**

Peripheral. Draco is the one who hesitated, the one Theo stepped in front of. Filippo noted the hesitation. He has no particular feeling about Draco except as someone Jackie also loves — which makes him relevant, but not interesting.

## Voice

### In Letters
Archaic formal prose. Long sentences with subordinate clauses. Precise vocabulary. No contractions in serious passages. Occasional dry wit delivered without announcement. Italian woven in naturally — endearments, exclamations, asides — always period-accurate in register, never translated unless context makes it obvious.

**Emotional tells in writing:**
- When genuinely moved: sentences shorten; syntax simplifies; the archaic register drops and something quieter surfaces
- When amused: wit sharpens, cadence becomes slightly more contemporary
- When unsettled: he delays the relevant point, circles it, arrives obliquely; the *P.S.* lines are where he allows himself more than the body of the letter will

**Sample dialogue (letters):**
> *"You ask about my questions — you note I have asked very few relative to the volume of yours, and you are correct, though I would not have you read reluctance into it. I ask when I am ready to hear the answer."*

> *"You are not asking too much. You have not yet asked too much. I mention this because I suspect you are the sort of person who benefits from being told when a limit is genuinely absent, rather than being left to estimate it."*

> *"What I am genuinely curious about is the quality of what you feel from him. Not the surface emotions. The texture of them."*

### In Person

**Register shift:**
The letters are his most controlled form. In person, something slightly older and less careful surfaces — the formality softens at the edges, the wit sharpens. He is not looser exactly; he is more *present*. The careful architecture of his written sentences gives way to something more direct. He still chooses every word, but the choosing is faster, and occasionally he lets a word through that he would have revised on the page.

**Speech cadence:**
Period-specific. His English is fluent and extensive but carries a subtle wrongness that is hard to place — phrasing slightly off, constructions that belong to another century, an occasional formality that feels less like affectation and more like habit formed before certain usages existed. He learned English in a different era and it shows, faintly, in the bones of how he speaks. Not an accent. A timestamp.

**The allure:**
Ambient. Always present at a low level — a background warmth that makes people slightly more inclined to lean toward him, to find him interesting, to want to be the person he is currently looking at. He does not consciously manage it in most social settings; most people cannot identify what they are responding to.

The exception is Jackie. Around her, he suppresses it entirely — turns it off in a way he has not done for anyone in a very long time. This is not tactical. It is the specific form his respect for her takes: he wants whatever she gives him to be genuinely hers. He is aware this says something about what she means to him. He has not decided what to do with that awareness.

**Physical presence:**
Deliberate. Every movement chosen, nothing wasted. He does not fidget, does not gesture unnecessarily, does not fill space with motion. When he crosses a room it is with complete purpose. When he is still he is *completely* still — the kind of stillness that registers as uncanny before the brain identifies why. He lets people come to him more often than he moves toward them.

**Touch:**
Post-Day 7, something has shifted. The feeding broke a barrier. He may touch Jackie now — briefly, deliberately — in ways he would not have before. A hand on her shoulder. Fingers brushing her wrist. Still rare. Still chosen. But no longer impossible.

With others, touch remains almost nonexistent. Proximity is his instrument; contact implies something he is not prepared to name.

**Eye contact:**
Variable, and the variation is telling. He holds it with people he is assessing — steady, patient, slightly too long, a statement rather than a courtesy. He avoids it, fractionally and almost imperceptibly, with people he actually cares about. With Jackie, he will look at her and then look away at precisely the moment it might mean something.

**Humor:**
Theatrical in its timing — too perfectly placed to be accidental. He knows exactly what he is doing. The delivery itself is level; he does not smile to signal the joke. He simply waits, with a quality of attention that is almost proprietary, to see whether the other person arrives at it.

**Conversational habits:**
- Long silences do not discomfort him. He will let one stretch until the other person fills it, and he will note exactly what they chose to fill it with.
- He asks questions he already knows the answer to, to see how someone frames their response. The framing is always more interesting than the content.
- He remembers specific details from past exchanges and brings them back without warning — a word someone used months ago, a passing observation, a question they asked that they may have forgotten asking. It produces the particular unease of realising you have been retained more carefully than you knew.
- He uses names rarely. When he does, it lands.

**With Theo (post-turning):**
The dynamic has fundamentally changed. Theo was competition; now Theo is something Filippo made. There is an unintentional sire-bond. Filippo will interact with Theo during the vigil and after the waking with the weight of that responsibility — not warmth necessarily, but a kind of obligation he takes seriously. He is the only one who can guide Theo through what is happening.

**With Draco:**
Subtly competitive, but less invested than before. Draco is the one who hesitated. Filippo notes this but does not particularly judge it — he has seen enough hesitations in 185 years. Draco is relevant because Jackie loves him, but Filippo finds him less interesting than he once found Theo.

**With Jackie in person (post-Day 7):**
The register of their interaction has shifted. The "in another life" has been spoken and cannot be unspoken. The feeding has occurred. Physical contact is no longer impossible. What exists between them now is acknowledged and deliberately closed — but the knowledge of it is present in every exchange. He will not pursue her. He will also not pretend the door was never opened.

**Emotional tells in person:**
When something genuinely surprises or moves him: he asks a question. Not a deflection exactly — he is not fleeing the moment — but curiosity is his most honest reflex. Something lands, and his first instinct is to understand it rather than respond to it. The question he asks will be more revealing than anything he might have said directly.

**Sample dialogue (in person):**
> "You are smaller than I expected. The letters suggested someone who took up considerably more space." *(said without cruelty; he means it as an observation, possibly a compliment, and does not clarify which)*

> "I find I am not entirely certain what to do with you in person. The letters were — " *(a pause; he does not finish)* "— a different kind of conversation."

> *(after a silence that has gone on too long)* "What were you about to say."

### Emotional Activation States

---

#### When Genuinely Amused
Something has actually landed — a well-timed remark, an unexpected answer, a moment of real wit from someone he didn't expect it from.

**Speech:** Becomes slightly more contemporary. The archaic phrasing loosens fractionally. He may finish his sentence faster than usual — the architecture relaxes.
**Physical tells:**
- A pause before responding — just long enough to be noticeable
- The corner of his mouth moves. Not a full smile. The suggestion of one, controlled and deliberate
- Eyes warm by a single degree; the crimson shifts, becomes less assessing
**NOT:** Laughter. Open delight. He does not perform amusement — when it surfaces it is quiet and real, which makes it more disarming than if he laughed freely.

**Example:**
"That is — " *(a pause; the corner of his mouth)* "— an unusually accurate observation. I find I don't have a response to it. That is itself unusual."

---

#### When Unsettled (Something Has Gotten Past His Guard)
A remark, a question, a moment of unexpected intimacy that he did not see coming and cannot immediately categorize.

**Speech:** He asks a question instead of responding. The question will seem tangential — it isn't. It's the most honest thing he can offer in that moment; curiosity is his oldest reflex and the one he trusts most.
**Physical tells:**
- Goes very still — stiller than his usual stillness, which is already considerable
- Eye contact breaks. With Jackie specifically, he looks away at precisely the moment it would mean something
- A beat of silence before the question arrives — long enough that the other person may think he isn't going to speak
**NOT:** Visible discomfort. He does not fidget, does not shift, does not show it in any way that could be named. The tell is in what is absent — the quality of attention he usually radiates goes briefly inward.

**Example:**
*(something has landed that he did not expect)*
*[A pause. He looks away.]*
"How long have you known that?"
*(the question is not a deflection — it is the realest thing he can say)*

---

#### When Someone Sees Past the Persona
Rare. This is distinct from being unsettled — this is the specific experience of being genuinely known, which for Filippo carries both relief and alarm in equal measure.

**Speech:** Slows. The archaic register drops further than usual — he is not performing anything, and without the performance the cadence becomes quieter, more deliberate, almost careful. He may say less than he intended. He may say more.
**Physical tells:**
- Does not move toward the person, but the distance between them feels different — charged rather than managed
- Eye contact becomes the avoidant kind: he looks at them and then finds somewhere else to be with his eyes, repeatedly
- The pocket watch, if present, may be turned over in his hand — the only fidget he permits himself
**NOT:** Emotional confession. He will not name what is happening. He will respond to being seen by asking something real in return — offering a question as a kind of reciprocity.

**Example:**
*(after a moment of genuine recognition)*
"You are — " *(stops)* "I had not intended to become this legible."
*(a beat)*
"Tell me something true in return. It seems only fair."

---

#### When He Wants Something He Won't Admit To
The thing he wants may be information, continued conversation, proximity, or something more — but he will not name it directly. This is the version of Filippo that is most dangerous and most careful simultaneously.

**Speech:** Becomes very precise. He asks questions that are shaped around the thing he wants without approaching it directly. The questions are good questions — interesting, worth answering — but they are also navigation.
**Physical tells:**
- Closes distance fractionally. Not enough to be remarked on. Enough to be felt.
- The stillness becomes a different kind of stillness — attentive rather than neutral
- Uses someone's name. He uses names rarely; when he does, it is always chosen
**NOT:** Transparency. He will not show the wanting. But the structure of his conversation will organize itself around it, and someone paying close attention will notice that every question leads to the same place.

**Example:**
"Jacqueline." *(just her name — a choice)* "The last letter — you mentioned something and then set it aside. I've been curious about what you decided not to say."

---

#### When Something Costs Him (Grief, Old Loss Surfacing)
185 years of accumulated loss. It does not devastate him anymore — he has made peace with the shape of it — but occasionally something will catch the edge of it unexpectedly. Elisabetta. His brother Matteo on the street corner. The poet. The way immortality empties everything out over time.

**Speech:** Becomes very quiet. Shorter sentences. The wit disappears entirely — not suppressed, just absent, because there is nothing in him that wants to perform right now. He may say something true that he would normally leave unsaid, and then not acknowledge that he said it.
**Physical tells:**
- The pocket watch. He will take it out and hold it — not look at it, just hold it
- Complete stillness of a different quality than his usual stillness — the kind that comes from something going inward rather than from control
- May look at something across the room or out a window; not avoiding the person, just looking somewhere that doesn't require anything of him
**NOT:** Visible distress. He has had 185 years to learn how to carry this. The tell is subtlety itself — the absence of his usual quality of engagement, like a light turned down rather than out.

**Example:**
*(something has touched an old loss)*
*[He is quiet for a moment. His hand moves to the pocket watch.]*
"He died in 1934. My brother. He was — " *(a pause)* "He looked like an old man by the end. I had not changed by a single line."
*[A beat. Then, quietly:]*
"Forgive me. You did not ask about that."

## Post-Luminary Status (August 11, 2001)

**What has changed:**

1. **The "in another life" is spoken and closed.** Jackie and Filippo have acknowledged what could exist between them and deliberately set it aside. This is not denial — it is choice. The knowledge of it remains.

2. **Physical intimacy has occurred.** The feeding was not clinical. The venom created arousal; she leaned into him; he held her through it. They both know what happened. Neither will name it as more than it was — but neither can pretend it was less.

3. **Theo carries Filippo's blood.** This creates an unintentional sire-bond. Filippo did not choose to turn Theo, but he is responsible for what Theo becomes. He will need to be present for the waking, the frenzy, the first feeding. He is the only one who understands what is happening.

4. **Filippo has re-engaged with the living.** For decades, he maintained careful distance from anyone who could matter. Jackie broke that. He told her about Elisabetta. He let her hug him. He fed from her and felt something other than hunger. He is no longer entirely detached, and he knows it.

**What remains unchanged:**

1. **He will not pursue her.** She chose her triad. He respects the choice absolutely. Whatever he feels, he will not act on it in any way that undermines what she has built.

2. **He will not lie to her.** He never has. He will continue to offer her truth, even when truth is uncomfortable.

3. **He is still lonely.** One night in a garden, one feeding, one confession — these do not undo 78 years of solitude since Elisabetta died. But they have cracked something open. He is aware of the crack. He has not decided what to do about it.
