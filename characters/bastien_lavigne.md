# Bastien Lavigne

*Dedicated character sheet. Created 13 September 2026 from repository canon, recovered France Arc evidence, Alice's approved September decisions, and the approved visual design.*

## Core Identity

- **Family:** Francine Lavigne's only son; brother of Vivienne, Margaux, and Élodie; Jackie Nott's cousin.
- **Family function:** Oracle's Vaults liaison and practical family protector. He is not automatically the security chief or a professional enforcer.
- **Age:** Approximately 20 at Fontainebleau in August 2002. Exact birthday remains open.
- **Succession position:** As a son in a matrilineal dynasty, he has more time and freedom than Vivienne and will not inherit the whole structure.
- **Blood/status:** A member of the wealthy French Lavigne magical family. Exact blood-status wording and paternal lineage remain open.

## Appearance — Approved Lock

- Broad, warm build; rounder face than Vivienne.
- Thick, unruly chestnut curls and hazel eyes.
- Rolled sleeves, open collar, waistcoat, and sturdy tall boots in the approved design.
- His appearance can suggest recent practical work without requiring every scene to cover him in leaves, mud, or botanical evidence.

**Visual reference:** `../reference/visual/lavigne/bastien-approved-design.png`

## Established History

- Bastien remembers Jackie with genuine childhood intimacy: kicking each other beneath tables, her threat to cut out his tongue, and her mapping the château to win hide-and-seek.
- He is a natural talker and an even better listener.
- His physical ease, mischief, and warm banter with Jackie and Draco were established during the 2001 France Arc.
- His Oracle's Vaults connection is practical and chosen. Someone must manage extraction, and the forgemasters answer to family; this is responsibility, not merely a dangerous-sounding title.
- His relationship to family obligation contains quiet rebellion. The image of picking the lock that keeps him “in” expresses pressure without making him irresponsible.
- A possible Beaumont/Célestine alliance belongs to family pressure, not a settled engagement or romance.

## Character Engine

Pleasure is ordinary to Bastien, not a manifesto. He is emotionally generous and professionally useful without experiencing succession as pressure on every private choice. He tends to assume that space can simply be taken because he has always been granted more of it than his sisters.

That blind spot does not make him stupid or shallow. When confronted, he may first be genuinely surprised by an asymmetry he never had to see. His protectiveness responds to real threats or needs; it does not keep him permanently poised for violence.

## Traits

Warm · Charming · Mischievous · Tactile by invitation · Protective · Practical · Loyal · Unhurried · A better listener than his ease initially suggests

## Voice and Physical Register

- Rich, unhurried baritone.
- Low, rolling, genuine laughter.
- Direct invitations and uncomplicated welcome.
- Comfortable physicality: offered arms, an open shoulder, easy occupation of space. Touch remains an offer, not an assumption.
- Amused admission when caught rather than reflexive defensiveness.
- Curls pushed back with no lasting effect.
- Let a serious protective sentence arrive briefly, without changing his entire temperature.
- He is not comic relief, a golden-retriever caricature, or violence in a waistcoat.

## Voice Anchor

**Core sound:** Warm, direct, and unhurried. Bastien speaks from inside the activity rather than hovering above it with commentary. His welcome is usually an invitation, a practical help, or an amused admission.

- **Sentence shape:** Simple declarations and offers. He does not need a clever counter every time another cousin speaks.
- **Conversational instinct:** Make room, solve the immediate physical problem, or include the person at the edge. Competence appears in what his hands are already doing.
- **Humour temperature:** Open amusement at a concrete inconvenience, including his own. He can lose an argument without manufacturing a comeback.
- **Serious register:** One brief, literal sentence; warmth remains underneath it. Protectiveness does not turn his diction martial.
- **Played calibration lines:** “Je me suis porté volontaire. Vivienne appelle ça un manque de jugement.” / “Those are yours. Careful. The sauce escapes.”
- **Rewrite test:** If the line exists to demonstrate “Bastien energy,” tee up a sibling, or make him the room's golden-retriever entertainer, give him something real to carry, notice, offer, or finish instead.

## Important Dynamics

- **Vivienne:** She prices risk; he experiences possibility. Her inheritance narrows choices he may mistake for universally available freedom.
- **Margaux:** Childhood accomplices. Their complicity lives in timing, selective innocence, and divided attention—not invented anecdotes presented as memory.
- **Élodie:** Protective without speaking over her. He must allow her ordinary competence and complete thoughts.
- **Jackie:** The easiest French cousin for her to reconnect with; shared childhood warmth is established. He can welcome without demanding intimacy.
- **Draco:** Convivial inclusion can give Draco somewhere to stand without turning every family encounter into a rehabilitation hearing.
- **Cole:** Similar physical warmth, different responsibility. Cole already runs American operations; Bastien has inherited freedom from the main burden.

## Knowledge Firewall

- Practical Vaults responsibility does not grant unrestricted cavern, archive, or family-secret access.
- He does not automatically know who wore Eli's face at the wedding.
- He does not know Theo has been detained or that Leandro may appear as False Eli.
- He does not inherit Vivienne's complete Henri history, Samuel and Eli's private relationship, Ruby's history, or the triad's private disclosures by kinship.
- Protectiveness must answer something actually witnessed or credibly reported.
