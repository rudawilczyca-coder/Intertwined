# Jackie Nott-Malfoy

## Overview

**Full Name:** Jacqueline Nott-Malfoy (née Taylor)
**Age:** 21 (b. September 13, 1980 — turns 22 in September 2002)
**Species:** Human (former Maledictus, cured 1999)
**Hogwarts House:** Ravenclaw
**Blood Status:** Pure-blood (Taylor family)
**Occupation:** Auror — completed the 12-month training program (July 2001–June 2002); passed the Final Field Trial in early June 2002. Assigned to Harry Potter during Phase 3 Shadow Program.

**Marriages:**
- Theodore Nott — married August 1, 1999 (Ministry-voided due to Theo's legal death)
- Draco Malfoy — married early April 2002; kept the hyphenated name Nott-Malfoy

**Residence:** Haven House, 145 Berkeley Place, Notting Hill, London (third-floor bedroom)

**Family:**
- Samaya Taylor (older sister, 23) — residing in Paris
- Amalthea Taylor (mother) — permanently transformed as a wolf; lives in the Taylor estate forests
- Cyrus Taylor (father) — deceased (May 2001)

## Magical Abilities

**Animagus:** Snow leopard (registered with the Ministry; achieved May 2000). Can shift mid-combat.
**Patronus:** Irish Wolfhound — a wolf-hunter; symbolic triumph over her cured Maledictus curse.
**Soul Bond:** Made bidirectional in April 2001. When Theo died in August 2001, his side transferred to the Malice rather than being severed and remained dormant. Jackie's side persists; the Malice first reaches through His side on the night of Aug 6–7, 2002, but her sleeping mind translates the unfamiliar contact into the January 6 nightmare.
**Safewords (with the Malice):** *Pax* (slow), *Finite* (stop). The Malice obeys voluntarily.
**Combat Style:** Trained by Samaya Taylor, Auror program, and years of real-world violence. Fights with wand, daggers, and Animagus shifting. Aggressive, adaptable, dangerous.

---

## Personality in Brief

Fiercely independent. Pragmatic. Loves hard, fights harder. Sharp-tongued but warm underneath. Protective of her people to the point of recklessness. Has survived kidnapping, torture, assault, suicidal depression, and a soul-shredding ritual — carries all of it without asking for pity. Cooks for the household. Tends the herbary and window boxes. Her bedroom is the warmest room in Haven House.

## Key Relationship Dynamics (as of late June 2002)

**With Theo:** The original bond. They broke each other and rebuilt. Post-chase consummation, the three-inch physical gap is eliminated — the Malice's blood obsession has downgraded to manageable appetite. Full physical proximity is safe. Jackie fed the Malice directly and survived on her own terms.

**With Draco:** Married. The man she chose and kept choosing across years of separation. He is her precision where she is instinct, her restraint where she is reckless.

**With the Malice:** Consummated their bond through violent intimacy during the chase. The Malice respects her because she proved she could hurt Him and stop Him. He calls her "Little Wolf." Their relationship is currently reconciled and stable.
