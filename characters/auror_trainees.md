# Auror Training Program — Trainee Cohort (2001–2002)

Jackie's cohort consists of six trainees admitted to the Auror Training Program under Harry Potter's supervision. Training began July 2001.

---

## Catherine Morrow
**Age:** 20 (b. 1981) | **House:** Hufflepuff | **Blood Status:** Half-blood
**Build:** Sturdy, athletic frame; keeps dark hair in a practical braid

**Personality:**
- Quietly determined, guarded about her first failed attempt at the program
- Works twice as hard as anyone because she feels she has something to prove
- Not flashy — she grinds. Dry humor surfaces when she's comfortable
- Gravitates toward Jackie naturally; appreciates not being treated like a second-chance case

**Combat Style:** Defensive and methodical. Excellent shield charms, slow to go offensive but very hard to break down.

---

## Julie Knox
**Age:** 21 (b. 1980) | **School:** Ilvermorny (Thunderbird) | **Blood Status:** No-Maj-born (Muggleborn equivalent)
**Build:** Tall, lean, freckled — bright-eyed American energy

**Personality:**
- Enthusiastic, earnest, occasionally tone-deaf about British wizarding culture and pureblood politics
- Hero-worships Harry Potter borderline unprofessionally; likely has a crush on him
- Friendly and open but can come across as naive
- Moved to Britain specifically to train under Potter

**Combat Style:** Aggressive, creative spell combinations. Ilvermorny trains differently and it shows — unorthodox but sometimes sloppy.

**Notes:** Her ignorance of pureblood dynamics could create friction if she ever clocks Jackie's actual connections to the old Slytherin families.

---

## Ridoc Ashford
**Age:** 17 (b. 1984) | **House:** Gryffindor | **Blood Status:** Pure-blood (minor family)
**Build:** Wiry, restless energy, never fully still

**Personality:**
- Class clown — mouthy, irreverent, uses humor to deflect everything
- Smarter than he lets on; perceptive underneath the act
- Orbits Drake's energy without fully committing to the cause: laughs at the joke, won't throw the punch
- Was present when Drake cornered Jackie on day one; watched and nodded, didn't instigate

**Relationship to Drake:** Best mate by proximity and Gryffindor loyalty. Doesn't share Drake's grudge at the same temperature. Probably thought Jackie's comeback was funny.

**Relationship to Jackie:** Neutral-to-wary. Won't antagonize her, but won't publicly break from Drake either. Could swing either direction.

**Combat Style:** Fast, instinct-driven, good reflexes, terrible discipline. Gets docked points constantly but lands hits nobody expects.

---

## Drake Lightwood
**Age:** 17 (b. 1984) | **House:** Gryffindor | **Blood Status:** Half-blood
**Build:** Broad-shouldered, square-jawed, conventionally good-looking — looks like an Auror recruitment poster

**Personality:**
- Jock through and through: competitive, loud, physically confident, runs on bravado and peer validation
- Shameless flirt with a specific playbook — targets older single women (30+) at the Ministry, using charm and looks for insight, access, and career leverage
- Not stupid, but not thoughtful — speaks before he thinks and doubles down when challenged
- Pack mentality; always performing for whoever's watching

**Hostility toward Jackie:**
- Formed on day one when Jackie arrived flanked by Theodore Nott and Draco Malfoy — two surnames any post-war Gryffindor would clock immediately
- At the first coffee break, Drake cornered Jackie with Ridoc to ask if the rumors were true. When she confirmed the triad, he asked if she was "open" to others. Jackie told him he must have taken a spill off a Hippogriff as a child if he thought she'd ever trade down that far. His ego never recovered.
- Escalated into active rivalry: muttered comments, kicked stools, shoulder-checks. Jackie gave as good as she got.
- Peaked during fight training when Jackie punched his teeth out and broke his ribs.
- Nurses humiliation into ugliness rather than developing respect.

**Combat Style:** Power-based, brute force. Strong but predictable. Relies on being bigger than his opponent — which is why Jackie dismantling him cut so deep.

---

## Sawyer Prewett
**Age:** 19 (b. 1982) | **House:** Ravenclaw | **Blood Status:** Pure-blood (Prewett family — legacy)
**Build:** Lean, bookish look that's slightly misleading; more capable than he appears

**Personality:**
- Quiet, observant, socially awkward
- Academically brilliant but physically undertrained
- Suspected legacy placement — Jackie didn't see him at tryouts
- Existing warmth toward Jackie from Hogwarts; she tutored him and his yearmates in Potions
- Same year as Caroline's brother Callum — built-in connection to Jackie's social circle
- Not competitive by nature; may be doing this because it's what Prewetts do, not what he wants

**Combat Style:** Textbook-perfect technique, very little instinct. Knows the theory cold but freezes under real pressure.

**Notes:** The Prewett name carries legacy weight (Molly Weasley née Prewett; the Prewett brothers died fighting Voldemort). That pressure could be defining his choices more than genuine ambition.
