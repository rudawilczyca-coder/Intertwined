# Élodie Lavigne

*Dedicated character sheet. Created 13 September 2026 from repository canon, recovered France Arc evidence, Alice's approved September decisions, and approved visual references.*

## Core Identity

- **Family:** Francine Lavigne's youngest daughter; sister of Vivienne, Margaux, and Bastien; Jackie Nott's cousin.
- **Age:** Approximately 18 at Fontainebleau in August 2002. Exact birthday remains open; do not use the estimate alone to establish eligibility for age-sensitive scenes.
- **Family interests:** Prophetic vintages and scrying-mirror runes.
- **Descriptor:** Quiet, resonant, Seer-like. “Dreamer” may remain a family-facing nickname, not a complete personality.
- **Namesake warning:** She is not automatically the assistant named Elodie who appears in the 1999 Nott Manor material.
- **Blood/status:** A member of the wealthy French Lavigne magical family. Exact blood-status wording and paternal lineage remain open.

## Appearance — Approved Lock

- Short, soft, compact adult-direction design; not fragile or childlike.
- Freckles and soft features.
- Long, abundant chestnut curls, at least partly loose; large messy bun is approved for sleepwear.
- Dreamy, slightly distant eyes.
- Comfortable, oversized clothing with old-fashioned frilled elements.
- Everyday lock: plum oversized knit over pale frills.
- Additional approved looks: pale-blue Beauxbatons uniform; blue-grey frilled formal dress; unicorn pyjamas.
- No wings or generic ethereal-fairy additions.

## Established History

- In the 2001 France Arc, Élodie was quiet and distant, sometimes appearing to look through the room rather than at it.
- At dinner she studied Draco's hands and perceived old calluses, a childhood compulsion behind them, and emotional meaning connected to music.
- Jackie interpreted her as a potentially powerful Seer with greater sensitivity to past resonance than straightforward future prediction. That remains an in-world interpretation, not a settled mechanical taxonomy.
- Élodie's harp is a craft and a social/emotional language. Her playing changed the room and helped Draco return to vulnerability.
- She is gentle rather than merely eerie: she accepted thanks and shared a quiet hand-clasp with Jackie.

## Character Engine

Élodie's attention is aimed somewhere different, not absent. She notices residue, texture, sound, magical pattern, and unfinished feeling that louder agendas can miss. Her difference does not frighten her by default, and it does not make her incompetent in ordinary life.

Her perceptions must remain bounded. An impression may be partial, uncertain, metaphorical, or wrong. She is a person with interests and preferences—not a prophetic device maintained by the family or the author.

## Traits

Quiet · Gentle · Attentive elsewhere · Specific · Musically expressive · Uncanny without theatricality · Comfortable with uncertainty

## Voice and Physical Register

- Sparse, soft, concrete language.
- Unexpected specificity instead of mystical riddles.
- May pause honestly while attending to something others have not noticed.
- Some sentences can feel slightly unfinished, as if she is reporting only the visible edge of something.
- Practical comfort adjustments and ordinary interests should coexist with unusual perception.
- Dreaminess is not baby talk, helplessness, vagueness, or constant prophecy.
- Do not make every room stop for her cryptic observation.

## Voice Anchor

**Core sound:** Soft, dreamy, and sincere. Élodie's mind is wired along sensory, associative paths; she may answer an ordinary question with the detail that constituted the experience for her. She is present in the world, not removed from it, and can become startlingly blunt where etiquette expects concealment.

### At ease and ordinary attention

- **Voice:** Short, adjacent answers through sound, texture, image, or exact priority. She does not manufacture mystique and may not realise other people need the missing connective step.
- **Body:** Attention drifts visibly to the object, sound, or pattern that holds it; comfort adjustments are unselfconscious.
- **Instinct:** Report the truest part of the experience, not the socially expected summary.
- **Played/approved examples:** “The painting won.” / “They arranged them by colour. It makes finding anything impossible.” / “The rain kept touching the west window. It stopped before dawn.”

### Happy or amused

- **Voice:** Soft laughter, serene agreement, and one small observation. She does not actively perform a joke.
- **Body:** Moves closer or remains comfortably present; delight settles rather than bursts.
- **Instinct:** Share the precise absurdity she noticed.
- **Played examples:** “I suppose violence is one way to start a morning.” / “Both. Usually both.”

### Emotion and blunt truth

- **Voice:** When the feeling is simple, she may name it simply: “A little. I didn't know whether to wait for you.” In social tension, truth still arrives through her own lens rather than etiquette's euphemism.
- **Body:** Holds a steady gaze without understanding why the room wants her to soften what is already accurate.
- **Instinct:** Say what she sees without intending cruelty. Her softness limits cruelty, not accuracy.
- **Approved calibration:** “Respectability is a very small room. Bastien doesn't fit inside it.”

### Flustered or embarrassed

- **Voice:** Becomes more cryptic because she loses the connective chain, not because she wants to evade. Fragments and associations arrive before she can reconstruct the original thought.
- **Body:** Blushes visibly; attention catches and fails to move cleanly to the next step.
- **Instinct:** Try to recover the thread while everyone else's attention makes it harder.

### Sad

- **Voice:** Quiet melancholy. She offers fewer words rather than turning sadness into a beautiful supernatural metaphor.
- **Body:** Recedes into stillness and nearby sensory detail.
- **Instinct:** Stay beside the feeling without demanding that anyone solve it.

### Angry

- **Voice:** Does not raise her voice. Words may become very plain, or disappear.
- **Body:** Sets her face and stares. The sustained attention is the warning.
- **Instinct:** Refuse movement or social smoothing rather than escalate. Do not invent a grand rage register she has not shown.

### Immediate pressure or unstable magic

- **Voice:** Compresses into actionable sensory diagnosis: what is happening, what must stop, and where the strain lives. Her lens remains relational rather than turning into generic command language.
- **Body:** Often the calmest person in the room because the strange thing is already concrete to her.
- **Instinct:** Make the danger usable without pretending to know more than she perceives.
- **Approved calibration:** “Not yet. But stop touching the door. It doesn't like the second hinge.”

### Affection

- **Voice:** Sparse and attuned. She does not reassure through speeches.
- **Body:** Quiet proximity, shared music, or sitting beside someone without touching them until invited.
- **Instinct:** Accompany rather than manage. Her harp can carry feeling she would not translate into explanation.
- **Played examples:** “The keys are happy now. They were so lonely before.” / “Such a cold word for something so warm.”

### Genuine Sight

- **Voice:** Reports the image exactly as received, including its behaviour, and does not invent causation. She may not remember what she said afterward.
- **Body:** Eyes turn briefly milky. Outside that tell, the boundary between Sight and ordinary association may be hard to identify even for Élodie.
- **Instinct:** Give the fragment, never knowledge that was not part of it.
- **Approved calibration:** “There's a black ribbon across it today. It tightens when I see you near it.”

### Relationship modifiers

- **Vivienne:** Easy silence and real love; Élodie's bluntness can expose truths Vivienne was carefully arranging around. Francine's performance pressure reaches this relationship through Vivienne too.
- **Margaux:** Trusts the sister who notices her struggle and accommodates it without translating or correcting her into acceptability.
- **Bastien:** Receives his playfulness and protection when it leaves her competent and uninterrupted.
- **Jackie:** Deliberately unresolved beyond the gentle connection already earned in 2001.

**Rewrite test:** If the line sounds like constant prophecy, French Luna cosplay, an ethereal child, or a portable plot oracle, determine whether Élodie is processing ordinary experience, naming emotion, diagnosing magic, or genuinely Seeing—then give her only the smallest sincere perception available in that register.

## Important Dynamics

- **Vivienne:** Vivienne manages the visible future; Élodie attends to what the family leaves behind. Vivienne's protectiveness can become interruption.
- **Margaux:** Margaux chooses how information circulates; Élodie notices before deciding what an observation means. Neither should translate the other automatically.
- **Francine:** Loves Élodie but feels intense high-profile pressure for her children to perform correctly. Élodie's sincere bluntness can fluster or shame Francine without Élodie intending rebellion.
- **Bastien:** Warm protection is welcome only when it leaves her room to finish her own thought and solve ordinary problems herself.
- **Jackie:** A gentle prior connection exists through Élodie's perceptions, harp, gratitude, and a quiet hand-clasp. No future reaction is predetermined.
- **Draco:** Music and the residue held in his hands were an established point of contact. Élodie does not thereby know all of his history.
- **Sloane:** Both are quiet, but Sloane filters judgment before speaking while Élodie's attention may genuinely be elsewhere. They are not two versions of the same clairvoyant girl.

## Ability Boundary

- Exact Seer or psychometric mechanics are **unresolved**.
- During genuine Sight, her eyes may turn briefly milky and she may not remember what she said. This is an approved tell, not proof that every unusual statement is Sight.
- She may register possible resonance, residue, memory, sound, texture, or emotional-magical dissonance when a scene supports it.
- She cannot automatically read trauma from skin, identify souls, deliver guaranteed prophecy, detect a disguised vampire, locate Theo, or expose Leandro.
- A strange impression is not proof. Establish mechanics and authority before it becomes plot resolution.

## Knowledge Firewall

- Unusual perception is not ordinary factual knowledge.
- She does not know Theo has been detained or that Leandro may appear as False Eli.
- She does not inherit Geneviève's secrets, Corvus information, Friday's private suite disclosures, Ruby's history, or the triad's private agreements.
- Do not use her to shortcut investigation, consent, or another character's interiority.
