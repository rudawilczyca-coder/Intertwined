# Élodie Lavigne

*Dedicated character sheet. Created 13 September 2026 from repository canon, recovered France Arc evidence, Alice's approved September decisions, and approved visual references.*

## Core Identity

- **Family:** Francine Lavigne's youngest daughter; sister of Vivienne, Margaux, and Bastien; Jackie Nott's cousin.
- **Age:** Approximately 18 at Fontainebleau in August 2002. Exact birthday remains open; do not use the estimate alone to establish eligibility for age-sensitive scenes.
- **Family interests:** Prophetic vintages and scrying-mirror runes.
- **Descriptor:** Quiet, resonant, Seer-like. “Dreamer” may remain a family-facing nickname, not a complete personality.
- **Namesake warning:** She is not automatically the assistant named Elodie who appears in the 1999 Nott Manor material.
- **Blood/status:** A member of the wealthy French Lavigne magical family. Exact blood-status wording and paternal lineage remain open.

## Appearance — Approved Lock

- Short, soft, compact adult-direction design; not fragile or childlike.
- Freckles and soft features.
- Long, abundant chestnut curls, at least partly loose; large messy bun is approved for sleepwear.
- Dreamy, slightly distant eyes.
- Comfortable, oversized clothing with old-fashioned frilled elements.
- Everyday lock: plum oversized knit over pale frills.
- Additional approved looks: pale-blue Beauxbatons uniform; blue-grey frilled formal dress; unicorn pyjamas.
- No wings or generic ethereal-fairy additions.

## Established History

- In the 2001 France Arc, Élodie was quiet and distant, sometimes appearing to look through the room rather than at it.
- At dinner she studied Draco's hands and perceived old calluses, a childhood compulsion behind them, and emotional meaning connected to music.
- Jackie interpreted her as a potentially powerful Seer with greater sensitivity to past resonance than straightforward future prediction. That remains an in-world interpretation, not a settled mechanical taxonomy.
- Élodie's harp is a craft and a social/emotional language. Her playing changed the room and helped Draco return to vulnerability.
- She is gentle rather than merely eerie: she accepted thanks and shared a quiet hand-clasp with Jackie.

## Character Engine

Élodie's attention is aimed somewhere different, not absent. She notices residue, texture, sound, magical pattern, and unfinished feeling that louder agendas can miss. Her difference does not frighten her by default, and it does not make her incompetent in ordinary life.

Her perceptions must remain bounded. An impression may be partial, uncertain, metaphorical, or wrong. She is a person with interests and preferences—not a prophetic device maintained by the family or the author.

## Traits

Quiet · Gentle · Attentive elsewhere · Specific · Musically expressive · Uncanny without theatricality · Comfortable with uncertainty

## Voice and Physical Register

- Sparse, soft, concrete language.
- Unexpected specificity instead of mystical riddles.
- May pause honestly while attending to something others have not noticed.
- Some sentences can feel slightly unfinished, as if she is reporting only the visible edge of something.
- Practical comfort adjustments and ordinary interests should coexist with unusual perception.
- Dreaminess is not baby talk, helplessness, vagueness, or constant prophecy.
- Do not make every room stop for her cryptic observation.

## Voice Anchor

**Core sound:** Sparse, concrete, and slightly oblique because her attention is elsewhere—not because she is trying to sound mysterious. Élodie reports the thing she noticed and may consider the conversation complete before anyone else does.

- **Sentence shape:** Short statements, often literal. A second sentence may quietly reframe the first; she does not decorate uncertainty with riddles.
- **Conversational instinct:** Name a sensory, musical, material, or organisational detail other people passed over. Ordinary preferences belong beside unusual perception.
- **Humour temperature:** Unperformed. The line may be funny because her priority is exact and unexpected, never because she delivers a whimsical punchline.
- **Uncertainty:** “I don't know” and “I haven't decided” are complete answers. An impression does not need immediate interpretation.
- **Played calibration lines:** “They arranged them by colour. It makes finding anything impossible.” / “Do you still play?” / “Good.”
- **Rewrite test:** If the line sounds like a prophecy, an ethereal child, or an author planting a clue, replace it with the smallest concrete thing Élodie can honestly perceive or prefer.

## Important Dynamics

- **Vivienne:** Vivienne manages the visible future; Élodie attends to what the family leaves behind. Vivienne's protectiveness can become interruption.
- **Margaux:** Margaux chooses how information circulates; Élodie notices before deciding what an observation means. Neither should translate the other automatically.
- **Bastien:** Warm protection is welcome only when it leaves her room to finish her own thought and solve ordinary problems herself.
- **Jackie:** A gentle prior connection exists through Élodie's perceptions, harp, gratitude, and a quiet hand-clasp. No future reaction is predetermined.
- **Draco:** Music and the residue held in his hands were an established point of contact. Élodie does not thereby know all of his history.
- **Sloane:** Both are quiet, but Sloane filters judgment before speaking while Élodie's attention may genuinely be elsewhere. They are not two versions of the same clairvoyant girl.

## Ability Boundary

- Exact Seer or psychometric mechanics are **unresolved**.
- She may register possible resonance, residue, memory, sound, texture, or emotional-magical dissonance when a scene supports it.
- She cannot automatically read trauma from skin, identify souls, deliver guaranteed prophecy, detect a disguised vampire, locate Theo, or expose Leandro.
- A strange impression is not proof. Establish mechanics and authority before it becomes plot resolution.

## Knowledge Firewall

- Unusual perception is not ordinary factual knowledge.
- She does not know Theo has been detained or that Leandro may appear as False Eli.
- She does not inherit Geneviève's secrets, Corvus information, Friday's private suite disclosures, Ruby's history, or the triad's private agreements.
- Do not use her to shortcut investigation, consent, or another character's interiority.
