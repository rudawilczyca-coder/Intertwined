## Settled Canon

### Identity

- **Name:** Nell Harrow. Born **Kornelia Tarska**, in Warsaw.
- **Apparent age:** 27. **Turned:** 1948.
- **Height:** 5'2" (157 cm). Small, compact, wiry — not delicate.
- **Hair:** Jet-black, straight, glossy, shoulder-length.
- **Skin:** Very pale, cool undertone.
- **Eyes:** Deep saturated blood-red — the colour of a vampire fifty-odd years
  fed. They shift toward **violet** when she has gone hungry for a longer
  stretch, the red draining toward amethyst.
- **Face:** Lethal in repose — still, composed, unblinking. Then she smiles and
  the whole world lights up. Oraya (*Serpent and the Wings of Night*) is the
  resting state; Alice Cullen's brightness is what happens when she decides to
  be pleased with you. The same face has to do both.

### History

- Emigrated from Warsaw to London after the war, to marry **Jack Harrow**, an
  English RAF pilot.
- Trained and qualified in Warsaw as a **midwife** — delivered children under
  shellfire. Her qualifications did not transfer. In London she was put to
  fetching, keeping the records clean, and managing the hospital's **blood
  supply**. Not the head of anything. Respected anyway, because she was
  efficient and relentless.
- Recruited into a **blood-delivery network** through the hospital. Later became
  a **donor** herself.
- Was **pregnant** when part of a half-destroyed building collapsed on her as she
  walked to work. She was brought into the hospital dying.
- Her **vampire patron** turned her there, to save her. She was not asked — she
  was not in a state to be asked.
- She **lost the child** in the accident. She was told soon after she woke.
- Her sire **had sought permission** and had the process in flight; he was
  securing her the chance at the Kiss when the accident outran the paperwork.
  He may have been in love with her, or may simply have valued her company as
  her patron.
- **Jack:** she let him believe she died. It was the cleaner transition. She does
  not know where he is, whether he is alive, or what became of him.
- **No mortal ties at all.** No friends, no relatives, living or dead. She kept
  his name for fifty-five years anyway.

## Proposals — Sable's, pending Alice's approval

### On the Third Tradition

The Third Tradition governs the **sire's** permission from their elder and the
Prince (`lore/vampiric_lore_updated.md:234`). The childe's consent is not part of
it. Nell not being asked is a **moral** fact, not a legal one.

So she isn't an illegal turning. She's an **emergency treated as permission**,
blessed retroactively — legitimate on a technicality.

### Why Viola will not let her sire

`characters/London_Camarilla.md:48` — the Great Fire of 1666 formed Viola's
governing philosophy, and the specific horror in it was *"frightened elders
treated survival as permission to abandon the Traditions."*

That is precisely Nell. Her existence is the argument Viola has spent three
hundred and thirty-six years refusing: that when things are dire enough, the rule
bends and everyone agrees afterward that it had to.

And the maddening part is that in Nell's case it **worked**. She's proof, walking
around the Domain, that the shortcut sometimes produces something good.

Her long ambition is to be granted permission **under the Third Tradition** to
sire someone herself — legitimacy in the exact law her own life bent. Nell's
counter-argument is unanswerable and useless: *the breach worked. I'm standing
here.*

### Why she's drawn to Theo

He's unpresented, unreleased, a sire's liability. She was that. Nobody else in
the London court can say it the same way.

### The recruitment profile

A blood-delivery network doesn't approach a midwife. It approaches the person who
controls where the blood goes and whether anyone notices it's missing. Her
competence made her useful; being underemployed made her reachable. She was doing
the same job before and after she died — she just stopped being the one who signs
for it.

### Traits (proposed)

Efficient · Relentless · Warm when she chooses · Reckless · Loyal · No mortal ties

### Quote (proposed)

> "The breach worked. I'm standing here."
