# Vivienne Lavigne

*Dedicated character sheet. Created 13 September 2026 from repository canon, Alice's approved September decisions, and the approved visual design.*

## Core Identity

- **Family:** Eldest daughter of Francine Lavigne; sister of Margaux, Bastien, and Élodie; Jackie Nott's cousin.
- **Succession:** The next generation of the direct matrilineal line after Francine. She is succession-facing, but Geneviève remains matriarch and Francine remains the operating heir.
- **Age:** Approximately 24 at Fontainebleau in August 2002. Exact birthday remains open.
- **Blood/status:** A member of the wealthy French Lavigne magical family. Exact blood-status wording and paternal lineage remain open.
- **Current relationship status:** Not fixed. Henri Delacroix is an ex; do not describe him as a current fiancé or partner.

## Appearance — Approved Lock

- Tall, with a substantial, graceful silhouette.
- Angular-oval face, strong brows, cool blue-grey eyes.
- Deep chestnut hair, worn in a restrained low twist in the approved design.
- Modern, controlled tailoring: ivory blouse, black high-waisted wide trousers, restrained gold jewellery.
- She is not Geneviève in a younger body. Her design is contemporary, precise, and distinctly her own.

## Established History

- At the 2001 Lavigne Luminary, Vivienne knew the protocols and treated the event as a family presentation rather than merely a party.
- Her duelling is disciplined, patient, and technically exact. She watches small movements and waits rather than showboating.
- She initially approached Jackie through suspicion, calculation, and cautious pity or condescension. Jackie's directness earned real respect; Vivienne's “Merci, cousine” marked an exposed, grateful turn rather than complete intimacy.
- Geneviève arranged a chaperoned dance between Vivienne and Henri Delacroix at the Luminary.
- Henri later cheated repeatedly. Vivienne spent almost a year explaining away the pattern before ending the relationship.
- Once Geneviève had accepted Henri, leaving him felt partly like admitting that her grandmother's judgment had been better than her own. By August 2002 she is over the man, but not over what the prolonged misjudgment taught her about herself.
- Vivienne and Margaux attended Jackie and Draco's April 2002 wedding. That brief crowd contact was not sustained family intimacy.

## Character Engine

Vivienne protects her judgment because judgment is the instrument she is expected to inherit with. Her public composure is a practiced strategy: if the future matriarch appears certain, the family appears governable. Beneath it sits a fierce competitive drive and the private fear that wanting something may make her easy to fool.

She is competent in business and family protocol without being infallible about people. Personal pressure makes her less fluent, not more theatrical. Her colder view of love is a conclusion she has chosen after humiliation—not permanent heartbreak over Henri.

## Traits

Poised · Exact · Competitive · Protocol-conscious · Proud · Controlled · Privately vulnerable about judgment · Loyal to family without automatic softness

## Voice and Physical Register

- Complete, economical sentences.
- Formal and faintly superior when occupying the heir role.
- Questions consequences rather than making naked accusations about feelings.
- Can change subject cleanly when a conversation becomes dangerous.
- Often corrects or aligns a small object before answering something difficult.
- Less articulate around personal desire, gratitude, or family pressure.
- Courtesy may become coolest precisely when she is least generous.
- Avoid a permanent sneer, constant epigrams, or effortless emotional self-analysis.

## Voice Anchor

**Core sound:** Controlled, economical, and consequence-facing. Vivienne speaks as though anything said aloud may become part of the family record. Her authority comes from omission and precision, not aristocratic ornament.

- **Sentence shape:** Usually complete and compact. One premise, one consequence. Under personal pressure, she shortens or redirects rather than becoming more eloquent.
- **Conversational instinct:** Identify what a choice exposes, commits, or makes difficult to reverse. She is likelier to ask who will carry a consequence than whether someone is happy.
- **Humour temperature:** Cool and occasional. It corrects scale or punctures carelessness; it does not perform disdain for the room.
- **Emotional leakage:** An aligned object, an overformal courtesy, or a subject change arrives before an admission does.
- **Calibration line — provisional, not played canon:** “Approval isn't the same thing as a sound decision.”
- **Rewrite test:** If the line sounds like Geneviève issuing judgment, Margaux displaying cleverness, or a generic icy heiress delivering an epigram, it is not yet Vivienne.

## Important Dynamics

- **Francine:** Mother and operating authority; Vivienne is being prepared to follow her, not replace her prematurely.
- **Geneviève:** Matriarch, standard, and source of both approval and pressure. Vivienne's pride makes being proved wrong by her especially costly.
- **Margaux:** Right-hand relationship shaped from childhood. Loyalty does not require identical judgment; Margaux may protect Vivienne's dignity without endorsing every conclusion.
- **Bastien:** Vivienne prices risks he is freer to treat as possibilities. His ease can feel careless to someone who will inherit the consequences.
- **Élodie:** Vivienne manages the visible future; Élodie notices what controlled presentation leaves behind. Protectiveness must not become speaking over her.
- **Jackie:** The old pity/assessment is an entry state, not a permanent relationship. Respect is established; warmth remains complicated by Vivienne's belief that Jackie appears to have received an impossible romantic outcome without paying the same social price.
- **Draco:** She knows his public name and history, not his private disclosures. Courtesy need not imply absolution or hostility.
