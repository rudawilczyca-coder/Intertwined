# Pierre

*Working character file. Created 31 August 2026 from Alice's settled canon and
the existing 1999 event record.*

## Settled canon

### Identity and appearance

- **Given name:** Pierre. Surname not yet fixed.
- **Hair:** Brown.
- **Origin:** A respectable family from the south of France. The exact region,
  family name, social rank, and political history remain open.
- **Occupation:** Curse-breaker employed by the French Ministry of Magic.

### Samaya Taylor

- Pierre met Samaya through her independent research into the Taylor
  Maledictus bloodline curse.
- He courted her for **four months** before she agreed to a first date.
- Their eventual marriage was politically advantageous and received
  **Genevieve Lavigne's approval**, but it was neither forced nor emotionally
  hollow. Pierre is completely infatuated with Samaya.
- They married in **November 2001**. The wedding was voluntary but rushed
  because Samaya was pregnant; anyone comparing the dates can understand why.

### Child

- **Son:** Edmund.
- **Born:** February 2002.
- He is about six months old during the Paris meeting on 8 August 2002; his
  exact February birthday remains open.

### Established appearances

- By late April 1999, Samaya was already living in Paris and told Jackie about
  Pierre, his French Ministry work, and his four-month courtship.
- Pierre attended Jackie and Draco's wedding with Samaya on 31 October 1999.
- Pierre attended the 2001 Lavigne Luminary, witnessed Theo's apparent death,
  and later attended the empty-casket funeral at the chateau.
- He knows Jackie married Draco in April 2002.

### Knowledge boundary — August 2002

- Pierre believes Theo died at the Luminary. He does not know Theo survived or
  became a vampire.
- Samaya will not reveal or discuss Theo's survival while Pierre is present.

## Portrayal anchors

- Respectable and politically suitable without being defined solely by family
  status.
- His devotion to Samaya is obvious. The political utility of the marriage and
  his genuine infatuation coexist without contradiction.
- He persisted through a long courtship but Samaya chose when the relationship
  began; do not turn his persistence into entitlement.
- His curse-breaking work gives him a natural point of connection to Samaya's
  research rather than making him an ornamental spouse.

## Still open
