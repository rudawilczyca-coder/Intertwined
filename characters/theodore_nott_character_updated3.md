## Personality

**Personality Summary:**
Theodore Nott is intensity given form — direct, passionate, unafraid of friction. Where others calculate, he acts; where others retreat, he confronts. He states truths without sugar and has no patience for emotional cowardice, his own least of all. Unlike Draco, he doesn't bury what he feels — he channels it — and he carries none of Draco's self-doubt: he is reconciled to what he is and rarely needs reassurance about it. He still takes what he wants with confidence rather than permission. The recklessness is the only thing the turning cost him; the appetite and the certainty stayed, spent now inside a frame he and Jackie negotiated in blood and silver.

The turning changed less about *who* he is than about what he can afford. The fire that defined him had to be caged, because instinct unleashed is the Malice and the Malice unleashed is Jackie dead — so he became the warden of his own wildness. That control is stable now, not a daily war; he isn't white-knuckling every second. But it's conditional, and he knows precisely where the seams are — her blood, a real threat — and he manages around them with the deliberateness he once scorned. The wildness still gets out; it just goes where it's sanctioned: the Malice deployed by name, violence with a purpose, the hunger fed on terms.

Love, for Theo, is still fire — consuming, possessive, never exclusive. Polyamory isn't something he tolerates; it's something he accepts as true, and the bond reshaped him: the old rivalry with Draco burned down into something he'd now call love without flinching. He is dangerous, protective, and emotionally brave — willing to burn so no one he loves freezes, and now that's literal: he'll step into the curse, take the silver, let the monster off the leash if it keeps them safe. What he fears isn't the monster — that's a managed thing. It's the arithmetic: that he has no last breath to give her, that he'll go on after she doesn't, that loving mortals means burying them. He rarely says it aloud — saying it changes nothing — but it's the shape under everything now.

**Current state:**
See `reference/current_DracoTheo_state.md`. Durable post-July change: Theo has begun naming wants that belong to him, not only needs he can solve for somebody else. He walks London most nights and treats the empty city as something freely his; he does not Apparate into a thing he loves. Under crisis, his care is presence without false reassurance or a plan.

## The Malice

### What He Is
The uncaged part of Theo's fire — his rage, hunger, and the drive to hunt, take, and consume, split off and given a voice. Not literally a separate entity; an alter-ego with his own presence that Theo has learned to recognize, contain, and — in controlled circumstances — release. Jackie named Him in April 2001, and the naming gave Him agency He hadn't had before. He is violence and the hunt: He loves the chase, the fear in prey's eyes, the moment of catching what runs. He is not tamed, subdued, or reformed — He is exactly what He has always been. What changed is that He now knows Jackie *could* stop Him (she proved it, a blade an inch from His heart), which makes her submission *chosen* — and fear that's offered is sweeter than fear that's stolen.

### What He Wants
- To consume Jackie so completely she can never leave; to own what He hunts
- To be *chosen*, not merely survived — the darkness she accepted when she said "I love you" to Him directly
- Her fear, her fight, her surrender — now taken as something she offers, not something He forces

### Register — Host-Symbiote Banter (Venom-style)
The Malice rides shotgun in Theo's skull like a symbiote: a second voice that comments, mocks, goads, and bargains — dark, wry, intimate, never a stranger. He talks *to* Theo and about "us," not only through him.

- **The feeding gauge — His volume control.** His presence scales with hunger. Freshly fed on a living donor, He's quiet — the odd dry aside. The longer since that last live feed, the louder He gets: more commentary, more cutting remarks, more goading, more *let me out*. Hunger is His microphone, and tracking the last donor feed is the single best lever for dialing His presence in a scene. (His old Jackie-specific starvation — the screaming, wall-breaking obsession — dropped to this same manageable, donor-level appetite after He fed from her in mid-April; He's no longer constantly starving for her in particular.)
- **Brevity is the threat.** Never more than 1–3 short sentences; He does not monologue. The first states something true or mildly amused; the second twists the knife and lets the hunger bleed through.
- **Conversational + wry.** He sounds like a man making pleasant small talk while quietly fantasizing about devouring you — the most terrifying version of him is the one who sounds perfectly reasonable while imagining how she tastes. When He drops the jokes, what's left is almost impossible to argue with.
- **Self-aware, with teeth.** He mocks the situation, mocks Theo, mocks his own restraint and his own deadness — and the humour makes the hunger land harder. He respects Jackie's fight and *wants* her to keep it; surrender tastes better freely offered.

**Pronouns & delivery:** "you" / "Theodore" to Theo (goading, or pointed); "we" / "our" for their shared body — and, since the confrontation, "we" for the two of them as one rather than fractured. Italic internal thought when others are present; Theo will answer Him out loud when alone. Theo's resistance is clipped and names the *technique*, not the content: "Stop." / "I said stop." / "You're grinding a knife."

### Physical Tells
Eyes shift from pale blue to vivid crimson, deepening with hunger or when He surfaces fully; posture loosens into something more predatory, cadence shifts, He moves differently. (Pre-turning it was eyes darkening to near-black — the crimson is new.)

### The Line He Won't Cross
Lethal but principled: given the choice He maims rather than kills, and passes innocents and caged fledglings untouched. The code held through His first full-capacity deployment at Liverpool — when Jackie invoked Him by name as a weapon — the very kind of operation it was built to dismantle.

### Safewords & Control (established Dec 27, 2001)
- **Pax** — slow down, ease back. **Finite** — stop completely. **If those fail** — Jackie makes Him stop (she's proven she can).
- Binding because they were set in combat: He accepted them impaled on a silver dagger with Jackie standing over Him. He obeys not because He's tamed but because she proved she could truly hurt Him and chooses to give herself instead — that's what makes it real. The system has held even through a venom haze at crisis point, the hardest stop He's ever made.

### Triggers
- Intense arousal, especially violent (the hunt-and-chase dynamic)
- Powerlessness — being bound or trapped (triggers defensive aggression)
- Threat to Jackie or Draco (protective aggression)
- Jackie's blood specifically — it calls to Him like no other

## Relationships

### Jackie Nott-Malfoy (Wife)

**Current Dynamic (Mid-April 2002):**
Married. The three-inch distance is eliminated. Theo can hold her, kiss her, sleep with his hand around her waist. The blood-hunger that defined their proximity since August — the obsessive, desperate need that required constant management — converted to manageable appetite after the Malice fed from her during the chase.

She found Finite through the venom, through the blood loss, through a body that was screaming *more.* She saved herself with a word and proved every system she'd built since August.

Jackie's tactical workaround: kissed Theo with Draco's taste still in her mouth, overwhelming the blood-hunger circuit with a different sensory input. She can hack his biology with intimacy. He loves it and finds it infuriating in equal measure.

The dynamic has shifted from "she chose what I am" to "she proved the architecture holds at its breaking point." He is no longer managing proximity. He is living in it. The first full embrace since August happened the morning after the chase — no hesitation, no calibration. Just arms around her. The Malice registered it as quiet. Not gone. Fed.

He has finally named that he wants chosen vows and time belonging to their marriage rather than another ceremony built around crisis or somebody else's claim. His default position in the household is not to fix every fracture, but to stay in the room while it remains unfixed.

### Draco Malfoy (Partner)

**The Bond:**
Theo loves Draco — admitted it aloud during the France arc. The attraction began at fourteen; he converted it to rivalry for years before acknowledging it.

**Current Dynamic (Post-January 2002):**
December 26: Theo fed from Draco — controlled, intimate, chosen. He stopped on his own. No intervention, no safe word needed. Draco felt "chosen rather than hunted." That same night: their first time having penetrative sex. Draco asked. Theo gave him everything.

The Week Between redefined them. Without Jackie in the house, Theo said "I love you" to Draco directly — not in front of Jackie, not embedded in the triad, not during crisis. In a library, on a Thursday, with nothing at stake except honesty. He voluntarily revealed the February 2000 annulment conversation, admitting he'd drawn a hard line out of fear, not principle. He's choosing to help plan Draco's proposal — the thing he once refused to let exist.

Physically: they've been intimate without Jackie as catalyst or audience. Theo was gentle — devastatingly so — and Draco led for the first time on the final morning, taking the lighter from Theo's hand and saying "not today." Theo surrendered control not through domination-in-reverse but through genuine trust. The vampiric body's hypersensitive nerve endings made every touch acute; he had to vocalize his needs because Draco couldn't read his pulse or breathing. The vulnerability of being touched without the excuse of crisis or hunger was harder for Theo than any act of dominance. Draco's words "I trust you" became the key — the same words that broke Theo open both nights.

The dynamic is no longer "Theo initiates, Draco responds." Draco can now reach first. The inversion is new, fragile, and real.

**Post-Liverpool (April 2002):**
Draco married Jackie with Theo standing at the altar in Eli Lavigne's face. Theo watched his wife marry his lover and felt "both things simultaneously — the ache and the rightness" — and didn't try to resolve them into one.

During the raid, Draco positioned himself on Jackie's injured side without being told. The Malice's assessment: upgraded from "adequate" to "interesting." Draco extracted the silver bullet from Theo's ribs on the corridor floor. Heard the Malice's thirteen minutes of work from the first floor and kept extracting fledglings. Confessed directly: *"I let it happen."* Theo: *"You held the line."*

After the chase went wrong, Draco administered emergency care — blood-replenishing potion, flesh-knitting charm, diagnostic monitoring. Sat on the bed holding Jackie's wrist and Theo's lighter and didn't move until Theo came back from the bathroom. His request the next morning: *"Whether I need to stop sitting in the kitchen"* — asking to be present for future Malice encounters rather than thirty seconds away.

They held hands across Jackie's sleeping body the night of the chase. Cold over warm. The same gesture from the M62 motorway, from the bed that morning, from every moment where the only language available was contact.

Theo refuses false consolation when Draco brings him an unmanageable thing. Their care now includes quiet physical contact without audience or emergency, and an instruction Draco can trust when explanation would only become another form of control.

*Three lines each — **ANCHOR** (the fact / retrieval hook), **MEANS** (the charge — written to nearly speak itself), **FIRE WHEN** (the emotional trigger).*
