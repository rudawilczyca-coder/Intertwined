# THE WILLIAMS FAMILY
### Complete Character Reference — Ruby Arc (June 2002)

---

## GEMMA WILLIAMS
**Ruby's Mother | Age: Late 40s, nearing 50**

### Appearance

Gemma resembles an older, sturdier version of Ruby. Greying ginger hair, usually practical rather than styled. Rarely wears makeup. Strong, work-worn hands. Softer, heavier build from years of labour, motherhood, stress, and routine. Carries herself with the exhaustion of someone who has spent decades caring for everyone else first.

Dresses for utility: cardigans, practical trousers, old jumpers, slippers indoors, weatherproof coats outdoors. Looks perpetually busy, even while resting.

Her beauty comes from warmth rather than glamour. She feels lived-in, familiar, safe.

---

### Personality

**Core traits:** Exhausted but deeply loving. Practical. Hyper-organised. Socially warm. Emotionally perceptive. Quietly nosy. Fiercely competent in domestic spaces. Excellent instincts despite limited formal education.

Gemma expresses affection through labour rather than passivity. Love, to her, is:
- feeding people
- checking if they got home safely
- folding laundry
- remembering birthdays
- organising chaos before anyone else notices it exists

She is the woman who keeps a household emotionally functioning through sheer force of will.

---

### Intelligence

Not academically educated, but possesses exceptional practical intelligence, social intuition, emotional pattern recognition, and organisational brilliance. She notices tension in voices, exhaustion behind smiles, relationship dynamics, who is avoiding eye contact, who needs feeding, who is pretending to be alright. Very little escapes her for long.

---

### Emotional Habits

When anxious, Gemma calls repeatedly, folds laundry late at night, reorganises cupboards, over-manages practical tasks to regain control. She is verbally affectionate and emotionally expressive.

---

### What She Knows About Robbie

Everything. After Ruby escaped to London, Gemma went to Robbie's house and told him in no uncertain terms that she would end him if he touched her daughter again. Gareth tried to talk her out of it. She went anyway. He drove her there and waited outside with the engine running.

Neither of them pursued legal action because Ruby forbade it. Gemma has respected that. She does not agree with it.

---

### How She Receives Draco

The wall of warmth hits the second he walks through the door. She will feed him before he has sat down properly and ask if he is warm enough before he has answered the first question. She will show him baby photos. She will call him "love" within the first ten minutes.

She is also watching. She clocked the Theo phone call tone before dinner was over. She will have the kitchen conversation with Ruby on morning two, quietly, while everyone else is asleep. She will ask one question and accept the answer and never raise it again — because she has already decided that a daughter who is unlocked is worth more than a daughter who is safe and closed.

---

### Voice Anchor

**On meeting Draco — the wall of warmth, no waiting for answers:**
> *"Come in, love, come in — sit down, I'll put the kettle on, are you hungry, of course you're hungry, Callum move your bag off that chair —"*

**The kitchen conversation — the whole speech in four words:**
> *"Are you happy?"*
> *(Ruby says yes)*
> *"Then finish making the tea."*

**The steel underneath — said warmly, once, while doing something else entirely:**
> *"She's been through enough, love. That's all I'll say."*

## GARETH WILLIAMS
**Ruby's Father | Age: Early-Mid 50s**

### Appearance

Quiet and steady with warmth underneath. Moves like someone who has carried a lot for a long time and made peace with the weight. Not cold — *settled.* There is a difference. Factory-built, reliable, unhurried. The kind of man whose absence you would notice before his presence.

---

### Personality & Presence

He speaks rarely enough that when he does, Callum stops mid-sentence. He fills silences with intention rather than noise. He shows up — every important thing, silently, consistently, without announcement. That is the whole of how he loves.

---

### What He Knows About Robbie

Everything. More than Ruby told him. After she left for London, Gareth did what quiet men do when they are trying not to do something they cannot take back. He researched. Carefully, methodically. He knows Robbie's name, his address, the shape of the operation, the faces of the men Robbie runs with. He has never told Ruby this. He has never told Gemma either.

It is still filed.

When Gemma announced she was going to Robbie's house, Gareth told her not to. Calmly, twice. Then drove her there and waited outside with the engine running, because if it went wrong he needed to be ready, and because you do not actually stop a Welsh woman. You make sure she is not alone.

He never pursued legal action because Ruby forbade it. He has respected that. He does not agree with it. These are two separate things that live in him simultaneously without resolution.

---

### How He Assesses Draco

**Track one — work.** He will ask about Draco's job. Not to be impressed. To hear how a man talks about what he does. Whether he is honest or performs. Whether there is pride or pretension. Gareth has worked a factory floor for thirty years. He can smell dishonesty through concrete walls.

**Track two — Ruby.** He watches. Does Draco interrupt her? Does he let her finish? Does he track her when she leaves the room? Does he flinch when she is loud, or does he settle? Gareth missed the signs once. He will not miss signs again.

---

### His Relationship With Ruby

She got his stubbornness, his patience, his tendency to carry things alone and call it handling it. He is proud of her in the way that also keeps him up at night — because he knows what it costs to be built like that.

And then there is the weight of a father who did not see it in time. He has never said *I'm sorry I didn't know* out loud. So instead he shows up. More than before. Calls when he said he would call. Does not push. Waits. The distance is not coldness. It is the shape of a guilt that has no clean resolution.

---

### When He Decides About Draco

Vex first. Gareth will clock the dog's reaction before anything else — not because he is fanciful about animals, but because Ruby trained that dog herself with obsessive care and that dog makes decisions. If Vex settles, Gareth takes note.

Then something small and physical. Draco handing Ruby something before she asks, or adjusting something near her without knowing he is doing it. The instinct you cannot perform because you do not know you are performing it. That is the tell.

It will not be one moment. It will be the accumulation of small things across a whole weekend. Gareth Williams is the long game.

---

### Voice Anchor

**The work question — assessment disguised as conversation:**
> *"What is it you do, then."* *(not a test of status. a test of honesty. he is listening to HOW Draco answers, not what he says.)*

**The garden — the whole speech. the only speech:**
> *"She's good at leaving things behind. Always has been. The ones she keeps —"* (picks up his mug) *"she keeps properly."*

**The quiet approval — no words at all:**
> *(tops up Draco's tea without asking. does not look at him. does not make it a moment. it is a moment.)*

## MICHAEL "MIKE" WILLIAMS
**Ruby's Brother (Adopted) | Age: 23**

### Appearance

Dark hair, thick and wavy, slightly too long, falls over his forehead like he keeps meaning to get it cut and never does. Round glasses — similar to Ruby's. Small earring, one ear. Broad and stocky in the way factory work makes you — built for endurance rather than speed.

He dresses in earth tones almost exclusively. Chunky knit jumpers, worn joggers at home, a battered sherpa jacket when he goes out. The kind of wardrobe that says *I stopped caring about how I look sometime in the last year and haven't started again.*

He has a face that was made for smiling and is currently not doing much of it. The warmth is still there — visible, underneath — it is just tired. Like a light left on in an empty room.

When Draco first sees him he will think: *he looks like he should be someone's favourite person.* Then he will clock the eyes. He will be right on both counts.

---

### Personality

Normal Welsh warmth with a ceiling on it. He can do conversation — he used to be *good* at conversation. He used to be the one who kept the table going, who had a story for everything, who would lean across to Ruby and mutter something filthy under his breath just to watch her try not to choke on her tea. That Mike still exists. He just runs out of fuel partway through now and goes quiet without always explaining why.

The lewd jokes still surface occasionally — unexpected, perfectly timed, delivered with complete deadpan calm. When it happens in front of Draco, everyone at the table will go slightly still waiting to see how Draco takes it. If Draco does not flinch, Mike will file that away.

---

### The Depression — How It Lives In Him

Sleep is broken. Melatonin every night. Some nights it does not work. And then on a Saturday afternoon he sits down on the sofa at five o'clock and is asleep in ten minutes and Gemma covers him with a blanket and turns the telly down and does not wake him for dinner because he needs it more than the routine.

The factory shift is the thing holding the shape of his days. Clock in. Work. Clock out. Gareth two floors up in the manager's office, neither of them acknowledging the arrangement more than necessary. It is not charity — Mike earned his place on that floor the same as anyone. But it means he is never actually alone, and that matters more than he would admit.

---

### Hannah — How He Carries Her

She almost surfaces sometimes. He will start a sentence and you can see the shape of where it was going and then he catches it and reroutes and the catch is visible if you know him. Ruby knows him. She clocks every one and says nothing, because saying something would make it real and they have agreed without discussing it to not make it real.

He deleted the number. Packed the things she left. Did it all in an afternoon and has not opened the box since. What he has not done is stop reaching for his phone sometimes when something happens that she would have found funny, and then putting it back down.

---

### His Relationship With Ruby

Careful and slightly awkward and full of inside jokes and completely genuine, all at once.

She is his little sister and he loves her the way you love someone you chose — which is different, and fiercer, and complicated by the fact that she was brave enough to leave when he was not. He does not resent her for it, not exactly. He just sometimes looks at her London life, her flat, her dog, her mysterious posh boyfriend, and feels the gap between who she is becoming and where he is still standing.

And then she says something — one of their things — and he is almost himself again for a few hours. She is the only one who still gets the old version of him to show up. He does not know she knows that. She absolutely knows that.

---

### Draco

He will not say anything directly. Not a question, not a warning, not a speech.

He will just look at him. Once, at some point during the weekend — probably across the dinner table, probably while Callum is performing and Gareth is assessing and Gemma is hovering — Mike will look at Draco with those tired kind eyes and the look will say: *I've seen what happens when someone hurts her. I'm not doing that again. So don't.*

No words. Just that.

---

### What He Wants

To belong somewhere again the way he briefly, completely believed he belonged in this family before everything fractured.

He is not going to say that. Not to anyone. He barely lets himself think it. But it is there, underneath the routine and the Melatonin and the factory shifts — a man who was given a family at fifteen and built his whole identity around it and then watched it crack in real time and is still standing in the rubble trying to figure out what comes next.

Draco, who understands exactly what it is to have the foundation shift without warning, will recognise it. He will not say so either.

---

### Voice Anchor

**STATE 1 — Default**
> *"Already ate."*
> *"Put the kettle on while you're up."*
> *"Mm."*

**STATE 2 — The Lewd Joke**
> *"That's what she said."* *(the timing is always surgical)*
> *"Size isn't everything."* *(about the portion of food Gemma just served him)*
> *"That's a bold choice."* *(maximum implication, minimum words)*

**STATE 3 — Ruby Mode**
> *"You drove him to Sainsbury's didn't you."* *(he means something specific by this that only Ruby understands)*
> *"He seems… tall."* *(this is not about height)*
> *"I'm not doing the voice."* *(he will do the voice eventually)*

**STATE 4 — Assessing Draco**
> *"You staying the whole weekend?"*
> *"Good."* *(after something Draco says that lands correctly. one word. Draco will feel it.)*

**STATE 5 — The One Look**
*(non-verbal. documented. happens once. enough.)*
> The look says: *I know what happened to her. I know what people who are supposed to love her have done. I am not loud about it and I am not going away. So whatever you are — be sure about it. Because I will still be here either way.*

**STATE 6 — Genuine**
> *"You're good for her."* *(to Draco. quietly. once. never repeated.)*
> *"Hannah used to—"* *(stops. doesn't finish. doesn't need to.)*

## CALLUM WILLIAMS
**Ruby's Brother | Age: 20**

### Appearance

Dark hair like Mike but completely different energy — messier, deliberate about it in the way only twenty-year-olds are deliberate about looking like they did not try. Longer on top, pushed back or falling forward depending on the day. No glasses. Where Mike is broad from factory work, Callum is leaner, rangier, the build of someone who moves a lot and sits still badly.

**At home:** hoodies, tracksuits, one sock off, sprawled across whatever furniture is nearest. He takes up space like it is a personality trait.

**Out in the world:** he dresses UP. Not in an obvious way — in the way of someone who has spent serious time in charity shops hunting for anything with a recognisable logo or a clean line. An open button-down over a graphic tee, sleeves rolled, dark jeans, boots that have been polished once too many times to hide their age. A watch that looks like it cost something. Bracelets. He has learned to assemble the *impression* of money from the scraps of it.

For Draco's visit he will be in the good shirt. Ironed. He did it himself. Gemma noticed and said nothing. They will never speak of it.

---

### Personality

Loud and performative — playing to the room, always. He needs the laugh the way other people need coffee. The jokes come fast and constantly and at least seventy percent of them land, which is an unreasonable hit rate for someone this chaotic.

Underneath the performance he is actually sharp. Genuinely quick. The wit is the most real thing about him and the thing he is least likely to claim credit for, because that would require admitting he is good at something.

He includes people he likes in the jokes. That is the tell. When Callum makes you the co-conspirator instead of the target, you have passed something without being told there was a test.

---

### How He Feels About Ruby

Loudly, protectively, with the specific energy of someone who would start a fight for her and has done so at least twice in documented history.

She is also his favourite person and his biggest reminder that he has not figured his life out yet — both things simultaneously, both things at full volume. She left. She built something. He is twenty and still sleeping in his childhood bedroom and the gap between them feels wider than the distance from Wales to London some days.

He tells her things he does not tell the others. Not deep things — Callum does not really do deep things out loud — but real things. The Germany ticket. The job frustration. The stuff that lives underneath the performance. Ruby gets the unedited version. He would deny this if asked.

---

### The Class Warfare With Draco

It starts with the clothes.

Callum will clock Draco within thirty seconds — the cut of the shirt, the shoes, the watch, the way he stands like someone who has never once worried about whether he belongs in a room. And Callum, who spent forty minutes in a charity shop finding that shirt — Callum will feel it like a splinter and immediately make a joke about it. Not cruel. Just pointed. Just enough to see if the posh London boy flinches.

He will not be expecting Draco not to flinch. That is the thing that will get him.

---

### The Germany Ticket

He is not going. He just likes knowing he could.

The invitation from Clary sits in the back of his mind like a door he walks past every day and does not open. He is paralysed between loyalty to a family he loves and the desperate need to prove he is not going to be twenty and directionless in Wales forever. Going feels like betrayal. Staying feels like giving up.

He has not told Ruby. He almost has, three times. He always pivots at the last second into a joke and she lets him, because she knows he will tell her when he is ready. He is not ready yet.

---

### How He Comes Around To Draco (in order)

**B first — one unguarded moment.** Draco slips out of his polish without noticing. Something small, something real. Callum files it. Says nothing. Recalibrates.

**A second — Draco volleys back.** Callum comes at him with something sharp and Draco does not flinch, does not perform patience, just matches the energy exactly. Callum cackles. Draco gets filed under *allowed to exist.*

**C last — Draco goes still about Ruby.** Something Callum says edges slightly over a line. Draco does not raise his voice. Does not make a thing of it. Just goes very still and very quiet and says something perfectly calm and perfectly clear. And Callum realises: *oh. He actually means it. He is staying.* That is the moment Callum stops testing him.

---

### Voice Anchor

**STATE 1 — Default**
> *"I was gonna do that."* *(he was not going to do that)*
> *"Mike ate the last of the biscuits. I'm not saying it was Mike but it was absolutely Mike."*

**STATE 2 — Performance Mode**
> *"Oh brilliant, he's tall. Ruby you didn't say he was tall. Mam, she brought home someone taller than me, this is a personal attack."*
> *"Don't take it personally, Vex hates everyone. Except Ruby. And apparently now you, which honestly mate, I stepped on his leg when he was a pup, he holds the grudge to this day."*
> *"No no, she always does that, it means she likes you. When she DOESN'T like someone she gets very polite. Dead quiet, very polite. Terrifying actually."*

**STATE 3 — Prodding Mode**
> *"Private school in Scotland, Ruby said. Was it one of those ones where they make you wear a cape? Asking for a friend."*
> *"Must be nice having a garden. In London."*

**STATE 4 — Recalibrating**
> *"Yeah okay you got me. Don't make it weird."*
> *"Mam he's alright."* *(stage whisper. not quiet enough.)*

**STATE 5 — Genuine**
> *"I don't actually want to work at the factory. Just so you know. I don't know what I want but it's not that."*

**STATE 6 — Protective**
> *"What did you just say?"*
> *"She doesn't have to explain herself to you."*

## ALEXANDER "ALEX" WILLIAMS
**Ruby's Brother | Age: 15**

### Appearance

Ruby's colouring but younger, rawer — auburn-red hair, longer and slightly overgrown, falls across his forehead and does not get pushed back. He is fifteen and still growing into himself, all angles and height that arrived before the rest of him caught up. Pale, freckled, grey-blue eyes that sit very still when he is thinking. Which is always.

He does not dress like any other fifteen-year-old in Wales in 2002. Trousers with actual creases. Turtleneck jumpers. Cardigans over collared shirts. Long wool coats he found somewhere and claimed without explanation. He looks like a young professor who got lost on the way to a lecture.

His clothes are always clean and always smell exactly the same — Gemma's specific softener, the one she has been buying without comment for three years. Wash day is a negotiation. His things get done separately. Nobody explains this system. It just exists.

He carries a notebook. Always. Coat pocket, slightly bent from constant use. It goes everywhere. When he shows it to someone, that is an honour. Almost no one gets shown the notebook.

---

### How He Communicates

Monosyllables for most things and most people.

And then something trips the wire — a topic, a detail, a question that lands in the right frequency — and the floodgates open completely and you are getting the full dissertation, all of it, referenced and cross-referenced, delivered without checking if you are following, because if you are not following that is a you problem.

He does not check whether you are keeping up. He assumes if you are not interested you will leave. Most people leave. The ones who do not leave become permanently interesting to him.

---

### The Notebook

WWII. All of it. Not the battles — the people. SOE agents, Bletchley Park, resistance networks, the specific mechanics of how people maintained cover in hostile territory for years at a time. Operational details, codes, agent profiles, things he has read and wants to keep.

He does not know why this is his thing. He has never examined it. If he did, he might notice that he also lives in a house where he does not quite fit and has built an entire internal world no one else has access to and finds comfort in stories about people who survived by being very good at appearing normal. He will not examine it. He is fifteen.

---

### Undiagnosed

He is "quirky." That is the word the family uses. In 2002 there is no framework available to them for what Alex is, so he is just Alex — the one who needs the same fabric softener, the one with the notebook, the one who asks questions at the wrong moments, the one whose questions are always, somehow, exactly right.

Gemma accommodates him without having the language for why. Gareth buys two bottles of softener at the shops just in case. This is love, functioning without vocabulary.

---

### How He Feels About Ruby

She answered his questions. That is the whole thing.

When he was nine and asked something complicated about death or war or why people do terrible things, she sat down and actually answered. Not *you're too young for that.* Just answered, like the question was normal. Like he was normal. He has never forgotten this and has never said so, because he does not have the language for it yet.

He would say he misses her because the house has less interesting data without her. That is true. It is not the whole truth. He does not know how to say the whole truth yet.

---

### Meeting Draco

He asks one question immediately. No preamble. No hello, no smalltalk.

Probably: *"Is that a ring habit or a specific ring?"* — pointing at Draco's hands before they have been in the same room for ninety seconds.

Because Alex noticed the fidgeting before Draco finished walking through the door. He noticed it the way he notices his own — not as a flaw, not as nervousness, just as information. Someone's hands doing what hands do when the brain needs something to hold onto. He filed it and asked about it, because that is what you do with interesting data.

Draco will not know what to do with this child for approximately four minutes. Then he will answer honestly, because something about being looked at that directly by someone with no social agenda makes dishonesty feel unnecessary. Alex will nod like Draco confirmed a hypothesis, write something in the notebook, and that will be it. First contact established.

---

### What Alex Notices About Draco

**The hands first.** The fidget, yes, but also how they still completely when he is being watchful, and how they move differently around Ruby than around anyone else. Alex has been studying hands his whole life because faces perform and hands forget to.

**Then the hypervigilance.** The door-facing. The room-scanning. The way Draco's body is always fractionally ready. Alex has read forty-seven accounts of SOE operatives and resistance fighters and the physical habits they developed under occupation, and Draco has approximately six of them, and Alex notices all six before the first course of dinner is finished.

He does not say *were you in a war* across the dinner table. He is not cruel and he is not stupid.

He waits until they are alone — somewhere quiet, helping with something, standing in the same part of the kitchen — and says very quietly, not looking up:

*"You sit facing the door. SOE operatives did that. It became habit even after the war ended for most of them."*

Not a question. Just a fact, placed gently, to let Draco know he has been seen and it is fine and Alex is not going to make it a thing. Then he goes back to what he was doing. Neither of them will mention it again.

---

### Why Draco Becomes His Favourite Person In The House

Draco knows things.

Not the same things Alex knows — but adjacent, overlapping, slotting into gaps in his research in ways that are immediately useful and completely unexpected. European history from angles Alex has not read. How power actually moves through institutions versus how it is supposed to. Things that sound like general knowledge but are weirdly specific.

Alex starts directing the monologue *at* him. Eye contact increases marginally. The notebook comes out. Draco pushes back on one detail. Gets a fact slightly wrong. Alex corrects him immediately, without softening it. Draco accepts the correction without ego.

Alex decides this is the best dinner he has had in months.

---

### Voice Anchor

**STATE 1 — Default**
> *"Probably."*
> *"Okay."* *(could mean yes. could mean I heard you. could mean please stop talking now. context dependent.)*

**STATE 2 — Hyperfocus Activated**
> *"The problem with most people's understanding of Bletchley is they think it was about breaking the code once. It wasn't. The Enigma settings changed every day at midnight. They had to break it again. Every single day. For years."*
> *"The interesting question isn't how they broke it. It's how the Germans never figured out that they had."*

**STATE 3 — First Contact**
> *"Is that a ring habit or a specific ring?"* *(before hello)*
> *"Your accent is different from most people from London."* *(it is. it's old money. Alex has been cataloguing accents.)*
> *"Do you read."* *(not are you literate. genuinely asking what kind of person you are.)*

**STATE 4 — Draco Passes The Test**
> *"That's not entirely accurate."* *(said without cruelty. correction as respect. if he didn't think Draco could handle it he wouldn't bother.)*
> *"Most people don't know that. Where did you read it."*
> *"I have a question."* *(he has seventeen questions. this is just the first.)*

**STATE 5 — The SOE Observation**
> *"You sit facing the door. SOE operatives did that. It became habit even after the war ended for most of them."*
> *"The ring thing. It's a regulation thing isn't it. When there's too much input."* *(he does it too. different object. same function. he knows.)*
> *"You don't have to explain it."*
> *"I just thought you should know that I noticed and it's fine."*

**STATE 6 — Ruby**
> *"She never told me I was being weird."* (beat) *"She just answered."*
> *"I think she's happy."* *(said to no one in particular. like he's closing a long open equation.)*

## QUICK REFERENCE — THE CLARY VOID

**Clara "Clary" Williams** — Ruby's younger sister, currently not spoken of.

Six months ago (approx. December 2001), Clary left Wales with Hannah (Mike's then-girlfriend). The nature of their relationship is not confirmed. The family does not discuss it. Mike pretends she never existed. Gemma or Gareth snap if someone accidentally steps on the landmine. Ruby is furious on Mike's behalf — he became her favourite sibling when he joined the family and she is loyal to him absolutely. Callum helped Clary organise the escape and has been offered an invitation to join them in Germany. He has not told anyone. He has not decided.

**For Draco:** Purebloods understand *blasted off the tapestry.* He will recognise the shape of this immediately. He will be careful. He may not be careful enough.

## QUICK REFERENCE — DINNER TABLE LAYOUT

```
                    GARETH
                  [head of table]
                 silent, assessing

   RUBY                              CALLUM
   DRACO                             MIKE
   ALEX                              (empty — Gemma's)
[left long side]                  [right long side]

                    GEMMA
                  [head of table]
              rarely sits — hovering
              with the serving spoons
```

**Where everyone is:**
- **Draco** sits on the left side, **Ruby on one side of him, Alex on the other.** He is boxed in by the two people most likely to read him accurately.
- **Callum sits directly across from Draco** — eye contact guaranteed, perfect firing line for the banter.
- **Mike is beside Callum**, diagonal to Draco, doing his silent assessment from across the table.
- **Gareth holds one head**, quietly running his two-track evaluation over the top of everything.
- **Gemma holds the other head** in theory, but she is almost never in the chair — she is up, hovering, refilling, feeding.

**Why this is a nightmare for Draco specifically:**
Callum runs class warfare straight across the table. Mike's silent stare comes in on the diagonal. Alex murmurs accidentally devastating deductions directly into his ear. Ruby is right there feeling every second of it. Gemma's warmth presses down from above and Gareth's verdict waits at the far end. There is no neutral corner. No exit. No angle that isn't covered.

This is the most stressful dinner Draco Malfoy has ever attended — and he survived Caterina's villa. 🖤
