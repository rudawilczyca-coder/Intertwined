# Ruby's Friend Group — Lake Day / Campfire Scene

**Setting:** June 2002, heatwave weekend. A cottage property in Kent with lake access, owned by Meg's parents.

**Saturday logistics:** Draco and Ruby are in Marylebone until ~3pm (Draco reserved a room with a piano to play for her). Liam & Sophie pick them up on their way down — Sophie works Saturday mornings as a junior solicitor, Liam collects her, they swing through Marylebone around 4–5pm. Most of the group is already at the cottage by then (drove down in the morning). Saturday evening: swimming, barbecue, campfire. They stay the night.

**Sunday:** Full day at the property. Drive back Sunday evening.

---

## The Core Friend (Host)

### Meg Harlow — 23, veterinary nurse

Ruby's closest London friend. They met at Vex's puppy vaccination appointment — Meg was the vet nurse who held Vex still for his first jab and said "Oh, you're going to be a handful, aren't you, gorgeous?" Ruby came back for every appointment after that. They started grabbing coffee post-checkups, then it became a standing thing.

Meg is warm, loud, and completely incapable of not mothering everyone around her. She's the friend who texts "have you eaten today?" and means it. Drives a battered Vauxhall Corsa with dog hair permanently embedded in the seats.

**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered and sturdy, short dark hair she's always pushing behind her ears, nose ring, freckled arms.

**Her dog:** Bonnie — three-year-old Border Collie, absolute lunatic, Vex's best friend. They met at the vet and it was instant chaos. Bonnie is why Ruby keeps saying yes to group things — Vex needs the socialization and Bonnie tires him out.

**The property:** Her parents' place in Kent — a converted farmhouse near Sevenoaks with a big garden that backs onto a small private lake (really more of a large pond, but "lake" sounds better). Her parents use it as a weekend retreat from their Bromley semi. There's a fire pit, a rickety dock, and the water is clean enough for swimming if you don't think too hard about it.

**Draco friction:** Meg will absolutely interrogate him. Not aggressively — warmly. She'll hand him a beer, sit next to him, and ask seventeen questions in four minutes because she's genuinely invested in whether this man is good enough for Ruby. She already knows about the abusive ex (Ruby told her after the third coffee). She's protective.

## The Couples

### Jess Kowalski & Adam — both 22

Jess is Ruby's other regular contact — they met through Meg (Jess is Meg's childhood friend from Bromley, so Ruby was folded in by proximity). She works in retail management at a Topshop on Oxford Street. Blunt, funny, competitive, the one who suggests drinking games and then wins them. Polish-English, switches between languages when drunk or frustrated.

**Appearance:** Dark blonde hair, sharp eyeliner, always in something slightly too fashionable for the occasion.

**Adam** is Jess's boyfriend of two years. Electrician. Easygoing, a bit laddish but not obnoxious. He's the one who'll throw a ball for Vex without asking (Meg will tell him off). Calls everyone "mate" including people he's just met. Will try to talk to Draco about football and hit a wall immediately.

**Appearance:** Stocky, buzzcut, sunburn-prone, permanently in a football shirt and cargo shorts.

**Draco friction:** Adam will ask Draco what team he supports. This is a landmine. Jess will ask what he does for work and not accept "government" as a full answer. She'll push — not meanly, just because she's nosy and blunt. She'll also clock that Draco's clothes are too expensive for "government work" and mention it to Meg later.

### Liam & Sophie — 24 and 23

Liam is Meg's older brother. Quiet, dry sense of humour, teaches secondary school history. The one who brings a guitar to the campfire and is actually good at it. He's been dating Sophie for about a year.

**Appearance:** Tall and lean, same dark hair as Meg but longer and messier, wire-rimmed glasses, looks like he should be in a folk band.

**Sophie** is a junior solicitor, a bit posh by the group's standards, still finding her footing with the others. Reads on a blanket while everyone else swims.

**Appearance:** Brown hair in a low ponytail, delicate features, the only person who brought actual sunscreen and reapplies it on schedule.

**Travel note:** Liam and Sophie are the ones who pick up Ruby and Draco from Marylebone on Saturday afternoon and drive them down to Kent. This gives Draco a softer introduction — a car ride with the quietest, most book-inclined member of the group before facing everyone else.

**Draco friction:** Liam is the person Draco might actually get along with. He's well-read, doesn't push, comfortable with silence. If they end up sitting near each other at the fire, they could have a genuine conversation about history or literature. Sophie might clock Draco's mannerisms as old-money and feel a quiet kinship — or quiet suspicion.

## The Singles

### Danny Tsang — 22

Meg's coworker at the vet clinic. Cheerful, relentlessly social, the group's unofficial photographer. Always has a disposable camera. Gay and out — casually mentions his ex-boyfriend in conversation, nobody blinks. He's the one who'll suggest swimming first and strip down to his shorts before anyone else has found the towels. Hong Kong-British, grew up in Croydon.

**Appearance:** Compact and wiry, black hair with bleached tips, a silver hoop earring, grin that arrives before he does.

**Draco friction:** Danny will take a photo of Draco without asking and Draco will have a brief internal crisis about magical evidence. Danny will also be the first to notice if Draco seems uncomfortable and will casually redirect — he's emotionally perceptive under the extroversion. Might make a comment about Draco's cheekbones that Ruby finds hilarious.

### Priya Anand — 21

The newest addition to the group — Jess's coworker at Topshop. Quiet, observant, a little shy in group settings but sharp when she does speak. First-generation British-Indian, lives with her parents in Ealing and is saving to move out. She and Ruby bonded at the last barbecue over both being the quiet ones at the edge of the group. They exchanged numbers but mostly communicate through memes.

**Appearance:** Long dark hair usually in a braid, warm brown skin, small gold studs in both ears, wears oversized band t-shirts she's stolen from an older cousin.

**Draco friction:** Minimal direct friction, but she'll be watching. She's the one who'll notice things — that Draco doesn't know how to use a bottle opener, that he holds his drink oddly, that he looked genuinely confused when someone mentioned EastEnders. She won't say anything at the gathering, but she might text Ruby later: "your boyfriend is fit but is he from another planet?"

## Group Dynamic Notes

- **Ruby's position:** She's the quiet one who mostly comes for Vex and Meg. The group likes her but doesn't fully *know* her — she keeps things surface-level. Bringing Draco is a bigger deal than she's letting on.
- **The barbecue precedent:** Ruby came to one previous gathering at this property (late spring, before she met Draco). She sat with Priya, threw a ball for Vex and Bonnie, ate two burgers, and left before the campfire. This time she's staying.
- **Draco's cover story pressure points:** Work (government/classified), school (obscure Scottish boarding school), how he and Ruby met (dating app — fine), his other relationships (he will NOT mention Jackie and Theo to this group), why he doesn't have a phone... wait, he DOES have a phone now. Jackie gave him one. But he's still bad at it.
- **Campfire dynamics:** Guitar (Liam), drinking games (Jess), photography (Danny), quiet conversation (Sophie, Priya), dog management (Meg), and Draco trying to figure out how to toast a marshmallow without magic.
