# The Honeymoon They Owed

## Tuesday, August 6, 2002

- Before the 09:23 Eurostar, Draco checks the tickets repeatedly while Theo teases him about disguising travel anxiety as logistics. Draco has booked the Pissarro Suite at the Hotel du Louvre; Theo stays behind at Haven House with instructions to feed Luna, manage the blood supply, let Malice run Friday, and take the dusk Portkey to Fontainebleau on Saturday. Jackie tells him the prepared Polyjuice and Eli Lavigne's supplied hair are together in the office desk's second drawer, fixing Eli as his cover for the Final.
- Jackie and Draco leave by Muggle cab and train. On the journey they agree not to impose a schedule, discuss Draco’s former weekends with Ruby, and trade affectionate jealousy over Jackie’s short-lived Beauxbatons “relationship” with an unnamed blond boy. Draco admits his old crush on Samaya, acknowledges the hurt he helped cause, and leaves any meeting with Samaya to Jackie’s choice.
- Jackie sleeps through the Channel crossing and arrival in Paris. Draco carries her from Gare du Nord through the car and hotel to the Pissarro Suite, a high corner room overlooking the Opéra Garnier and Louvre, then lets her sleep another forty-three minutes.
- At *Au Coq de Cuivre*, Draco arranges Jackie’s preferred coq au vin despite their arrival between meal services. Conversation about Narcissa’s love and control opens the subject of children: Draco says he wants children with Jackie for himself, not because of Narcissa’s three-year heir deadline, and asks what Jackie wants. Jackie says she is not discussing it over lunch but promises to return to it that night; she tells him he would be a wonderful, indulgent father.
- They walk toward the Louvre through Paris, joking about Pigalle and agreeing Theo would be insufferable company there. In the museum’s Delacroix gallery, Draco gives Jackie space to sit with a cavalry painting, then tells her a smaller portrait reminded him of the private expression he used to watch across the Hogwarts Great Hall. When she recounts Pansy’s school cruelties, he admits he should have stopped them and that being fourteen explains, but does not excuse, his inaction.
- Jackie asks a young École des Beaux-Arts artist, Luc Moreau, to draw them. Luc follows them to the suite, rejects a formal pose, and sketches the way Draco watches Jackie. His teasing exposes Draco’s jealousy; the finished graphite portrait captures their long-established intimacy rather than hotel-room grandeur.
- Jackie tells Draco she loves and forgives him for the times he watched instead of acting and for his earlier cruelties. Draco accepts the forgiveness without denying the harm, says he loves her, and credits her with forcing him to become someone better.
- Jackie proposes that Luc draw Draco nude the following evening as a present to stash until Theo's November birthday. Draco does not refuse outright: he sets terms of explicit permission, Jackie's presence, no retained studies or reproductions without their consent, and the finished portrait going to Theo only after both spouses have seen it. He admits the exposure—and Jackie watching it—is arousing precisely because it makes his scars and history legible.
- After Luc leaves, Draco admits he shaved on Sunday because he wanted to feel new for Jackie and wanted rare privacy away from Theo, the Malice, crises, and constant interruption. Jackie recalls their first night, says she wants Draco to touch her as his only husband tonight, and tells him that one day she wants to carry his child. Draco rejects the idea that he ever merely endured their marriage, kneels, and asks her to lead.
- Their first private honeymoon intimacy moves from the vanity to the bed: Jackie gives clear instructions, then stops orchestrating her own responses and lets herself move freely; Draco shifts from careful, cataloguing control into openly forceful, trusting desire while holding her securely. Afterward, Jackie confesses jealousy and guilt over how she treated Ruby. Draco distinguishes her fear-driven cruelty from his own past decision to leave rather than remain complicit in Theo’s assault, tells her the apology is still owed to Ruby, and refuses to let guilt take the night.
- Jackie offers, if Ruby returns, to ask whether Ruby would share Draco for a night, and says she wants to hold him through being seen and loved by someone else. Draco makes her examine whether that offer is desire or self-punishment, then says Ruby is not coming back and asks Jackie not to build a future around that possibility; she is already the person who can dismantle his fear of not being enough.
- In bed, Jackie makes Draco revisit the three France sentences: “I deserve good things,” “I’m allowed to want,” and “I know how to hold something without breaking it.” He says the first two under pressure, then demonstrates the third through the way he holds and protects her. He later says the sentence is finally a fact rather than a prayer he does not believe.
- Afterward, Draco confirms that his love and desire for Jackie are real, recalls choosing her in both the October 1999 and April 2002 weddings, and jokes that he would marry her a third time to establish a pattern. Their second coq au vin arrives; Jackie answers the door naked while Draco appears in a bedsheet, and the hotel staff remain professionally unimpressed.
- Draco reheats the delivered meal with a precise warming charm he developed for Theo’s blood supply, then dresses for the night. Jackie changes into a black dress and corset with daggers, and they walk east after noting tomorrow’s catacombs visit and her proposed Thursday meeting with Samaya in the Bois de Boulogne.
- At Rue Chapon, they find the unmarked third-floor crêperie hidden inside Solange’s crowded apartment. Aurélien Vasseur, a Paris Gringotts curse-breaker who remembers the 2001 Luminary, recognizes them and warns that people from that crowd will scrutinize them at Saturday’s World Cup Final. Jackie stops him from discussing Theo’s death and redirects the conversation to Sylvie Roux, a Muggle paper conservator who has lived openly with Vasseur for eleven years.
- Sylvie explains that French magical secrecy uses a registered wizard as collateral rather than Britain’s memory charms. Jackie offers her three unusual old books for discreet conservation; Sylvie is interested, while Vasseur warns that sending them through the Lavigne raven network would itself be noticed. Jackie says she will send them by raven because none are illegal, and goes to help Naïs make galettes.
- Vasseur describes Saturday’s perimeter: Gringotts raises four outer rings from Thursday midnight, the French Ministry handles the gates, and anything crossing the third ring is read. Draco also recognizes Sylvie’s test for genuine old wizarding books—paper that remains warm like a sun-heated stone—and quietly connects it to an unexplored book in a Swansea attic, choosing not to pursue that lead tonight.
- Solange makes Jackie work the billig; Naïs teaches her to turn the rake from the wrist, and Jackie’s first, burnt galette is served to Draco. He eats the ruined one rather than complain, tells Jackie that nothing important missed her, and is handed her cardigan as Solange closes the apartment. The final played beat leaves them at the table with Jackie’s banana, whipped-cream, and Nutella crêpe before her while Draco prepares to take her down the unrailed stairs and postpones Saturday’s problems until Friday.

## Wednesday, August 7, 2002

- During his nightly London walk, Theo is met by Viola's unnamed Seneschal and given a formal invitation to court. He goes voluntarily because refusal would turn Haven House and everyone inside it into the site of Viola's next approach.
- Viola confronts him over seven months in London without presentation: Dante's Rome assessment established control but carried no authority in her Domain; Filippo expected Theo's return to be temporary and then delayed; Ferrara quietly banked three Primogen debts around the eventual presentation. Viola offers a consenting court donor partly to observe whether Theo or the Malice reacts. Theo refuses because he will not feed from a person placed before him as a test.
- After a civil interrogation, Viola orders Theo held while she determines the conditions of presentation. Theo refuses and attempts to leave; prepared court wards stop him and draw the Malice forward. Viola calls Theo an unreleased, unpresented “guest” while making clear that he cannot leave.

- The bond direction that transferred to the Malice when Theo died had remained dormant: He had neither needed nor knowingly tried to reach Jackie through it. During the taking, He reaches for her for the first time. Sleeping Jackie does not recognise the contact as Theo; her mind translates it into the near-extinct January 6 nightmare.
- At dawn Draco wakes to an empty bed. In the Pissarro Suite's main room, the table and sofa have been pushed aside and Jackie is doing push-ups in the cleared space, eyes puffed and her face holding grief, anger, and focus. Draco does not know about the reach or the taking. He sets water within reach, sits outside the cleared space, and offers presence without demanding an explanation.
- **Itinerary ahead:** near midday, an ordinary Muggle catacombs tour followed by a specialist guide into the cursed/magical area; Luc Moreau's second sitting at **20:00**.

## Thursday, August 8, 2002

- Samaya's precisely courteous note sets the meeting for 13:00 beside the lower lake at the Bois de Boulogne, with Pierre and six-month-old Edmund attending. Draco brings Jackie Earl Grey and half a croissant in bed, names the concealed Theo problem beneath the visit, and leaves her free to remain horizontal.
- Jackie climbs over Draco without settling onto his lap, scatters croissant flakes across his robe, and dares him to explain how he means to charm Samaya. Draco says his civility will be merciless, admits Samaya ended their adolescent relationship because he kept asking about Jackie, and places both hands lightly at Jackie's waist without closing the distance she left.
- Jackie forces Draco's ambiguous hip movement into the open, settles fully onto his lap, parts his robe, kisses his navel, and asks whether this is what he wanted. Draco admits the hot-tea timing was misjudged but repeats the movement deliberately, says he wanted her to stop threatening him from a safe distance, invokes Samaya's demand for punctuality against his own murder, and challenges Mrs Nott-Malfoy to proceed.
- Jackie praises Draco while kissing from his navel to his jaw, removes his hands, and orders them onto the headboard—the exact instruction Ruby used during their first sex. The words briefly carry Ruby's voice and grief, but Draco neither pursues nor rejects the memory: he remains present with Jackie, obeys, holds himself still by choice, and asks her to say whether stillness is what she wants.
- Jackie answers by kissing Draco gently, unveiling his erection with excruciating slowness, looking without touching, and abruptly choosing scrambled eggs, toast, and a shower. Draco concedes the contest with one disbelieving laugh, orders breakfast in immaculate French, folds his robe despite remaining openly hard, and stops naked at the bathroom threshold to announce that delivery in twenty minutes leaves them nineteen.
- Jackie enters the glass shower, looks Draco over without concealment, and invites him to join while soaping a sponge. Draco steps under the spray, closes the glass door, comes close without touching, offers one open hand for the sponge, and asks whether she means to wash him or test whether frustration can kill a man standing in cold water. He reminds her that breakfast is coming and accuses her of cheating.
- Jackie washes Draco while avoiding the touch they both want, then remembers asking him to wash her at Nott Manor after her month in St Mungo's. Draco says that trusting him with the request was already the thank-you, washes her with the same deliberate restraint, and tells her she was never difficult to want: she was hurt, and making his hands safe mattered more than desire. He warns that three years of practice plus fourteen remaining minutes make her experiment methodologically flawed.
- Jackie's attempted pivot against the shower wall becomes a fall on soapy tile. Her elbow strikes Draco's left eye while her hip and folded left leg take the landing. They laugh; Draco concedes to the floor, checks whether she can move her toes, and calls the arriving room service divine intervention. Jackie retrieves her wand to treat his eye. Breakfast, healing and preparations are compressed.
- At 12:57 beside the lower lake, Draco's cut has closed and swelling is reduced, but a dark violet shadow remains. Samaya maintains her bright social surface beside Pierre and six-month-old Edmund, immediately notices the bruise, and joins Draco in refusing to explain the shower accident. She turns the pram toward Jackie and invites her to meet her nephew for the first time.
- The four-hour meeting closes with Jackie and Samaya reconciling, Christmas agreed at Samaya and Pierre's home, and Jackie named as Edmund's aunt. On the taxi ride back to the Hôtel du Louvre, Jackie places Draco's hand beneath her dress and asks him to touch her slowly and make her beg. Their familiar contested-control dynamic sharpens: Draco moves from teasing through her underwear to direct touch and counts her earlier broken requests against her. Jackie refuses to fold immediately, asks for only "a little," coaxes his finger deeper, and retaliates by calling the Malfoy heir depraved for fingering his wife in a Muggle cab. The praise hits Draco hard without making him careless; with roughly six minutes remaining he grants exactly a little, controlled and slow, then withdraws until only the tip remains and orders her to finish the sentence "I need..." He promises that whatever she refuses to ask for in the taxi she will ask for upstairs.
- Jackie stops the taxi intimacy before release, removes her lace, and puts it in Draco's right pocket. She orders him to follow ten paces behind, count every man who watches her cross the Hôtel du Louvre foyer, and withhold touch until she asks whether he remembered them all. Draco counts six: the driver, doorman, two seated men, the caller with the suitcase, and Jacques at the concierge desk. Jackie prolongs the test with Jacques, booking an intimate table for two at 20:00 and arranging champagne, oysters, and smoked-salmon blinis for the Pissarro Suite in one hour while trading deliberately suggestive lines with him. Draco hears only fragments, does not count an uninvolved man who joins the queue, and remains beside the column until Jackie leaves. Jacques then notices Draco and understands the exchange was witnessed. Draco restores the ten-pace distance, follows Jackie to the lifts without touching her, holds the open door with his left hand while his right stays around her lace, and says, "After you, Mrs Nott-Malfoy."
