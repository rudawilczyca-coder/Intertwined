# The Week Between — Session Summary
## January 28 – February 2, 2002
## Haven House, 145 Berkeley Place, London

---

### Monday, January 28th

- Jackie leaves for the US trip at approximately 2 AM. Theo listens from the fourth floor but chooses not to listen to her goodbye with Draco. Draco walks her out, then retreats to his own room.
- Theo feeds from Jackie's labeled blood bag supply in the cellar.
- Theo explores the house alone for the first time. Discovers Jackie's shopping lists, the knife block positioned for a left-hander, and her annotated systems throughout the kitchen.
- Enters Jackie's bedroom. Finds her wedding ring and chain left on the dresser (too risky to travel with, given the band and Eli). Sees Samuel Carrow's poster on the back of her door. Leaves without lingering.
- Spends the afternoon in the library, in Jackie's armchair, wrapped in her Nott Manor blanket. Luna (black-furred Kneazle, bobcat-sized, golden eyes) settles on his chest where his heartbeat should be.
- Draco comes home from work at 6:12 PM. Makes tea. Tells Theo he's written to Pansy and Blaise inviting them for dinner Tuesday evening, with a postscript warning there's something to discuss in person.
- First evening alone together. Tentative, careful. Both adjusting to the two-person configuration.

### Tuesday, January 29th

- Draco leaves for work. Theo trains in Jackie's gym in the cellar — heavy bag, practice blades, quarterstaff. Practices slowing to human speed deliberately.
- At dusk, Theo goes into the garden for the first time. The Malice pushes to run, to hunt — "I want to MOVE, Theo." Theo refuses. The Malice states "Tomorrow" as intent, not request.
- Draco comes home early with wine. Prepares for the Pansy/Blaise dinner. Caramel (house-elf) handles the meal.
- **Evening: Pansy and Blaise dinner** (events as per existing session summary — the reveal, Pansy's vindication, Blaise's Depulso, the July truth surfacing).
- **Aftermath (late evening):** Draco processes at the front door. Confronts Theo about Jackie and Samuel — Blaise observed Samuel touching Jackie's arm at the Soirée. Theo reveals the full July truth: Jackie went to Samuel's flat, was nearly naked, it almost became everything. She stopped it. Came home. Told Theo the same night.
- Draco spirals toward "I don't deserve what I have." Theo stops him. Sits on the coffee table in front of Draco's chair. Reaffirms the three France sentences: "You deserve good things. You're allowed to want. And you know how to hold something without breaking it."
- Draco asks "Tell me what to do." Theo: "We trust her."
- Forehead to forehead. Hands joined. Luna sits in Jackie's exact spot on the sofa.

### Wednesday, January 30th

- **Draco takes the day off work.** First sick day ever. Sends a terse owl to Henderson.
- Morning: Draco attempts to cook breakfast. Burns the eggs catastrophically. Compares Theo's blood bags to Capri Sun. First genuine laughter of the week.
- Draco places a mug of tea on the right side table for Theo — who cannot drink it. "Because that's where it goes."
- Draco finds Jackie's wedding ring on the dresser. Theo pulls him out of the spiral: "You're staring at a ring you can't wear belonging to a marriage you can't have, in a bedroom with a poster of another man on the door."
- **Evening: Theo goes out after dark.** Runs across London rooftops — Notting Hill, Bayswater, Hyde Park. The Malice unfurls, senses expand. Theo promised Draco to stay north of the river. Keeps the promise.
- Sits by the Serpentine. The Malice admits to wanting Jackie — "Not to feed from. Just—" — and can't finish the sentence. First time He's articulated a want that isn't hunger. Theo: "We're not becoming less. We're learning how to be something without her in the room."
- Returns at 12:20 AM, an hour late. Draco waited with cold tea and an unread Prophet. "We won't speak of the fox."

### Thursday, January 31st

- **Draco takes the rest of the week off.**
- The house has developed a routine: tea and blood at the counter, library mornings, meals together (only Draco eats), Theo's mug placed untouched in its spot.
- **Morning: Proposal conversation begins.** Draco asks for Theo's help. Jackie said she doesn't want a ring (she already has the Protean ring from Draco). "Not a ring" forces them to rethink what a proposal IS.
- Extended brainstorming. Theo challenges Draco: "You're thinking like Lucius. Jackie doesn't want a Malfoy proposal. She wants a Draco proposal." They identify the direction: not claiming, but giving. Something she's never had. Something freely given without pain or obligation.
- **Afternoon: Theo reveals the February 2000 annulment conversation.** Jackie once asked Theo about annulling their marriage so she could marry Draco (post-dowry ruling). Theo shut it down — said he'd leave if she annulled. Admits now it was fear disguised as principle. He was afraid Jackie would choose Draco if the contract wasn't binding her.
- **Theo says "I love you" to Draco.** Unprompted. In the library. On a Thursday. "She's not here now. So I'm saying it again. Just to you."
- Draco responds not with the words but with the want: "I want to be the person who gets to see that. Not in a room full of Lavignes. Just — me."
- Draco needs a framework, a word for what they are. Theo: "Then we don't need a word." Draco: "I need *something*."
- **Evening: Draco plays *Rêverie* (Debussy) in the library.** Theo listens. The music does what words couldn't — moves through the space without architecture or labels.
- Theo sits on the floor beside Draco's chair. Draco's fingers find his hair. They stay like that.
- Theo names the small acts: "Tea you can't drink, placed on the right side table because that's where it goes." Draco has been loving Theo on Tuesday without recognizing it.
- **Late evening:** Draco falls asleep on the living room sofa. Theo stays beside him all night. At 3:30 AM, Draco's sleeping hand finds Theo's on the cushion between them. Neither lets go.

### Friday, February 1st

- **Morning:** Draco wakes on the sofa with a destroyed neck and his hand in Theo's. Discovers Theo watched him sleep for seven hours. Theo: "You reached." Draco chooses not to pull his hand away.
- Theo fixes Draco's neck — massage on the sofa, cold hands on warm muscle. Intimate in the register of maintenance and care, not crisis.
- Draco finishes the Franck duet confession: "I wanted to be the person who got to see that. Just me." His version of "I love you."
- **Afternoon: The proposal gift crystallizes.** They walk through Haven House room by room and discover Jackie's love letters — written in sticky notes, labels, marginalia, sewn-in tags across every room. Shopping lists annotated with personality. Blood bags ranked by preference. Sauce jars with instructions. A plant labeled "Night-bloomer. Flowers only open after sunset. Thought of T." A note on Theo's pillow he never read: "Welcome home. I'm sorry it took so long."
- The proposal gift: **a letter. Written by both of them. Two handwritings on the same page.** Draco writes the proposal in his hand. Theo writes his part in his. The thing Jackie's never had — someone writing BACK. Not in code, not disguised as logistics. Directly. The words themselves.
- They agree not to write it today. "We write it when we're ready."
- **Evening: First kiss on the living room sofa.** Draco crosses the empty cushion. "Tonight I'm choosing." Chosen, not crisis-driven, not hunger-justified.
- They move from living room to stairs to Theo's bedroom on the fourth floor.
- **Intimate scene (Theo leads).** Slow, gentle, deliberate. Theo undresses Draco. Learns his body methodically — every reaction catalogued, every gasp mapped. The temperature differential (cold hands on warm skin) makes every touch a small shock. Draco comes apart. Theo holds him through it.
- Theo doesn't let Draco undress him — "tonight is yours." Not reciprocal by design: Draco needed to receive without performing.
- Draco: "Your turn. Tomorrow." Theo promises.
- Draco asks Theo to stay in the bed. Theo stays. The Malice asks "Are you happy?" without edge. Theo: "Yeah. I am."

### Saturday, February 2nd

- **Morning:** Draco wakes in Theo's bed. Hand still joined. Neck destroyed again.
- **Intimate scene (Draco leads).** Draco takes the lighter from Theo's hand — "Not today." Removes his grounding object. Undresses him. Leads for the first time without Jackie present, without crisis, without hunger as justification. Reads Theo's body through grip and jaw rather than pulse and breath. Theo lets go. The Malice is present and acknowledged — Draco: "I meant it for both of you."
- **Afternoon:** They clean the house together. Dishes, cushions, blankets. Domestic preparation for Jackie's return. Draco washes, Theo dries.
- Draco sits at the piano and composes — original music, a melody in C minor. Not from sheet music. Built in real time, phrase by phrase. Theo tells him to write it down. Draco notates it on the back of his Chopin score.
- **Evening — the last hour:** Jackie's flight has landed. She'll be home between 7 and 8 PM.
- They stand in the entry hall. Theo notices the third coat hook Jackie installed before leaving — for a man who doesn't own a coat and can't use the front door in daylight.
- Theo says it aloud: "This week wasn't because she was gone. It wasn't loneliness or proximity. It was real." Draco: "I know where your mug goes."
- They hold hands in the hallway. The house waits for Jackie to come home.

## Key Narrative Artifacts from This Arc

- **The mug:** Earl Grey, placed on Theo's side table daily though he can't drink it. "Because that's where it goes."
- **"Tuesday love":** Private vocabulary for small domestic acts as emotional language. Originated from Draco: "I don't know how to love you when it's just Tuesday."
- **The proposal gift:** A joint letter — two handwritings on one page. Not yet written.
- **Draco's composition:** Original melody in C minor, notated on the back of a Chopin score. Untitled.
- **Jackie's note to Theo:** "Welcome home. I'm sorry it took so long." — found face-down under his lighter, unread all week until Friday.
- **The third coat hook:** Brass, installed by Jackie before departure. Empty. Waiting.
- **The clock:** Charmed Hogwarts-era clock in Draco's room. Announces temperature, moon phase. Emotional state function disabled after it said "anxious" fourteen times in one morning.
