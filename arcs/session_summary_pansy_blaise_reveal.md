## **SESSION SUMMARY: The Reveal**

### **January 29, 2002 (Evening)**

---

### **PRE-SCENE: Letters**

**Draco's Invitation (sent January 28):**

* Casual tone designed to seem like loneliness, not orchestration
* Invited Pansy and Blaise to Haven House for dinner, citing Jackie's departure
* Postscript gave away the real stakes: "There's something I need to discuss with you both. In person. Not by owl."
* Warned them to walk from the corner — wards bounce Apparition into the neighbour's hedgerow

**Pansy's Reply (returned within the hour):**

* Called the postscript "the most suspicious sentence in the English language"
* Confirmed attendance; brought wine; Blaise "brought himself, which he considers a gift"

---

### **SCENE 1: Pre-Arrival — Draco and Theo Prepare**

**Location:** Haven House, hallway and dining room
**Time:** 6:47 PM

**Setup:**

* Table set for four by Caramel — three plates, one empty goblet with folded napkin swan
* Draco spiraling: changed ties three times, rearranged candelabra repeatedly, abandoned tie entirely
* Theo on the staircase, watching, too still

**Key Beats:**

* Theo calls out Draco's spiraling ("You've touched that candelabra six times")
* Both acknowledge Jackie's absence — Theo: "She'd make Pansy feel like she was figuring it out herself instead of being told." Draco: "And instead they get me."
* Theo reframes Draco's role: "Jackie opens doors. You hold rooms. Different skill."
* Both carry resentment that Samuel Carrow forced this timeline — Theo admits Samuel was right: "Pansy and Blaise should have known months ago. I was a coward about it."
* Theo asks Draco to let Blaise's hex land: "He's earned that much."
* Theo retreats to the library; Draco takes position downstairs alone

---

### **SCENE 2: Arrival**

**Time:** 7:28 PM (two minutes early — Pansy's doing)

**Key Moments:**

* Pansy enters first, immediately clocks: three coat hooks in use (was two in October), new photographs, Draco looking terrible
* She navigates the house from memory — this is not her first visit (Snake Soirée, October)
* Blaise follows: unhurried, unreadable, says Draco's name once and lets it do the work
* Caramel has cooked rosemary lamb and fig compote

**The Fourth Setting:**

* Pansy spots the empty goblet immediately but doesn't comment
* After one round of deflection ("Eat first"), she confronts it directly: "There are four place settings."
* Draco tells them: "The fourth setting is for Theodore."

---

### **SCENE 3: The Reveal**

**Pansy's Reaction:**

* No surprise — confirmation. Eyes close for two breaths, then open bright with vindication
* "I *knew* it."
* Fury follows: six months outside the circle, watching Jackie grieve wrong and unable to ask why
* Grips Draco's wrist hard enough to leave nail marks: "How long have you known?"
* Chooses fury over hurt because fury is easier to hold

**Blaise's Reaction:**

* Goes flat. Emptied. Mask pulled across fast to cover what's underneath
* One word: "Explain."
* Stands, draws wand, holds it at his side — not raised, not aimed. Present.
* "Call him down."

---

### **SCENE 4: Theo Enters**

**The Entrance:**

* Theo comes downstairs at human pace — deliberately audible, giving Blaise the warning
* Stops in the dining room doorway
* Looks at Pansy first (easier to read), then Blaise

**The Hex:**

* Blaise: "You look like shit." / Theo: "I'm dead. Technically."
* Blaise casts *Depulso* — deliberate, not reflexive. Banishing Charm sends Theo into the hallway wall, cracking plaster
* "That is for six months."
* Theo catches himself in a crouch, brushes off plaster dust: "Fair."
* Blaise's voice cracks on "You absolute bastard" — doesn't bother to fix it

---

### **SCENE 5: Dinner — Questions**

**Information Exchanged:**

* Full account of the turning: France, August, the curse, Filippo's blood, heart stopping, waking
* Filippo de' Medici identified as sire (unintentional)
* Five months in Florence learning control
* Jackie visited twice in December (weekend trip + Christmas)

**Pansy's Deductions:**

* Connected Jackie's December trips — "She told me it was Lavigne family business"
* Realized why Jackie's grief never tracked: "I watched Jackie walk around pretending to be fine and I *knew* something didn't fit"

**Who Knows:**

* Jackie, Draco, Filippo and household, Madame Lavigne
* NOT Potter, NOT the Ministry
* Samuel Carrow — found out two days ago when he visited Haven House

**Vampiric Details Shared:**

* Enhanced senses: hearing (heartbeat through walls), speed, strength
* Wand magic permanently gone
* Weaknesses: sunlight, silver, garlic, sensory overwhelm, invitation requirement
* Jackie's invented sunproofing spell (Heliophobus) allows Theo to stand behind glass
* Can't eat — "Food's off the table. Literally."
* Pre-fed before dinner ("Like a toddler before a dinner party")

**Emotional Beat:**

* Pansy's composure cracks — eyes wet, voice hitching — when she admits she couldn't interrogate a widow about whether her husband was really dead
* Seals it back up with: "I need more wine"

---

### **SCENE 6: The Samuel Carrow Discussion**

**Pansy's Assessment:**

* Notes Samuel has changed his pattern with Jackie — no longer the boy who rotated through girls weekly
* "He's figured out that patience works better than pressure, and he is *very* patient with her"
* Points out Jackie is currently in New Orleans with Samuel and his band for a week

**Theo's Admissions:**

* Samuel has been visiting Jackie since August, positioning himself as present without pushing
* "He thought I was dead, and he saw a door and he's been standing in it"
* Samuel set the ultimatum that forced this dinner — tell Pansy and Blaise before he returns from the States

**Samuel's Parting Words (from the Lamb & Flag meeting):**

* Theo reveals to the table that Samuel said he won't touch Jackie "unless she asks" — and if she begs, nothing will stop him
* Theo acknowledges he still owes Samuel a debt from the pub meeting

**Pansy's Uncomfortable Question:**

* "Jackie has never been monogamous. She's forced both of you into this arrangement. So if she wanted something with Samuel — is it even fair to stop her?"
* Theo's answer: "Jackie makes her own choices. I don't own her."
* Nobody at the table believes him — his hand is white-knuckled in Luna's fur, his eyes briefly shift darker at the edges

**The Malice (Internal, Not Spoken Aloud):**

* *"We would drain every last drop of blood from her veins before We let her take up with Samuel Carrow."*
* Theo suppresses this; answers with the "right" words instead

**Blaise's Observation:**

* Notes that Samuel's change in behavior is either a longer con than he's capable of, or genuine feeling — "Neither option is comfortable"

**Draco's Admission:**

* Acknowledges that Samuel stayed when both he and Theo left Jackie in different ways
* "He didn't stay for noble reasons. But he stayed."

**Pansy Discovers the Proposal:**

* Jackie told Pansy about Draco's proposal permission three days ago
* Pansy incredulous that Draco is planning a proposal "while this is happening"
* Draco confirms: February timeline, no ring (Protean ring), Jackie's rules

---

### **SCENE 7: Evening Winds Down**

**Quieter Moments:**

* Pansy demands to see the Quiet Room — "I want to see where the vampire sleeps"
* Her reaction to the mini-fridge of blood bags: "These are just bags? I expected something more gothic."
* Conversation shifts to Florence details: Filippo's palazzo, Emanuele the ghoul, repairing chairs, the Franck sonata

**Blaise and Draco Alone (while Pansy tours the Quiet Room):**

* Blaise: "He moves differently. Too fast. Then he catches himself and slows down."
* Reveals Theo spent three months at his flat in 2000 when the triad was fractured — showed up, drank whiskey, slept on the sofa, dueled in the garden
* "I'm glad he's alive. And I'm furious you didn't tell me. Those two things aren't going to resolve tonight."
* Drops a critical observation: at the Soirée, Samuel touched Jackie's arm twice, held contact two seconds longer than friendly. She didn't pull away.

**The Goodbye:**

* Pansy kisses Theo's cheek — hard, deliberate, lipstick-leaving: "If you ever die again, I will find a necromancer, drag you back, and kill you myself"
* Blaise shakes Theo's hand, holds it for five seconds, grip tightening once
* To Draco about the proposal: "Don't overthink it."
* Blaise's parting line: "Don't be sorry. Be worth it."

---

### **SCENE 8: Theo and Draco Alone — The Reckoning**

**Location:** Haven House sitting room
**Trigger:** Pansy's question about Jackie's choices is now a live wire between them

**Draco's Unraveling:**

* Admits he spent October through January believing Samuel's presence was good for Jackie — she was laughing, lighter, herself again
* Told himself a story because the alternative was admitting he didn't deserve what he had
* Begins to say "I don't deserve—" before Theo cuts him off

**Theo's Intervention:**

* ***"Don't."*** — single word, enough force to stop the room
* Sits directly in front of Draco, knees almost touching, inescapable
* Recites the three France sentences back to him: "You deserve good things. You're allowed to want. And you know how to hold something without breaking it."
* "You don't get to unlearn them because Pansy Parkinson asked a hard question at dinner."

**The July Truth:**

* Draco senses Theo is holding back — "You know more than you've told me. About Samuel."
* Theo reveals: July wasn't just a kiss attempt. Jackie was in Samuel's flat, in his bed, almost naked. They almost slept together. She stopped it and told Theo the same night.
* THAT was the real reason for the July restrictions — not the kiss, but the *almost*
* Draco's reaction: not rage. Stillness. His hands fall open on his knees. Structural collapse.
* "She told you. Not me."
* Theo explains: the bond meant she couldn't hide it from him. She chose truth over concealment. He chose not to tell Draco because Draco would have broken.
* Draco doesn't deny it.

**The Heartbeat:**

* Theo admits he heard Jackie's heartbeat speed up when Samuel arrived at Haven House on the 25th
* "It doesn't mean she would act on it. But it's there. It's real. And pretending it isn't doesn't protect any of us."

**Draco's Breaking Point:**

* "Tell me what to do." — first time he's ever asked Theo that outside of a bedroom
* Theo's answer: "We trust her. That's all we can do."
* Theo's reassurance: "She's the one she chose. He's the one she *almost* chose. And almost isn't the same thing."

**The Malice (Present but Silent):**

* Running underneath Theo's measured words the entire scene
* His certainty that He would destroy Samuel before allowing Jackie to choose him is the bass note beneath Theo's faith in Jackie's choices
* The dissonance between what Theo says and what He feels is unresolved

---

### **CHARACTER STATES AT SESSION END**

**Theo:**

* Relieved the secret is out with Pansy and Blaise — Samuel's condition met
* Carrying the weight of having revealed the July truth to Draco
* The Malice is watchful, possessive, and completely certain about Samuel in a way Theo can't afford to be
* Trusts Jackie's choices with his conscious mind; his body and the Malice tell a different story

**Draco:**

* Structurally shaken — the July revelation has recontextualized months of assumptions
* The three France sentences are under threat: "I deserve good things" is wobbling
* First time asking Theo for direction outside of intimacy — a significant shift in their dynamic
* Holding onto "almost isn't the same thing" like a lifeline

**Pansy:**

* Fury and relief in equal measure — vindicated in her suspicion, wounded by the exclusion
* Now has the full picture (minus Draco/Theo's romantic involvement)
* Will be processing the Samuel situation independently — she has Opinions
* Left with lipstick on Theo's cheek and an empty bottle of Côtes du Rhône

**Blaise:**

* Glad and furious simultaneously — won't resolve tonight
* Has filed the Soirée arm-touch observation for future use
* Reassembling his picture of Theo against the reality of what Theo has become
* "Don't be sorry. Be worth it." — the only five words that mattered
