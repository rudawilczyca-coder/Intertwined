## Scene summary

Jackie, Draco, and Filippo arrived at the London court with bound Leandro as leverage. The Seneschal led them through a sitting room where humans were fed upon, into the audience room where Viola received them. Nell began treating Leandro's silver wound. Draco demanded to see Theo; Viola required settling the purpose of the visit first.

Jackie declared herself part of Filippo's household. Viola asked Filippo to acknowledge them; he did. The terms discussion began: Viola wanted presentation, summons compliance, and unspecified services. Filippo insisted on seeing Theo first.

Downstairs, the Malice's proximity overwhelmed Jackie through the soul bond — hunger and fury slamming through her shields. She collapsed outside Theo's door. Draco helped her to a separate room. She disclosed the bond contact to Draco: the Malice could speak to her and push emotion through, and she could not fully shield at close range.

Nell obtained permission for a stored-blood feed. Theo drank the flask with Filippo present. The Malice, slightly quieted by the feed, sent one measured message down the bond: "Fed now, Little Wolf. Softer." Theo could not stop that contact.

Jackie asked Draco to go to Theo. Draco saw him weakened on the bed, dressings on both arms. Theo asked whether Jackie could bear being near him; Draco refused to promise on her behalf. He returned to ask her; she chose to cross the landing herself.

**Reunion:** Jackie ran to Theo, gathered him against her. She called him an idiot. Theo held still, then slowly brought his arms up. He admitted choosing detention over releasing the Malice, apologised for deciding alone, and said he was done making that decision without his family.

**Dragon confession:** Jackie told Theo about crossing the dragon enclosure wards at the World Cup. He was angry but admitted he would have wanted to see her run. The Malice's private commentary drew reluctant amusement. Draco reminded them both that he had had to wait for the part where she came back.

**Live feed from Draco:** Jackie offered either spouse's blood to get Theo upstairs. Theo chose Draco — to keep Jackie's shields intact and because feeding from her remained the harder risk. Filippo supervised. Draco told Theo to stop; Theo stopped. Wound healing began but was not complete.

**Return upstairs:** Theo walked the stairs using the rail, with support. The trio and Filippo returned to the audience room. Theo bowed to Viola. He was standing, bandaged, visibly depleted but upright.

**Final negotiation:**
- Jackie mentioned the three Ferrara-backed Primogen debts. Viola asked whether she was invoking or merely noting them.
- Jackie warned they had their own London ties. Viola corrected her sharply: leverage is not the issue; jurisdiction is. Every vampire in London answers to Viola, full stop.
- Viola presented the choice to Filippo: keep your sire's claim and take Theo to Florence, or **release him** — surrender formal sire authority so Theo is presented to London directly. Haven House recognised as residence. He answers to Viola for feeding, secrecy, conduct, and summons.
- Three days granted for Theo to recover and return with his answer. No summons required; he will present himself.

**Scene endpoint:** The trio departed the audience room. Theo observed: "She's not offering freedom. She's offering belonging. Those aren't the same thing." Draco answered: "But you already know which one you want." The Malice murmured *Home*. Theo's hand brushed Jackie's as they walked.
