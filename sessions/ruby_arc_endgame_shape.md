## THE AGREED SPINE (decided 2026-07-06)

1. **NO transformation in the Flat scene.** Locked reasoning, so future-us doesn't relitigate:
   - It would invert Thursday's theme — the wand left on the hall table means the danger and the courage are *human*; a wolf eruption makes the supernatural solve a human problem.
   - Mechanics don't support it: trigger is dormant gene + vampire proximity; no Theo in the flat, no full moon (July 2002 full moon = **Wed July 24**, verified by Alice). A trauma-trigger would be a rule invented at the moment of maximum convenience.
   - **Robbie doesn't get to own her first transformation.** It's the biggest event of Ruby's life; spending it in his scene puts his name in it forever. He's stolen enough.

2. **The reveal is a PRICE, not an accident and not a confession.** The Flat scene drives at its own ceiling ("nothing short of death/disfigurement") until Draco does the thing he swore not to: **wandless magic, in front of her**, because the alternative is watching someone die. He pays the one cost he came wandless to avoid — and pays it for her. *(Divergence note: if Thursday resolves human-only — cold fury and bare hands suffice — the reveal shifts to the aftermath instead; see OPEN HINGE below. The arc still works; the fight just arrives by a different door.)*

3. **Ministry at plot-speed, not flat-speed.** The magic pings; the response is an *inquiry* (that night / next morning), not an ambush mid-scene. Keeps the Flat three-body to the end.

4. **The Obliviation question becomes the engine of the fight.** Ruby learns everything at once — magic is real, Draco is a wizard, the marriage, the "records job" — AND that the standard procedure for a Muggle who knows is having it taken out of her head. **The carve-out:** werewolves sit inside the Statute, and Ruby is a dormant carrier. Discovery route: Ministry screening, and/or **Charlie stepping out of his two-floors-up life** to invoke it for her — the neighbor revealed as a werewolf, the first face of the world she's about to belong to. She keeps her memory on a technicality written for monsters, and finds out she *is* the technicality.

5. **The fight and the split (aftermath days, ~Jul 12–14).** Not just "you lied to me" — the deeper wound: *"your world had a procedure for un-knowing me, and you dated me anyway with that in your pocket."* The purple tabs, the "yours" parallel, the sealed annex — none of it required; the fight has teeth on the reveal alone. Split-up lands. Draco does not fight the verdict; he takes it standing, the way he took the interrogation at the table.

6. **The fortnight with a clock on it (~Jul 12–23).** NOT dead air — the estrangement runs concurrent with the countdown to the moon. Ruby processing betrayal AND impending monsterhood; Charlie the only person who knows the road she's on; every day a decision about who she wants in the room when it happens. Draco at Haven House, wrecked, holding to the split because she asked. Jackie/Theo lanes react per canon (their scenes live elsewhere).

7. **Wed July 24 — THE MOON.** First transformation, the arc's true summit. **The relocated irony lives here:** first turnings are supposed to be feral, mindless, dangerous to everyone — and wolf-Ruby, mid-split, furious with him in every human register she has, **recognizes Draco. Protects him.** Her body chooses him before her heart re-does. The irony Alice wanted ("her saving him instead of the other way around"), spent where it's earned — about *them*, not about Robbie. Reconciliation engine, not chaos beat. *(How feral, who's present, whether Theo's vampire-proximity plays a triggering role — all unplanned, deliberately. Summit visible, route unclimbed.)*

## OPEN HINGE (explicitly undecided)

**Forced reveal vs. chosen reveal.** Default shape above = forced (the desperate spell at the ceiling). The alternative both of us rate: Thursday resolves *without* magic, and Draco — in the wreckage, before the Ministry or anyone else can — **tells her himself.** Quieter, and it changes the fight's flavor (a chosen truth vs. a caught one). Decide nothing; Thursday's live play will make this choice for us, which is the point of playing it live.
