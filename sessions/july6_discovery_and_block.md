## §III. The apology conversation → THE LETTER (new canon obligation)

- Ruby: "Have you ever come clean and apologized to your Carrie?"

## §VI. The confrontation (~9:00 a.m.)

Staging: chair across the room, seated, below her sightline, hands visible, phone dark — full Wales protocol. She woke, saw the phone: "Fuck, Draco… I can explain—"

- **Order of guilt, his design:** confession first ("I read them. All of them… That's mine. I won't dress it as concern"), *I am not angry with you* delivered before anything else.

He's negotiating for a doorway"; the Corsa text = "a leash, shown to you across a distance… Restraint that is volunteered as a gift is a threat wearing its Sunday coat. - **THE CLAUSE:** "You don't carry him alone anymore… the *alone* part ends this morning." → **Ruby accepted: "I can promise you I'll tell you everything moving forward."** (Canon, witnessed, entered.)
- **Ruby's push-back (her state, canon):** terrified since Monday, five nights of nightmares; Robbie "poisons everything good I have until I don't believe I deserve it"; wanted to **meet him** (public place + Vex) — fear ladder: meeting → or he finds her in Morriston → or taunts the family; *"if I cannot deal with him on my own, then I really am as weak as he claims."* Also flagged his projected escalation register ("'You either come back to me or I drag you myself'… 'you ungrateful bitch'"). - **Draco's dismantling:** (1) "Who wrote that exam?" — strength-as-defined-by-your-abuser is rigged both ways; his credential: *"when I was seventeen I spent an entire year trying to fix something catastrophic completely alone… People nearly died of my strength. Responsibility clause: *"If silence makes him escalate, then he escalates against both of us and whatever else loves you… You will never again be the only person in the room when it hurts."* Grass promise reaffirmed (won't go looking).
