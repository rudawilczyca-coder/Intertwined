## Diagnosis (why it felt clunky)

The scene is NOT clunky — §V (Alice's own Jackie+Ruby block) is the best-voiced part of the file. The blockage was anxiety + two real jams, not a failure of the writing:

1. **Ruby was carrying a beat that isn't hers.** The werewolf-gene awakening is rendered exterior-only — Theo's loaded gun, Ruby's interior untouched, she does not know. So Ruby has nothing to *play* there. Her sole scene-job: **survive the assessment without performing for it.** One clean want. The supernatural plot runs silent on Theo's side of the table.

2. **Two-character load is a switching problem, not a voice problem.** Cold-switching Ruby→Jackie in the same breath is what clunks.

## Techniques (the fix)

- **Never write the two girls back-to-back.** Put a boy's beat (Theo/Draco — Sable's) between every girl-beat as a buffer/reset. This is the volley the file is already built on.
- **Anchor each to a different body.** Jackie = menu, forearms on table, the look people slide away from. Ruby = glasses-push, claw clip, the uncrossed inch of cloth, empty plate. Unsure who's speaking? Go to the hands first; diction follows the body.
- **Opposite leak-styles.** Jackie says the quiet part LOUD (announces the assessment). Ruby holds everything and lets it LEAK (counted breath, the not-reaching). Oversharer-on-purpose vs overcontroller-who-fails. Lean on the split; they can't blur.
