## ONE-PARAGRAPH SUMMARY

Robbie came to the Soho flat late morning Thursday July 11 — premeditated, drunk-sharp, armed (yellow-handled Stanley knife; no gun ever appeared). Ruby endured, appeased, lied, and finally stood up to him; he smashed her phone, put a knife to her, and she barricaded in the bathroom. Jackie's warning charm (planted in Ruby's jacket at Veeraswamy) burned black; Draco handed Jackie his wand at the Ministry and Apparated to the back alley **deliberately wandless**. He came to the door as a delivery man, was warned off by Ruby's written card (*GO AWAY. HE HAS A KNIFE.*), refused, broke in, and fought Robbie hand-to-hand — took real wounds rather than use magic while the knife was on HIM. When Robbie put the blade to **Ruby's** throat, Draco broke the Statute without hesitation: wandless, wordless Protego — a silver-white dome inches from her skin, a bell-toll through the building — that hurled Robbie into the wrecked door. Charlie (revealed to Ruby as a GP) stitched Draco; police took Robbie away in cuffs; Ruby lied smoothly to the police (Robbie "slipped") and is **pressing charges for today AND Swansea last year**. Then, in the aftermath, Draco told her **everything** — wizard, Hogwarts, Ministry of Magic, Jackie a witch/Auror, "Muggle," and the Obliviation law. Ruby's world broke on one hook: *he knew from the first message that her memory could be taken, and let her fall in love anyway.* She threw him out. He went — via Charlie's flat (offscreen conversation, toolbox sounds), then home. **The split landed same-night, in the flat, not in the aftermath days.**

## WHAT DIVERGED FROM THE PLAN (important)

1. **OPEN HINGE resolved: FORCED reveal.** The desperate spell at the ceiling, exactly as the default shape had it. But then Draco chose the full verbal confession *the same evening* — Ruby demanded "now is later" and he delivered all of it on the spot. So it was forced AND owned, in sequence.
2. **The fight/split (endgame spine #5) compressed into Thursday night.** No Jul 12–14 fight window needed — "Get out" already happened. The estrangement clock (~Jul 12–23, spine #6) starts **tomorrow morning, July 12**.
3. **Robbie witnessed magic and went into Muggle custody un-Obliviated.** Live thread the Ministry inquiry must handle (see OPEN THREADS).
4. **Charlie's reveal is PARTIAL.** Ruby now knows he's a GP (the "you're a fucking GP!" moment — she hadn't known). She does NOT know he's a werewolf. Charlie and Draco clearly read each other as non-Muggle (the held looks, "some fellas knit quicker than others," the June pavement standoff referenced) — but nothing was said aloud in front of Ruby.
5. **No transformation, per lock.** Held. Robbie owns nothing of the wolf.

## BLOW-BY-BLOW (condensed, for canon queries)

- **Setup:** Ruby on her period, day off, alone. Charlie took Vex for the day (Jordie playdate) — Robbie watched the dog leave and timed it. Knock ~11:30.
- **Entry:** She opened wide without checking. He forced entry softly — palm on door, foot, shoulder, never a "blow." Clean-shaven, presentable, drunk in the sharp way (vodka under beer). Certificate of Completion — 26-week anger management, Sketty church hall — carried in an army-surplus bag *with the Stanley knife*.
- **The rewrite, performed:** four years vs her "one year" correction; "bad man vs sick man"; Crissy fed him everything (Wales, the shop, the collapse, "the posh fella," the married-man lie); he's been in London **since Tuesday**, hotel by the station, watched her Wednesday with Vex.
- **The trap:** demanded she ring Draco and dump him on speaker. Ruby refused — and Draco's just-arrived text (*report how you feel / I'll come tomorrow*) made faking impossible anyway.
- **Escalations:** he caught her correcting Draco's age ("nobody corrects details on a bloke who's nothing"); read the text over her wrist; **smashed her phone against the kitchen wall** (her only line out — severed). Hair-grab, counter-pin, "make a sound and I'm done before anyone's on the stairs. You know my times."
- **Ruby's gambits, in order:** appease/diffuse/redirect (steak in Regent's Park); bathroom barricade + panic attack (he talked her breathing through the door — in fours, obscene); the surrender-performance ("you really love me" + the kiss, which he *checked* and believed); "Tenerife, I'll go home with you" while pulling him off Draco. Every one bought time; the last one died when he named it: *"You stepped in front of a knife for him. Everything today was for him. He got the real one."*
- **Draco's track:** charm burned black → Jackie ran to his office → he gave HER his wand ("If I take it, I'll use it"), stated the math aloud (magic in front of Ruby = Obliviation = the one cost he won't pay; Aurors out for the identical reason) → Apparated to the back alley → "Delivery for Williams," catalogue-posh.
- **The card:** Ruby signed the fake delivery slip **GO AWAY. HE HAS A KNIFE** in shaking capitals. Draco read it, pocketed it, and stayed. He kept the card (produced it later, blood-marked: "the bravest thing anyone has ever written me — you spent your last message getting ME out").
- **The break-in:** heel beside the chain plate, door into Robbie's skull on the hinge side. "Draco. The posh fella. Crissy undersold me." Jacket wound round the left forearm: **"Show me your times."**
- **The fight:** Draco competent-economical, first counters landed (split lip, rocked him) — then Robbie stopped fencing, ate a punch to buy the clinch, and the hallway did the rest: coat hooks, wall, headbutt (Draco's nose broken), dead-leg knee, blade through the unwound jacket (deep forearm slashes), takedown, ground-pin, head bounced off boards, knife flat on Draco's cheekbone. Draco made **no sound at any point** and drove the gathering static DOWN while the knife was on him. Ruby clocked the silence later: it meant he'd been through worse.
- **Ruby stepped into the knife-line TWICE.** First lunge: Draco yanked her out of it by the torn shirt and took the slash himself. Second: the plea-and-pull-him-off performance.
- **The switch:** once hurting Draco failed to control anything, Robbie took Ruby hostage — blade high under her jaw, presented her at Draco: *"You watch what she costs."*
- **THE MAGIC:** no word, no gesture, one open hand. Silver-white dome inches from her throat, thin as soap-film, absolute; bell struck in the bones of the building; every glass rang; Robbie peeled backward the length of the hallway into the ruined door. Concussed, folded, breathing. The knife spun to the skirting board, "small and yellow and suddenly ridiculous."
- **Aftermath in the flat:** Vex arrived (Ruby's whistle) — textbook grab-and-hold on Robbie's forearm. Charlie arrived, read the room (and Draco) in one long look, offered her the two calls. **Ruby chose: police, yes; ambulance — overruled to "no" by Draco+Charlie** (knife-wound report = his name in records; "nothing he has done to me will outlast the week" — flagged as one of the answers owed). Charlie-the-GP stitched the forearm with her **pink thread** (she knew it was thinner and stronger — Charlie visibly understood what that knowledge cost her), set the broken nose on "one." Ruby taunted Robbie: *"I trained Vex specifically for you."* Draco: "the coldest declaration of war I've ever heard."
- **Police (30 min):** full statement incl. Swansea history; cover story = door swung into him, he slipped on blood, she used his weight. "Not her first time lying to the police — smooth like butter." **Pressing charges, both incidents.** Card left, follow-up promised. Robbie cuffed out early in the process, gone small, white-ringed eyes — no shelf in his head for the light.
- **Her collapse:** two feet from the sofa, knees, shaking. Draco knelt at distance, hands visible, would not touch uninvited (Wales lesson held). **Vex did the work** — trained pressure-weight, heartbeat metronome. Charlie made himself a neighbour again and withdrew.
- **Her anger:** "My life is mine to spend… no one my death would break." "You have Jackie and Theo — I'd rather die than tell them I got you taken from them." And the trap-question: *"You were never in mortal danger, right?"*
- **His corrections, before the reveal:** he WAS in mortal danger the whole time and knew it; the wandless magic "doesn't come when called — it's a flood or nothing, and it came for HER, not for me; I couldn't have summoned it for my own throat and didn't try." The collar terms invoked: *"the clause runs both directions. It always did."* (Her Sunday collar-promise to protect him ↔ his obedience/trust.)
- **THE REVEAL (full, verbal, at her demand — "now is later"):** wizard; Hogwarts named; Ministry of Magic (the "records job" true-but-translated); Jackie a witch and effectively police; **the Veeraswamy charm** (how he knew — "Not intuition. Her."); "Muggle" (the *Carrie* night lie named and owned as "the worst hour of lying I've ever done"); **the Obliviation law** — a Muggle who sees magic has the memory taken, he has watched it done; why he came wandless (magic ≠ saving her; magic = LOSING her — "every scar he gave you, you kept and owned; I would not let them take today and leave the shaking"); tonight registered ("their instruments will have felt it, someone will come asking — tomorrow, perhaps sooner"); his one non-negotiable: **she does not forget.** Withheld, explicitly: **Theo's secret** ("Theo's is Theo's — I'll not hand it over even now"). Werewolves never mentioned. Charlie's nature never mentioned.
- **Her verdict:** the poison hook — he knew the price existed from the first dating-app message and let her pay it blind. Through every milestone (listed them to herself: the matching, first date, first night, the scars, the pond, her parents, meeting his wife). "Get out. I'll be GLAD when they take the memory."
- **His exit:** "Yes." — signed the charge, no defence. Two-item inventory: (1) the plan was always that they'd never touch her memory because he'd never give them cause, whatever it cost him — "I bled today so you would get to keep today"; (2) **if they come and she still wants it gone — choose it in the morning, sober of him; he'll stand aside.** "Wanting to forget a man and being GLAD to are different transactions, and you were raised too honest to confuse them for long." Then: *"Get out is a word."* He obeyed it.
- **The leaving:** up TWO flights first — knuckles on Charlie's door, low voices, a toolbox clink (offscreen; unwritten on purpose) — then down past her landing without pausing, favouring the leg, into Carlisle Street. Ruby alone with Vex, plaster dust, no door, a radio playing down the airshaft.
