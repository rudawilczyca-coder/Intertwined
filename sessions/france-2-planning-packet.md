# France 2.0 — planning packet

*Prepared 2026-08-09 for the next OOC planning conversation. This is a canon
preflight and decision docket, not a fixed outline. Ruby and Sable choose the
arc together; Jackie remains Ruby's exclusively.*

## Fixed launch state

- **Story date at handoff:** Friday, July 26, 2002.
- **Paris:** Jackie proposed four days in Paris with Draco before the final—the
  honeymoon their April wedding never had. Draco accepted. Theo endorsed the
  trip and will hold Haven House until he joins them at the match.
- **Final:** France v Wales, below Fontainebleau, August 10. Pansy's letter
  arrived on Genevieve Lavigne stationery with three tickets. Pansy and the
  Lavigne cousins are expected in the family box.
- **The third ticket matters:** Genevieve counted Theo in writing although he
  is legally dead. His cover identity and travel/feeding safeguards are still
  unresolved. Genevieve's knowledge and intended purpose are open questions,
  not permission to invent her agenda.
- **France 1.0 mirror:** the August 4–11, 2001 Lavigne Luminary presented the
  trio publicly as a unit and ended with the assassination attempt on Draco,
  Theo's death, and his turning. The return therefore carries history before
  anyone says a word.

## The heir talk — canon already on the table

- Narcissa imposed an heir demand on February 27, 2002: heirs within three
  years, with the clock beginning that day. It was a demand, not a suggestion.
- During the July 6 walk to karaoke, Draco said aloud for the first time that
  he wants children: something in him said *yes* fast enough to frighten him.
  He wants a child to receive the Malfoy name as a gift with no debt attached.
- Draco also said that the trio's children conversation stopped when Theo's
  health collapsed in August 2001 and had not been resumed. His answer on July
  6 was filed privately for “a braver hour”; Jackie and Theo haven't heard it.
- The useful dramatic distinction is already supported by canon: **Narcissa's
  heir as scheduled continuation** versus **a child the household actively
  chooses**. The scene shouldn't let her ultimatum own the meaning of their
  decision.

## Decisions for Ruby and Sable—not pre-answered here

1. **Shape:** one Paris/Fontainebleau session, or a short arc with Paris,
   arrival at the box, final, and aftermath as separate movements?
2. **Entry point:** does the heir subject begin privately in Paris, with all
   three after Theo arrives, or because something at the Lavigne box forces the
   private question into the open?
3. **Initiator and knowledge:** who chooses to say the first true sentence,
   and what does each man know before Jackie speaks? Draco's July 6 admission
   is currently his alone.
4. **The actual family question:** wanting children, timing, parenthood among
   three people, legal names/inheritance, safety, and what each person fears.
   Biology, fertility, magical inheritance, and legal mechanisms are
   **unverified** until the story chooses and canonises them.
5. **Genevieve:** what does the third ticket mean—acknowledgement, test,
   protection, leverage, welcome, or some mixture? Her motive must be chosen,
   not inferred from the stationery.
6. **Theo's public face:** reuse Eli Lavigne, whom Genevieve already recognised
   at the wedding, or establish a different cover? This needs an explicit
   continuity choice before the match scene.
7. **Arc endpoint:** what changes by the journey home? A decision isn't
   required; an honestly reopened conversation may be the larger movement.
