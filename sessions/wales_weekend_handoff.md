## Style Reminders

- **Prose target:** 400–800 words per reply, 4–9 paragraphs.
- **Banned patterns:** No "found himself," no "the [noun] of a man who," no "there's a difference" announcers, no "something [verbed]," no triple-dash more than once per scene, no "the kind of [X] that [Y]" more than once per reply. Full list in CLAUDE.md.
